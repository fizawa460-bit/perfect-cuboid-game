Stage35 is the dedicated attack on the Stage29 Class-3 kernel
`K16-C3-MOVING-FIBER-ARITHMETIC` / receiver `R29-FIB2`.
Its purpose is to obtain uniform arithmetic control over the audited moving
fibration architecture, or to prove an exact globally exhaustive finite-fiber
reduction with endpoint lift reconstruction.

## Stage35 proof firewall

Closure of `R29-FIB2` requires more than successful arithmetic on selected
fibers. At minimum Stage35 must certify:

1. the exact fibration/model and field-of-definition interface;
2. the parameter base and all exceptional/bad fibers relevant to the claim;
3. the exact residual space-square/lift predicate back to endpoint data;
4. either a uniform arithmetic/specialization theorem over the moving family or
   a globally exhaustive reduction to finitely many fibers;
5. exact discharge of every exceptional class left by that theorem/reduction;
6. an audited implication back to `R29-FIB2`.

Individual-fiber Chabauty, Mordell--Weil, descent, or integral-point results do
not quantify over the moving base. Geometric fibration coverage does not imply
rational-section coverage, and a marginal rational point does not imply an
endpoint point without the residual lift condition.

Do not promote Stage35 progress to `J12-PARAMETRIC` closure or perfect-cuboid
existence/nonexistence without a separate audited full implication.
