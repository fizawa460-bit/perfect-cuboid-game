# Stage12 post-Stage13 publication plan

Status: deferred until Stage13 reaches a clear milestone.

After Stage13 reaches a clear milestone, return to the frozen Stage12 R09 result and prepare it for possible journal submission.

## Plan

1. Treat R09 as the mathematical baseline and build a submission-grade master proof from it.
2. Before choosing a journal, perform a serious literature review to determine what is already known, whether an equivalent asymptotic has appeared, and what the actual novelty and positioning are.
3. Choose a small set of candidate journals only after that literature review, using journal scope, recent related papers, audience, and author guidelines.
4. Adapt the master manuscript to the first-choice journal rather than preparing a separate general-mathematician version first.
5. Keep standard external results such as Selberg–Delange as cited external theorems; do not re-prove general theory unnecessarily.
6. Fully expose the Stage12-specific proof obligations needed for audit: applicability and uniformity of external theorems, primitive decomposition, region/rectangle transfer, weighted error transfer, and constant derivation.
7. Aim for a manuscript that a competent referee can audit without having to invent missing Stage12-specific lemmas, while avoiding textbook-level expansion of standard background.
8. Before submission, independently re-audit the proof, constants, and computational checks used by the argument.
9. Use normal journal peer review as the principal route to independent third-party mathematical scrutiny, since no private external reviewer is currently assumed.

## Writing policy

The master manuscript should separate mathematical completeness from exposition level. The core theorem, definitions, lemmas, error control, and constants must remain fixed; introduction length, background explanation, appendix placement, notation, bibliography style, and presentation may be adjusted for the target journal.

A separate general-audience or general-mathematician manuscript is not a prerequisite. If later useful, it can be derived from the master manuscript without changing the mathematical core.

## Scope caution

This plan records a publication workflow only. It does not assert novelty, journal suitability, or acceptance. Those questions remain open until the literature review and journal-selection stage are completed.
