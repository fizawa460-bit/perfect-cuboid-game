## 3. Exact raw physical-box degree

\[

All three are homogeneous of degree
\[
2d+4=4+4(a^2+b^2).
\]
Moreover the space-diagonal identity is
\[

\[
\boxed{L(a,b)=4+4(a^2+b^2).}

Any irreducible common factor must divide one of `N,M,A,B`; reducing `X,Y` modulo that factor forces simultaneously a forbidden common factor of `N,M` or of `A,B`. Hence the projective physical-box map has no base point. For each fixed class `(a,b)`, primitive specialization can therefore change height only by an absolute class-dependent constant, and
\[
\boxed{H_{\rm box}(u)=\Theta_{a,b}(H(u)^{L(a,b)}).}
