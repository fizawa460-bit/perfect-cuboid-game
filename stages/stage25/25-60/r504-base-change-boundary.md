## 3. Low-degree base change and rational multisection are the same residual mechanism

Let `phi:P1_u -> P1_k` be a rational finite base change. A new section over `Q(u)` maps its graph to a rational curve on the original surface whose projection to the `k`-line has degree `deg(phi)`: a rational multisection.

Conversely, normalizing a rational multisection gives precisely such a rational base change and a section after pullback. Therefore, for positive-power one-parameter constructions,

```text
R504_RATIONAL_BASE_CHANGE_EQUIVALENT_TO_RATIONAL_MULTISECTION=true
```

A fixed multisection of genus `>1` has only finitely many rational points. A fixed genus-one multisection has only polylogarithmically many rational points of bounded naive height (canonical height is quadratic in the Mordell--Weil index). Hence a new one-parameter positive-power family must come from a rational multisection/base change, or from an additional independently varying family of curves.

This collapses the previously separate `low-degree base change` and `multisection` bullets into one exact rational-curve search.

## 5. Candidate BC1 — monomial quadratic base change `k=u^2`

This is the canonical ramified quadratic self-map of the base. Its twist-killing curve is

\[
C_2:\quad y^2=u^8+1,
\]

a genus-three curve. It has the Klein-four involutions generated by `u->-u` and `u->1/u`. The three elliptic quotients are

\[
Q_0:\ Y^2=X^4+1,
\]
\[
Q_-:\ Y^2=X^4-4X^2+2,
\qquad
Q_+:\ Y^2=X^4+4X^2+2.
\]

Their binary-quartic invariants give

\[
j(Q_0)=1728,\qquad j(Q_-)=j(Q_+)=8000.
\]

The pullbacks of differentials from the three quotients span the three-dimensional space of holomorphic differentials on `C2`; hence

\[
J(C_2)\sim_Q Q_0\times Q_-\times Q_+.
\]

`Q0` is the inherited `E0` factor. At the good prime `3`,

\[
\#E_0(\mathbf F_3)=4,
\quad
\#Q_-(\mathbf F_3)=2,
\quad
\#Q_+(\mathbf F_3)=6.
\]

Thus neither extra quotient is `Q`-isogenous to `E0`. Therefore `Hom_Q(J(C2),E0)` has only the inherited rank-one factor, and the pullback twist over `Q(u)` has no new independent free section.

\]
so generically `g(C_phi)=3`. A new independent pullback section requires an additional `E0`-isogeny factor in `J(C_phi)` beyond the inherited factor.

Thus the remaining degree-two search is no longer an unspecified algebra mutation. It is the precise moduli/isogeny problem

```text
FIND phi of degree 2 such that Hom_Q(J(C_phi),E0) has rank >=2,
```

## 8. Growing multiples — now quantitatively closed against a quarter-power upgrade

The Kummer description identifies the graph curves generated by the audited rank-one lattice with the Lattes maps induced by multiplication on `E0`. If `t_n(k)` is the quartic parameter produced by the multiple `[n]P`, then for odd physical multiples

\[
\deg(t_n:\mathbf P^1_k\to\mathbf P^1_t)=n^2.
\]

This follows from the degree-two quotient `pi:E0->P1`: `pi o [n]=L_n o pi`, so

\[
2n^2=2\deg L_n.
\]

The displayed `3P` formula has degree `9`, providing the first nondegenerate check.

Now let a primitive physical R504 box have space diagonal at most `B`, and let `H_X,H_Y` be the two integral face diagonals adjacent to the shared edge. Similarity ratios are unchanged by primitive reduction and

\[
\left(\frac{t}{k}\right)^2=\frac{H_X-X}{H_X+X},
\qquad
(kt)^2=\frac{H_Y+Y}{H_Y-Y}.
\]

Every integer in these four numerator/denominator slots has absolute value at most `2B`. For logarithmic projective height `h`,

\[
2h(t/k)\le\log(2B),\qquad
2h(kt)\le\log(2B),
\]
so

\[
\boxed{h(t)\le\tfrac12\log(2B).}
\]

Let `h_L` be the canonical quotient height for the fixed Lattes system. It satisfies

\[
h_L(t_n(k))=n^2h_L(k),
\qquad h_L=h+O(1),
\]
with the comparison constant independent of `n`. Hence

\[
h(k)\le \frac{\log(2B)}{2n^2}+O(1).
\]

There are `O(exp(2H))` rational numbers of logarithmic height at most `H`, so for each odd `n>=3` the number of rational `k` parameters is

\[
O(B^{1/n^2}).
\]

For rational `k`, a lift to `E0` has algebraic degree at most two. Northcott finiteness gives a positive minimum canonical height among non-torsion degree-`<=2` lifts. Therefore `h(t)<=O(log B)` forces

\[
|n|=O(\sqrt{\log B})
\]
outside a finite torsion/preperiodic set. Summing all nondegenerate odd multiples gives

\[
\boxed{
N_{R504,\mathrm{all\ multiples}}(B)
\ll B^{1/9}\sqrt{\log B}.
}
\]

This counts parameter pairs before the exactly-two and canonical postfilters, so those filters can only decrease it. In particular

\[
B^{1/9}\sqrt{\log B}=o(B^{1/4}).
\]

Thus growing multiples of the already-audited rank-one graph lattice cannot improve the global quarter-power lower.
