## Mathematical handoff

The two known independent directions lie in different `V4` characters on the untwisting genus-three curve, forcing zero Rosati cross term.  Both basis directions have exact twist-descent map degree `8`; therefore the lattice norm is `8(a^2+b^2)`.  For a physical lift, `x/H=-4t^2/(t^4+1)` has receiver degree four, so `deg(t)=2(a^2+b^2)`.

The known physical free-lattice coset has `a` odd and `b` even.  Norm `1` is only the degenerate `+/-P` class; norm `5` is the first nondegenerate possibility and is attained by `+/-P +/-2R`.  The physical box degree is `4+4(a^2+b^2)`, so every fixed nondegenerate class contributes at most `O(B^(1/(2(a^2+b^2+1))))` parameter values before the exactly-two filter.  The audited `P+2R` class already attains `Theta(B^(1/12))`.
