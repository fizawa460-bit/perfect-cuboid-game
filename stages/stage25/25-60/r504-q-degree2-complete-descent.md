## Exact equivalence relation

R504 does **not** classify quadratic dynamical systems up to conjugacy. It classifies finite degree-two base-change maps
\[
\phi:\mathbf P^1_u\to\mathbf P^1_k
\]
with the target coordinate `k` fixed, modulo source reparametrization by `PGL_2(Q)`.

This distinction is essential.

## The deck involution is Q-rational

Let `phi in Q(u)` have degree two and characteristic zero. Then
\[
[\mathbf Q(u):\mathbf Q(\phi)]=2.
\]
The extension is separable, hence Galois of degree two. Its unique nontrivial automorphism
\[
\iota\in\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathbf Q(\phi)}\mathbf Q(u)
\]
is a Q-automorphism of `Q(u)`, therefore is represented by an involution in `PGL_2(Q)`.

Conversely, `Q(phi)=Q(u)^<iota>`.

Thus source-equivalence classes of degree-two maps are controlled first by Q-conjugacy classes of involutions in `PGL_2(Q)`.

## Q-conjugacy classes of involutions

Choose a trace-zero matrix representative
\[
M=\begin{pmatrix}p&q\\r&-p\end{pmatrix},\qquad M^2=(p^2+qr)I.
\]
Since `M` is invertible, `p^2+qr != 0`. Its invariant squareclass
\[
\Delta=[p^2+qr]\in \mathbf Q^*/(\mathbf Q^*)^2
\]
is the fixed-point discriminant squareclass.

There are two cases.

### Split case

If `Delta` is a square, the involution has two Q-rational fixed points and is Q-conjugate to
\[
u\mapsto -u.
\]
Its invariant field is
\[
\mathbf Q(u^2).
\]
Therefore every split degree-two map is, after a source `PGL_2(Q)` change,
\[
\boxed{\phi(u)=\frac{A u^2+B}{C u^2+D}},\qquad AD-BC\ne0.
\]
The previously used family `(au^2+b)/(u^2+1)` is only a strict subfamily and is not all split maps.

### Nonsplit case

If `Delta` is nonsquare, the involution has conjugate quadratic fixed points. For a representative `d in Q*/(Q*)^2` of the squareclass, it is Q-conjugate to
\[
u\mapsto d/u.
\]
The fixed field is generated by
\[
t=u+d/u,
\]
so
\[
\mathbf Q(u)^{\langle\iota\rangle}=\mathbf Q(t).
\]
Therefore every nonsplit degree-two map is, after source `PGL_2(Q)` change,
\[
\boxed{\phi(u)=\frac{A(u+d/u)+B}{C(u+d/u)+D}}
\]
or, after clearing `u`,
\[
\boxed{\phi(u)=\frac{A(u^2+d)+Bu}{C(u^2+d)+Du}},\qquad AD-BC\ne0.
\]
Changing `d` by a rational square gives a source-equivalent form by scaling `u`; hence only the squareclass of `d` matters.

## Completeness proof

Let `phi` be arbitrary of degree two over Q.

1. Its unique deck involution `iota` is Q-rational.
2. Q-conjugate `iota` to the split representative `u->-u` or the nonsplit representative `u->d/u` according to its discriminant squareclass.
3. In the resulting source coordinate, `Q(phi)` equals the fixed field of that representative.
4. The displayed invariant `t=u^2` or `t=u+d/u` generates that fixed rational function field.
5. Any two generators of `Q(t)` over Q differ by a target Möbius transformation, so with target coordinate fixed the given function `phi` is exactly `phi=M(t)` for some `M in PGL_2(Q)`, yielding the two formulas above.

Hence the split+nonsplit list is complete for the exact R504 source-equivalence problem. This certifies completeness of the normal-form species, not uniqueness of the coefficient tuple `(A,B,C,D)` after residual source-centralizer actions.
