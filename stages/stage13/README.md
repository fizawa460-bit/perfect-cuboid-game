## Post-R03 explicitness — Stage13-12ag

Stage13-12ag writes the geometry bridge without a definitional shortcut. In `q`-adapted spherical coordinates,

\[
w_q=\frac1{\sin\theta},
\qquad
d\omega=\sin\theta\,d\theta\,d\alpha,
\]

so

\[
\boxed{w_qd\omega=d\theta d\alpha}.
\]

With `psi=pi/2-theta`, the chamber slice at fixed `psi` has inner-angle length `ell_q(psi)`, and Fubini gives

\[
I_q=\int\ell_q(\psi)d\psi.
\]

Together with `psi=2phi-pi/2` and `k_q=4ell_q/pi`, this yields

\[
\boxed{J_q=\frac{2I_q}{\pi}}
\]

with the complete Jacobian chain visible.

For inert `p=3 mod 4`, Stage13-12ag also proves the unit-state finite-field identity symbolically. If `chi` is the quadratic character, then

\[
S=S_0+S_1+S_2+S_3
=0+(p-1)+(p+1)-2
=2(p-1).
\]

There are `T=p^2-1` total unit states and exactly `N_0=4` states with `X^2+Z^2=0`, hence

\[
N_{\rm acc}
=\frac{T+S+N_0}{2}
=\boxed{\frac{(p+1)^2}{2}}.
\]

Thus

\[
\alpha_p=\frac{p+1}{2(p-1)}
\]

is now supported by a written Legendre/Jacobi-sum derivation rather than finite enumeration.
