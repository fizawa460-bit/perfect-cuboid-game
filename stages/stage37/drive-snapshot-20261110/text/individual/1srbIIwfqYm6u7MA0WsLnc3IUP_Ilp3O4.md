## M3-HIST14-ROOT-GATE-001
TYPE: THEOREM_GATE
STATUS: PAUSED


CLAIM:
The remaining Stage14 s-route reciprocal obstruction is the theorem-ready conditional density problem:
on the canonical allocation-bearing primitive-slope family, each slope has only B^o(1) charged opposite-reciprocal candidates (C0,X0,Y0), and one asks for a uniform fixed delta>0 deficit in the proportion for which at least one candidate lies on the prescribed primitive Gaussian root line modulo C0.
No second independent growing modulus is produced.
Divisor switching changes the representation only by B^o(1) fibers.
A pointwise root-line count does not uniformly close the receiver because C0 and the candidate vectors are correlated by the physical allocation equations.


POPULATION:
Stage14 s7-71 canonical allocation-bearing primitive slopes.


HEIGHT:
primitive slopes of height B^(1/4+o(1)) inside Stage14 physical d<=B.


DEPENDS_ON:
M3-HIST14-SECONDRECIP-001.


IMPLIES:
further progress requires a same-measure averaged density/dispersion theorem for the correlated Gaussian root congruence, or a rigorous no-go showing full exponent can survive.

DO_NOT_REPEAT:
