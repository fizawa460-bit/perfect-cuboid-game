CLAIM:
Stage18 uses the primitive canonical exactly-two-face population
M_2(B) ~ C_M2 B(log B)^5, C_M2>0,
under the literal physical cutoff
R=sqrt(a^2+b^2+c^2)<=B
with no integral-space-diagonal requirement.
Combining this frozen Stage15/Stage18 target theorem with the matched Stage16 ambient source
U(B)=pi/(36*zeta(3))*B^3+O(B^2)
gives the exact ambient-density asymptotic
M_2(B)/U(B)
~ [36*zeta(3) C_M2/pi] * (log B)^5/B^2
-> 0.
Thus the exactly-two-face condition has certified net two-power polynomial thinning relative to the unrestricted primitive canonical cuboid population, with a compensating fifth power of log B.
Since C_M2>0, Stage18 also records that the population is infinite.
