2. Primitive edge gcd decomposition and the six-leg product


Let (e1,e2,e3) be the positive primitive endpoint edges, gcd(e1,e2,e3)=1. Put
g12=gcd(e1,e2), g13=gcd(e1,e3), g23=gcd(e2,e3).


The three gij are pairwise coprime: any common prime divisor of two of them would divide all three edges.


Hence there are positive integers a1,a2,a3 with
e1=g12*g13*a1,
e2=g12*g23*a2,
e3=g13*g23*a3.


The two primitive legs on each reduced physical face are therefore
face12: g13*a1, g23*a2,
face13: g12*a1, g23*a3,
face23: g12*a2, g13*a3.


Their product is
(g13 a1)(g23 a2)(g12 a1)(g23 a3)(g12 a2)(g13 a3)
=(g12*g13*g23*a1*a2*a3)^2.


Primewise, because min(v_p(e1),v_p(e2),v_p(e3))=0, one checks
v_p(g12*g13*g23*a1*a2*a3)=max_i v_p(e_i).
Therefore, with
L := lcm(e1,e2,e3),
we have the exact identity


PRODUCT OF THE SIX REDUCED FACE LEGS = L^2.


This is a three-face identity; no independence of faces is used.

4. Exact bridge from edge LCM to the retained h_i / tau structure


Retain the M3-T11-004 notation
h_i=gcd(R,e_i),
H:=h1*h2*h3,
tau=R/H,
c1=h2*h3, c2=h1*h3, c3=h1*h2.


If a prime p divides R and two different edges, then from
R^2=e1^2+e2^2+e3^2
it divides the third edge as well, contradicting gcd(e1,e2,e3)=1. Therefore, for every p|R, at most one edge has positive p-valuation.


Primewise this gives the exact identity
H = gcd(L,R).


Define the R-free edge-LCM factor
L_free := L/H.
Then
tau = R/H,
gcd(L_free,tau)=1.


Moreover
L_free = lcm(e1/h1, e2/h2, e3/h3).
Indeed, the p-adic exponent on either side is max_i max(v_p(e_i)-min(v_p(e_i),v_p(R)),0).


Thus the old common residual tau and the new three-face edge-LCM have the same removed common factor H:
L = H*L_free,
R = H*tau,
gcd(L_free,tau)=1,
and L_free/tau = L/R.
