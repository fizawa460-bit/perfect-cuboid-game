## M3-EULERIAN-001
TYPE: THEOREM
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
On the one-factor locus x^2=A-B with gcd(A,B)=1, let S and T be the odd-prime supports of the squarefree kernels of A and B.
Form the bipartite nonresidue graph with edge ell--q exactly when (q/ell)=-1, adjoining the deterministic vertex 2 when the 2-adic parity requires it.
Every vertex has even degree.
For fixed odd supports with m=|S|>=1 and n=|T|>=1, the odd-prime Legendre sign patterns satisfying the one-factor constraints form a linear subspace of codimension
m+n-1,
with exactly
2^((m-1)(n-1))
admissible matrices out of 2^(mn).


POPULATION:
one-factor squarefree-support strata.


HEIGHT:
exact combinatorial/local reciprocity statement; no global physical distribution claim.


DEPENDS_ON:
M3-ONE-004 and quadratic reciprocity.


IMPLIES:
the moving-prime compatibility is an Eulerian cycle-space constraint, with exact symbol codimension increasing linearly in total squarefree support.


ALIASES:
OneFactorEulerianResidueGraph;
OneFactorSymbolCodimension;
Legendre incidence cycle space.


DUPLICATE_OF:
none.


SUPERSEDES:
treating each moving-prime residue condition as an unrelated independent bit.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
The codimension count is not a theorem that Legendre symbol patterns are equidistributed on exact M3(B).


REOPEN_CONDITION:
none.
