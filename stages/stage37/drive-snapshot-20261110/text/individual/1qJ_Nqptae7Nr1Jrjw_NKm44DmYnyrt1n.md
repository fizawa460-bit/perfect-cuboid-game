## M3-HIST14-HEAVYRAY-001
TYPE: EXACT_STRUCTURE
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
On the Stage14 heavy primitive reciprocal-ray branch, fixing the primitive ray freezes the primitive reciprocal vector and its norm factor, but not the radial scale h.
For each exact h, the reciprocal factorization dictionary has only divisor-many B^o(1) possibilities, so polynomial heavy-ray mass cannot come from factorization entropy.
Every reciprocal-side signed-quotient pair lies on one exact one-dimensional radial line parameterized by h.
Hence heavy-ray saturation must occur by either:
(A) concentration on B^o(1) exact h values, forcing a potentially polynomial reverse fiber from one fixed reciprocal tuple back to canonical physical backgrounds; or
(B) genuinely polynomially many radial dilations along the fixed primitive ray.
The bound from fixed reciprocal data back to canonical background is explicitly unproved.

HEIGHT:
Stage14 physical d<=B.


DEPENDS_ON:

IMPLIES:
divisor factorization is not a third polynomial source; the live obstruction is radial support or reverse multiplicity from fixed reciprocal data.

DO_NOT_REPEAT:

Do not charge reciprocal factorization entropy after fixing h.
