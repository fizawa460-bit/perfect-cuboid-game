## M3-HIST14-RADIALNORM-001
TYPE: EXACT_STRUCTURE
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
The late Stage14 heavy-ray packet admits an exact normalized radial coordinate n after peeling fixed rational-square coefficients.
One fixed denominator d0 gives h=d0*n, and the fixed numerator c0 can be absorbed at B^o(1) cost into frozen prime-power allocations.
The moving multiplicative structure becomes a coefficient-free ternary product
n=J1*a1*b1
with J1 squarefree.
The root pair has a fixed-coefficient normal form with J1 as common squarefree dilation and a1/b1 as the projective square-ratio coordinate.
This separates common radial dilation from projective ratio without introducing a new density factor.


POPULATION:
Stage14 s7-87 through s7-90 normalized heavy-ray packets.

the heavy-ray obstruction may be studied on normalized integers n with a shared squarefree dilation and primitive ratio, rather than on the original four-factor packet.

DO_NOT_REPEAT:
