RESULT:
For the trivial split partition, write
D=(epsilon/2)(a^2+b^2),
C=epsilon*a*b,
W=epsilon*Z,
and p/q=(D+X)/Q in lowest terms.
The t28 cover gives
Z^2=(bp-aq)(aq+bp)(bq-ap)(bq+ap)
and reconstructs
X=D(p^2-q^2)/(p^2+q^2),
Q=2Dpq/(p^2+q^2),
Y=W/(p^2+q^2),
so Q^2=C^2+Y^2.
Actual simultaneous physical completion additionally requires
P_1^2=C^2+X^2=D^2-Y^2.
Equivalently, with
K=(a^2+b^2)(p^2+q^2),
one must have
S^2=K^2-4Z^2,
i.e. S^2+(2Z)^2=K^2.


In T=p/q coordinates the two square polynomials are
F(T)=(b^2T^2-a^2)(b^2-a^2T^2),
H(T)=(a^4+6a^2b^2+b^4)(T^4+1)-2(a^2-b^2)^2T^2.
Exact identities:
Res_T(F,H)=(a^2+b^2)^16;
disc(F)=16a^4b^4(a^2-b^2)^4(a^2+b^2)^4;
disc(H)=2^16 a^4b^4(a^2+b^2)^4(a^4+6a^2b^2+b^4)^2.
Hence at every odd good auxiliary prime lambda, F,H,FH are nonsquare/squarefree and the simultaneous nonzero-square density is
lambda/4+O(sqrt(lambda)).
This is a proved same-variable double-character gate, not a heuristic independence assumption.

A_{1,1}^{(2sq)}(B)=o(B^(1/3))
is sufficient for P(B)/M3(B)->0.
