5. Uniform support-restricted integer lemma


Lemma. Fix any constant A>0. Uniformly for integers 1<=m<=B^A,
#{n<=B : rad(n)|m}=B^o(1).                            (177.11)


Proof. Put k=omega(m). Since the product of the first k primes is at least k!, m<=B^A implies
k=O_A(log B/log log B).


Take sigma=(log log B)^(-1/2). Rankin's trick gives
#{n<=B:rad(n)|m}
<= B^sigma * sum_{rad(n)|m} n^(-sigma)
= B^sigma * product_{p|m}(1-p^(-sigma))^(-1).


Uniformly for p>=2 and small positive sigma,
(1-p^(-sigma))^(-1) <= C0/sigma
with an absolute C0. Hence the logarithm of the Euler product is
O(k log(1/sigma))=o(log B),
while sigma log B=o(log B). This proves (177.11).


Only this elementary support count is used. No distribution of primes or independent local density is asserted.
