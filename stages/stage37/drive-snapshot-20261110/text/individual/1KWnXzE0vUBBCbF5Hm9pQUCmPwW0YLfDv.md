RESULT:
Let p^a|tau. Then p^a|R, and every odd p|R satisfies p==1 mod 4.


From


R^2=e1^2+e2^2+e3^2


one gets for each i modulo p^(2a)


e_j^2+e_k^2 == -e_i^2.


Because -1 is a square modulo p^(2a), each of the three face-square congruences is automatically locally soluble once the space-diagonal congruence is imposed.


Thus the three Gaussian root signs above p^a are local labels of an already-forced condition; they cannot be multiplied as independent sieve savings.


For fixed tau and c<=C, primitive Pythagorean directions with D=tau*c have total multiplicity C*B^o(1), but summing over tau gives no fixed-power gain because split-prime support alone is only logarithmically thin.


The natural huge-tau split has exact historical ancestry:


rough tau -> moving Gaussian-prime / Stage14-t38 type averaging;
smooth tau -> cover-conditioned friable / Stage14-t27 type counting.


Therefore the cyclic huge-tau route does not close by local prime multiplication alone.
