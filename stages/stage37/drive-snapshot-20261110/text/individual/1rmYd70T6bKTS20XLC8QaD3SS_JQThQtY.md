## M3-FIB-006
TYPE: INTERMEDIATE_LEMMA
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
A rational fibration map on K_c may be valid even when the quadratic relation that motivated its construction vanishes identically on K_c.
The fibration is defined by the nonconstant rational ratio map to P^1 and its curve fibers; nontriviality of the motivating quadric as an additional equation is not required.


POPULATION:
rational fibration constructions on K_c.


HEIGHT:
structural only.


DEPENDS_ON:
the explicit ratio constructions and the source paper's analogous mechanism.


IMPLIES:
identical vanishing of the motivating quadratic combination is not by itself a reason to discard a candidate fibration.


ALIASES:
identically-vanishing-quadric fibration correction;
ratio-map fibration criterion.


DUPLICATE_OF:
none.


SUPERSEDES:
the mid-session claim that an identically true quadric cannot underlie a genuine fibration.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
Test nonconstancy and the actual fiber geometry of the rational map; do not reject it solely because the motivating ideal relation vanishes on K_c.


REOPEN_CONDITION:
none.


DETAIL_DOC:
LEGACY-COLD Section30.2.
