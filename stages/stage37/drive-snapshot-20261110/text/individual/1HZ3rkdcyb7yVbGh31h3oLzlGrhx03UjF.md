## M3-HIST14-RECIPROCAL-ADAPTER-NEG-001
TYPE: NEGATIVE_RESULT
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
Stage14 global and s-route heavy receivers were exactly unified as one normalized radial reciprocal divisor-window selector on coordinates (n,L), with only B^o(1) divisor fiber after outer data are fixed.
The fixed-U route has a superficially similar reciprocal hyperbola, but its arithmetic measure is different: it counts a weighted prime/projective-class condition rather than squareclass divisor-window existence.
The common reciprocal-window geometry does not provide a measure-preserving arithmetic adapter, so savings cannot be cross-promoted between the two routes.
Likewise global and s copies of the same normalized divisor coordinate are not independently chargeable.


POPULATION:
Stage14 heavy global/s reciprocal divisor-window receiver versus Stage14 fixed-U projective-prime receiver.


HEIGHT:
Stage14 physical d<=B.


DEPENDS_ON:
Stage14 Work-brX30, with q14 and tH29 as later audits.


IMPLIES:
visual or algebraic similarity of reciprocal hyperbolas is insufficient for theorem transfer; population, weights, quantifiers, and prime/divisor conditions must match.


ALIASES:
reciprocal-window arithmetic-adapter no-go;
radial divisor versus projective prime mismatch;
Work-brX30;
q14 boundary.


DUPLICATE_OF:
Arsenal AR-024 is the generic same-kernel/different-measure firewall; this card records the concrete late-Stage14 reciprocal-window identification and failed cross-route adapter.


SUPERSEDES:
the proposed direct transfer between global/s divisor-window occupancy and fixed-U projective-prime depletion.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
Do not cross-promote divisor-window theorems to the fixed-U prime/projective measure or vice versa without an explicit physical-measure-preserving adapter.
Do not multiply global and s counts after their common normalized radial coordinate has been identified.


REOPEN_CONDITION:
an explicit adapter preserving outer measure, quantifier order, squareclass-divisor versus prime/projective conditions, and physical masks.
