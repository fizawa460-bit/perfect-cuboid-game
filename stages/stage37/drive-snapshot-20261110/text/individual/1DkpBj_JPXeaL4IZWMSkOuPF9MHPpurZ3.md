## M3-HIST14-RATIO-ENDPOINT-FW-001
TYPE: FIREWALL
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
The Stage14 inner primitive-ratio endpoint is not the same object as the already-discharged outer radial endpoint.
Outer radial endpoint means n lies near an endpoint of the reciprocal product window.
Inner ratio endpoint means one side of the coprime factorization, such as u or v, is small or subpolynomial.
The outer endpoint theorem does not imply that these inner ratio configurations are fixed-power sparse.
Across polynomially many outer integers n, a small factor side can persist even though every fixed-n ratio fiber has only B^o(1) candidates.


POPULATION:
Stage14 s7-94/s7-95 normalized interior ratio receiver.


HEIGHT:
Stage14 physical d<=B.


DEPENDS_ON:
M3-HIST14-UNITARY-001 and the merged outer radial endpoint discharge.


IMPLIES:
endpoint terminology must preserve which coordinate is becoming extreme; an endpoint saving cannot be transferred between outer n-space and inner divisor-ratio space without proof.


ALIASES:
inner ratio endpoint no-go;
outer radial versus inner divisor endpoint firewall;
s7-94.


DUPLICATE_OF:
none.


SUPERSEDES:
using the radial endpoint estimate to delete small-u or small-v unitary-divisor configurations.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
Do not reuse the outer endpoint saving on the inner ratio coordinate.
Do not infer outer-family sparsity from a B^o(1) fixed-n factorization fiber.


REOPEN_CONDITION:
a uniform same-measure theorem proving that inner endpoint unitary-divisor configurations occupy a fixed-power-small set of outer n.
