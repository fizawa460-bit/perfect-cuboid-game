TYPE: EXACT_STRUCTURE
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
After the Stage14 ordinary-product capacity normalizations, all four active s heavy realizations admit a common nested-support decomposition
ambient -> precompletion -> physical completion.
For each exact candidate chi,
C_phys(chi)=C_pre(chi)*C_ext(chi),
where C_pre contains predicates already determined by reconstructed candidate data and C_ext is the existential nonemptiness of the still-required reverse/post-column extension set.
The known reverse reconstruction bound #R(chi)<=B^o(1) controls multiplicity only, not existence.
With exponents kappa, sigma, tau,
delta_pre=kappa-sigma,
delta_ext=sigma-tau,
and heavy survival is exactly
kappa-delta_pre-delta_ext>=mu.
Near the ambient threshold, both deficits must be exponent-zero on any survivor.
