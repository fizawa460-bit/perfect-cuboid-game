Stage26 removes the one-parameter Saunderson bottleneck by allowing all primitive Pythagorean input triples.

The hostile audit therefore accepted
M_3(B)>>_epsilon B^(1/3-epsilon)
for every fixed epsilon>0.

Stage26 did not prove an epsilon-free B^(1/3) lower or identify the true M_3 exponent.
Later Stage28 strengthened the current reusable lower to
liminf M_3(B)/B^(1/3) >= 27/(40*pi^2).
Thus the Stage26 one-third-minus-epsilon theorem is preserved as the mechanism breakthrough, while Stage28 owns the current endpoint-strengthened lower.
