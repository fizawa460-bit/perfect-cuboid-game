CLAIM:
Stage26 compares the disjoint exactly-two and exactly-three no-space strata.
M_3 is not a literal subset of M_2, so M_3/M_2 is only an adjacent-stratum population-size ratio.
The literal physical-object completion host is
H_{>=2}(B)=M_2(B)+M_3(B),
with
Phi(B)=M_3(B)/[M_2(B)+M_3(B)].
The raw shared-edge incidence host is
P(B)=M_2(B)+3M_3(B),
with
Theta(B)=3M_3(B)/[M_2(B)+3M_3(B)].
Stage26 proves
Phi(B)->0,
Theta(B)->0,
Theta(B)/Phi(B)->3,
and
Phi ~ M_3/M_2,
Theta ~ 3 M_3/M_2.
For every fixed epsilon>0 and fixed 0<delta<1/46,
B^(-2/3-epsilon)(log B)^(-5)
<<_epsilon Phi(B),Theta(B)
=
o((log B)^(-delta)).
Also
H_{>=2}(B) ~ P(B) ~ M_2(B) ~ C_M2 B(log B)^5.
The factor three in Theta is an exact incidence multiplicity adapter, not an additional arithmetic probability.
