## M3-ONE-001
TYPE: EXACT_STRUCTURE
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
For a primitive physical Euler brick with unique odd edge X, even edges Y,Z, adjacent face diagonals P,Q, and
G=gcd(PQ,YZ),
define
L_-=(PQ-YZ)/G,
L_+=(PQ+YZ)/G.
Then L_-,L_+ are positive odd coprime canonical integers and
L_-*L_+=(XR/G)^2.
Moreover
R in Z
iff
L_- and L_+ are both integer squares.


POPULATION:
exact primitive physical M3.


HEIGHT:
exact physical space diagonal R.


DEPENDS_ON:
the identity
(PQ-YZ)(PQ+YZ)=X^2R^2
and primitive parity/gcd structure.


IMPLIES:
the perfect-endpoint predicate admits a canonical two-coprime-factor physical decomposition.


ALIASES:
PhysicalTerminalDifferenceOfProducts;
CanonicalCoprimeTerminalFactors;
PhysicalCoprimeEndpointFactorization;
canonical Lminus/Lplus factors.


DUPLICATE_OF:
M3-TERM-001 gives the terminal Pythagorean allocation after endpoint assumption; this card is the prior physical factorization valid on all primitive M3.


SUPERSEDES:
terminal-factor formulations requiring Master coordinates.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
Do not assume either factor is square on general M3.
Do not multiply L_- and L_+ filters as independent without proof.


REOPEN_CONDITION:
none.
