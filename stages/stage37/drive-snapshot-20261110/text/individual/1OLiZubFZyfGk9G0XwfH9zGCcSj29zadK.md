## M3-HIST14-FIBER-FW-001
TYPE: FIREWALL
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
In the Stage14 fixed-Q Gaussian background, each live completion fiber has only B^o(1) states.
Consequently any nonempty principal event already has conditional density at least B^(-o(1)).
A fixed-power density loss cannot be created solely inside such a subpolynomial fiber except by making the accepted fiber empty at fixed-power frequency.
The first remaining polynomial-scale object is therefore the support/weight of the outer Q fibers, not within-fiber density or variance.


POPULATION:
Stage14 fixed-U/fixed-Q Gaussian completion tower on the t-route.


HEIGHT:
Stage14 physical d<=B with the frozen fixed-U outer measure.


DEPENDS_ON:
Stage14 t89 finite-fiber bound and t105 exact two-level fiber decomposition; conceptually adjacent to Arsenal AR-016.


IMPLIES:
small-fiber conditional density is not an independent polynomial-length saving mechanism; one must attack occupied outer-fiber support or another polynomial-scale variable.


ALIASES:
background Gaussian fiber density no-go;
outer-Q support reduction;
Stage14 t105.


DUPLICATE_OF:
M3-FIB-001 records B^o(1) multiplicity on a different Stage37 fibration; this card records the distinct Stage14 deduction that a B^o(1) fiber cannot host an independent fixed-power density deficit.


SUPERSEDES:
attempts to obtain a polynomial saving from centered variance or fractional density entirely inside the tiny fixed-Q completion fiber.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
Do not treat B^o(1)-sized fiber density as a new fixed-power length.
Do not identify the Stage14 fixed-Q outer measure with Stage37 M3 without an exact adapter.


REOPEN_CONDITION:
a theorem showing fixed-power sparsity of occupied outer Q fibers, or a new polynomial-scale variable inside the actual target measure.
