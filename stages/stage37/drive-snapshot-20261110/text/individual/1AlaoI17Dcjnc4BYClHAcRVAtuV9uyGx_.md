CLAIM:
For A=a^2+b^2, the second Pythagorean lift has the exact self-correspondence
T^2=[A*t+ab*(1-t^2)]/[A*t-ab*(1-t^2)].
With N=A*t+ab*(1-t^2), D=A*t-ab*(1-t^2),
F_{a,b}(T)=[A/D]^2*F_{a,b}(t)
and
N*D=F_{a,b}(t)+(2ab*t)^2.
Thus on y^2=F(t), rationality of T is equivalent to y^2+(2ab*t)^2=w^2.
