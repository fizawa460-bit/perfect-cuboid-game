TYPE: EXACT_STRUCTURE
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
Let
p=gcd(P,Z),
d=gcd(Q,Y).
Then
L_- square
iff
Xi(E)=p*(Q+iY)/[d*(P+iZ)]
is a square in Q(i).
Equivalently the two physical Gaussian factors have the same squareclass.
The same condition is also equivalent to the rational scalar
Psi(E)=p*(T+PQ+YZ)/(2*d*T)
being a rational square,
and to the positive physical integer
S_-(E)=2*h*T*(T+PQ+YZ)
being an integer square.
