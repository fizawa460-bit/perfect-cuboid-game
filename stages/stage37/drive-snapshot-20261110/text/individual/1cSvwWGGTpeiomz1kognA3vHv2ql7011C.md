## M3-HIST14-SELECTOR-FW-001
TYPE: FIREWALL
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
In the Stage14 square-root saturation selector, the Gaussian/root-orientation component admits an exact B^o(1)-cost Walsh/Hecke expansion, and finite gcd/primitivity masks admit divisor/Mobius expansion.
However the full physical selector remains nonmultiplicative because balanced divisor splits, dyadic/range windows, charged-once chart masks, and reciprocal-completion masks are not multiplicative.
Therefore a multiplicative expansion of the orientation component cannot be promoted by itself to a multiplicative expansion of the full physical selector or to a fixed-power saving.


POPULATION:
Stage14 full-conductor interior-dense square-root saturation packets.


HEIGHT:
Stage14 physical d<=B.


DEPENDS_ON:
Stage14 s7-58 and the prior exact orientation expansion.


IMPLIES:
future multiplicative/Hecke attacks must identify which remaining physical masks are compatible with the claimed factorization rather than treating the entire selector as multiplicative.


ALIASES:
Stage14 nonmultiplicative mask barrier;
partial Hecke factorization;
s7-58.


DUPLICATE_OF:
none.


SUPERSEDES:
the attempted promotion from orientation-factorization to a factorization of the complete physical selector.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
Do not infer multiplicativity of a physical selector from multiplicativity of only one factor.
Balanced-divisor existence, range windows, charged-once masks, and reciprocal completion must be handled on their actual measure.


REOPEN_CONDITION:
an exact decomposition or adapter that makes the residual physical masks multiplicative or proves their influence power-small on the same charged measure.
