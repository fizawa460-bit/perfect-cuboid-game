## M3-HIST14-WITNESS-FW-001
TYPE: FIREWALL
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
For the Stage14 primitive-slope physical predicate, each accepted slope has only B^o(1) physical witnesses after canonical allocation and reciprocal reconstruction.
This finite witness multiplicity is a multiplicity statement only.
It does not imply that the set of accepted primitive slopes has B^o(1) size, zero density, or any fixed-power density deficit.
Balanced divisor-window existence by itself is also not fixed-power sparse on the relevant ambient ranges; full-exponent semiprime-type families can support balanced splits.
Any saving must therefore use correlation between the binary-form values, their allowed allocations, and the live reciprocal/root selector.

finite witness multiplicity firewall;
balanced divisor window no standalone saving;
primitive-slope witness warning.


DUPLICATE_OF:
generic B^o(1)-is-not-saving principles appear elsewhere, but this card records the concrete Stage14 primitive-slope/balanced-window failure mode.


SUPERSEDES:
