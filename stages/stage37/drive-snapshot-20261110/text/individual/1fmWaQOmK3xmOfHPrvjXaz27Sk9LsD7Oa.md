## M3-GCD-002
TYPE: THEOREM
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
For the historical parameter-side mean-gcd series
sum_d phi(d)*(rho(d)/d^2)^2,
the Euler local factors are exactly
L_2=4,
L_p=1+4/p+1/p^2 for odd primes p.
Hence the literal infinite Euler product/mean diverges because L_p-1~4/p.


POPULATION:
Master-Hit/Pythagorean parameter measure before exact physical-M3 transfer.


HEIGHT:
natural parameter truncation required; no standalone physical R<=B theorem.


DEPENDS_ON:
M3-GCD-001 and the proved odd-prime rho(p^k) law.


IMPLIES:
the mean gcd cannot be treated as a finite constant; any use must retain a finite truncation.


ALIASES:

E[gcd(U1,U2)] Euler factors.

DO_NOT_REPEAT:

Do not infer a physical-M3 distribution theorem from this parameter-side calculation.
