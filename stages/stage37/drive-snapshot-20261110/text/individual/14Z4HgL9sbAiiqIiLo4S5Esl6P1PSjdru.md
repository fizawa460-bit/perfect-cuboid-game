CLAIM:
Stage24 extends the Stage15-derived algebraic family beyond the historical odd/odd specialization.
For coprime positive parameters satisfying
p^4+q^4=17Z^2,
with
e=4pq,
x=4p^2-q^2,
y=4q^2-p^2,
D=17Z,
the two designated face diagonals and the space diagonal are integral.
The odd/odd specialization remains impossible modulo 16 because p^4+q^4==2 mod 16. Stage24 shows that this obstruction is specific to that parity stratum and does not exclude the broader formula.
The mixed-parity genus-one curve
17z^2=t^4+1
has positive rank and infinitely many physical points in the cone
1<q/p<(1+sqrt(2))/2.
Only finitely many of these acquire a third integral face, because the corresponding exception curve has genus five.
Hence infinitely many primitive canonical exactly-two integral-space objects survive; historically this gives N_2(B)>>sqrt(log B).
The quantitative lower was later superseded by Stage25, but the parity-stratum distinction remains valid.
