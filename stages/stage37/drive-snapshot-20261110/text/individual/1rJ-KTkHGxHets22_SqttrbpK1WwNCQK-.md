## M3-KC-001
TYPE: EXACT_STRUCTURE

CLAIM:
The Euler-brick K3 surface is
K_c:
b1^2=a2^2+a3^2,
b2^2=a1^2+a3^2,
b3^2=a1^2+a2^2
inside P^5.
For a nondegenerate primitive canonical Euler brick, permutations of (a1,a2,a3) and independent signs of the six coordinates give exactly 3!*2^6=384 distinct K_c(Q) points, while physical height R^2=a1^2+a2^2+a3^2 is invariant.
Hence exact physical M3 counting is the corresponding primitive K_c count divided by 384.


POPULATION:
nondegenerate primitive canonical Euler bricks / physical rational points on K_c.

M3-to-Kc exact host.
