## M3-HIST17-SURVIVAL-001
TYPE: COMPARISON_RESULT / CAUSAL_DECOMPOSITION
STATUS: PROVED_AUDITED
CLAIM:
Stage16 gives
M_1(B) asymp B^2 log B
for the same primitive canonical exactly-one-face population before space-diagonal integrality is imposed.
Stage17 is the literal subset obtained by adding R in Z.
Combining the exact Stage17 asymptotic with the frozen Stage16 denominator gives
N_1(B)/M_1(B) asymp (log B)^2/B -> 0.
No ratio asymptotic with a certified leading constant is claimed because Stage16 has no proved leading constant for M_1(B).
Structurally, if x^2+y^2=p^2 is the unique integral face and z is the remaining edge, then the added Stage17 condition is exactly
p^2+z^2=d^2.
Thus Stage16 -> Stage17 is a second Pythagorean extension sharing the face diagonal p.
The audited net conditional survival scale is Theta((log B)^2/B).
Canonicalization, primitivity, unique-face multiplicity, the common cutoff, and d=R are accounting identities, not additional thinning mechanisms.
