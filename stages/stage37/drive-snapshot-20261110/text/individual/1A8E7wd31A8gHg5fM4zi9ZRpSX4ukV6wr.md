There are constants kappa,k>0 such that, for every sufficiently large B,
 E_raw,{c>kappa sqrt(B)}(B) >= k B^(1/4).                 (156.1)
Consequently the proposed bound E_raw,{c>C}(B)<=B^(1/2+o(1)) C^(-1+o(1)), UNIFORMLY over all C, is FALSE. More generally it fails at C=B^gamma for every fixed 1/4<gamma<1/2.

## 2. Reuse the existing R502 family
For coprime positive m,n with 7/2<m/n<4, put
 A=(m^4-n^4)(m^4-81n^4),
 B0=4mn(m^2-3n^2)(m^4+2m^2n^2+9n^4),
 C0=16m^2n^2(m^4-9n^4),
 D1=m^8+46m^4n^4+81n^8,
 D2=4mn(m^2-3n^2)(m^4+10m^2n^2+9n^4),
 D=(m^4-2m^2n^2+9n^4)(m^4+10m^2n^2+9n^4).
Exact identities:
 A^2+C0^2=D1^2, B0^2+C0^2=D2^2, A^2+B0^2+C0^2=D^2. (156.2)
The fixed cone has 0<A<B0<C0; this is the source R502 cone. Divide by g=gcd(A,B0,C0). The primitive cuboid has R=D/g and two integer faces, with no condition on its third face needed here. Divisibility of every diagonal by g follows primewise from g^2 dividing its square.


Let g1=gcd(A,C0), g2=gcd(B0,C0), K=4mn(m^2-3n^2),
 J=(m^2+n^2)(m^2+9n^2), L=(m^2-n^2)(m^2-9n^2).
Then A=JL, D2=KJ. Also
 g2=K e, e=gcd(m^4+2m^2n^2+9n^4,4mn(m^2+3n^2)).
Write b=1 if m,n both odd, else0; a=1 if3|m, else0. Exact formulas are
 g1=2^(7b)3^(4a)<=10368, e=2^(2b)3^(2a)<=36.           (156.3)
These formulas are proved below, not inferred from a finite census.


The two reduced primitive faces sharing the physical C0 edge are
 F1=(C0/g1,A/g1,D1/g1), F2=(C0/g2,B0/g2,D2/g2).
Their cross-gcd factors are c12=gcd(D1/g1,B0/g2) and c21=gcd(D2/g2,A/g1).
Since D2/g2=J/e and A/g1=JL/g1, put u=J/e,v=JL/g1. The identity g1 v=e u L implies u|g1 v, so u/gcd(u,v) divides g1. Therefore
 c_raw=c12 c21>=c21>=J/(e g1)>=J/373248.                (156.4)
This arithmetic inequality survives primitive reduction and measures the actual raw edge, not an ambient parameter lattice.


### Proof of (156.3)
For any p>3, p|m or p|n makes A and the quartic norm factor units modulo p, so cannot contribute. If p does not divide mn and p|C0, then m^4=9n^4 modulo p. The two factors of A become8n^4 and -72n^4; neither vanishes for p>3. Hence g1 has no prime>3.
For e, again a prime dividing mn cannot divide its quartic F=m^4+2m^2n^2+9n^4 unless p=3. If p>3 divides m^2+3n^2, substitution gives F=12n^4 modulo p. Thus e also has no prime>3.
At2, opposite parity makes A and F odd. When both are odd, v2(C0)=7 while both factors of A have valuation at least4; hence v2(g1)=7. Also F=12 modulo16, so v2(F)=2, while4mn(m^2+3n^2) has valuation at least4, giving v2(e)=2.
At3, when3 does not divide m, C0 is a3-unit (if3|n also both A and F are units), and either F is a3-unit or the complementary denominator is, so both minima are0.
When3|m with r=v3(m)>=1, v3(C0)=2r+2. The first factor of A is a3-unit; the second has valuation at least4, and exactly4 for r>=2. At r=1 the C0 valuation is4. Hence v3(g1)=4 in every case.
For e, the denominator4mn(m^2+3n^2) has valuation r+1. At r=1, F has valuation at least2 and denominator exactly2; at r>=2, F has valuation exactly2. Hence v3(e)=2. This proves all claims.


## 3. Count actual primitive raw objects
Set T=floor((B/240)^(1/8)). Take n in (T/8,T/4], and m=4n-k with 1<=k<n/2 and gcd(k,n)=1. Then m,n<=T, 7/2<m/n<4, and m>7T/16. The usual Mobius/totient count on this fixed cone gives >>T^2 distinct reduced parameters.
D<=240 T^8, so R=D/g<=B. Also J>=m^4>=(7T/16)^4, and (156.4) implies c_raw>=const*T^4>=const*sqrt(B).
Primitive reduction identifies at most8 of these parameters per canonical cuboid: the largest-edge/space-diagonal ratio is
 C0/D=16t^2(t^4-9)/[(t^4-2t^2+9)(t^4+10t^2+9)], t=m/n,
a nonconstant rational function of degree at most8. For a fixed ratio, its cross-multiplied polynomial has at most8 roots. The cone fixes the largest edge, and this ratio is invariant under primitive scaling. Thus there are >>T^2>>B^(1/4) distinct primitive raw objects and raw edges with large c. Choosing kappa smaller than the resulting constant proves (156.1).
This argument does NOT need Faltings, the third-face-square exception set, a perfect-cuboid example, or a numerical absence result.


## 4. Consequences for globalization budget
At C=kappa sqrt(B), the claimed uniform upper bound is B^o(1), contradicting B^(1/4). At C=B^gamma, 1/4<gamma<1/2, all these objects eventually lie in the tail while the upper bound would be B^(1/2-gamma+o(1))=o(B^(1/4)).
For the intended C=B^(1/6+eta), the proposed upper exponent is1/3-eta. If eta>1/12 (and gamma<1/2), it contradicts this family; if0<eta<1/12, its exponent exceeds1/4, so this family is compatible with that upper bound. At eta=1/12 the exponents agree, so no contradiction is established.
The R502 family itself is only B^(1/4) in power scale, below the M3 lower benchmark B^(1/3). It obstructs the chosen strong RAW certificate, not P/M3→0. Its presence shows why imposing endpoint/M3 masks matters rather than demanding excessive decay of all exactly-two objects.
