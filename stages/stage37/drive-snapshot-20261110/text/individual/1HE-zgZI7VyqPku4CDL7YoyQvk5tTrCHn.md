## M3-PHYS-SQ-002
TYPE: EXACT_STRUCTURE
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
The minimal integral scalar receiver is
F_-(E)=G*(PQ-YZ),
where G=gcd(PQ,YZ):
L_- square iff F_-(E) is an integer square.
In half-angle coordinates u,v,
PQ-YZ=X^2*(u^2+v^2)/(2uv),
PQ+YZ=X^2*(1+u^2v^2)/(2uv).
Thus the canonical terminal factors correspond to
A_-=u^2+v^2,
A_+=1+u^2v^2
up to the common squareclass normalization.

exact physical M3.
