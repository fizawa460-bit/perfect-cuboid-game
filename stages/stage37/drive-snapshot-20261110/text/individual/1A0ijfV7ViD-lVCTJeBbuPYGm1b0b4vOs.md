## M3-SQUARE-DENSITY-NEG-001
TYPE: NEGATIVE_RESULT
STATUS: FROZEN


CLAIM:
For fixed outer Master parameters, the third-face square condition is not correctly modeled by the density of squares among random integers of comparable size.
The equation
y^2=M(n)
is generically a genus-one quartic in n.
The archive's fixed-triple search enlarged n from 50 to 20000 without producing a second integral hit beyond the known one, illustrating the fixed-fiber arithmetic sparsity.
The correct object is a family of genus-one rational/integral-point problems, not an independent random-square event.


POPULATION:
fixed-source slices of the Master-Hit third-face condition.


HEIGHT:
fiberwise arithmetic statement and computational negative; no uniform global count follows from the experiment.


DEPENDS_ON:
the quartic form of M(n).


IMPLIES:
naive hyperbola methods that insert a random-integer square density into the Master parameter volume are not valid models of M3(B).


ALIASES:
random-square-density failure;
genus-one square-density correction;
Master M(n) density heuristic rejection.


DUPLICATE_OF:
M3-FIB-002 gives a later explicit K_c elliptic fibration; this card records the earlier conceptual failure of random-square density.


SUPERSEDES:
the withdrawn Section5.8 square-density heuristic.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
Do not multiply a raw parameter-volume count by approximately 1/sqrt(M) to model the M(n)=square condition.


REOPEN_CONDITION:
none.


DETAIL_DOC:
LEGACY-COLD Section5.8.
