2. Every normalized factor in 166 is already a perfect square


Retain one primitive space triangle
u^2+v^2=D^2,
gcd(u,v,D)=1.


If u is odd, then v is even and D is odd. Define
F_- = D-v,
F_+ = D+v.
Then F_-F_+=u^2. Both factors are odd and
gcd(F_-,F_+)=1:
a common divisor divides 2D and 2v, hence is 1 because it is odd and gcd(D,v)=1.
Since the product of two coprime positive integers is a square, there are unique positive coprime integers a,b with
F_-=a^2,
F_+=b^2,
u=a*b.


If u is even, primitive parity gives v,D odd. Define
F_-=(D-v)/2,
F_+=(D+v)/2.
Again F_-F_+=(u/2)^2 and gcd(F_-,F_+)=1, so there are unique positive coprime integers a,b with
F_-=a^2,
F_+=b^2,
u/2=a*b.


Thus in the notation of166, every normalized linear factor is not merely divisible by a square: it is exactly a square.

hat_u=u/2 for even u.

Equivalently, these are the exact Euclid-root coordinates of the primitive space triangle.
