## M3-CURVE-003
TYPE: THEOREM
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
There are no nonconstant physical rational curves of degrees 1, 2, or 3 meeting the nondegenerate positive chamber of K_c.
For every fixed positive physical degree d, the aggregate endpoint contribution from rational carriers of degree d is o(B^(1/3)).
Consequently every fixed finite band of rational-curve degrees is negligible in P(B)/M3(B).


POPULATION:
physical rational curves on K_c and their endpoint lifts.


HEIGHT:
exact physical degree/height.


DEPENDS_ON:

IMPLIES:
Method D's rational-curve obstruction is reduced to degrees growing with B; moving genus-one families and residual surface points remain separate.
