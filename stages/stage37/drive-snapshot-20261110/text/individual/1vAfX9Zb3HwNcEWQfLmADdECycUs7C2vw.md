Stage24 checkpoint50 first proved an infinite primitive mixed-parity C17 family and
N_2(B)>>sqrt(log B),
so Stage19 unboundedness became certified.
Stage25 later strengthened the current global lower interface to
N_2(B)>>B^(1/4),
and after directional backflow certified
N_{2,a}(B)>>_a B^(1/4),
N_{2,b}(B)>>_b B^(1/4),
N_{2,c}(B)>>_c B^(1/4).
Thus the current certified whole-family corridor is
B^(1/4)<<N_2(B)<<_epsilon B^(1/2+epsilon).
The historical Stage19 finite-floor-only OPEN_GATE is therefore superseded as a current receiver, but remains valid provenance.
Still unresolved are any exponent strictly above 1/4, a matching half-power lower bound, a strict whole-family sub-square-root upper theorem, and identification of the true N_2 exponent.
