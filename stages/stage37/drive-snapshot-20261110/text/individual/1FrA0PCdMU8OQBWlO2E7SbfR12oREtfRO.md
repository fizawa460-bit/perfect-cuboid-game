For every good odd prime p, the complete four-variable character sums for
E4,
H4,
and
E4*H4
are each O(p^(7/2)), giving square-root-quality saving relative to p^4 volume.
The exact physical dyadic region satisfies
max(AF,BC)*max(AC,BF) << G*B_phys
and in particular
ABCF << G*B_phys.

complete local four-variable family plus exact physical dyadic boxes.

the second statement is the exact physical product constraint.

M3-FOUR-001 and two-variable Weil bounds.
