CLAIM:
Stage20 defines the primitive canonical Euler-cuboid population
M_3(B)=#{0<a<b<c, gcd(a,b,c)=1, R<=B, all three face diagonals integral},
with no integral-space-diagonal requirement.
At Stage20 audited closeout the strongest certified envelope was
B^(1/6) << M_3(B) << B(log B)^(5-1/50),
and more generally
M_3(B)<<_eta B(log B)^(5-eta)
for every fixed eta<1/46.
The upper theorem is the exact frozen Stage14-e11 interface; the lower theorem is Stage20's explicit primitive one-parameter Saunderson family.
Thus Stage20 proved that the Euler-cuboid population is infinite and has a positive-power lower bound, while the true exponent, a matching lower bound, an asymptotic formula, and any square-root law remained open.
The Stage20 lower exponent 1/6 is historical, not the current strongest M_3 lower.
Later Stage26 restored the full two-parameter primitive Pythagorean input and obtained M_3(B)>>_epsilon B^(1/3-epsilon); Stage28 then promoted the stronger current reusable lower
liminf M_3(B)/B^(1/3) >= 27/(40*pi^2).
The Stage20 audit is not revoked: only its lower interface has been superseded for current use.
