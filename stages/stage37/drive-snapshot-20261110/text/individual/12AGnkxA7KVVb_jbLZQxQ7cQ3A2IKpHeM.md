Stage22 compares the primitive canonical exactly-one-face and exactly-two-face populations under the same physical cutoff R<=B, with no integral-space-diagonal requirement.
Using
M_1(B) ~ [3/(4*pi^2)] B^2 log B
and
M_2(B) ~ C_M2 B(log B)^5,
C_M2>0,
Stage22 proves
M_2(B)/M_1(B)
~ [4*pi^2*C_M2/3] * (log B)^4/B
-> 0.
Hence the adjacent-stratum comparison has exact net polynomial loss B^-1 and logarithmic compensation (log B)^4.
At the audited causal level, the source has one scaled primitive Pythagorean face plus a complementary edge free at polynomial order B, while the target replaces that free edge by a second Pythagorean relation coupled through the unique shared edge.
The third-face nonsquare exclusion is lower order because the three-face locus is o(B(log B)^5), so it is not a leading cause.
