CLAIM:
Let tau^sharp be the part of tau supported on odd split rational primes not dividing the fixed content product c_1*c_2*c_3.


Under the four-core orientation decomposition,


tau^sharp=tau_0^sharp*tau_1^sharp*tau_2^sharp*tau_3^sharp


with pairwise coprime factors and


max_j tau_j^sharp >= (tau^sharp)^(1/4).


If a maximal fresh core is relative, j in {1,2,3}, then M3-T11-026 supplies a squared Gaussian ideal modulus


mathfrak_M_j=(G_j^sharp)^2


with


N(mathfrak_M_j)>=(tau^sharp)^(1/2)


and an exact diagonal quadratic endpoint incidence modulo mathfrak_M_j.


If the maximal fresh core is the all-same core j=0, the retained valuation balance v_pi(A)=2a for every fresh pi^a||G_0 gives


(G_0^sharp)^2 | Num_Gauss(A)


after the fixed coefficient-denominator normalization, with square-divisor norm at least (tau^sharp)^(1/2).


Hence every polynomially large fresh part enters either a large relative squared-modulus branch or a large all-same square-divisor branch. Only the small-fresh branch remains in the Stage14-t27 cover-conditioned friable/restricted-support theorem species.


A pre-endpoint four-core relaxation can separate the relative-core Fourier coefficients after fixing the modulus core and dyadic norm boxes, but that relaxation discards the already-charged triple-spin / torus-double-lift principal-incidence thinning. It is not certified exponent-sufficient for the one-twelfth target.
