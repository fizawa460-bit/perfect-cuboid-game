## M3-HIST14-ROOTSIGN-NEG-001
TYPE: FIREWALL
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
On the Stage14 physical two-face/integral-space-diagonal population, the odd good prime powers of the partner leg are routed deterministically into an exact 2x2 half-angle gcd matrix:
q--*q-+*q+-*q++ = X2_good.
The primewise root-sign choices are therefore divisor-allocation data already encoded by physicality, not independent Bernoulli trials.
A formal two-root choice at each prime does not justify an extra factor 2^(-omega(X2_good)) or any root-sign-only fixed-power saving.
The transferred cross-square equation is a necessary condition on the actual physical image; Stage14 did not prove that an arbitrary primitive-face pair satisfying that equation is physical.
