2. Exact divisor coordinate


For one primitive positive canonical endpoint write
h_i=gcd(R,e_i),
H=h_1 h_2 h_3,
R=H tau,
c_i=h_j h_k.


Use retained015 full (not merely fresh) four-core orientation decomposition
tau=tau_0 tau_1 tau_2 tau_3,
N(G_i)=tau_i.
The four tau_i are pairwise coprime, and G_i are primitive Gaussian integers.


Fix the standard positive Euclid representative for each oriented space triangle, a primary-prime convention, and the finite unit/parity conventions. Factor c_i and tau with the same orientation as that representative. These rules make a canonical packet for each endpoint. Alternatively any bounded/divisor-many retained cover gives the same upper bounds.


Let j be the smallest label attaining max(tau_1,tau_2,tau_3), and let {a,b}={1,2,3}\{j}, a<b. Define
mu=tau_a tau_b,
Sigma=H mu.


Then exactly
Sigma=H*(tau_1 tau_2 tau_3)/max(tau_1,tau_2,tau_3)
     =R/(tau_0 tau_j).                            (171.1)


In particular Sigma is a positive integer divisor of the actual physical R:
Sigma | R,  1<=Sigma<=R<=B.                       (171.2)


This is a canonical physical label, not an ambient root-line or an auxiliary modulus. No fresh-prime assumption is needed.


3. Uniform fixed-Sigma endpoint fiber


Fix Sigma=n. The positive integers
(h_1,h_2,h_3,tau_a,tau_b)
satisfy
h_1 h_2 h_3 tau_a tau_b=n.
There are at most d_5(n)=n^o(1) ordered possibilities. The omitted index j costs at most three choices.


After these five integers are fixed, the three coefficient norms c_i=h_j h_k are fixed. The number of primitive Gaussian B_i with N(B_i)=c_i, with the finite unit/parity choices retained, is B^o(1) by the sum-of-two-squares divisor bound. The representations of the two fixed relative norms tau_a,tau_b likewise cost B^o(1).


Thus the coefficient packet and G_a,G_b are fixed at divisor-many cost. By cyclic symmetry of retained016, the remaining relative core G_j has only B^o(1) endpoint-compatible possibilities.


The retained016 hypothesis check is essential. The pair of actual phases r_a,r_b is neither equal nor opposite on a positive endpoint: opposite phases give opposite physical normalized legs; equal phases force a zero leg through the triple-spin relation and sphere equation. The genus-one quartic is therefore nondegenerate. Its resultant is
Res_t(Q_a,Q_b)=-16(a-b)^2(ab+1)^2,
Q_a(t)=(1-a^2)(1-t^2)+4at.
Model coefficients and point coordinates have physical polynomial height. The uniform rational-torsion input already retained in016 therefore applies, including as content varies over n<=B. The adjective "small" attached historically to the content packet is not needed for this height check: c_i<=R<=B for every endpoint.


Once all three relative cores and the coefficient packet are fixed, set
A=r_1^2+r_2^2+r_3^2,
U=Q^2,
Q=q_0 q_1 q_2 q_3,
q_i=G_i/conj(G_i).
Retained015 gives
conj(A) U^2+2U+A=0.
If A=0 this would force U=0, impossible on the norm-one torus. Otherwise there are at most two U, finitely many Q, and finitely many q_0.


A rational q_0 determines its primitive Gaussian representative G_0 up to units: equality G/conj(G)=G'/conj(G') implies G/G' is rational, and primitivity removes rational dilation. Thus G_0 contributes O(1), not a free radial norm family.


The whole packet fixes tau and R=H tau, and the primitive space directions reconstruct the positive edges up to finite parity/sign choices. All sphere, face-integrality, actual-gcd and canonical-order checks are post-filters.


Consequently, uniformly for n<=B,
m_Sigma(n;B)
=#{primitive canonical endpoints with R<=B and Sigma=n}
<=B^o(1).                                        (171.3)


This proof includes the content budget inside d_5(n). There is no additional C^(3/2) outer factor. It uses one fixed-two elliptic count once and does not multiply cyclic congruence densities.


4. Summed small-Sigma sector


For every 1<=Z<=B,
P_{Sigma<=Z}(B)<=Z B^o(1).                         (171.4)


Hence, for any fixed delta>0,
Z=B^(1/3-delta)
gives
P_{Sigma<=Z}(B)<=B^(1/3-delta+o(1))=o(M3(B)),      (171.5)
using the already-retained denominator lower bound M3(B)>>B^(1/3).


This is a new scoped endpoint disposal, valid before the large-c/small-c split. It is not a global upper improvement or a proof of P/M3->0.


Comparison to corrected030: fixed full R_rel=tau_1 tau_2 tau_3 had divisor-many multiplicity per content packet. Here the largest relative norm is not fixed: retained016 counts it. Simultaneously H is included inside Sigma, so summing over content triples is charged at divisor cost. The content-weighted condition H R_rel<=Z implies Sigma<=Z; the new condition can also admit packets with much larger tau_j. No nonempty physical difference is asserted.


5. Sharpened necessary relative-core balance on the residual


After removing (171.5), every remaining endpoint satisfies
H*tau_(1)*tau_(2)>B^(1/3-delta),                   (171.6)
where tau_(1)<=tau_(2)<=tau_(3) are the three relative norms.


On branch(B), c_max<=C gives H<=C^(3/2). Thus
tau_(1) tau_(2)>B^(1/3-delta)/C^(3/2).             (171.7)


For C=B^(1/6+xi),

Choose delta+(3/2)xi<1/12 when a positive exponent is desired.

For branch(A) the universally valid residual condition is (171.6), without the small-c simplification.

7. Corrected quantifiers in169/170


From N(B)<=K(B)|S(B)| with K(B)=B^o(1), the assertion
|S(B)|=o(B^(1/3))
does not alone imply N(B)=o(B^(1/3)).
For example K(B)=ceil(log B), |S(B)|=floor(B^(1/3)/K(B)) has subpolynomial fibers and little-o support, but weighted mass/B^(1/3)->1. This is an abstract logical countermodel, not a physical population.


A rigorously sufficient power-scale target is instead
|P_rt(B;C)|<<B^(1/3-epsilon), fixed epsilon>0.     (171.11)


The same fixed-power target works for S_Sigma or A_ht. More generally, an explicit envelope K_170(B) is sufficient together with
K_170(B)|P_rt(B;C)|=o(B^(1/3)).
An exact occupancy-weighted sum o(B^(1/3)) is also sufficient.


Retained170 equations170.7-170.11, opposite-face compression and C^(1/2) loss removal remain accepted. Only the automatic bare little-o sufficiency in170.12 and its handoff is superseded. The analogous bare weighted little-o implication in169.9 and crude little-o version169.10 also require absorption of the retained B^o factor; use a fixed power margin or an explicit envelope. The fixed-content active-tau target B^(1/12-epsilon) in015-019 already includes such a margin and remains valid.
