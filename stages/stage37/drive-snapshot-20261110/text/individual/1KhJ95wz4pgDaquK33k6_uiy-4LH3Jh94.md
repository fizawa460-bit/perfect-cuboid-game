## M3-FIB-004
TYPE: THEOREM
STATUS: PROVED_CONDITIONAL


CLAIM:
On a generic valid t-fiber,
the rational torsion subgroup is exactly Z/4Z generated by the universal order-4 point (1,2t), with 2*(1,2t)=(0,0).
All torsion points correspond to degenerate zero/infinite-edge configurations.
Therefore any nondegenerate physical Euler-brick point on a generic valid fiber is non-torsion, so an occupied generic fiber has rank at least 1.
The archive retains a thin exceptional-t caveat.


POPULATION:
valid physical t-fibers of the K_c elliptic fibration.


HEIGHT:
structural; no global count by itself.


DEPENDS_ON:
M3-FIB-002 and the physical interpretation of u=0,+/-1,infinity.


IMPLIES:
rank positivity is automatic on generic occupied fibers; occupancy, not rank, is the moving-family issue.


ALIASES:
occupied-fiber rank-forced theorem;
degenerate torsion theorem.


DUPLICATE_OF:
none.


SUPERSEDES:
empirical observations that occupied small fibers merely appeared to have positive rank.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
Do not infer the density of occupied t-values from forced positive rank.


REOPEN_CONDITION:
remove the archive's thin exceptional-t caveat with a complete torsion classification if needed.

LEGACY-COLD Section 25.4.
