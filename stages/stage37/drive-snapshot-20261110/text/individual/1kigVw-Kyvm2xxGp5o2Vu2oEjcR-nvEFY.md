## M3-XG-005


TYPE: EXACT_STRUCTURE
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
On the whole Stage14 physical raw two-face graph with integral space diagonal R, let the two primitive oriented faces be
F1=(S1,X1,H1), F2=(S2,X2,H2).
Define
c12=gcd(H1,X2),
c21=gcd(H2,X1).
Then exactly


c12=gcd(H1,R),
c21=gcd(H2,R),


gcd(c12,c21)=1,


and therefore


c_raw:=c12*c21
=
gcd(H1*H2,R).


For the canonical Master raw pair sharing the unique odd edge,
(S1,X1,H1)=(U1,V1,W1),
(S2,X2,H2)=(U2,V2,W2),
so c_raw is exactly the Master cross-gcd c.


POPULATION:
whole Stage14 physical raw two-face graph: primitive/canonical physical cuboids, integral space diagonal, one raw pair of integral faces. This includes exactly-two objects and the three raw pairs from an all-three-face object.


HEIGHT:
exact integral space diagonal R<=B.


DEPENDS_ON:
Stage14 AR-003 exact two-face gluing and primitive-face coprimality.


IMPLIES:
c|R is not endpoint-only.
The Master cross-gcd is a restriction of a same-measure Stage14 raw-edge invariant.


ALIASES:
raw-edge cross-gcd;
Stage14 physical cross-gcd;
gcd(H1H2,R) invariant;
cross-gcd space-diagonal identity.


DUPLICATE_OF:

SUPERSEDES:
