rho_j(B)=o(B^(-1/12))
with the required log/epsilon control, or more concretely
|rho_j(B)|<<B^(-1/12-delta)
for some fixed delta>0,
is sufficient for P(B)/M3(B)->0.

exact directional physical two-face host.
