## M3-HIST14-ELOCAL-FW-001
TYPE: FIREWALL
STATUS: FROZEN


CLAIM:
In the late Stage14 heavy-ray unitary-divisor receiver, the physical Boolean can be split exactly into predicates depending only on the complementary dilation E and a residual canonical/reverse-completion Boolean without asserting independence.
On fixed subpolynomial E, the E-local mask is exhausted and cannot be charged again.
On polynomial-E fixed-product cells, the only named E-only arithmetic mask isolated in the merged source is gcd(sqf(E),K_Z)=1, and this mask has zero fixed-power deficit on the one-dimensional E interval.
Pointwise replacement of unitary divisors by ordinary divisors is legal only as an absolute upper envelope; it removes the unitary restriction at zero cost for capacity bounds but does not prove bounded-distortion transfer of the physical weighted selector.
