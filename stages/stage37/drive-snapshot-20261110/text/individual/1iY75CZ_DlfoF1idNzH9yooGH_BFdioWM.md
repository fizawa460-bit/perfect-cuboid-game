## M3-FIB-005
TYPE: EXACT_STRUCTURE
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
For the two rational K_c fibrations
t1=a2/a3
and
t2=(a2+a3)/(b2+b3),
t2 is nonconstant on a fixed generic t1-fiber and defines a degree-2 map
E_t1 -> P^1_t2.
In native coordinates,
t2=2u(t1+1)/(1+u^2+w),
and it is invariant under the involution
(u,w)->(1/u,w/u^2).
Thus t2 is exactly a coordinate on the genus-0 quotient by this involution.


POPULATION:
generic rational fibers of K_c.


HEIGHT:
structural; no counting saving alone.


DEPENDS_ON:
M3-FIB-002.


IMPLIES:
the two fibrations are transverse, but the second base on each first fiber is the natural degree-2 quotient coordinate.


ALIASES:
two-fibration degree-two quotient;
transverse K_c fibration map.


DUPLICATE_OF:
none.


SUPERSEDES:
the possibility that t2 is merely a Möbius reparameterization of t1.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
Do not count the two fibrations as independent coordinates without accounting for this degree-2 relation.


REOPEN_CONDITION:
none.


DETAIL_DOC:
LEGACY-COLD Section 31.
