## M3-HIST14-ENDPOINT-WEDGE-FW-001
TYPE: FIREWALL
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
The Stage14 fixed-U endpoint-headroom branch admits an exact triangular reciprocal-hyperbola corner-wedge description in logarithmic coordinates.
For fixed positive headroom parameter theta, this wedge is geometrically narrow but not deterministically fixed-power small.
Neither its cofactor projection nor its split-prime projection has a proved fixed-power density deficit, and principal mass may concentrate arbitrarily near the hyperbola corner at theorem level.
Therefore endpoint geometry alone does not close the branch.


POPULATION:
Stage14 t129 fixed-U endpoint-headroom cofactor/prime packets.


HEIGHT:
Stage14 physical d<=B under the fixed-U packet.


DEPENDS_ON:
Stage14 t128 endpoint/interior split and Work-brX30 measure boundary.


IMPLIES:
the endpoint branch must be treated as a charged reciprocal-hyperbola corner-wedge projective-prime depletion problem, not as a purely geometric thin-region estimate.


ALIASES:
endpoint logarithmic wedge;
reciprocal-hyperbola corner wedge;
t129.


DUPLICATE_OF:
none.


SUPERSEDES:
the idea that a visually thin endpoint wedge automatically gives a polynomial counting loss.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
Do not infer fixed-power thinness from fixed-width logarithmic geometry.
Do not import the global/s divisor-window measure onto this prime-weighted wedge without a measure-preserving adapter.


REOPEN_CONDITION:
a same-measure projective-prime depletion theorem on the endpoint wedge or a new measure adapter.
