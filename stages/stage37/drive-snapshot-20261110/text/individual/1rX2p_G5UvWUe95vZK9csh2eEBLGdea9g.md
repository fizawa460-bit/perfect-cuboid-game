CLAIM:
Stage28 places the Stage19 space-completion and Stage20 third-face-completion problems on the common base
Y=Bl_4(P1xP1), L=-K_Y.


Both are degree-two K3 covers with total branch class -2K_Y, but their reduced branch profiles differ:
Stage19 space cover = 4 genus-0 branch components;
Stage20 third-face cover = 2 genus-1 branch components.


Their physical quasi-polarizations satisfy
M_sp=pi_sp^*L,
M_face=pi_face^*L,
M_sp^2=M_face^2=8.


The audited fixed-curve facts are:
Stage19 physical M4 rational curves absent;
Stage19 every odd physical M-degree absent;
Stage20 Saunderson physical M-degree 6;
Stage19 physical M6 absence not proved.


Thus Stage20 attains degree six while Stage19 has no fixed rational curve below six, but neither branch topology nor the fixed-curve spectrum gives a global population ordering.
