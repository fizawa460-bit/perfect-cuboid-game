CLAIM:
On the late Stage14 fixed-U endpoint branch, additive prime width and distance of the cofactor norm from its top endpoint are the same scalar variable.
For a dyadic additive-width layer Y=B^(lambda+o(1)) with Y<=B^(1/2), the explicit cofactor annulus contains at most B^o(1)*Y relevant cofactor norms/points and the charged principal prime mass has capacity at most B^(2lambda+o(1)).
Therefore any endpoint sequence capable of carrying a B^(1/2-o(1)) obstruction must localize to a width exponent
lambda >= 1/4-o(1).
All fixed-power subquarter endpoint widths are discharged by absolute capacity, without using prime equidistribution.
After crossing this with the Mitsui-safe modulus split and consuming the tH32 partial positive theorem, the remaining safe-modulus endpoint range is only the intermediate band from about B^(1/4-o(1)) up to B^(1/2) exp(-c sqrt(log B)); near-full safe endpoint widths are discharged, while beyond-Mitsui modulus branches remain.
