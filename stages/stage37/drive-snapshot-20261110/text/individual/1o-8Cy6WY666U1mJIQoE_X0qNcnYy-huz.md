## M3-DEN-NEG-001
TYPE: NEGATIVE_RESULT
STATUS: FROZEN


CLAIM:
No rational surface, in particular no P^2, can dominate the smooth Euler-brick K3 surface K_c generically finitely in characteristic zero.
Therefore a naive bounded-multiplicity Zariski-dense rational parametrization with at least two projective parameters cannot exist.


POPULATION:
global K_c denominator geometry.


HEIGHT:
geometric no-go; independent of a specific height count.


DEPENDS_ON:
K_c is a smooth K3 surface and hence not uniruled.


IMPLIES:
a super-half-power M3 lower bound cannot come merely from adding free rational parameters to obtain a dominant rational surface parametrization.


ALIASES:
NoDominantRationalSurfaceParameterizationOfKc;
naive extra-parameter denominator-family no-go.


DUPLICATE_OF:
M3-LIN-NEG-001 closes one explicit linear family; this card is a global geometric obstruction.


SUPERSEDES:
random extra-free-parameter searches that ignore image dimension and fiber multiplicity.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
A denominator upgrade may still come from moving curves/fibers or intrinsic K3 counting; this card does not rule those out.


REOPEN_CONDITION:
a construction whose image is not a generically finite rational-surface dominance, with controlled physical multiplicity.


DETAIL_DOC:
LEGACY-COLD route-audit Section22.
