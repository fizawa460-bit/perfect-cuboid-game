## M3-DIV-001
TYPE: EXACT_STRUCTURE
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
Let X be the unique odd edge of a primitive Euler brick.
Every Pythagorean partner Y of X is uniquely represented by a divisor A|X^2 with 0<A<X via
Y=(X^2/A-A)/2.
For two even edges use A<C<X.
Writing d_X(A)=gcd(A,X^2/A),
the brick is primitive iff
gcd(d_X(A),d_X(C))=1.
The exact third-face and height conditions are
C^2(X^2-A^2)^2+A^2(X^2-C^2)^2 = square,
and
4A^2C^2R^2=(A^2+C^2)(X^4+A^2C^2).


POPULATION:
exact primitive canonical Euler bricks with unique odd edge X.


HEIGHT:
exact physical R<=B, with no rectangular parameter truncation.


DEPENDS_ON:
elementary factorization (h-Y)(h+Y)=X^2.


IMPLIES:
an exact one-object/one-count physical divisor-coordinate census for M3.


ALIASES:
odd-edge divisor coordinates;
physical divisor M3 adapter;
A-C-X coordinates.


DUPLICATE_OF:
M3-MASTER-001 is a different exact global coordinate system.


SUPERSEDES:
raw Master square-box truncations for physical counting.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
