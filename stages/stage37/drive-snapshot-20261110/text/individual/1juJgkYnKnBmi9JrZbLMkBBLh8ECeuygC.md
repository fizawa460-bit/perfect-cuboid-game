For the unique positive toric reconstruction of a primitive physical shared-edge incidence, define
A=m^2 r^2+n^2 s^2=N(mr+i ns),
B=m^2 s^2+n^2 r^2=N(ms+i nr).
The raw space-diagonal identity is
G^2 R^2=4AB,
where G is the exact common primitive normalization.
There is no divisibility gap after primitive reduction, so
R in Z
iff AB is a square
iff sf(A)=sf(B).
Equivalently there is a unique squarefree k>0 with
A=kP^2, B=kQ^2.
Odd primes in k are split primes p==1 mod 4; inert-prime Gaussian norm valuations are automatically even.
The positive toric pair is uniquely reconstructible from the physical incidence once the unique shared edge and x<y are fixed, so this normal form introduces no hidden polynomial multiplicity.
