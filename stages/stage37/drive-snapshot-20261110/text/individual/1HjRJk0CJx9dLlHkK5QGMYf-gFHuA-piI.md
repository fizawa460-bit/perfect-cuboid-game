## M3-TQ-GATE-002


TYPE: THEOREM_GATE
STATUS: OPEN


CLAIM:
TrivialPartitionSecondSquareclassEnergy.


Let H_F(B) be the exact t28 first-cover candidate state set after all physical primitive/canonical/height/reconstruction/off-diagonal filters and F=Z^2, but before imposing H=square.


For each rational squareclass kappa let


r_H(kappa;B)
=
#{s in H_F(B): [H(s)]=kappa},


and


Q_H(B)
=
sum_kappa r_H(kappa;B)^2.


The exact t27 raw-edge multiplicity gives


3P(B)<=r_H(1;B),


hence


9P(B)^2<=Q_H(B).


Therefore


Q_H(B)=o(B^(2/3))


is sufficient for


P(B)/M3(B)->0.


POPULATION:
same exact t28 first-cover physical candidate measure.


HEIGHT:
exact physical height.


DEPENDS_ON:
M3-TQ-001;
M3-TQ-002;
N_{1,1}=3P;
M3(B)>>B^(1/3).


IMPLIES:
M3 density-zero closure if the 2/3 energy receiver is proved.


GEOMETRIC_RECEIVER:
For generic fixed packets gamma,gamma',


E_gamma: u^2=F_gamma(x)


is elliptic, while


C_gamma: u^2=F_gamma(x), v^2=H_gamma(x)


is genus 5.


The off-fiber H-squareclass collision surface on the first-cover host is birational to


(C_gamma x C_gamma') /
<(v,v')->(-v,-v')>.


Its canonical resolution is a general-type surface with


K^2=64,
chi(O)=16,
q=2,
p_g=17,


away from the explicitly separated degenerate/exceptional packet locus.


ALIASES:
second-squareclass energy;
H-squareclass collision energy;
trivial-partition general-type collision receiver;
double-quartic energy gate.


DUPLICATE_OF:
not the t41 one-square Kummer energy gate.
The t41 surface is Kummer/K3-type; this receiver imposes the second physical square and changes the generic collision surface to general type.


SUPERSEDES:
M3-TQ-GATE-001 as the current primary research receiver only.
The direct A_{1,1}^{(2sq)}=o(B^(1/3)) target remains valid but is less adapted to the existing energy machinery.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:

Do not infer Q_H=o(B^(2/3)) from Faltings on fixed genus-5 fibers.
Do not infer a B^(1/4) endpoint bound unless a near-linear Q_H theorem on this exact measure is actually proved.
Do not use the finite zero-hit diagnostic as asymptotic evidence.
