主張の抜粋: For fixed core i, the Section-21 linear map T_i: (u_j,v_j) -> (F_ij^+,F_ij^-) has matrix [[u_i+v_i, v_i-u_i], [u_i-v_i, u_i+v_i]]. Exactly, det(T_i)=2H_i, and more strongly T_i^T T_i=2H_i I. Hence (F_ij^+)^2+(F_ij^-)^2=2H_i H_j. The image lattice Lambda_i=T_i Z^2 has covolume 2H_i, while every physical region is enlarged in area by the same factor 2H_i. Therefore the lattice-index gain cancels the Jacobian exactly on the pushforward measure. Thus 2H_i is not a new arithmetic sparsity factor. It is a coordinate Jacobian. The Plucker eliminants remain exact structural identities but carry no standalone determinant/lattice saving.
