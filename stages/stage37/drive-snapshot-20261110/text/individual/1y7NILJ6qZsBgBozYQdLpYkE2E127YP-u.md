## M3-HIST16S-INTERACTION-FW-001
TYPE: CORRECTED_RESULT / FIREWALL
STATUS: FROZEN_AUDITED
CLAIM:
Stage16S establishes that the polynomial B^-1 cost of imposing an integral space diagonal is already present in the unrestricted primitive canonical cuboid population:
N_S^all(B)/U(B) ~ const*B^-1.
Audited Stage17 separately has
N_1(B)/M_1(B) asymp (log B)^2/B
after conditioning first on exactly one integral face.
Hence the polynomial exponent of the space-diagonal cost matches in the ambient and one-face-conditioned settings.
The logarithmic profiles do not match.
Stage16S explicitly does NOT interpret this as probabilistic independence, positive/negative correlation, or a factorization law; final interaction classification is deferred to Stage21.
The exact set/cutoff interfaces are preserved, and no face or space condition is double-charged.
POPULATION:
comparison of Stage16 ambient source -> Stage16S space-integral baseline with Stage16 -> Stage17 exactly-one-face survival.
HEIGHT:
same exact R<=B convention; d=R on space-integral targets.
DEPENDS_ON:
M3-HIST16S-SPACE-001; frozen Stage16 and Stage17 interfaces.
IMPLIES:
later interaction analysis may treat one polynomial power of B as intrinsic to space integrality, but must account separately for the extra logarithmic interaction after one face is imposed.
ALIASES:
Stage16S intrinsic-space-cost firewall;
Stage17 polynomial-cost match;
log-profile mismatch;
Stage21 deferral.
DUPLICATE_OF:
none; this is a cross-population interpretation boundary rather than a reusable counting weapon.
SUPERSEDES:
claims that the Stage17 B^-1 power loss is created only by first imposing one integral face.
DO_NOT_REPEAT:
Do not call the matched B^-1 exponent independence.
Do not multiply the Stage16S and Stage17 losses as separate filters.
Do not infer correlation sign from the different logarithmic factors alone.
Do not promote this comparison to perfect-cuboid existence/nonexistence.
REOPEN_CONDITION:
Stage21 or a later exact interaction theorem on the matched populations.
