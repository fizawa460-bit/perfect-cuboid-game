CLAIM:
The four-linear terminal class G_t on the split-full-2 elliptic curve is generically not contained in the standard E_t[2] Kummer subgroup generated by x, x+4, x+4t^2.
Its quadratic extension is branched at eight generic points and therefore gives a genus-5 cover, not an unramified standard 2-isogeny/Kummer cover.


POPULATION:
genuine Pythagorean-base half-point family.


HEIGHT:
structural; no count.


DEPENDS_ON:
M3-HALF-001 and divisor parity at the eight branch points.


IMPLIES:
standard 2-isogeny/Kummer machinery does not directly identify or close the terminal squareclass receiver.

FourLinearClassIsBranchedNonKummer;
branched genus-5 half-point cover.


DUPLICATE_OF:
M3-DESC-NEG-001 concerns the earlier simple occupancy 2-descent; this card concerns the terminal four-linear class.


SUPERSEDES:
attempts to treat G_t as a standard full-2 Kummer class.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
Do not restart the same receiver as another 2-isogeny normalization.


REOPEN_CONDITION:
a moving-family theorem exploiting the special genus-5 branch geometry rather than standard Kummer identification.
