TYPE: EXACT_STRUCTURE
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
For an M3 endpoint, the raw/Master cross-gcd satisfies


gcd(c,X)=1,
c|R,
R^2=X^2+D^2.


Hence


c^2 | X^2+D^2


and


gcd(c,D)=1.


Therefore the primitive physical pair obtained from (X,D) lies on one of


2^omega(c)=c^o(1)


Gaussian root lines modulo c^2:


D == rho X mod c^2,
rho^2 == -1 mod c^2.
