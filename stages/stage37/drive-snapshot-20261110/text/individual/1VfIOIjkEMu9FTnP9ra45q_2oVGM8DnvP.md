## M3-CM-AUT-NEG-001
TYPE: NEGATIVE_RESULT
STATUS: FROZEN


CLAIM:
For the fixed-(delta,z) M3 quartic, the three natural base involutions generating the generic V4 automorphism group preserve both the M3 squareclass and the endpoint squareclass up to rational fourth-power factors.
On the nondegenerate physical rational open there is no rational j=0 or j=1728 enlargement.
Therefore finite V4 orbit pairing does not mix endpoint and non-endpoint states and cannot produce a half-density terminal contraction.


POPULATION:
fixed-(delta,z) M3 quartic and endpoint selector.


HEIGHT:
structural; no global count.


DEPENDS_ON:
the exact transformation formulas for H_q(t), P_q(t) and the j-invariant.


IMPLIES:
automorphism pairing is not an independent density-saving mechanism for this route.


ALIASES:
V4 endpoint-selector invariance;
automorphism-mixing failure.


DUPLICATE_OF:
none.


SUPERSEDES:
the idea that the quartic's finite automorphisms might pair endpoint survivors with nonsurvivors.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
Do not claim a factor-1/2 saving from V4 orbits when both squareclasses are orbit-invariant.


REOPEN_CONDITION:
a different automorphism or correspondence that provably changes the endpoint selector on the physical open.


DETAIL_DOC:
LEGACY-COLD route-audit Section34.4.
