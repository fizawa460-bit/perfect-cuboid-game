## M3-HIST16-OVERLAP-FW-001
TYPE: CORRECTED_RESULT / FIREWALL / HISTORICAL_GATE
STATUS: FROZEN_AUDITED
CLAIM:
Let H_1(B) count primitive canonical R<=B cuboids with at least one integral face diagonal.
Stage16 proves only the order comparison
H_1(B) asymp M_1(B) asymp B^2 log B,
using M_1(B)<=H_1(B), the Stage16 lower bound for M_1, and a three-face union upper bound for H_1.
It does NOT prove
H_1(B)-M_1(B)=o(B^2 log B),
does NOT prove a limit for M_1(B)/H_1(B),
and does NOT prove a directional limiting vector or leading constant for M_1(B).
AR-039 is only a narrower exactly-one family with integral space diagonal and a weaker B^(1/2) lower bound; it is a non-load-bearing regression interface and must not be used to infer the ambient Stage16 exponent.
Stage16 closes audited with no new Arsenal promotion because its portable ingredients are already AR-001, AR-002, and AR-039; the B^2 log B synthesis is a Stage16 population theorem, not a new cross-stage weapon.
POPULATION:
Stage16 exactly-one and at-least-one primitive canonical R<=B populations.
HEIGHT:
R<=B; no space-diagonal integrality in the Stage16 theorem.
DEPENDS_ON:
Stage16 final R01 and manifest R01; AR-001, AR-002, AR-039.
IMPLIES:
later stages may reuse the frozen M_1 and H_1 orders but may not silently strengthen them to overlap little-o, directional asymptotics, or a leading constant.
ALIASES:
Stage16 overlap firewall;
H1 versus M1 same-order boundary;
AR039 non-load-bearing lock;
Stage16 closeout boundary.
DUPLICATE_OF:

SUPERSEDES:

DO_NOT_REPEAT:

Do not infer M_1/H_1 ->1.

Do not create a new Arsenal weapon from this closed synthesis without a distinct reusable theorem species.
