## M3-HIST14-RATIO-001
TYPE: EXACT_STRUCTURE
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
The Stage14 reciprocal scale-free ratio family is a bidegree-(2,2) curve.
For nonzero lambda its projective fiber is singular/reducible exactly at lambda=+4 or -4; in the positive physical chamber the only singular value is lambda=4.
At lambda=4 the fiber splits into two (1,1) components, and exactly one meets the physical chamber:
u*v-u-v-1=0.
Equivalently,
A/D + P/Q + (A/D)(P/Q)=1,
or in host variables
D*(Q-P)=A*(Q+P).
For physical lambda!=4 the fiber is smooth and geometrically irreducible of genus one.
The rational singular branch and smooth genus-one branch therefore require separate counting treatments.


POPULATION:
Stage14 reciprocal biquadratic ratio packets on the physical chamber.


HEIGHT:
structural classification; no standalone whole-family power saving.


DEPENDS_ON:
Stage14 s7-27 exact ratio equation and X5 singular-locus calculation.


IMPLIES:
generic genus-one point bounds must not be applied across the lambda=4 rational component without splitting it off first.


ALIASES:
reciprocal biquadratic singular locus;
lambda=4 Mobius branch;
Stage14 X5.


DUPLICATE_OF:
M3-QUOT-001 is a later Stage37 bidegree-(2,2) model with different variables/population; this is a historical Stage14 ratio-fiber classification.


SUPERSEDES:
treating all ratio fibers uniformly as smooth genus-one curves.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
Do not merge the rational lambda=4 component back into a generic (2,2) genus-one count.
Do not transfer this Stage14 classification to exact M3 without an explicit coordinate/population adapter.


REOPEN_CONDITION:
none.
