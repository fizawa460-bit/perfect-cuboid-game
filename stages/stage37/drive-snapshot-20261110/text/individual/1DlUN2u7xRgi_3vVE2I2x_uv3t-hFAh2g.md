## M3-FIB-NEG-001
TYPE: NEGATIVE_RESULT
STATUS: FROZEN


CLAIM:
On the native quartic fibration, duplication is given by
u'=(u^4-1)/(2uw).
The associated canonical height multiplies by 4 under doubling, and a single positive-rank fiber contributes only B^o(1) physical points under R<=B.
Therefore finding one or finitely many high-rank fibers cannot by itself improve the global M3 exponent enough to close the density problem.


POPULATION:
fixed rational t-fibers of K_c.


HEIGHT:
physical R<=B through M3-FIB-003.


DEPENDS_ON:
M3-FIB-002 and the explicit duplication law.


IMPLIES:
global growth must come from many moving fibers rather than rank exploitation on a bounded fiber set.


ALIASES:
high-rank-fiber no-go;
duplication height-growth negative.


DUPLICATE_OF:
M3-FIB-001 later proves the stronger uniform fixed-fiber B^o(1) statement.


SUPERSEDES:
the historical hope that one lucky high-rank fiber could bypass the global denominator wall.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
Do not reopen fixed-fiber rank search as a global growth mechanism.


REOPEN_CONDITION:
a theorem producing polynomially many distinct occupied fibers or a genuinely different height mechanism.


DETAIL_DOC:
LEGACY-COLD Section 22.
