CLAIM:
The three Stage14 pairwise covariance branches obtained from the available projections are coordinate views of one common two-projection physical covariance mass.
They are not three independent receivers and cannot be double- or triple-charged.
Reparametrizing the same pairwise covariance does not create a second independent modulus-density saving.
