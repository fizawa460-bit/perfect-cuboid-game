## M3-LIN-NEG-001
TYPE: NEGATIVE_RESULT
STATUS: FROZEN


CLAIM:
For any fixed integer c, imposing a linear relation of the form
m-n=c*(r-s)
(and equivalently the same linear-family ansatz variants considered there)
reduces the Master square condition to a nondegenerate quartic genus-1 curve in the remaining parameter.
Therefore this linear ansatz does not generate a continuous two-dimensional Euler-brick family.


POPULATION:
Master-Hit parameter subfamilies defined by the historical linear decomposition ansatz.


HEIGHT:
not a global counting theorem.


DEPENDS_ON:
explicit quartic reduction in LEGACY-COLD Section 20.1.


IMPLIES:
the Section15/16 linear-decomposition route is closed as a denominator-growth mechanism.


ALIASES:
linear ansatz genus-one closure;
m-plus-minus-n proportional route failure.


DUPLICATE_OF:
none.


SUPERSEDES:
the earlier idea that changing the integer coefficient c might recover a large-dimensional family.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
Do not reopen the same linear relation with a different small coefficient c.


REOPEN_CONDITION:
a genuinely nonlinear relation or a proof that a distinct parameterization increases effective physical dimension.


DETAIL_DOC:
LEGACY-COLD Section 20.1.
