## M3-HIST14-SLOPE-001

CLAIM:
After Stage14 primitive reduction r=g a, s=g b with gcd(a,b)=1 and g=B^o(1), the reduced rational slope u=a/b determines the primitive pair uniquely.
Thus projective slope and apparent scale are not two independent polynomial coordinates; only B^o(1) scale-copy multiplicity remains.
After freezing one subpolynomial Gaussian prime, root orientation, and one of B^o(1) atomic mover charts, the entire balanced-allocation plus reciprocal-completion condition collapses to a single Boolean physical-acceptance predicate A_phys(a,b) on primitive rational slopes, with B^o(1) witness multiplicity per accepted direction.
B^o(1) witness multiplicity does not imply sparse acceptance.
