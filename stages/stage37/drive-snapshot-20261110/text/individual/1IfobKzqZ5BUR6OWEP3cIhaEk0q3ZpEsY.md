## M3-DESC-NEG-001
TYPE: NEGATIVE_RESULT
STATUS: FROZEN


CLAIM:
For E: y^2=x(x^2+Px+1), classical 2-isogeny descent has only squarefree classes d=+/-1.
The physical d=1 homogeneous space is exactly the original quartic
w^2=m^4+Pm^2e^2+e^4,
while d=-1 is nonphysical for positive edge data and is real-obstructed when |t|<1.
Thus this 2-descent does not produce a simpler occupancy criterion than the original physical equation.


POPULATION:
the t=a2/a3 elliptic fibration.


HEIGHT:
not a new counting theorem.


DEPENDS_ON:
M3-FIB-002.


IMPLIES:
the simple 2-isogeny-descent shortcut for occupied-fiber detection is closed.


ALIASES:
occupancy 2-descent no-go;
d-plus-minus-one descent collapse.


DUPLICATE_OF:
none.


SUPERSEDES:
the plan to replace physical occupancy by a cheaper finite local descent test on this model.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
Do not cite local solubility of the bare quartic as evidence for nondegenerate physical occupancy; torsion makes that test vacuous.


REOPEN_CONDITION:
a descent targeting the nondegenerate locus or a genuinely different quotient.


DETAIL_DOC:
LEGACY-COLD Section 28.
