CLAIM:
A genuine rank-4 quadric in the ideal of K_c is
b1^2+b3^2=b2^2+2a2^2,
obtained as the exact linear combination -Q1+Q2-Q3 of the three defining quadrics.
Factoring this relation over the natural quadratic extension and solving both induced linear relations yields the verified auxiliary fibration
W^2
=
2(t^4+1)
+
b2^2(t^2-1)^2
-
4it*b2*(t^2-1).
For real Euler-brick points the natural parameter t is generally complex, so this fibration is bona fide K_c geometry but is not presently a direct real-height counting coordinate.
