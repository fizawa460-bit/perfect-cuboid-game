## M3-FIB-FW-002
TYPE: CORRECTED_BUG
STATUS: FROZEN


CLAIM:
A candidate K_c fibration/point construction is not physically valid until all three defining K_c equations are checked on the reconstructed point.
A third-fibration experiment enforced only two relations and appeared to produce a genus-0-like family with six hits.
After the omitted equation
b1^2=a2^2+a3^2
was restored, the same search produced zero valid points.
This repeats the earlier unrestricted-t omission and establishes an explicit validation checklist.

constructed candidate points/fibers on K_c.


HEIGHT:
all computational or symbolic fibration experiments.


DEPENDS_ON:
M3-KC-001.


IMPLIES:
partial satisfaction of the equations used during parametrization can create entirely spurious low-genus/rich families.


ALIASES:
all-three-Kc-equations firewall;
third-fibration self-caught bug;
partial-Kc reconstruction bug.


DUPLICATE_OF:
M3-FIB-FW-001 says t must satisfy the missing base equation; this card generalizes the validation rule to every new fibration construction.


SUPERSEDES:
the spurious t=119/123 genus-0-looking search.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
For every reconstructed candidate, explicitly verify b1^2=a2^2+a3^2, b2^2=a1^2+a3^2, and b3^2=a1^2+a2^2 before assigning geometric or counting meaning.
