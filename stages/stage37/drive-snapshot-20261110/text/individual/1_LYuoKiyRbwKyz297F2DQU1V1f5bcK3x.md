## M3-TERM-001


TYPE: EXACT_STRUCTURE
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
At an endpoint, with G=gcd(PQ,YZ),
there are unique coprime opposite-parity alpha>beta>0 such that
PQ=G*(alpha^2+beta^2),
YZ=2*G*alpha*beta,
XR=G*(alpha^2-beta^2).
Thus
(XR/G,YZ/G,PQ/G)
is a primitive Pythagorean triple.


POPULATION:
exact physical endpoints.


HEIGHT:
unchanged.


DEPENDS_ON:
(PQ-YZ)(PQ+YZ)=X^2R^2.


IMPLIES:
canonical terminal Pythagorean allocation.


ALIASES:
terminal product Pythagorean allocation; third terminal triple.


DUPLICATE_OF:
none.


SUPERSEDES:
none.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
The allocation alone has no independent counting saving.


REOPEN_CONDITION:
a new factor-allocation theorem that produces an independent endpoint deficit.
