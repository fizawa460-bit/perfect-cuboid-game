3. Exact opposite-face genus-one point


For one of the two opposite-face edges, say e_k, define

Every physical endpoint satisfies


1-x^2
=
(f_jk^2-e_k^2)/f_jk^2
=
e_j^2/f_jk^2,


so 1-x^2 is a rational square.


Also


rho*x
=
(f_jk/R)*(e_k/f_jk)
=
e_k/R.


Therefore


1-rho^2*x^2
=
1-(e_k/R)^2
=
f_ij^2/R^2,


which is also a rational square.


Consequently every endpoint maps to the rational genus-one over-cover

This is exactly the same one-parameter full-rational-2-torsion family used in retained M3-T11-010.

At this stage h_j and tau are already fixed by Sections 2 and the fixed pair (r,tau). Therefore

Thus one rational genus-one point determines the formerly-moving content h_i uniquely whenever the divisibility and positivity filters required by a physical endpoint hold.
