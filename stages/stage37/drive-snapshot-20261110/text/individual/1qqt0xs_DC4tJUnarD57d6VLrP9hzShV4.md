## M3-CM-DESC-NEG-001
TYPE: NEGATIVE_RESULT
STATUS: FROZEN


CLAIM:
In the xi=1 two-point CM-twist receiver, the standard 2-isogeny Kummer classes of the first-conic point P1 and the endpoint quotient point P2 are equal:
alpha(P1)=alpha(P2)=[1+q^2].
The tempting claim that the two classes differ by [-1] is false.
Therefore simple 2-isogeny descent does not separate the points, prove rank at least 2, or yield an extra Selmer-dimension saving.


POPULATION:
the fixed-twist two-point CM receiver.


HEIGHT:
structural; no global count.


DEPENDS_ON:
the exact rational 2-torsion cubic model and M3 square relation.


IMPLIES:
the genuinely new information in this route is off-diagonal two-point incidence inside one descent class, not two independent descent classes.


ALIASES:
CM two-point Kummer-class equality;
2-isogeny separation failure.


DUPLICATE_OF:
M3-DESC-NEG-001 concerns the earlier one-point occupancy descent; this is the later two-point CM receiver.


SUPERSEDES:
the false [-1]-class separation claim.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
Do not credit rank>=2 or Selmer growth from the existence of the second point without a different independence argument.


REOPEN_CONDITION:
a genuine Mordell-Weil independence theorem or a different descent invariant separating P1 and P2.


DETAIL_DOC:
LEGACY-COLD route-audit Section33.5.
