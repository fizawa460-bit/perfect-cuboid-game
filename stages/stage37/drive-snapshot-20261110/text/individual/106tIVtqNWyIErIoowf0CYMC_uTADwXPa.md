## M3-GAUSS-001
TYPE: EXACT_STRUCTURE
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
There exists a Gaussian integer kappa_h of norm h and zeta such that
X+iD = epsilon*kappa_h^2*zeta,
Norm(kappa_h)=h.
Moreover
Norm(zeta)
=
(X^2+D^2)/h^2
=
(N_-/h)*(N_+/h).
On the one-factor locus this becomes
Norm(zeta)=u^2*v
with gcd(u,v)=1.
For every fixed pair (h,J) with
J=(X^2+D^2)/h^2,
the exact M3 multiplicity is B^o(1).
Also for every fixed D<=B, the exact M3 multiplicity is B^o(1).


POPULATION:
exact physical M3 common-core strata.


HEIGHT:
exact R<=B.


DEPENDS_ON:
M3-CROSSCORE-001 and Gaussian factorization in Z[i].


IMPLIES:
h is literally a matched Gaussian square factor of the physical terminal pair (X,D).


ALIASES:
PhysicalMatchedGaussianSquareExtraction;
FixedMatchedCoreQuotientMultiplicity;
FixedOppositeFaceDiagonalMultiplicity.


DUPLICATE_OF:
none.


SUPERSEDES:
abstract common-core interpretations without a physical Gaussian square factor.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
Fixed (h,J) or fixed D anti-concentration does not control the total moving pair count.


REOPEN_CONDITION:
none.
