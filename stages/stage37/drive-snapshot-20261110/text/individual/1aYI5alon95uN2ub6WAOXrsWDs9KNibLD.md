## M3-HIST14-COLLISION-FW-001
TYPE: FIREWALL
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
For the Stage14 fixed subpolynomial mover prime, proportional copies of one primitive divisor-pair direction contribute only B^(1/2+o(1)) energy and cannot explain the forced B^(1-o(1)) collision energy.
Thus nonproportional energy is genuinely forced.
Nevertheless the fixed-prime norm-ratio collision equation and its Lagrange determinant identity follow from the two individual fixed-prime norm equations; they add no fresh codimension and cannot be charged as an independent determinant saving.
Collision energy is a localization device here, not a second arithmetic constraint.


POPULATION:
Stage14 s7-63 fixed-prime primitive divisor-pair receiver.


HEIGHT:
Stage14 physical d<=B.


DEPENDS_ON:
M3-HIST14-MOVER-001.


IMPLIES:
large nonproportional collision energy need not imply an exploitable incidence theorem when the collision equation is algebraically tautological.


ALIASES:
proportional collision peel;
fixed-prime collision tautology;
s7-63.


DUPLICATE_OF:
none.


SUPERSEDES:
the idea that the forced collision pair automatically supplies an extra determinant/codimension saving.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
Do not double charge collision energy after it has only served to select a heavy prime/family.
Check whether the pair equation is independent of the one-state equations before invoking determinant heuristics.


REOPEN_CONDITION:
a genuinely independent pair relation not implied by the two single-state norm equations.
