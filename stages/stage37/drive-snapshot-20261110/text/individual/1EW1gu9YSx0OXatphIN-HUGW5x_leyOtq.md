CLAIM:
Let C_w be the collision content of M3-T11-037. Then
gcd(T_1,M_rel^4)=M_rel^2*C_w.
Since gcd(T_3,M_rel)=1 and r=T_3/T_1,
gcd(Den(r),M_rel^4)=M_rel^2*C_w.
For u=A_u/B_u reduced,
Den(r)=(A_u^2+B_u^2)/g_u with g_u in {1,2}, hence
gcd(A_u^2+B_u^2,M_rel^4)=M_rel^2*C_w.
Therefore C_w>=M_rel amplifies the canonical common-u Gaussian modulus from M_rel^2 to at least M_rel^3.
