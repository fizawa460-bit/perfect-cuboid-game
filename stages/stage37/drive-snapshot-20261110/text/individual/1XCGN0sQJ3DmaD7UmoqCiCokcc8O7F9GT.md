## M3-DELTA-001
TYPE: EXACT_STRUCTURE
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
In the Section34 integral model, after primitive normalization there is a signed squarefree kernel delta with
C^2-D^2=delta*r^2,
c^2a^2-d^2b^2=delta*s^2.
For every odd prime,
delta_odd divides rad(PQ).
Hence for fixed genuine Pythagorean base (L,M), the number of possible odd squareclass slices is at most
2^omega(PQ)=(PQ)^o(1).


POPULATION:
exact M3 points in the Section34/35 transverse quotient coordinates.


HEIGHT:
exact physical model retained; this card is squareclass structure, not a global count.


DEPENDS_ON:
M3-QUOT-001 and the four linear-factor gcd graph.


IMPLIES:
the full 2-descent has subpolynomial squareclass complexity over a fixed base.


ALIASES:
restricted delta descent;
four-edge squareclass allocation;
delta_odd support on rad(PQ).


DUPLICATE_OF:
none.


SUPERSEDES:
treating delta as a free squareclass.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
Do not conclude each fixed-delta slice is automatically toric: the coupled system retains a genus-1 core in general.


REOPEN_CONDITION:
a uniform exact physical count over the coupled delta slices.


DETAIL_DOC:
LEGACY-COLD Section 35.
