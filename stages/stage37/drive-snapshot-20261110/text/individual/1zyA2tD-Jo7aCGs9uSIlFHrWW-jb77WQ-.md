## M3-PARAM-001
TYPE: THEOREM
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
For every sufficiently large good odd prime p, on the admissible Master-Hit parameter measure,
Pr(H is a nonzero square | M is a nonzero square)
=
1/2+O(p^(-1/2)),
with an absolute implied constant.
The proof is by uniform Weil bounds for the one-variable quartics M(n), H(n), and M(n)H(n), with only an O(p)-thin discriminant exception in the outer parameters.


POPULATION:
admissible Master-Hit parameter residue classes modulo p.


HEIGHT:
local parameter measure only; no physical R<=B transfer.


DEPENDS_ON:
explicit quartic discriminants and Weil character-sum bounds.


IMPLIES:
the correct conditional local factor on the raw Master parameter measure is asymptotically one half.


ALIASES:
Step1.6 conditional local law;
Master parameter conditional square law.


DUPLICATE_OF:
M3-LOCAL-003 is the later half-angle local law for canonical terminal factors; this card concerns the historical M/H Master predicates.


SUPERSEDES:
heuristic conditional local probabilities for M and H on the parameter measure.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
Do not call this a theorem on exact physical M3(B).


REOPEN_CONDITION:
none.


DETAIL_DOC:
LEGACY-COLD Section6.
