## M3-LOCAL-003
TYPE: THEOREM
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
For every good odd prime p, on the complete local parameter measure conditioned on the Euler-square predicate E(u,v):
Pr(chi_p(A_-)=+1 | E square)
=
1/2+O(p^-1/2),
and similarly for A_+.
Moreover the pair is locally asymptotically uniform:
Pr(chi_p(A_-)=eps_-, chi_p(A_+)=eps_+ | E square)
=
1/4+O(p^-1/2)
uniformly in eps_-,eps_+ in {+1,-1}.


POPULATION:
complete good-prime local half-angle parameter measure.


HEIGHT:
pure local statement; no physical global transfer.


DEPENDS_ON:
Weil-type character-sum bounds for E,A_-,A_+.


IMPLIES:
the canonical terminal factors are locally unbiased after conditioning on the Euler-square condition.


ALIASES:
OneFactorLocalMarginal;
PlusFactorLocalMarginal;
LocalTerminalFactorPairUniformity.


DUPLICATE_OF:
stronger and more tailored than the old product-character law for the current one-factor receiver.


SUPERSEDES:
using only the product character when the separate factor marginals are needed.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
Do not promote this local law to exact-M3 global equidistribution.


REOPEN_CONDITION:
none.
