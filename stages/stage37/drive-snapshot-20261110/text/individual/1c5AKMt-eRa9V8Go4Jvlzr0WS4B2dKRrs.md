CLAIM:
In the fully factored four-variable model, the cross gcd
g=gcd(ab,cf)
is exactly a product r*s*t*u of four cross factors.
The raw terminal factors M_-,M_+ satisfy
M_-=(r*u)^2*N_-,
M_+=(s*t)^2*N_+,
and
gcd(M_-,M_+)=gcd(N_-,N_+)=h.
Hence
L_- square iff M_-/h square,
and similarly for L_+.
The M3 third-face reduction also forces the exact 2-adic mask
v2(b^2-a^2) != v2(f^2-c^2).
