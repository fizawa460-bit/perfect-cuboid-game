## M3-PAIR-GATE-001


TYPE: THEOREM_GATE
STATUS: OPEN_UNTESTED


CLAIM:
Let
W_B={(X,D): some E in M3(B)}
and
E_B={(X,D) in W_B: X^2+D^2 is a square}.
Because fixed-(X,D) multiplicity is B^o(1), any fixed-power deficit
|E_B|<=B^(-delta+o(1))|W_B|
would close P/M3->0.


POPULATION:
occupied exact-M3 physical (X,D) image.


HEIGHT:
exact R<=B inherited from M3.


DEPENDS_ON:
fixed-(X,D) multiplicity B^o(1).


IMPLIES:
METHOD F closure.

R10 occupied-pair expansion;
occupied (X,D) incidence;
physical pair image deficit.

fixed-q occupied residue expansion is related but NOT automatically identical: this card asks for a global occupied-image deficit, while M3-LOCAL-GATE-001 asks for finite-prime contraction on exact M3 measure.


SUPERSEDES:
ambient Pythagorean sparsity as a sufficient argument.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
Do not compare endpoint pairs with all ambient integer pairs.
Do not assume the occupied M3 image is equidistributed.
Before merging with M3-LOCAL-GATE-001, prove the exact implication in the same measure.

an occupied-image expansion/incidence theorem or a rigorous reduction to M3-LOCAL-GATE-001.
