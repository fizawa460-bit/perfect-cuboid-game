## M3-HIST14-MOVER-001
TYPE: AUXILIARY
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
On the pure arithmetic, range-stable Stage14 mover branch, the active mover prime must satisfy ell=B^o(1).
A fixed-power-size ell necessarily changes the archimedean scale and belongs to the separately charged range/sector branch.
The prime-flip boundary is given by degree-at-most-two divisor graphs in ell.
Consequently the total range-stable mover-prime image has size B^o(1).
If the mover incidence saturates at B^(1/2-o(1)), Cauchy therefore forces collision energy at least B^(1-o(1)); the diffuse-image alternative disappears.
