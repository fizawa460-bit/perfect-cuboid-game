## M3-HIST17-N1-ASYMPTOTIC-001
TYPE: THEOREM_RESULT / EXACT_INTERFACE
STATUS: PROVED_AUDITED
CLAIM:
Let N_1(B) count primitive canonical cuboids
0<a<b<c, gcd(a,b,c)=1,
with exactly one integral face diagonal, integral space diagonal, and
R=sqrt(a^2+b^2+c^2)<=B.
Stage17 proves the full asymptotic
N_1(B) ~ [kappa/(24*pi)] B(log B)^3,
with kappa/(24*pi)>0.
This is transferred exactly from the frozen Stage13 theorem after proving that the Stage17 physical population, face multiplicity, primitivity, canonical ordering, and space equation are literal matches, while the cutoff adapter is the identity
d=R>0,
hence d<=B iff R<=B.
No measure, multiplicity, orientation, or quantifier adapter is required.
The absolute Stage17 population law is therefore settled at polynomial exponent 1, logarithmic power 3, with a proved positive leading constant.
POPULATION:
primitive canonical exactly-one-face cuboids with integral space diagonal.
HEIGHT:
literal R<=B; exactly identical to d<=B on the target.
DEPENDS_ON:
frozen Stage13 exactly-one-space theorem; Stage17 population/cutoff lock.
IMPLIES:
the Stage17 absolute population has true order B(log B)^3 with positive leading constant.
ALIASES:
Stage17 N1 asymptotic;
exactly-one plus space population;
kappa/(24*pi) theorem;
Stage13-to-Stage17 exact transfer.
DUPLICATE_OF:
none; Stage16S records the ambient space-integral population, not the exactly-one-face Stage17 asymptotic.
SUPERSEDES:
finite-data or lower-bound-only descriptions of the Stage17 population.
DO_NOT_REPEAT:

Do not change exactly-one to at-least-one or two/three-face populations.
Do not infer an effective error term not present in the frozen upstream theorem.
REOPEN_CONDITION:
a new effective error term or sharper secondary asymptotic for N_1(B).
