## M3-HIST14-EQ-NEG-001
TYPE: NEGATIVE_RESULT
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
For the Stage14 charged projective-class block,
T_Omega = M_Omega + D_Omega,
where M_Omega is the uniform principal-class mass and D_Omega is the centered physical discrepancy.
Ordinary equidistribution, meaning D_Omega=o(M_Omega), preserves the exponent of M_Omega.
Since the projective principal factor is only B^(-o(1)), small centered discrepancy alone cannot yield a fixed B^(-delta) saving.
A fixed-power depletion with positive principal mass requires principal-scale negative discrepancy: the physically selected class must be systematically underrepresented relative to its uniform share.


POPULATION:
Stage14 fixed-U charged cofactor/projective-class blocks.


HEIGHT:
Stage14 physical d<=B.


DEPENDS_ON:

IMPLIES:
"better equidistribution" can be the wrong theorem species when the desired conclusion is a power-saving deficit relative to a subpolynomial principal share.


ALIASES:
ordinary equidistribution no-go;
principal-scale depletion criterion;
Stage14 t113.


DUPLICATE_OF:
none; current M3 local-equidistribution gates ask for transfer on different populations and do not contain this principal-vs-centered sign criterion.


SUPERSEDES:
the expectation that a small centered projective discrepancy automatically gives a fixed-power survivor saving.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
Do not cite ordinary class equidistribution as a power-saving theorem unless the principal mass is already power-small or the discrepancy has the required negative principal-scale sign.


REOPEN_CONDITION:
a same-measure theorem proving power-small principal mass or systematic principal-scale negative discrepancy for the target selected classes.
