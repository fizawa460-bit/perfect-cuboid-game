## M3-HIST14-COV-NEG-001
TYPE: NEGATIVE_RESULT
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
For the Stage14 dense pair-covariance receiver, square-root exponent saturation forces only an exponent-zero covariance ratio eta=B^(-o(1)).
It does not force near-deterministic coupling or eta=1-B^(-delta+o(1)).
The exact Bernoulli Frechet envelope permits positive exponent-zero covariance that stays a fixed distance from deterministic equality.
Therefore no fixed-power saving follows merely by replacing a square-root-saturating pairwise interaction with a "nearly deterministic coupling" description.

IMPLIES:
exponent saturation is weaker than structural determinism; an arithmetic factorization or correlation theorem is still required.
