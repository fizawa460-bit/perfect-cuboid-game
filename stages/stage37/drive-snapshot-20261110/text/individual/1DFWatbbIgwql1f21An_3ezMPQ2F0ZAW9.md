CLAIM:
Stage28 is the audited Stage19 -> Stage20 bridge comparison under the common primitive/canonical physical cutoff R<=B.


It compares the disjoint exact-face populations
N_2(B): exactly two integral face diagonals plus integral space diagonal,
and
M_3(B): exactly three integral face diagonals, with no space-diagonal requirement.


With
H_ge2=M_2+M_3,
Sigma_19=N_2/H_ge2,
Phi_20=M_3/H_ge2,
one has the exact matched-host identity
M_3/N_2=Phi_20/Sigma_19.


The audited theorem corridor is
B^(1/4) << N_2(B) <<_epsilon B^(1/2+epsilon),


liminf M_3(B)/B^(1/3) >= 27/(40*pi^2)>0,


and for every fixed 0<eta<1/46,
M_3(B)<<_eta B(log B)^(5-eta).


Consequently
M_3/N_2 >>_epsilon B^(-1/6-epsilon),


and for every fixed 0<delta<1/46,
M_3/N_2=o(B^(3/4)(log B)^(5-delta)).


Neither true population exponent nor eventual source/target ordering is identified.
