## M3-HIST14-PROJCOLL-001
TYPE: EXACT_STRUCTURE
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
On a concentrated exact polynomial common-core modulus C, exponent-zero centered Stage14 root discrepancy forces quadratic-scale character energy.
Diagonal self-pairs are fixed-power too small, so a saturating sequence must contain exponent-zero off-diagonal projective collision mass.
For two primitive candidate vectors, zero determinant means the same primitive projective ray up to frozen finite labels; nonzero determinant gives a genuine mover relation det=kC.
Thus concentrated saturation splits exactly into either heavy repeated primitive-ray incidence or genuine nonzero-determinant mover energy.
The same C is already charged and neither C nor k is automatically a second independent modulus.

DO_NOT_REPEAT:

Do not count diagonal energy as capable of supporting the concentrated saturation scale.
