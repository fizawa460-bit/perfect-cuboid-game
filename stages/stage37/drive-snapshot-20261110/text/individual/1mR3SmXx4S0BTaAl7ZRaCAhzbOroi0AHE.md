## M3-ONE-003
TYPE: EXACT_STRUCTURE
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
For a primitive physical Euler brick define
g1=gcd(P,Y),
g2=gcd(Q,Z),
p=gcd(P,Z),
d=gcd(Q,Y).
These four gcd channels are pairwise coprime and
gcd(PQ,YZ)=g1*g2*p*d.
After
P=g1*p*r,
Z=g2*p*s,
Q=g2*d*u,
Y=g1*d*v,
one has
gcd(ru,vs)=1,
ru odd,
16|vs,
and exactly
L_-=ru-vs,
L_+=ru+vs.


POPULATION:
exact primitive physical M3.


HEIGHT:
unchanged.


DEPENDS_ON:
M3-ONE-001 and primitive face coprimality.


IMPLIES:
the one-factor condition is the canonical primitive bilinear equation
x^2=ru-vs.


ALIASES:
PhysicalFourGcdSkeleton;
PrimitiveBilinearFactorization;
one-factor rectangular core.


DUPLICATE_OF:
none.


SUPERSEDES:
Master-only presentations of the one-factor normalization.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
Do not assume same-side gcd(r,u) or gcd(v,s) is individually 1; only the stated cross coprimalities are proved.

none.
