## 1. Exact E3 model and exceptional points

Use h=t-1/t and the E3 quartic from TI-001:

eta^2=(V^2 h^2+4U^2)(W^2 h^2+4U^2).

Set alpha=(W+V)^2=(m+n)^4, beta=(W-V)^2=(m-n)^4. The smooth projective curve is birational, over Q, to

E: Y^2=X(X-alpha)(X-beta).

On h!=0 the exact map is

X=V^2+W^2+2(eta+4U^2)/h^2, Y=4UX/h.

On Y!=0 the inverse is h=4UX/Y, eta=(h^2/2)(X-V^2-W^2)-4U^2. This is an isomorphism of smooth projective genus-one curves after choosing (h,eta)=(0,4U^2) as origin; it is not merely a degree-two isogeny. Thus rational ranks and torsion groups agree with E3.

Proof: put S=V^2+W^2, a=4U^2. The quartic identity is eta^2=V^2W^2 h^4+aS h^2+a^2. Since alpha=S+2VW and beta=S-2VW,

X(X-alpha)(X-beta)=4X((eta+a)^2/h^4-V^2W^2)=4aX^2/h^2=Y^2.

The inverse follows by substitution. X cannot vanish at a finite h!=0 point: substituting eta+a=-Sh^2/2 in the quartic would imply (S^2/4-V^2W^2)h^4=0, but S^2-4V^2W^2=U^4!=0. Hence Y!=0 on this locus. The two h=0 points map to O and (0,0); the two points at infinity (eta/h^2=+/-VW) map to (alpha,0) and (beta,0). These are precisely E[2](Q). In particular a physical, nondegenerate t!=0,+/-1 lifts to an E-point outside E[2]. The smooth projective h=0/infinity extensions matter: do not discard them and miscount torsion.

## 2. A globally minimal integral semistable model for every primitive source

The exact variable change X=4x+beta, Y=8y+4x gives

E_min: y^2+xy=x^3+A2 x^2+A4 x,
A2=(2beta-alpha-1)/4,
A4=beta(beta-alpha)/16=-beta VW/4.

Both coefficients are integral: m+n and m-n are odd, their fourth powers are 1 mod 16, and 4|V. The invariants are

c4_min=U^4+16V^2W^2,
Delta_min=U^8 V^2 W^2/16.

The nonscaled split model has c4=16c4_min and Delta=4096Delta_min=256U^8V^2W^2. The coordinate change divides invariants by 2^4 and 2^12 respectively.

For an odd prime dividing Delta_min, pairwise coprimality shows c4_min is nonzero modulo that prime: if it divides U the term 16V^2W^2 is a unit; if it divides V or W the term U^4 is a unit. At 2, c4_min is odd. Consequently gcd(c4_min,Delta_min)=1. At a prime dividing Delta_min, a unit c4 prohibits any further integral minimization (c4 changes by a fourth power); the reduced curve has a node, so reduction is multiplicative. At every other prime the discriminant is a unit, so reduction is good and the equation minimal. This proves global minimality and semistability, including at 2.

At 2, v2(Delta_min)=2v2(V)-4: reduction is good exactly when v2(V)=2, and multiplicative otherwise. The conductor therefore is the odd radical of UVW, multiplied by 2 exactly when v2(V)>2. For m=2,n=1: [a1,a2,a3,a4,a6]=[1,-20,0,-5], c4=6481, Delta=164025=3^8*5^2, conductor 15. No rank assertion is made for this example.

Semistability is a uniformly proved E3 prerequisite, not proof of L(E3,1)!=0. It neither validates the actual 504 ambiguous workers nor covers the E_uV family. The original 1072 aggregate remains HOLD.

## 3. Uniform exclusion of order four; exact torsion-four screen

For a split cubic, a rational point is twice a rational point iff each of its three root differences is a rational square (Bekker–Zarhin Theorem 2.1). The order-two points (0,0) and (beta,0) cannot be halved because at least one difference is negative. The point (alpha,0) can be halved iff alpha-beta=4VW is a square, since alpha already is a square.

But VW cannot be a square for a primitive source. As gcd(V,W)=1, this would give V=r^2 and W=s^2 with r,s positive integers. Then U^2=s^4-r^4, and (x1,y1)=(s^2/r^2,sU/r^3) is a rational point on y1^2=x1^3-x1 with y1!=0. Such a point produces a nonzero rational right triangle of area 1: legs |(x1^2-1)/y1| and |2x1/y1|, hypotenuse |(x1^2+1)/y1|. Fermat's descent proves no such triangle exists. Therefore E has no order-four point, hence none of order eight. Mazur's noncyclic rational torsion classification leaves only

E(Q)_tors = Z/2 x Z/2 (4 points) OR Z/2 x Z/6 (12 points).

Define Qtest={7,11,13,17},
G_tor={primitive p: at least one q in Qtest does not divide UVW},
X_tor={primitive p: each q in Qtest divides UVW}.

The latter condition is exactly 17017=7*11*13*17 dividing UVW. The primes can divide different factors; this does NOT require any one factor divisible by 17017.

For good source classes over F_q, the COMPLETE projective enumeration is:

| q | #E(F_q) : number of good projective [m:n] classes |
|---|---|
| 7 | 8 : 4 |
| 11 | 8 : 4; 16 : 4 |
| 13 | 16 : 8 |
| 17 | 16 : 12 |

All good classes have n!=0, so [m:n]=[k:1] exhausts them. Skip exactly k with (k^2-1)(2k)(k^2+1)=0; count every x,y and the point at infinity on Y^2=X(X-(k+1)^4)(X-(k-1)^4). The attached standard-library script performs this exhaustive finite proof, not a sample of integer sources. Each group order is prime to 3. At good q!=3, rational three-torsion injects under reduction, so there is no rational three-torsion on E. Together with the preceding classification this proves

p in G_tor => #E3(Q)_tors=4.

No claim of torsion-four outside G_tor is made. A rational three-division polynomial root alone would not suffice to certify rational three-torsion: its Y-coordinate must also be rational. The screen is sufficient rather than claimed maximal.

## 4. Positive-rank / exceptional-source receiver on the SAME physical measure

Use exactly the active route's symmetric mu_B(p,q), total mass M3(B), and F_B(p)=sum_q mu_B(p,q). Define the mathematical set

G_plus={p in G_tor: rank E3(p)(Q)>0}.

For p in G_tor, rank zero implies E3(Q)=E3[2](Q), and section 1 shows there is no physical nondegenerate quartic lift. Equivalently TI-001 criterion A closes the endpoint fiber. Thus every endpoint source belongs to A=X_tor union G_plus, and

P(B) <= R_AA(B) <= R_plusplus(B)+2 sum_{p in X_tor}F_B(p),
R_plusplus(B)=sum_{p,q in G_plus}mu_B(p,q).

The second inequality is the union bound for objects with at least one exceptional source. Symmetry gives equal marginal masses for the two positions. It remains valid for coincident sources and counts each canonical object once. No independence assumption is used.

An actual mathematical complement of the FULL sufficient TI criterion is contained in A. However a finite certification registry's UNKNOWN/hard set is NOT automatically contained in A: an uncomputed source can have rank zero. UNKNOWN must never be relabelled positive rank. The endpoint inclusion and bound above do not require computation of every rank or this epistemic inclusion.

A sufficient new decomposition of the main goal is

R_plusplus(B)=o(M3(B)) AND sum_{p in X_tor}F_B(p)=o(M3(B)).

Neither term has yet been proved small. In particular a fixed modulus 17017 does not itself yield vanishing physical weight, and positive unweighted density of G_tor does not close the gate. The receiver can also be improved by applying E_uV criteria to sources remaining in A.
