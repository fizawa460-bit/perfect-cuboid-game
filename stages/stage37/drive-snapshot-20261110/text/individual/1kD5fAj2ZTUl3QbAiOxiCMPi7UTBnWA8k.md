## M3-FIB-002
TYPE: EXACT_STRUCTURE
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
Fixing t=a2/a3 on K_c gives the genus-one quartic
w^2=u^4+(4t^2-2)u^2+1.
For t!=0,+/-1 it is nonsingular and has the Weierstrass model
Y^2=X^3-432(t^4-t^2+1)X+1728(t^2+1)(2t^2-1)(t^2-2),
with
j(t)=256(t^4-t^2+1)^3/[t^4(t-1)^2(t+1)^2].
Thus the fibration is non-isotrivial.


POPULATION:
K_c rational fibration; physical fibers are the Pythagorean-base subfamily.
