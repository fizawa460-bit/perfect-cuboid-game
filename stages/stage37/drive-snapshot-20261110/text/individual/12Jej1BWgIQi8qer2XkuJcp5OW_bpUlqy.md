CLAIM:
For the Stage14 fixed-E principal rectangular product envelope with finite integer intervals D and V, the ambient distinct-product set has full polynomial exponent up to polylogarithmic loss.
When both intervals are polynomial, the directly applicable asymmetric arithmetic-progression product-set theorem yields no fixed B-power compression.
If one side is B^o(1), freezing one nonzero element of that side already gives an injective copy of the larger interval, again preventing fixed-power compression.
Therefore the ambient multiplication image itself cannot supply the missing Stage14 power saving.
Any remaining saving must come from the conditional physical lift inside the product support, not from multiplication-table capacity.
