TYPE: EXACT_STRUCTURE

CLAIM:
With genuine Pythagorean base coordinates
P=L^2-M^2,
Q=2LM,
A=P-Q,
B=P+Q,
and c=C/D,
the transverse-fibration quotient gives the exact M3 equation
K^2=(C^2-D^2)(C^2A^2-D^2B^2).
The raw K_c sextuple is
a1=K,
a2=2CDP,
a3=2CDQ,
b1=2CD(L^2+M^2),
b2=D^2B-C^2A,
b3=D^2B+C^2A.
The space-diagonal numerator is the plus/plus twin
G^2R^2=(C^2+D^2)(C^2A^2+D^2B^2)
after primitive normalization G.


POPULATION:
exact K_c/M3 points in the transverse-fibration integral model.
