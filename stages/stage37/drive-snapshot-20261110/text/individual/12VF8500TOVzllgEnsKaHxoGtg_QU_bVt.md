CLAIM:
Stage24 studies the literal subset transition from the primitive canonical exactly-two-face population M_2(B) to the same physical population with integral space diagonal N_2(B), under the identical cutoff R<=B.
At Stage24 closeout,
M_2(B) ~ C_M2 B(log B)^5,
sqrt(log B) << N_2(B) <<_epsilon B^(1/2+epsilon),
and hence
B^(-1)(log B)^(-9/2) << N_2(B)/M_2(B)
<<_epsilon B^(-1/2+epsilon)(log B)^(-5).
Thus N_2(B)/M_2(B)->0 and N_2(B)->infinity: Stage24 classified the transition as THIN_BUT_INFINITE.
Later Stage25 strengthened the lower to N_2(B)>>B^(1/4), so the historical sqrt(log B) lower remains valid provenance but is not the current strongest lower.
