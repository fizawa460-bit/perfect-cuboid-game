## M3-HIST14-BINFORM-001
TYPE: EXACT_STRUCTURE
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
On the Stage14 primitive-slope receiver with gcd(a,b)=1, the two primitive arithmetic cores are
F_-=ab
and
F_+=a^2+b^2
up to already-frozen B^o(1) scale/endpoint/2-primary decorations.
They are coprime:
gcd(a^2+b^2,ab)=1.
Hence fixed-power cross-sign prime sharing is impossible and cross-sign disjointness is automatic rather than an independent density selector.
The minus allocation localizes canonically to divisors of the coprime coordinates a and b.
Every odd prime dividing the primitive plus core is 1 mod 4, and the slope a/b fixes the Gaussian conjugate above each such rational prime; the remaining plus allocation is only a subset/divisor allocation among these split primes.

Stage14 s7-65 through s7-67 primitive-slope physical packets.

IMPLIES:
cross-sign separation, plus-prime Gaussian splitting, and plus-prime conjugate orientation are structural consequences of the primitive coordinates and cannot be charged again as separate density savings.
