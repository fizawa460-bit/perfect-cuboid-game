## M3-HIST14-FOURFACTOR-001
TYPE: EXACT_STRUCTURE
STATUS: PROVED_EXACT


CLAIM:
On the Stage14 fixed-heavy-ray radial receiver, the fixed-kernel square-value condition is equivalent to one exact four-factor squareclass parity relation.
If the accepted radial support is polynomial, then at least one of the four physical factors must have polynomial value support.
That factor's mobility splits quantitatively into either polynomially many squarefree kernels or polynomially many square parts within one fixed kernel.
No per-prime Bernoulli model or generic square-density factor is justified because the four factor squareclasses are reconstructed from one correlated physical background.


POPULATION:
Stage14 s7-81 through s7-83 heavy primitive reciprocal-ray packets.


HEIGHT:
Stage14 physical d<=B.


DEPENDS_ON:
M3-HIST14-HEAVYRAY-001 and merged fixed-kernel square-value incidence.


IMPLIES:
polynomial radial mobility must appear at factor level, but only through a correlated kernel/square-part dichotomy.


ALIASES:
four-factor squareclass parity;
factor mobility split;
s7-81;
s7-82;
s7-83.


DUPLICATE_OF:
none.


SUPERSEDES:
generic random-square or independent factor-kernel models for the fixed-kernel heavy-ray packet.


DO_NOT_REPEAT:
Do not charge one half per prime from the valuation-parity relation.
Do not treat the four factor kernels as independent random squareclasses.
Do not infer a saving merely from selecting one polynomially mobile factor.


REOPEN_CONDITION:
a same-measure theorem controlling either the diffuse factor-kernel correlation or the fixed-kernel polynomial square-part incidence.
