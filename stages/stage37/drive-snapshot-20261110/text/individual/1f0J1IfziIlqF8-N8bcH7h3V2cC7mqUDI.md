CLAIM:
The unresolved Stage14 positive zero-mode uplift admits an exact bounded martingale/telescoping decomposition into O(1) physical-mask influences.
After the already-controlled orientation/gcd/common-prime components are removed, any square-root-saturating sequence must have exponent-zero response in at least one residual physical influence.
The four residual mask influences contract arithmetically to one coupled receiver:
disjoint-prime balanced allocation plus reciprocal completion.
Balanced allocation and reciprocal completion are not independent savings.

s7-59.
