A. Structural reduction — PASS


Theorems 2.1, 2.2 and 2.4 are mathematically coherent.


Theorem 2.1:
g_+ g_-=gcd(U1,U2), gcd(g_+,g_-)=1.
The odd-prime valuation allocation argument is valid.


Theorem 2.2:
f1=(W1W2-V1V2)(W1W2+V1V2)=L*R,
with
L=(am-bn)^2+(an-bm)^2,
R=(am+bn)^2+(an+bm)^2.
Independent symbolic expansion verifies these identities.


Theorem 2.4:
every primitive Euler brick with its odd edge designated arises from the two primitive Pythagorean source pairs with the stated gcd scaling. The proof via d=gcd(X,Y), e=gcd(X,Z), gcd(d,e)=1 is valid.


This independently corroborates Stage37 M3-MASTER-001 and is compatible with M3-ONE-002.


B. Section 3.1 resultant identity — FAIL AS WRITTEN, NONFATAL


The paper states
Res_s(P,Q)=256 U2^4 V2^4.


For the displayed P and Q, independent SymPy and Wolfram calculations instead give
Res_s(P,Q)=16 U2^8.


Numerical check at (m,n)=(2,1), hence (U2,V2,W2)=(3,4,5):
actual resultant=104976=16*3^8,
while the paper's displayed expression gives 5308416.


This is a real algebraic error in the manuscript, but it does not destroy the smoothness argument: the corrected resultant is still nonzero because U2 != 0. The displayed discriminants
disc(P)=16 U2^2(U2^2-V2^2),
disc(Q)=-16 U2^2 V2^2
are correct, and P(0),Q(0) are nonzero. The genus-3 conclusion survives.


C. Genus-3 quotient formulas and torsion-intersection theorem — PASS


The three quotient equations were independently rederived from the displayed H_{m,n}; the V4 decomposition is standard and the quotient formulas are consistent.


Lemma 4.1's eight degenerate rational points are correct. There is a minor wording slip referring to “four affine points”; the displayed set actually has six finite affine points plus two points at infinity.


Lemma 4.2 and Theorem 4.5 pass:
- E_3 quotient has no rational ramification fixed point;
- E_uV has four rational ramification images;
- rank zero plus the stated torsion/lift cardinalities forces at most eight H-points;
- the eight degenerate points force equality.


This generic theorem is strong enough to import into Stage37 independent of the 1,072-row computation.


D. Section 4.4 rank-zero theorem — PASS IF L_ratio IS ACTUALLY USED


The main text says ambiguous ellrank cases are resolved by Sage
E.lseries().L_ratio()
after checking semistability.


Sage documentation states L_ratio returns exact rational L(E,1)/Omega and is provably correct under the Manin-constant condition; semistable curves satisfy the required condition. Česnavičius proves the Manin conjecture in the semistable case. Nonvanishing L(E,1), together with modularity and Kolyvagin, gives algebraic rank zero.


Thus the mathematical certification route described in section 4.4 is valid.


The attribution in the paper is loose: the clean modern theorem c_E=1 for semistable curves is Česnavičius, not simply the Edixhoven statement as phrased. This is bibliographic rather than fatal.


E. Appendix B computational pipeline — FAIL/HOLD


Appendix B states that ambiguous [0,k] cases call Sage
analytic_rank_upper_bound
and calls its output a rigorous unconditional upper bound.


Current Sage documentation explicitly states that analytic_rank_upper_bound is conditional on the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis.


Therefore Appendix B does not, as written, support the manuscript's unconditional claim for the modular-symbol/Kolyvagin rows.


The paper counts:
E_3: 359 sharp ellrank + 468 modular-symbol rows;
E_uV refined: 209 sharp ellrank + 36 modular-symbol rows.


Hence 504 of the claimed 1,072 rows depend on the disputed second certification strand. The remaining 568 rows are unaffected by this particular rank-certificate problem, but this audit did not replay their torsion/lift computations row-by-row, so no separate 568-fiber theorem is promoted here.


Resolution condition:
inspect or rerun the actual current worker and verify that every one of the 504 ambiguous rows uses exact L_ratio (or another unconditional nonvanishing certificate), with semistability checked, rather than analytic_rank_upper_bound. If so, this blocker can be cleared.

G. Stage37 connection — PASS


For exact M3 objects the H_{m,n} condition is not merely a broad relaxation:
P_p(t^2)=M/b^4 is already square on M3, so
v^2=P_p(t^2)Q_p(t^2)
is equivalent to Q_p(t^2)=f1/b^4 being square.
Thus Theorem 4.5 is an exact fixed-source endpoint killer on Stage37's population.


Applying the same theorem after swapping the two Euclid sources shows that every perfect endpoint must have BOTH primitive source pairs in the torsion-intersection-hard set. This yields the exact two-source receiver recorded as M3-PESCH-TI-001 and the open weighted-hard-locus gate M3-PESCH-TI-GATE-001.
