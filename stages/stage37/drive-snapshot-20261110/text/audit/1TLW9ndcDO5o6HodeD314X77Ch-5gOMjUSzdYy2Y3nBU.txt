5. M3-T11-105 residual quartic normalization and Galois group — PASS

Direct substitution gives exactly

=-2^4*u^2*A^2*(25*u^2-2)^3

The leading coefficients do not vanish at u=1, so reducibility over Q(u) would specialize to reducibility at u=1. Therefore both the quartic and resolvent are irreducible over Q(u).

This is generic only; no claim is made for every rational specialization u.
