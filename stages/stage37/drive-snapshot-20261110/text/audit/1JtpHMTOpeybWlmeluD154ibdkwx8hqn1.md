## Three distinct populations, kept separate

The scalar set consists of products pq with distinct split primes in [T,2T], outside152's S_B, with B=4T^2. Its cardinality is asymptotic to a positive constant times B/(log B)^2. This is NOT a lower bound for physical endpoints.

The Gaussian norm-relation graph retains existence of nonzero cofactor C with p^2|Norm(C). It is a necessary relaxation. Its two directions can use different witnesses; neither mutual acceptance nor finite graph membership is a physical cuboid assertion.

The physical population retains the original canonical masks, positive primitive edges, face diagonals, same-witness reconstruction, sphere identity and exact R cutoff.153's reconstruction bounds this population from the necessary witnesses. No reverse implication is used.

## Certificate-cost theorem

For any alphabet S, k=|S intersect V_T|. Every covered semiprime pq contributes a positive term of at least1 to W_S(B,sigma), for EVERY sigma>0. Therefore inf_sigma W_S>=binom(k,2). The other cap C0*K^|S| also exceeds binom(k,2), since C0>=1 and K>=2. Taking their minimum is safe in the lower-bound direction. Covering every pair forces the sum of pair incidences to be at leastbinom(v,2), even with overlaps. This proves a lower bound on the NUMERIC UPPER CERTIFICATE, and hence its inability to certify the sufficient o(B^(1/3)) scale. It says nothing about a hypothetical improved physical cap or a cover of a separately proved sparse physical subset.

The polylog-alphabet family-size obstruction is secondary; the main theorem permits arbitrary alphabet sizes. This avoids claiming that a sufficient exponent budget is a necessary condition for every possible method.

## Geometric theorem applicability and height check

The cuboid equations are four monic square-root extensions of the edge-coordinate algebra. Over an algebraically closed field, each radicand has an odd branch component absent from the others; their squareclasses are independent. The generic extension is a field of degree16. Freeness of the monic presentation makes the coordinate algebra torsion-free over the base and injective into that generic field, proving geometric integrality. The resulting projective surface has codimension4 and degree2^4=16.

This is enough for Salberger's general Theorem0.9(b); smoothness or an embedding in P^3 is not needed. The theorem's degree-d curves/exceptions budgets were checked directly in the primary paper. Theorem0.5 for nonsingular surfaces in P^3 was not substituted. On every original positive primitive endpoint H=R exactly. Thus the supplied budgets are B^(3/8)log B and B^(3/4+o(1)), both above the benchmark. This is failure of the supplied bounds, not proof that no sharper cover exists.

Under a k-th Veronese embedding the surface degree is multiplied by k^2 and the exact height is raised to k. Primitive monomial coordinates have gcd1: a prime dividing all pure k-th powers would divide every original coordinate. The maximum monomial is R^k. Both B exponents therefore stay3/8 and3/4, respectively. Ignoring this height change would create spurious progress.
