2. Exact Pythagorean cover identification


Define
    phi(t)=2t/(1-t^2).


For w in Q, the equation w=phi(t) is
    w t^2 + 2t - w = 0.
Its discriminant is
    4(1+w^2).


Therefore this quadratic fibre is reducible over Q if and only if 1+w^2 is a rational square. Hence
    T_Pyth = Red(phi)
for the degree-two cover phi:P^1 -> P^1, with the usual projective interpretation at t=infinity.


This identifies the PAIR thin set with a literal reducible-fibre set, not only an abstract type-II thin image.


RESULT_229H_PYTH_IS_REDUCIBLE_FIBRE_SET=PROVED.


3. Stoll–Siksek G_m Hilbert irreducibility: fixed-support sector


New source not previously registered in Stage37:
Michael Stoll and Samir Siksek, “Hilbert's Irreducibility for G_m”, arXiv:2609.04551.


Their Theorem 3 describes, for an irreducible cover pi and fixed finite S,
    O_S^* intersect Red(pi)
as a finite set together with finitely many power cosets.


More strongly, their Theorem 5 says that for an absolutely irreducible cover and a finitely generated infinite subgroup Theta of K^*, the intersection
    Theta intersect Red(pi)
is infinite only if the covering function is, up to a constant, an ell-th power for a prime ell, or the exceptional -4 times a fourth power.


For
    phi(t)=2t/(1-t^2),
the divisor has simple zeros and simple poles. In particular its divisor multiplicities are not all divisible by any ell>=2, so phi cannot have either exceptional power form.


Therefore for every fixed finite set S of rational primes,
    T_Pyth intersect O_S^*
is finite.


Equivalently, any PAIR sector whose reduced w=D/X has numerator and denominator supported on one fixed finite prime set S contains only finitely many Pythagorean w-values.


RESULT_229H_FIXED_S_UNIT_PYTH_INTERSECTION_FINITE=PROVED.


Scope firewall:
- the finite set and its size depend on S;
- Stage37 does not have one fixed S for all physical M3 points;
- the prime support of w is allowed to grow with B;
- no uniform bound in S or support complexity is supplied by this theorem;
- n_B(w) is still the exact occupied M3 weight.


Hence this is a genuine scoped structural theorem but not a B-uniform global PAIR count.


PAIR_FIXED_S_SECTOR=STRUCTURALLY_FINITE_BUT_NO_UNIFORM_GLOBAL_CREDIT.


4. Anders Mah quantitative rational-height Hilbert irreducibility


New source not previously registered in Stage37:
Anders Mah, “Counting rational points on affine hypersurfaces”, arXiv:2512.03490.


Mah Theorem 1.4 gives, for one rational parameter t of height <=H, an upper bound
    S(f,H) << H (log ||f|| + log H)^5
for reducible specialisations of a fixed irreducible polynomial.


Apply it to
    F(W,T)=W T^2 + 2T - W.
This polynomial is irreducible over Q(W), since 1+W^2 is not a square in Q(W). For every nonzero rational w, reducibility after W=w is exactly w in T_Pyth. The value w=0 is the single affine degree-drop exception for F, while projectively it is still a reducible fibre of phi; this O(1) exception does not affect the height bound.


Thus
    #{w in T_Pyth : H(w)<=H}
      << H (log H)^5.


Since all rational points of P^1 of height <=H have order H^2, this is a genuine ambient fixed-power rational-height deficit:
    ambient relative density << H^(-1) (log H)^5.


RESULT_229H_AMBIENT_PYTH_HEIGHT_COUNT=PROVED_USING_MAH.


This is stronger in B-power type than the qualitative Hilbert/value-set inputs audited in 229G, but it is still an unweighted target-value count.


Exact transfer firewall:
    sum_{w in T_Pyth} n_B(w)
cannot be bounded by the distinct-value count without a bound on the concentration of n_B(w).


Even a strong ambient count of distinct w does not exclude the exact M3 pushforward from placing most of its mass on those w-values. This is precisely the 229G qualitative-versus-weighted firewall, now tested against a quantitative ambient theorem.

6. Consequence for the missing theorem species


229G named the missing theorem M3_WEIGHTED_HILBERT_PROPERTY. The present audit sharpens where its missing uniformity lies.


Two facts are now available:
(A) fixed finite prime support in w: the Pythagorean thin intersection is finite;
(B) unrestricted ambient rational height: the Pythagorean thin set has a fixed-power deficit among all rational target values.


What remains uncontrolled is the simultaneous movement of
    - prime support of w with B, and
    - the exact occupied weight n_B(w).


Thus a direct PAIR theorem may be sought in any equivalent form that gives one of:


1. growing-support uniform Hilbert irreducibility:
   a quantitative bound uniform in the support set S or a suitable support-complexity parameter, strong enough after summing over physical sectors;


2. exact M3 pushforward anti-concentration:
   a theorem bounding concentration of n_B(w) on ambient thin sets, so that Mah-type target-value counts become usable;


3. source-side exact-height counting:
   a fixed-power count directly on the physical M3 source whose pullback condition is w in T_Pyth, preserving primitive/canonical multiplicity and exact R<=B.


This is a refinement of the existing reopen contract, not a replacement:
    PAIR_REOPEN_CONTRACT=M3_WEIGHTED_HILBERT_PROPERTY
remains canonical.
