3. Audit B — current number-theory literature


3.1 GCD-height theorems
Yu Yasufuku, “GCD inequalities arising from codimension-2 blowups”, BLMS 58 (2026), e70349, DOI 10.1112/blms.70349:
unconditionally proves a weaker Silverman/Vojta-type GCD-height inequality using Ru–Vojta/Subspace-theorem machinery.
229O already performed the exact adapter/budget audit: the direct Silverman-style coordinate-transversality hypothesis fails for F1=eR,F2=xy on the physical projective model, and even the counterfactual coefficient budget is too weak for the one-third gate. No reopen.


Benjamín Barrios, “An upper bound for the generalised greatest common divisor of rational points” (2025), with correction published 22 July 2026, DOI 10.1017/S0004972726101543:
the correction states that the subvariety must be positively embedded, equivalently with ample normal bundle, for the original height conclusion.229O's corrected componentwise audit remains quantitatively insufficient for the one-third target. No reopen.


Wang–Yasufuku, “Greatest common divisors of integral points of numerically equivalent divisors”, Algebra & Number Theory 15 (2021):
controls codimension>=2 subscheme heights for points integral with respect to a suitable divisor. The Stage37 population is not supplied with the required integrality contract, and the theorem is a height inequality rather than the needed absolute bounded-height count. WRONG_POPULATION / MISSING_ADAPTER.


3.2 Squarefree/power-free binary-form literature
Bhargava–Shankar–Wang, “Squarefree values of polynomial discriminants II”, Forum Math. Pi 13 (2025) e17, and Stanley Yao Xiao, “Power-Free Values of Binary Forms and the Global Determinant Method”, IMRN 2017:
these are strong quantitative theorems for avoiding p^k divisibility / obtaining power-free values. Their principal tail is LARGE SQUARE FACTOR / NON-POWER-FREE behavior.
Stage37 asks for LARGE SQUAREFREE KERNEL of a COMMON GCD. This is directionally different.229BE gives an explicit warning: after dropping the extra selectors, a large squarefree radical is abundant, not exceptional. Therefore these theorems do not black-box into (229BH.1).


3.3 Geometric/rational-point sieves
Browning–Loughran, “Sieving rational points on varieties”, Trans. AMS 371 (2019):
gives upper-bound sieves for rational points on suitable varieties, with applications to thin sets, local solubility, and friable points; quadrics receive sharper Selberg-sieve estimates.
The issue is not availability of a generic sieve formalism. The Stage37 missing input is a same-physical selector-conditioned distribution theorem with enough growing-modulus uniformity and the correct absolute B-exponent.


Zhizhong Huang, “Equidistribution of rational points and the geometric sieve for toric varieties”, arXiv:2111.01509:
proves effective adelic equidistribution and an Ekedahl-type geometric sieve on smooth projective SPLIT toric varieties over Q.
This is the closest theorem species to the229AH codimension-2 radical center, but it does not directly apply to the fixed canonical perfect-cuboid surface X_pc, which229BG identifies as a general-type complete intersection, not a smooth split toric variety. Applying it on the toric base X0 would drop the selector cover;229BE and229AH forbid that as a route to the desired tail.


Tim Santens, “Manin's conjecture for integral points on toric varieties”, arXiv:2312.13914 / PLMS 2026:
handles toric varieties, including nonsplit situations in the retained Stage37 audit, using universal torsors/harmonic analysis. It supplies base/integral-point counting, not the required selector-conditioned growing squarefree-modulus radical sieve on X_pc.


3.4 Thin-set counting
Buggenhout–Cluckers–Santens–Vermeulen, “Serre's question on thin sets in projective space”, arXiv:2506.13471 (2025):
for a quasi-finite dominant cover f:X->P^n of degree d, gives
  B^n log(B)^O(1) for d=4,
and B^n for d>=5.
This is a major degree-4 thin-set theorem, but it is a theorem over projective space and controls the cover image as a whole. It does not carry the multiplicative radical event k|gcd(eR,xy), and its scale is nowhere near an absolute o(B^(1/3)) target in the present surface problem. It does not replace a selector-conditioned radical sieve.


3.5 Current squareclass theorem from229BG
Asiryan 2026, already audited in229BG, controls the complementary squareclass

The common G_* occurs squared and cancels. Its weighted zero-density theorem is therefore structurally complementary to the current large-common-radical gate. The direct R-height transfer is quantitatively far too weak, as certified in229BG.

does not directly control the radical tail.
The needed input is uniform simultaneous local-condition control for SQUAREFREE MODULI.


Define for squarefree odd k

         R(P)<=B, k|G_*(P)}.                           (229BH.9)
A useful theorem must control N_phys(B;k) uniformly over a growing k-range, on the exact physical selector measure, strongly enough that a divisor/large-sieve treatment of

5. Audit D — one-step conversions to known counting problems


5.1 Strongest existing one-step conversion:229AR
The exact macro-Pythagorean map
  P |-> (eR,xy,UV)
turns G_* into the exact content of one integral Pythagorean triple, and W_-=UV-eR turns its squarefree kernel into the squarefree kernel of one scalar up to the controlled 2-bit:
  W_-=2^epsilon G_* r^2.                            (229BH.11)
Therefore the large-K gate can be restated without threshold loss as:
  count exact X_pc physical points of height R<=B
  for which the regular physical scalar W_-=UV-eR=O(B^2)
  has sf_odd(W_-)>~B^(1/3-delta).                  (229BH.12)
This is currently the best one-shot reduction. It is a squarefree-kernel-of-a-function-on-a-fixed-surface problem, not an arbitrary integer squarefree-value problem because W_- is forced to be content times a square on X_pc.


5.2 Fixed-outer known problem:229BF
Fixing the outer primitive Pythagorean triple transforms the same tail into primitive lattice points in one index-tD congruence lattice with D>T, giving the exact 1/T bound (229BH.3). This is already a geometry-of-numbers counting problem; the unresolved difficulty is summing the occupied outer fibers / one-per-outer floor.


5.3 Fixed-support known problem:229AX
The endpoint population pushes with B^o fibers to a rank-one arithmetic support Omega_B. A theorem counting occupied Omega_B support would immediately transfer. This is more precise than old PAIR scalar support, but no standard current theorem was found for this exact rank-one atom/root support with moving squareclasses.


5.4 Canonical-surface known problem after229BG
The global problem is now one fixed canonical surface, not a genuinely moving genus-five family:
  rational/integral points of exact projective height R
  on X_pc
  satisfying a multiplicative codimension-two radical condition.
This is a recognizable counting-problem species:

  MULTIPLICATIVE CODIMENSION-TWO SIEVE INSIDE A DEGREE-FOUR THIN COVER.
