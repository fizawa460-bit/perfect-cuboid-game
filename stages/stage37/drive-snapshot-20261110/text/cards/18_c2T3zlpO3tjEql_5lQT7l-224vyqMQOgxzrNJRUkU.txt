2. PROVED WEIGHTED GENERALIZED DIVISOR INEQUALITY
Let Ω(n)=sum_p v_p(n), counted WITH multiplicity; d_alpha(n) defined for alpha>0 by ζ(s)^alpha=sum_{n>=1}d_alpha(n)n^(-s) for Re(s)>1, i.e. d_alpha(p^j)=prod_{i=0}^{j-1}(alpha+i)/(i+1). For 0<t<=1 put beta=8t. At each prime power p^j:
   d_8(p^j)t^j=prod_{i=0}^{j-1}[t(8+i)/(i+1)] <=prod_{i=0}^{j-1}[(8t+i)/(i+1)]=d_beta(p^j)
because t(8+i)<=8t+i for i>=0. Therefore, by multiplicativity,
   d_8(n)t^{Ω(n)} <=d_beta(n)  for ALL n>=1.                        (ER.1)
For Ω(n)<=K and 0<t<1, t^{Ω(n)}>=t^K; hence
   d_8(n) <=t^(-K)d_beta(n).                                      (ER.2)
This is coefficientwise, needs no assertion that q- or Gres-local conditions are independent.

in particular <=C_beta X(log X)^(beta-1). This is a published analytic-number-theory input, not a new Stage37 theorem and does not assume GRH or rank-genus9.
