2. Parity and three exact pair-sum norm receivers

Define the three positive pair-sum products
H_A=(A+Bv)(A+C),
H_B=(A+Bv)(Bv+C),

3. Uniform fixed-value physical fiber bound
Fix n=H_A. The representation n=A^2+2d^2 is a norm equation in Z[sqrt(-2)]. This ring is norm-Euclidean: after dividing in Q(sqrt(-2)), rounding both coefficients leaves norm at most 1/4+2/4=3/4<1. Hence it is a UFD.
For n=product p^{v_p(n)}, the number of element divisors of n in Z[sqrt(-2)] is at most a constant times product_p(v_p(n)+1)^2 <= O(tau(n)^2). Thus the number of positive integer representations n=A^2+2d^2 is O(tau(n)^2).
For each such (A,d), write s=A+Bv and t=A+C. Then st=n. There are at most tau(n) ordered positive factor choices for s, with t=n/s, and Bv=s-A, C=t-A are forced. Positivity and the physical chamber only delete candidates. Finally D=2d and 229BU reconstruction gives at most one labelled physical endpoint from (A,Bv,C,D).
Therefore
# {physical P:R<=B, H_A(P)=n} << tau(n)^3 = B^o(1),             (BY.4)
uniformly for n<=B^2. The same argument applies cyclically to H_B and H_C.
