The Eisenstein local support rule (all p==2 mod3 inert primes have even n-valuation) and the q=gcd(R,S)=gcd(R,d) square-divisor / p==1 mod12 facts do not force q large: q can equal 1. Neither fact currently estimates the number of n>B^(1/3-epsilon) realized by hard13 physical endpoints.


To expose the ambient-model gap, consider the positive OUTER-CONE family
A=Bv=2k, C=k, d=2k, S=5k, R=9k (k>=1).
It satisfies ABv+AC+BvC=8k^2=2d^2 and N_an=S^2-6d^2=k^2. There is one such represented scalar for every k. The derived formal diagonals U=V=7k,z=8k give e^2=R^2-z^2=17k^2, not a physical square for nonzero k. This family is not primitive nor is it an Euler/perfect cuboid source; it shows that the CONE and dual norm identity ALONE do not imply enough occupied-N sparsity. Any global theorem must use the omitted physical selectors. Do not promote this cone family to a counterexample to a theorem on exact hard13.
