5. Euclid-parameter meaning of the four packets


The space triangle (U,y,R) has content c12. Write
  U=c12 U0, y=c12 y0, R=c12 R0
with (U0,y0,R0) primitive.


If U0 is even, write
  U0=2mn, y0=m^2-n^2, R0=m^2+n^2.
Then
  R-y = 2 c12 n^2,
  R+y = 2 c12 m^2.                               (229AJ.6)

Likewise, for the primitive quotient of (V,x,R) by its content c21, the a_- and a_+ packets are carried by the two Euclid square-root components of R-x and R+x.

Therefore, defining the exact same-physical packet counts
  N_{a,-}(B;M)=#{positive primitive physical endpoints R<=B : A_->M},
  N_{a,+}(B;M)=#{positive primitive physical endpoints R<=B : A_+>M},
  N_{b,-}(B;M)=#{positive primitive physical endpoints R<=B : B_->M},

7. Generic linear-gap square-divisor sieve is not an independent saving


Equations (229AJ.6)-(229AJ.7), and their x-analogues, show that on every physical endpoint each of
  R-y, R+y, R-x, R+x
is already equal to its Pythagorean content times a square, or twice its content times a square.


Therefore the mere event
  “there exists a large squarefree m with m^2 dividing one of R±x,R±y”

Consequently one may not convert (229AJ.11) into a power saving by applying a generic large-square-divisor or codimension argument to the four gap values alone. A valid quantitative theorem must retain the realized physical packet identity: the relevant inert square-root factor must be the a- or b-content packet coupled simultaneously to the nested face/space Pythagorean structure and exact R-height.


This is the same-measure anti-loop point missing from a naive smooth-radical treatment.
