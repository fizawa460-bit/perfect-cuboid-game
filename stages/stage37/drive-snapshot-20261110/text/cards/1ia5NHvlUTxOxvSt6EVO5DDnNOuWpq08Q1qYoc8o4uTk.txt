4. Why CROSS-GCD is primary rather than merely another residual route


It has four advantages simultaneously.


First, exact measure match:
M3 endpoints inject into the Stage14 raw-edge population, and c_raw is defined on that whole physical population before the third-face endpoint condition.


Second, an exact coordinate adapter already exists:
the former “identify c_raw inside Stage14 coordinates” blocker was removed by M3-XG-008. The remaining variable is H3/gcd(H2,H3), not an unnamed auxiliary gcd.


Third, the target is quantitatively explicit:
for C=B^(1/6+eta), a tail at approximately
    E_raw,>C(B) <= B^(1/2+o(1)) C^(-1+o(1))
would put the large-c endpoint branch below B^(1/3). A weaker theorem may also suffice if combined with the already-paid complementary sectors, but the budget must be written before counting.


Fourth, unlike 219–223, this route has not yet been exhausted by a same-measure no-go. What was rejected is:
- multiplying an ambient c^(-2) density by the global square-root theorem;
- a uniform all-C 1/C tail;
- uncapped low-order moments.
A direct threshold-specific stratified Stage14 count remains genuinely open.
