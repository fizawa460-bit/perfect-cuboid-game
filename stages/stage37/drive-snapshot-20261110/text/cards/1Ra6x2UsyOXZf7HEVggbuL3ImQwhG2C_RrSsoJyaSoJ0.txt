2. The missing-face common content is coprime to both e and R


Let
  d0 = gcd(x,y).
The retained primitive physical condition is
  gcd(e,x,y)=1.


Since d0 divides x and y,
  gcd(e,d0)=1.                                      (229O.1)


Now suppose a prime p divides both R and d0. Then p divides R,x,y. The physical sphere equation
  R^2 = e^2 + x^2 + y^2
implies
  e^2 == 0 mod p,
hence p divides e. This contradicts gcd(e,x,y)=1.


Therefore
  gcd(R,d0)=1.                                      (229O.2)


Combining (229O.1) and (229O.2),
  gcd(eR,d0)=1.                                     (229O.3)

5. Yasufuku 2026 codimension-2 GCD inequality: direct route is closed


Yu Yasufuku, “GCD inequalities arising from codimension-2 blowups,” Bulletin of the London Mathematical Society 58(4), 2026, DOI 10.1112/blms.70349, gives an unconditional codimension-2 GCD inequality for two homogeneous forms.


For the 229O ambient adapter:
  n=3,
  d1=d2=2,
  F1=eR,
  F2=xy.


The center
  Y={eR=xy=0}
is a codimension-2 complete intersection because F1 and F2 have no common polynomial factor. However Yasufuku Theorem 1.2 also requires every coordinate point of P^3 to lie outside Y. Here every coordinate point has eR=0 and xy=0, hence every coordinate point lies in Y.


Therefore the theorem is not directly applicable to (F1,F2).


Moreover, even if this coordinate-point hypothesis were ignored, the equal-degree coefficient allowed by Yasufuku is any number larger than
  [-(n+1)-(n-1)d
   +sqrt((n+1)^2 d^2+2(n^2-1)d+(n+1)^2)]/2.
At n=3,d=2 this equals
  (-8+sqrt(112))/2
  = 2 sqrt(7)-4
  = 1.2915... .


Stage37 requires control at the B^(1/3-delta) radical scale. A pointwise logarithmic GCD coefficient already exceeding 1.29, before Yasufuku's additional nonnegative coordinate-boundary term, cannot provide that receiver.

6. Barrios 2026 corrected generalized-GCD theorem: componentwise route is also too weak


Benjamín Barrios, “An upper bound for the generalized greatest common divisor of rational points,” together with the published 2026 correction, gives a generalized GCD height bound outside a proper Zariski-closed exceptional set. The correction states that the height-bound results remain valid after adding the requirement that the relevant subvariety have ample normal bundle.


The reducible center Y={eR=xy=0} is the union of four coordinate lines

  N_{P^1/P^3}=O(1) direct-sum O(1).

For X=P^3, A=O(1), n=3, a line has d=1 and codimension c=2, while
