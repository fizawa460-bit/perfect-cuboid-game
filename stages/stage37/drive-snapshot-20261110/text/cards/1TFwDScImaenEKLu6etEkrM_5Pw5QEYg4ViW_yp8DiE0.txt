1. Scope


229M proved for every positive physical endpoint
  d = sf(2^epsilon A_edge C_raw),
where
  A_edge = a b = gcd(e,xy),
  C_raw  = c12 c21,
and a,b,c12,c21 are four pairwise-coprime physical Pythagorean contents.


229M then used raw thresholds on A_edge and C_raw. That is sufficient but not optimal, because the 229L fixed-d theorem only sees the squarefree twist class. Large square parts of A_edge or C_raw do not create new d.


This card collapses the two raw content variables to one exact physical gcd and replaces the two-tail target by one squarefree-kernel tail.


2. Normalize the missing-face triple


Retain 229D notation:
  d0 = gcd(x,y),
  x = d0 b c,
  y = d0 a f,
with
  gcd(bc,af)=1.
Define
  x' = x/d0 = b c,
  y' = y/d0 = a f.
Then
  gcd(x',y')=1
and
  x'^2+y'^2=q^2
is the primitive missing-face Pythagorean triple.

with
  gcd(g,c)=gcd(g,f)=1.


3. A_edge is exactly gcd(e,x'y')


Since
  x'y' = a b c f,
we have
  gcd(e,x'y')
    = gcd(gab,abcf)
    = ab gcd(g,cf)
    = ab.
Therefore
  A_edge = gcd(e,x'y').                          (229N.1)

4. The two space contents are exactly gcd(R,f) and gcd(R,c)


Recall
  c12 = gcd(U,y),
  c21 = gcd(V,x),
with
  c12|f,
  c21|c.


First prove
  gcd(R,f)=c12.
The inclusion c12|R follows because c12 divides both U and y and
  R^2=U^2+y^2.
Also c12|f by 229M.


Conversely let r divide both R and f. Since f|y, r|y. Then
  U^2=R^2-y^2
is divisible by r^2, so r|U. Thus
  r|gcd(U,y)=c12.
Hence gcd(R,f)=c12.


Identically,
  gcd(R,c)=c21.                                  (229N.2)


Since gcd(c,f)=1,
  gcd(R,cf)
   = gcd(R,c) gcd(R,f)
   = c21 c12
   = C_raw.                                      (229N.3)


RESULT_229N_CRAW_EQUALS_GCD_R_CF=PROVED_EXACT.


5. C_raw is exactly gcd(R,x'y')


We need to remove the factors a,b from x'y'=abcf.


If a prime divides R and a, then it divides R,e,y because a|e and a|y. The sphere equation
  R^2=e^2+x^2+y^2
then forces that prime to divide x, contradicting the primitive physical condition
  gcd(e,x,y)=1.
Therefore
  gcd(R,a)=1.
Similarly
  gcd(R,b)=1.


Hence
  gcd(R,x'y')
   = gcd(R,abcf)
   = gcd(R,cf)
   = C_raw.                                      (229N.4)


RESULT_229N_CRAW_SINGLE_GCD=PROVED_EXACT.


6. Exact single-gcd collapse


Define the physical joint gcd
  G_* := gcd(eR,x'y').                           (229N.5)


No prime dividing x'y' can divide both e and R: if p|e,R and p divides x' or y', then p divides one of x,y, and the sphere equation forces p to divide the other edge as well, contradicting gcd(e,x,y)=1.


Therefore the e-side and R-side gcds with x'y' are coprime, and
  G_*
   = gcd(e,x'y') gcd(R,x'y')
   = A_edge C_raw.                               (229N.6)


Combining with 229M,
  d = sf(2^epsilon G_*).                         (229N.7)


Equivalently,
  G_* = gcd(eR, (xy)/gcd(x,y)^2).                (229N.8)


This is the exact whole-endpoint twist-support receiver.
