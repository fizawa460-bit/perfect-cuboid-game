229Q proved that for the 229L congruent-number twist
  E_d: Y^2=X^3-d^2 X
over K=Q(i), the CM trace/anti-trace construction sends a non-torsion P in E_d(K) to at least one non-torsion point of E_d(Q). It also proved that for positive squarefree d>1 the physical 229L point P is non-torsion.

2. Setup and exact CM pair


Let sigma be complex conjugation and
  iota(X,Y)=(-X,iY)
the j=1728 CM automorphism of E_d over K.


For the physical P in E_d(K), write
  X(P)/d = p^2 =: z,
  X(sigma P)/d = q^2 =: w,
where q=conj(p), and p has nonzero positive real and imaginary parts on the positive physical chamber.


Define
  A=P+sigma(P),
  C=iota(P-sigma(P)).
As in 229Q,
  A,C in E_d(Q),
and
  2P=A-iota(C).                                  (229R.1)


Retain the 229Q torsion firewall
  E_d(Q)_tors=E_d[2]
for positive squarefree d>1.


3. Translation by rational 2-torsion gives four Möbius transforms


Let
  T0=(0,0), T+=(d,0), T-=(-d,0).


For any non-2-torsion point with normalized x-coordinate z=X/d, translation by these points has normalized x-coordinate


  x(P+T0)/d = -1/z,
  x(P+T+)/d = (z+1)/(z-1),
  x(P+T-)/d = (1-z)/(1+z).                     (229R.2)


The point at infinity gives the identity transform z.


Because x(-P+T)=x(P+T), the same four transforms occur if
  sigma(P)=T-P
or
  sigma(P)=P+T.


RESULT_229R_2TORSION_MOBIUS_PACKET=PROVED_EXACT.


4. The trace A cannot be torsion


Assume A is torsion. By the retained rational torsion statement, A is one of
  O,T0,T+,T-.
Since A=P+sigma(P), this gives sigma(P)=A-P. Hence w must be one of the four transforms in Section 3.


Case A=O:
  w=z.
Then z is real. But z=p^2 and p has nonzero real and imaginary parts, so p^2 is non-real. Contradiction.


Case A=T0:
  w=-1/z.
Then
  zw=-1.
But zw=p^2 q^2=N(p)^2 is a positive rational square. Contradiction.


Case A=T+:
  w=(z+1)/(z-1).
Then
  N(p^2-1)
   =(z-1)(w-1)
   =2.
But the physical selector requires N(p^2-1) in Q^{*2}, and 2 is not a rational square. Contradiction.


Case A=T-:
  w=(1-z)/(1+z).
Then
  N(p^2+1)
   =(z+1)(w+1)
   =2,
again contradicting the selector square condition.


Therefore
  A is non-torsion.                                (229R.3)


RESULT_229R_TRACE_NONTORSION=PROVED_EXACT_ON_PHYSICAL_d_GT_1.


5. The anti-trace C cannot be torsion


Assume C is torsion. Since iota permutes E_d[2],
  P-sigma(P)=iota^{-1}(C)
is a rational 2-torsion point T.
Hence
  sigma(P)=P-T=P+T.
Taking x-coordinates gives exactly the same four Möbius transforms of Section 3.


The four contradictions from Section 4 therefore apply verbatim:
- identity transform makes p^2 real;
- T0 gives N(p)^2=-1;
- T+ forces N(p^2-1)=2;
- T- forces N(p^2+1)=2.


Therefore
  C is non-torsion.                                (229R.4)


RESULT_229R_ANTITRACE_NONTORSION=PROVED_EXACT_ON_PHYSICAL_d_GT_1.
RESULT_229R_BOTH_Q_RATIONAL_COMPONENTS_NONTORSION=PROVED_EXACT.


This is strictly stronger than the 229Q statement “at least one is non-torsion.” It is a refinement, not a retraction.


6. Exact canonical-height sum and improved one-point height transfer


The CM automorphism iota has degree 1 and preserves canonical height. Also sigma preserves canonical height. Therefore


  hhat(C)=hhat(P-sigma(P)).


By the parallelogram identity,


  hhat(A)+hhat(C)
   =hhat(P+sigma(P))+hhat(P-sigma(P))
   =2hhat(P)+2hhat(sigma(P))
   =4hhat(P).                                     (229R.5)


Since both A and C are non-torsion, both heights are strictly positive. In particular,


  min(hhat(A),hhat(C)) <= 2 hhat(P).              (229R.6)


Thus the rational small-point transfer in 229Q improves from the safe factor 4 to factor 2 for the physical d>1 population.


RESULT_229R_CM_HEIGHT_SUM=PROVED_EXACT.
RESULT_229R_RATIONAL_SMALL_POINT_FACTOR=2_INSTEAD_OF_4.


This changes no Stage37 exponent by itself because 229L only supplies hhat(P)=O(log B), and 229Q already showed that the retained d<=2B^2 envelope is far outside the Le Boudec one-third budget even in the optimistic alpha->0 limit.


7. Important non-claim: no rank-two conclusion


A and C are both non-torsion, but this does NOT prove that they are independent in E_d(Q) modulo torsion.


They may lie in the same rank-one Mordell-Weil line. Therefore this card does not route physical twists into the rank>=2 twist subfamily and does not invoke any rank-two density theorem.


Define the remaining CM-pair structural question:


  CM_PAIR_DEPENDENCE_GATE:
  control the physical endpoints for which A and C are dependent modulo E_d(Q)_tors.


A proof that dependence is impossible or sufficiently sparse would be genuinely new input. No such statement is proved here.


RESULT_229R_CM_PAIR_INDEPENDENCE=OPEN.


8. Positive-rank support alone cannot be the missing theorem


Alexander Smith proved that congruent numbers have positive natural density; in particular, a positive proportion of squarefree d give E_d(Q) a non-torsion rational point.


Therefore an unconditioned theorem that merely counts squarefree twists d<=X with E_d(Q) non-torsion cannot have o(X) support. At the Stage37 ambient envelope X~B^2 this is vastly too large.


Consequently the CM rationalization must retain genuinely restrictive information:
- the inherited small-height relation,
- the physical selector/descent origin,
- the radical-gcd realization,
- or an equally strong same-physical condition.


Discarding these and counting all positive-rank congruent twists is a mathematically invalid route to the Stage37 B^(1/3) target.


RESULT_229R_POSITIVE_RANK_SUPPORT_FIREWALL=PROVED_BY_EXISTING_POSITIVE_DENSITY_THEOREM.
