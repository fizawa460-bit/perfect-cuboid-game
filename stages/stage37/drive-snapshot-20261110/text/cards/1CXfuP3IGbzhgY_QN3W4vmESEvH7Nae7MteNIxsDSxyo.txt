3. Explicit fixed-kappa family


For all sufficiently large B, choose one odd prime q with
2T<q<4T.
Such q exists by Bertrand's postulate after replacing 2T by a neighboring integer if necessary.


Put
M=floor(B/(16 q^(3/2))).
Then M tends to infinity and M/q tends to infinity.


For each prime s in
M<=s<=3M/2,
set
m=2qs, n=1.
Since m is even and gcd(m,n)=1, the standard Euclid triple
L_1=2mn=4qs,
L_2=m^2-n^2=4q^2s^2-1,
H=m^2+n^2=4q^2s^2+1
is primitive.


Scale it by q:
X_s=4q^2s,
Y_s=q(4q^2s^2-1),
Z_s=q(4q^2s^2+1).                                      (CJ.1)


Then
X_s^2+Y_s^2=Z_s^2,
gcd(X_s,Y_s)=q.                                         (CJ.2)


The height is admissible:
for s<=3M/2,
4q^3s^2 <= 9B^2/256,
hence
Z_s<B^2
for all sufficiently large B.                            (CJ.3)


Thus the entire family lives inside the same macro box that contains the Stage37 physical product triple.

#{s prime:M<=s<=3M/2}
 >> M/log M
 >> B q^(-3/2)/log B

 = B^(1/2+(3/2)delta)/log B.                       (CJ.11)


Therefore the ambient macro host contains
>> B^(1/2+(3/2)delta)/log B
distinct JOINT radical pairs satisfying
kappa>T,
