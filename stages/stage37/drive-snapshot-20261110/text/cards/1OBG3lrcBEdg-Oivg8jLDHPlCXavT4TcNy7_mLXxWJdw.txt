# K16-C3-M3 — ROUTE: FIBRATION


## Route card

moving-fibration energy.

## Exact setup


Let t be the retained rational fibration coordinate on K_c.


Define


m_t(B)=#M3_t(B),
p_t(B)=#P_t(B).


For every fixed occupied t,


m_t(B)=B^o(1)


uniformly.


Card:
M3-FIB-001.


## Consequence of fixed-fiber anti-concentration


Since


M3(B)>>B^(1/3),


the number of occupied t-values is at least


B^(1/3-o(1)).


Any family of at most


B^(1/3-epsilon)


fixed t-fibers is M3-negligible.


This is useful anti-concentration but does not control where endpoints lie among the moving fibers.


## Sufficient receiver


A sufficient theorem is


sum_t p_t(B)^2/m_t(B)
=
o(M3(B)).


Card:
M3-FIB-GATE-001.


This is an M3-weighted moving-base second-moment statement.


## Why fixed fibers are not enough


The bound


m_t(B)=B^o(1)


only prevents M3 from concentrating on a bounded or very small set of fixed fibers.


It does not imply


p_t/m_t


is usually small.


It does not give independence between endpoint selection and the moving base.


It does not supply the required second moment.


## Alias reconciliation


Treat these as the same theorem species unless a genuinely different quantity is controlled:


MovingFiberEndpointEnergy
M3-weighted moving-base second moment
R3 moving-fibration energy
moving-base incidence energy


## Do-not-repeat


- do not infer average endpoint sparsity from fixed-fiber sparsity;
- do not rename the second-moment receiver;
- do not count a new fibration coordinate unless it changes the actual measure/energy estimate;
- do not claim a local or pointwise quotient theorem automatically averages over physical M3 fibers.


## Reopen condition


Reopen as primary effort only when there is:


- a theorem for M3-weighted moving-base second moments;
- a two-fibration incidence estimate with the same effect;
- a height-compatible large-sieve/energy theorem on the actual M3 fiber measure.
