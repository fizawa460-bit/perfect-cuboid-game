- M3-LOCAL-001 already proves the exact local half-set geometry.
- The required contraction is relative; it does not demand a new B^(-delta) absolute endpoint bound.
- The order of limits fixes S first and then sends B->infinity. Therefore the next legitimate test is a fixed finite modulus Q, not a growing-modulus character theorem.
- Cards157–158 do NOT refute that fixed-Q exact-physical route. They refute two particular transfer attempts:
  (i) naive Abel/interval transfer from ordinary character sums;

- primitive/canonical masks;

- the same squareclass-energy large-sieve gate from158;
- recomputation of the known local half-set.


Assessment:
