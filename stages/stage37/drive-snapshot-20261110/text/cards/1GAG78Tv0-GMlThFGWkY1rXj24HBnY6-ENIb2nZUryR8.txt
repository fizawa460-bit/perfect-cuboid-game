6. Four atomic linear-linear gcd buckets

We first split the central matching:
  Q_- = A_- C_-,
  Q_+ = A_+ C_+.                                 (229AS.16)


To justify the products, note that an odd prime dividing e,R and lambda would satisfy
  U+V ≡ 0 mod p,
  R ≡ e ≡ 0 mod p.

The same argument works with S in place of lambda.


Hence the e- and R-parts of each Q packet are coprime and (229AS.16) is exact.
