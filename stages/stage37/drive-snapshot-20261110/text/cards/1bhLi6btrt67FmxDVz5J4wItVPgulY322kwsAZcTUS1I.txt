Moreover gcd(q,Z)=t*gcd(h,d0)=t, so

4. Exact positive reconstruction and inverse

  t=gcd(q,Z),
  h=q/t,
  d0=Z/t.


Since gcd(h,d0)=1, reconstruct
  e=h E=(q/t)E,
  x=d0 X=(Z/t)X,
  y=d0 Y=(Z/t)Y,
  z=d0 q=h Z,
  R=h H=(q/t)H.                                  (229BD.5)

  x^2+y^2 = d0^2 q^2=z^2,
