2. Exact outer-gap quadratic reconstruction


From
  e^2+x^2=U^2,
  e^2+y^2=V^2,
  x^2+y^2=z^2,
  e^2+z^2=R^2,
and (229BU.2),
  y^2 = R^2-U^2 = A(2R-A),                         (229BU.3)
  x^2 = R^2-V^2 = Bv(2R-Bv),                       (229BU.4)
  e^2 = R^2-z^2 = C(2R-C).                         (229BU.5)


Using x^2+y^2=z^2=(R-C)^2 gives
  A(2R-A)+Bv(2R-Bv)=(R-C)^2.
Equivalently
  R^2-2R(A+Bv+C)+(A^2+Bv^2+C^2)=0.                (229BU.6)


Let
  S=A+Bv+C.
Then
  (R-S)^2 = 2(ABv+AC+BvC).                         (229BU.7)


The sign is fixed physically. Indeed
  R-S=U+V+z-2R
     =(U+V-R-e)+(e+z-R).
The first parenthesis is lambda_e>0 by the retained 229BL central-gap theorem, and the second is >0 because R^2=e^2+z^2 with e,z>0. Hence
  R-S = sqrt(2(ABv+AC+BvC)) >0,                    (229BU.8)
so for a fixed labelled positive triple (A,Bv,C), R is uniquely determined:
  R = A+Bv+C + sqrt(2(ABv+AC+BvC)).                (229BU.9)


Once R is fixed,
  U=R-A, V=R-Bv, z=R-C,
and e,x,y are the unique positive square roots in (229BU.3)-(229BU.5), if those quantities are integral squares. All primitivity, selector, orientation and remaining consistency conditions only discard candidates.


Therefore
  #{P:R(P)<=B,(R-U,R-V,R-z)=(A,Bv,C)} <= 1.        (229BU.10)

3. Fixed outer-gap product has subpower physical fiber


Define
  Omega(P):=(R-U)(R-V)(R-z)=A Bv C.                (229BU.11)


For a fixed positive integer n, the number of labelled positive triples
  A Bv C=n
is at most the ordered ternary divisor count d_3(n). By the standard uniform divisor bound, for n<=B^3,
  d_3(n)=B^o(1).


Each labelled triple has physical fiber at most one by (229BU.10). Therefore uniformly in n,
  #{P:R<=B,Omega(P)=n} <= B^o(1).                  (229BU.12)


For completeness, Omega has polynomial height. From (229BU.3)-(229BU.5),
  A=y^2/(R+U),
  Bv=x^2/(R+V),
  C=e^2/(R+z).
Hence
  Omega = e^2 x^2 y^2 / ((R+U)(R+V)(R+z))
        < e^2 x^2 y^2/R^3.
Since e^2+x^2+y^2=R^2, AM-GM gives
  e^2 x^2 y^2 <= R^6/27,
therefore
  Omega < R^3/27 <= B^3/27.                        (229BU.13)

4. Fixed radical of the product also has subpower fiber


Define the squarefree support scalar

Retain the uniform prescribed-prime-support lemma used in 229BQ and 229BT: for fixed height exponent, the number of polynomial-height integers supported on a prescribed squarefree integer is B^o(1), uniformly in that support.


Thus for fixed s there are B^o(1) possible Omega values. Combining with (229BU.12),
  #{P:R<=B,k_gap(P)=s} <= B^o(1),                  (229BU.15)
uniformly in s.
