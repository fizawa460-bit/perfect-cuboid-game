3. Exact radical center on the full selector cover

Over an algebraically closed field of characteristic not two, (229AH.3) splits into four disjoint branches.


(A) e=0, x=0.
Then y^2=z^2, R^2=z^2, U=0, V^2=y^2.
After projective scaling y=1 there are

hence exactly 8 geometric points.


(B) e=0, y=0.
Symmetrically, after x=1 there are exactly 8 geometric points.

(D) R=0, y=0.
