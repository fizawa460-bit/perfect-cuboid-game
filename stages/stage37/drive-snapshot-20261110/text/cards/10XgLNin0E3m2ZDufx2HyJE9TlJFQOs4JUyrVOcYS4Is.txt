## Endpoint cover


The endpoint condition defines a degree-two cover


pi:S->K_c.


For a fixed integral degree-6 rational carrier


C subset K_c,


the endpoint lift is non-split of degree 12.


The contribution from one fixed carrier is


<<B^(1/6+o(1)).

## Fixed-degree progress


Retained geometry shows that fixed rational-carrier degrees can be controlled individually, and the low-degree physical rational-carrier analysis removes the naive degree 1/2/3 danger in the retained framework.


The important firewall is:


fixed degree
is not the same thing as
the union of all relevant carriers with height/complexity growing with B.


## Global two-layer gate


To close the whole endpoint population by this method one still needs both:


1. accumulating-family summation:
a bound on the number/complexity of relevant endpoint-carrying carrier curves strong enough to sum their individual endpoint contributions;


2. residual surface control:
a bound on endpoints not captured by the accumulating families.

## Useful exponent budget


A fixed degree-6 carrier contributes


B^(1/6+o(1)).


Therefore, if the number of relevant such carriers were bounded by


B^(1/6-delta),


their union would contribute


B^(1/3-delta+o(1)),


which would be negligible against the certified denominator scale.


This is a sufficient threshold, not a proved global family count.


## Testa-Stoll firewall


Low-degree curve classification on the endpoint surface does not automatically provide:


- denominator-side count of relevant carrier families on K_c;
- physical-height family growth;
- a residual Zariski-dense endpoint bound.


Do not promote classification alone to whole-population control.

## Do-not-repeat


- do not sum fixed-curve estimates without a family-count theorem;
- do not identify surface curve classification with physical denominator-family count;
- do not ignore the residual Zariski-dense population;
- do not claim fixed-degree negligibility implies all-degree/global negligibility.


## Reopen condition


A genuinely new result should provide at least one:


- summable family count by physical height;
- uniform union bound over accumulating carriers;
- residual rational-point theorem under exact physical height;
- structural theorem reducing the residual set to already-controlled carriers.
