with

and the rational quartic carrier

2. Exact norm-five Lattes coordinate


Take alpha=2+i. Retain 229T:
  p^2 = x(P)/d = F(r)=r H(r)^2,
where
  H(r)=(((2-i)r^2+i)(r^2-1+2i))
       /(5r^4-2r^2+1).                          (229U.1)


Set
  D(r)=5r^4-2r^2+1,
  A(r)=r^4-2r^2+5.                              (229U.2)


For real rational r,
  H(r) conjugate(H(r)) = A(r)/D(r).             (229U.3)


The other norm-five slopes are obtained from 2+i by conjugation, sign, or multiplication by the unit i. Conjugation leaves all norms below unchanged; sign leaves x unchanged; multiplication by i changes F to -F and swaps the plus/minus endpoint selectors. Hence it is enough to compute for alpha=2+i.


RESULT_229U_NORM5_NORM_FACTOR=PROVED_EXACT.


3. Endpoint selector saturation: N(p^2 plus/minus 1) are automatic squares


A direct exact factorization gives


  N(F(r)+1)
   = [
       (r-1)(r^4+4r^3+10r^2+4r+1)
       /D(r)
     ]^2,                                        (229U.4)


  N(F(r)-1)
   = [
       (r+1)(r^4-4r^3+10r^2-4r+1)
       /D(r)
     ]^2.                                        (229U.5)


Therefore for every rational r at which the non-torsion norm-five map is defined,
  N(p^2+1) in Q^{*2},
  N(p^2-1) in Q^{*2}
hold identically.


Thus the two endpoint selectors that were nontrivial on the ambient 229J torus give NO further arithmetic thinning on the norm-five rational CM carrier.

4. The base norm condition is the surviving selector


229J also requires
  N(p) in Q^{*2}.


Since r=epsilon t^2 and p is a square root of F=rH^2, one may take
  p=tH(r)          when epsilon=+1,
  p=i t H(r)       when epsilon=-1,
up to the harmless sign.


In either case
  N(p)=t^2 A(r)/D(r).                            (229U.6)


Because t^2 is already a rational square, the physical base condition is equivalent to
  A(r)/D(r) in Q^{*2}.                           (229U.7)


Equivalently, with U in Q,
  C_5:
  U^2=A(r)D(r)
     =(r^4-2r^2+5)(5r^4-2r^2+1).               (229U.8)


RESULT_229U_NORM5_BASE_SELECTOR_EQUIVALENT_TO_FIXED_CURVE=PROVED_EXACT.

5. C_5 is a smooth genus-three curve


Let

6. Faltings gives finite rational r-support


By Faltings' theorem (Mordell conjecture), a smooth projective curve of genus greater than 1 over Q has only finitely many Q-rational points.


Applying this to C_5 gives
  C_5(Q) finite.                                 (229U.11)


Therefore the set of rational r that can occur in a norm-five physical dependence endpoint is finite.


No effective enumeration of C_5(Q) is claimed or needed for the exponent statement.


RESULT_229U_NORM5_RATIONAL_r_SUPPORT=FINITE_BY_FALTINGS.


7. Finite r implies finite realized twist support


For every surviving rational r, 229T also has
  r=epsilon t^2
with at most finitely many rational t.


The rational point Q=(dr,y_Q) on E_d satisfies
  (y_Q/d)^2=d*r*(r^2-1).                        (229U.12)


Thus the squareclass of d in Q^*/Q^{*2} is uniquely determined by r:
  [d]=[r(r^2-1)]                                (229U.13)
because inversion is identical in the squareclass group.


The canonical positive squarefree representative d is therefore unique if it exists. Hence the norm-five physical dependence branch realizes only finitely many canonical twists d.


RESULT_229U_NORM5_REALIZED_TWIST_SUPPORT=FINITE.


This is stronger than the 229T finite-degree/genus-one carrier statement: the surviving physical base selector has converted the moving norm-five branch into a finite twist-support set.
