2. Bounded-radius collision theorem


The line graph of a graph of maximum degree Delta has maximum degree at most 2(Delta-1). Hence for every raw edge e, the number of distinct raw edges within line-graph distance at most L is at most


    sum_{j=1}^L [2(Delta-1)]^j
    <= L(2Delta)^L.


The squareclass and unequal-value conditions only delete pairs. Therefore, for every fixed L,


    E_off,<=L
      <= N * L(2Delta)^L
      <= B^(1/2+o(1)).                         (222.1)


This is uniform in kappa, y, contents and the inherited masks. It does not use the fixed-M multiplicity D, short multipliers, high-kappa, character cancellation, an endpoint assumption, or any independence heuristic.


For L=1 there is a sharper direct incidence form. If d_X(F) is the degree of a face vertex inside the selected X* image, then

      <= sum_F d_X(F)(d_X(F)-1)
      <= Delta * sum_F d_X(F)
      = 2N Delta
      <= B^(1/2+o(1)).                          (222.2)


Thus all off-value same-squareclass pairs that reuse one primitive face are already paid.
