2. Full directional host: four exact primitive-face charts


Fix one directional host H_j(B). Use physical edges (e,x,y), where e is the labeled shared edge and the two already-integral faces are


    e^2+x^2=u^2,
    e^2+y^2=v^2.


Neither x^2+y^2 nor e^2+x^2+y^2 is imposed.


For coprime positive odd parameters a<b define the primitive Pythagorean functions


    O(a,b)=ab,
    E(a,b)=(b^2-a^2)/2,
    N(a,b)=(a^2+b^2)/2.


Every primitive Pythagorean triple occurs uniquely with odd leg O, even leg E and hypotenuse N in this form.


Take a second coprime positive odd pair c<f. For a chart


    theta=(theta_1,theta_2) in {O,E}^2


let


    L1=theta_1(a,b),
    M1=the other member of {O(a,b),E(a,b)},
    L2=theta_2(c,f),
    M2=the other member of {O(c,f),E(c,f)},


and N1=N(a,b), N2=N(c,f).


Here L1,L2 are the primitive shared-leg values in the two faces. Put


    g=gcd(L1,L2).


For a primitive physical three-edge object the two face scales d1,d2 are coprime. From


    d1 L1=d2 L2


it follows exactly that


    d1=L2/g,
    d2=L1/g.


Hence the physical state is


    e = L1 L2/g,
    x = L2 M1/g,
    y = L1 M2/g,
    u = L2 N1/g,
    v = L1 N2/g.                                  (228.1)


Conversely a primitive labeled two-face state has unique primitive reductions in its two faces; the parity of each primitive shared leg determines theta, and coprimality of the physical face scales forces (228.1). Thus the four theta charts form an exact disjoint finite partition of the labeled directional host, before the usual canonical-order/direction chamber mask is imposed.


No polynomial chart multiplicity is introduced.

5. All four charts inherit the Section112/113 finite-field bounds


For one face define the linear change, valid over every odd characteristic,


    T(a,b)=((b-a)/2,(a+b)/2).                      (228.9)


Then


    O(T(a,b)) = E(a,b)/2,
    E(T(a,b)) = O(a,b)/2.                          (228.10)


The determinant of T is nonzero at every odd prime.


If a chart uses E as its primitive shared leg in m of the two faces, apply T to precisely those m parameter pairs. The resulting chart is O,O and


    E_(O,O)(T_theta variables) = 4^(-m) E_theta,
    H_(O,O)(T_theta variables) = 4^(-m) H_theta.   (228.11)


Because 4^m is a square modulo every good odd prime, the quadratic characters of E_theta, H_theta and E_theta H_theta are unchanged after the invertible linear variable substitution.


The additive Fourier transform is also merely reindexed by the inverse-transpose linear map. Since T acts inside one variable pair, the Section113 frequency classes are preserved:
- zero frequency remains zero;
- one-pair-degenerate remains one-pair-degenerate;
- generic nonzero remains generic nonzero.


Therefore, for every one of the four theta charts and every good odd prime p, the retained complete-sum hierarchy extends verbatim in exponent:


    complete zero-frequency character sum: O(p^(7/2)),
    one-pair-degenerate Fourier coefficient: O(p^(5/2)),
    generic nonzero Fourier coefficient: O(p^2),    (228.12)


and the corresponding squarefree incomplete-box estimate has the same exponent shape after the linear chart change.


Caveat:
T need not preserve the integral parity chamber. This theorem is a statement about complete finite-field/Fourier sums at odd p. It does not identify the physical integral domains between charts.

7.3 Condition 3 — square/finite-character expansion preserving centering


FAIL for the currently retained square-sieve architecture.


For an integer n,


    1_{n is an integer square}


is not equal to any fixed finite average of Legendre/Jacobi characters. A finite-prime character score detects necessary local quadratic-residue conditions and has false positives.


The standard square-sieve identities/inequalities are designed for nonnegative square counts. Applying them separately to the four terms obtained by expanding


    (F-alpha)(S-beta)


does not preserve the signed cancellation defining the empirical global marginals alpha and beta.


No retained identity supplies an exact bounded-conductor character expansion of both global integer-square selectors on H_j with a controlled centered remainder.


An exact delta/circle representation would be a different method species and would require a fresh conductor/range theorem; it is not contained in Section112/113.


Therefore


    R1_CENTERED_PHYSICAL_SQUARE_SELECTOR_EXPANSION=false.   (228.15)


7.4 Condition 4 — zero mode equals product of GLOBAL marginals


FAIL under the same retained finite-character substitution.


The zero/principal term of a complete finite-field character expansion is a local residue mean, including its zero-divisor corrections. It is not identically


    alpha*beta


where alpha and beta are the empirical integer-square marginals on the physical H_j(B) population.


Centering the local character functions by their mod-p means only produces a locally centered statistic. It does not prove that the global physical zero mode is


    E_H[F] E_H[S].


Promoting that equality would itself require the missing same-measure transfer/equidistribution theorem.


Thus the key R1-TF requirement


    ZERO_FREQUENCY_EXACT_GLOBAL_PRODUCT_OF_MARGINALS


is not certified.

7.7 Condition 7 — can the retained square-sieve + Section113 range certify B^(-1/12-delta)?


NO.


There is a useful scoped budget calculation.


Suppose one tries to approximate one square selector by a normalized character score over K distinct primes and controls the replacement in L2 by the standard absolute square-sieve/character second-moment architecture.


The diagonal contribution in that available L2 upper-bound mechanism is on the scale


    H_j/K.                                         (228.16)


To make replacement of F harmless at Pearson scale B^(-1/12), Cauchy requires the squared L2 replacement error to be at most on the scale


    B^(-1/6) V_F.                                  (228.17)


Since alpha->0, V_F is asymptotic in scale to M3. The certified corridor gives only


    M3 >> B^(1/3),
    H_j ~ C_j B(log B)^5.


Thus a proof that works uniformly at the currently certified worst normalization can force the diagonal term (228.16) below (228.17) only after roughly


    K >= B^(5/6-o(1)).                             (228.18)


This is a METHOD-BUDGET statement: smaller K is not mathematically impossible, but the retained absolute L2 estimate does not certify the required replacement error there.


A family of K distinct primes necessarily reaches prime scale at least K^{1-o(1)}. The pair-character moduli appearing in the standard square sieve therefore reach/typically occupy scale at least


    q >= B^(5/3-o(1))                              (228.19)


at the cardinality forced by (228.18).


But the retained Section113 squarefree incomplete-box estimate contains an absolute generic remainder


    q^2,


which at (228.19) is at least


    B^(10/3-o(1)),                                 (228.20)


far above the H_j~B polylog host scale, before the one-pair-degenerate and moving-physical-weight costs are even paid.


Therefore the CURRENT combination


    standard character-score/square-sieve replacement
      + absolute Section113 completion
      + current physical weight handling


cannot certify the 1/12 Pearson receiver.


This does not rule out a new switching theorem, a relative/centered trace theorem, a delta-symbol argument with new cancellation, or another exact global square detector.
