1. Scope


229L gives, for every positive physical endpoint, a canonical positive squarefree twist d and a point
  P in E_d(Q(i)),   E_d: Y^2=X^3-d^2 X,
with inherited exponential height B^O(1), O(1) endpoint-to-point multiplicity, and uniform fixed-d endpoint fiber B^o(1). 229M/N/O identify d up to the harmless 2-adic squareclass with
  K_*=sf(gcd(eR,xy)),
and give d <= 2B^2. 229P shows that ordinary base codimension-two sieving cannot close the large-K_* tail.


The first question here is whether the field Q(i) blocks the strong literature on rational points in congruent-number twist families. It does not: the j=1728 CM automorphism gives an exact rationalization to Q. The second question is whether current small-point theorems then meet the Stage37 B^(1/3) budget. They do not at the current twist scale.


No endpoint saving is claimed.


2. Exact CM trace/anti-trace rationalization


Let K=Q(i), let sigma be complex conjugation, and let
  iota:E_d -> E_d,
  iota(X,Y)=(-X,iY).
Then sigma(iota)=-iota and iota^2=[-1].


For P in E_d(K), define
  A=P+sigma(P),
  C=iota(P-sigma(P)).
Then A is sigma-invariant, hence A in E_d(Q). Also
  sigma(C)=(-iota)(sigma(P)-P)=iota(P-sigma(P))=C,
so C in E_d(Q).


Moreover
  2P = A - iota(C).
Indeed -iota(C)=P-sigma(P).


Therefore if P is non-torsion, at least one of A,C is a non-torsion Q-rational point. Otherwise A and C would both be torsion and the displayed identity would force 2P, hence P, to be torsion.


Canonical height is preserved by sigma and by the degree-one automorphism iota. The parallelogram inequality gives
  hhat(A) <= 2(hhat(P)+hhat(sigma P)) = 4 hhat(P),
  hhat(C) = hhat(P-sigma P) <= 4 hhat(P).
Thus a non-torsion physical P of exponential height <=B^C produces a non-torsion Q-rational point on the same E_d of exponential canonical height <=B^(4C).

3. Physical tail points are non-torsion over Q(i)


For positive squarefree d, the rational torsion of the congruent-number curve is
  E_d(Q)_tors={O,(0,0),(d,0),(-d,0)}.


We prove that for d>1,
  E_d(Q(i))_tors=E_d[2].


First, there is no odd-order torsion over Q(i). If T has odd order, then
  T+sigma(T) in E_d(Q),
  iota(T-sigma(T)) in E_d(Q)
are odd-order rational torsion, hence both O. The identity
  2T=(T+sigma T)-iota(iota(T-sigma T))
then forces 2T=O, so T=O.


Second, there is no point of order 4 for squarefree d>1. A 2-torsion point (r,0) on
  Y^2=(X-0)(X-d)(X+d)
is divisible by 2 over a field of characteristic not 2 only if the two root differences from r are squares in that field. Thus:
- halving (0,0) requires d and -d square in Q(i);
- halving (d,0) requires d and 2d square in Q(i);
- halving (-d,0) requires -d and -2d square in Q(i).
For a positive rational d, d is a square in Q(i) iff it is a rational square. Hence for positive squarefree d>1 none of these conditions can hold. Any torsion of 2-power order >2 would contain a point of order 4, so no such torsion exists.


The 229L physical image is never 2-torsion: X/d=p^2, while p=0 is outside the physical torus and p^2=1 or -1 forces a zero physical edge, already proved impossible in 229L. Therefore every physical 229L point with d>1 is non-torsion.


On the current preferred large-radical tail, d is comparable to K_* up to a factor of 2 through d=sf(2^epsilon K_*), so for B large the tail automatically has d>1.

4. Le Boudec 2016 now matches the field, but not the Stage37 exponent


Le Boudec studies the Q-rational congruent-number twist family in the equivalent model
  d y^2=x^3-x
and defines N_alpha^*(X) by summing non-torsion rational points of exponential canonical height at most d^(1/8+alpha) over squarefree d<=X. For the full rational 2-torsion family he proves, for fixed alpha>0 and epsilon>0,
  N_alpha^*(X) << X^(1/2+alpha+epsilon).


Sections 2-3 remove the previous Q(i)-versus-Q quantifier mismatch: every physical tail twist d now genuinely contributes a non-torsion Q-rational point on E_d, with polynomial physical-B height.


However the exponent budget is still wrong. Current Stage37 only has
  d <= 2B^2.
Even in the formally best possible limit alpha -> 0+, the Le Boudec bound at X~B^2 is only
  B^(1+o(1)),
far above the required o(B^(1/3)) twist-support budget.


The actual physical height transfer is weaker still: if hhat(P)<=C log B and the large-radical tail begins at d >= c B^kappa with kappa=1/3-delta, then a uniform use of Le Boudec over the whole tail requires a fixed
  alpha >= 4C/kappa - 1/8
(up to harmless constants), which only increases the exponent.


A useful budget corollary is exact at the method level. If an independent argument truncated every realized physical twist to
  d <= B^beta
and one could work in the near-minimal alpha->0 regime, Le Boudec's X^(1/2+alpha) mechanism could beat B^(1/3) only if
  beta < 2/3.
More generally the condition is
  beta(1/2+alpha) < 1/3.
The current beta=2 envelope is far outside this range.

5. Integral-point results do not black-box transfer


Chan's current congruent-number result bounds the total number of non-trivial integral points on E_D for squarefree D<=N by
  O_epsilon(N (log N)^(-0.137061...+epsilon))
(and in the abstract by the weaker displayed O(N(log N)^(-1/4+epsilon)) formulation across versions).


This is a strong average theorem on integral points, but the CM rationalization in Section 2 uses elliptic-curve addition. The resulting rational points A or C are not proved integral, or even uniformly S-integral with bounded S, on the Stage37 physical image. Therefore Chan's theorem does not currently count the realized Stage37 twists.


Even if integrality were granted for free, substituting the present N~B^2 twist envelope yields only B^2 times a logarithmic saving, still far above B^(1/3). The 2025 Browning-Chan “almost all twists have no integral points” theorem has the same integrality mismatch for this route and does not supply the required Stage37 fixed-power exceptional-set rate.

6. Consequence for the theorem watch


The CM field mismatch is retired for non-torsion physical tail points. The moving-twist route can now be phrased entirely over Q:


Every endpoint in the current large-K_* tail yields a squarefree d comparable to K_* and a non-torsion rational point on E_d(Q), with a distinguished inherited 2-descent origin and polynomial physical-B height.


What remains missing is not “Q(i) rational points” but a quantitatively much thinner twist-support theorem. A sufficient new input may take one of these forms:


(A) PHYSICAL/DESCENT-CONDITIONED SMALL-POINT TWIST COUNT:
count the squarefree d actually arising from the 229L/229M distinguished physical 2-descent image by o(B^(1/3)), rather than all twists with a small rational point.


(B) LARGE-d DISPOSAL PLUS SMALL-POINT THEOREM:
first prove that all but o(B^(1/3)) physical endpoints have d<=B^(beta) with beta<2/3 (or beta satisfying beta(1/2+alpha)<1/3 at the available height exponent), then Le Boudec-type bounds become budget-compatible in principle.


(C) STRONGER RATIONAL-POINT EXCEPTIONAL-SET THEOREM:
a theorem over Q whose exceptional twist count at the inherited polynomial physical height is itself o(B^(1/3)) over d<=2B^2.


Integral-point sparsity alone is not a restart trigger without an exact integrality/quasi-integrality adapter and a fixed-power rate meeting the Stage37 budget.


The preferred gate remains
  #{primitive positive physical endpoints R<=B:
      sf(gcd(eR,xy))>B^(1/3-delta)}
    = o(B^(1/3))
for some fixed delta>0.
