5. Uniform root-line packing
Fix an odd m and one root rho^2==-2 mod m^2. Consider
Lambda(m,rho)={(X,Y) in Z^2 : X==rho Y mod m^2}.               (CB.21)
For every nonzero lattice vector,
m^2 | X^2+2Y^2,
hence X^2+2Y^2>=m^2.                                          (CB.22)
Therefore distinct lattice points have separation at least m in the weighted norm sqrt(X^2+2Y^2). A disjoint-ellipse packing argument in 0<X,Y<B gives
#{(X,Y) in Lambda(m,rho):0<X,Y<B}
 << 1+B^2/m^2.                                                 (CB.23)
Summing over the 2^omega(m)=B^o(1) admissible roots,
#{(X,Y):0<X,Y<B, m^2|X^2+2Y^2, gcd(m,XY)=1}
 << B^o(1)(1+B^2/m^2).                                        (CB.24)

#{P:R<=B, g_A(P)=m}
 << B^o(1)(1+B^2/m^2),                                        (CB.25)
uniformly in m. The same bound holds cyclically for g_B and g_C.
