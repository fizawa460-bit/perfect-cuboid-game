2. Uniform binary norm/Pell count


Lemma 229BB-Pell.
Let positive integers P,Q,N,H be given. The number of positive integer solutions
  P X^2 - Q Y^2 = N,
  X,Y <= H
is
  <<_epsilon (PQNH)^epsilon
uniformly in P,Q,N,H.


Proof.


Multiply by P:
  (PX)^2 - PQ Y^2 = PN.                            (229BB.5)


Put D=PQ and M=PN.


If D=t^2 is a square, then
  (PX-tY)(PX+tY)=M.
Thus the number of positive solutions is at most the number of divisor pairs of M, hence M^o(1).


If D is not a square, write
  D=t^2 d
with d>1 squarefree and work in the maximal order O_K of K=Q(sqrt d).


Every positive solution gives
  alpha=PX+tY sqrt d in O_K,
with
  Norm(alpha)=M
and both real embeddings positive.


Then the principal ideal (alpha) is an integral ideal divisor of (M).
For p^v||M, the number of local ideal-divisor exponent choices is O((2v+1)^2), uniformly in the splitting type. Hence the total number of possible integral ideals (alpha) is
  <= tau(M^2)^2=M^o(1).                            (229BB.6)


For a fixed principal ideal, all totally positive generators of norm M differ by powers of a totally positive norm-one unit.
A nontrivial totally positive norm-one unit has integer trace at least3, hence is at least
  (3+sqrt5)/2>1.
Inside the polynomial height box there are therefore only
  O(log(PQNH))
unit translates.


Multiplying with (229BB.6) proves the lemma.


RESULT_229BB_UNIFORM_BINARY_NORM_COUNT=PROVED_EXACT.


This is the same abstract mechanism harvested as SR-STR-090, but its proof is included here so Stage37 does not depend on the source-stage population contract.

  R=h C R0 <= B.

7. Primitive outer Pythagorean receiver


Retain h as the content of
  e^2+z^2=R^2.


Set
  Z0:=z/h.
Using e/h=A E0 and R/h=C R0 gives
  (A E0)^2 + Z0^2 = (C R0)^2.                    (229BB.17)


Because h is the exact content,
  gcd(AE0,Z0,CR0)=1,
and in particular
  gcd(AE0,CR0)=1.                                 (229BB.18)


Therefore the following stronger coprimalities hold:
  gcd(A,C)=1,
  gcd(E0,R0)=1,
  gcd(A,R0)=1,
  gcd(C,E0)=1.                                    (229BB.19)


RESULT_229BB_PRIMITIVE_OUTER_PYTHAGOREAN_RECEIVER=PROVED_EXACT.


The root-free support is thus not an arbitrary six-variable box. Its compressed outer coordinates satisfy one primitive Pythagorean relation in which:
- A=A_-A_+ is the edge-content side and may carry inert odd primes;
- C=C_-C_+ is split-prime-only;
- h is a separate split-prime-only common content;
- E0,R0 are cross-coprime to the opposite content products.
