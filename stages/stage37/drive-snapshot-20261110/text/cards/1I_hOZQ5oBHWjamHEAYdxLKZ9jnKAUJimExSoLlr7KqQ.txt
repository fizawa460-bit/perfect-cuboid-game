5. 2-adic lemma: N(s^2 plus/minus 1) cannot equal 2


Let K=Q(i), pi=1+i, and normalize v_pi(pi)=1. Then
  v_2(N_{K/Q}(z))=v_pi(z).


LEMMA 229T.1.
For every s in K,
  v_pi(s^2-1) != 1,
  v_pi(s^2+1) != 1.                             (229T.11)


Proof.
If v_pi(s)>0, then s^2 has positive even valuation and s^2 plus/minus 1 is a unit, so the valuation is 0.


If v_pi(s)<0, then s^2 dominates plus/minus 1 and
  v_pi(s^2 plus/minus 1)=2v_pi(s),
which is even.


If v_pi(s)=0, the residue field is F_2. Hence s is congruent to 1 mod pi; also i is congruent to 1 mod pi. Thus
  s^2-1=(s-1)(s+1)
has both factors divisible by pi, and
  s^2+1=(s-i)(s+i)
also has both factors divisible by pi. In either case the valuation is at least 2.


So valuation 1 never occurs. Consequently
  N(s^2-1) != 2,
  N(s^2+1) != 2.                                (229T.12)


RESULT_229T_2ADIC_NORM2_SQUARE_LEMMA=PROVED_EXACT.

Thus there are

  x(Q)/d=epsilon t^2.                            (229T.14)
