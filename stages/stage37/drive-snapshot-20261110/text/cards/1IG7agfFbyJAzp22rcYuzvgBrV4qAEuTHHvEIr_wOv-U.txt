2. Hilbert-90 squareclass descent lemma over Q(i)


LEMMA 229L.1.
Let z in K^* with K=Q(i). If
  N_{K/Q}(z)=r^2
for some r in Q^*, then the K-squareclass of z comes from a rational squareclass:
  z = delta * w^2
for some delta in Q^* and w in K^*.


Proof.
Put xi=z/r. Then N(xi)=1. By Hilbert 90 there is w in K^* such that
  xi=w/conj(w).
But
  w/conj(w)=w^2/N(w),
and N(w) is rational. Hence
  z=(r/N(w))*w^2.
Take delta=r/N(w) in Q^*. QED.


The rational squareclass is unique only modulo the kernel of
  Q^*/Q^{*2} -> K^*/K^{*2};
for K=Q(i), the sign class disappears because -1=i^2. We therefore use a positive squarefree integer representative whenever a canonical rational representative is needed.

3. Apply the lemma to the 229J Gaussian norm triple


For every nondegenerate 229J selector point there exist
  delta_0, delta_+, delta_- in Q^*
and
  c,a,b in K^*
such that
  p       = delta_0 c^2,
  p^2+1   = delta_+ a^2,
  p^2-1   = delta_- b^2.                       (229L.1)


The first identity is equivalent to the base condition N(p) square at the squareclass level. The second and third are the two endpoint selector conditions.


Put
  D = delta_+ delta_-.
Multiplying the last two equations gives
  p^4-1 = D (ab)^2.                             (229L.2)


The squareclass [D] in Q^*/(Q^{*2}*{+-1}) is canonically determined by p; changing delta_+ or delta_- by the sign kernel does not affect the elliptic curve below because only D^2 occurs.

4. Exact congruent-number elliptic twist router


Define
  X_E = D p^2,
  Y_E = D^2 p a b.


Using (229L.2),
  Y_E^2
    = D^4 p^2 a^2 b^2
    = D^3 p^2(p^4-1)
    = X_E^3-D^2 X_E.


Thus every nondegenerate Gaussian norm-triple point maps to
  E_D: Y^2=X^3-D^2 X                              (229L.3)
over K=Q(i).


This is the standard congruent-number quadratic-twist family, now used only as an exact Stage37 router. E_D has the K-rational 2-torsion points
  (0,0), (D,0), (-D,0).


The original two selector squareclasses are visible on E_D:
  X_E+D
    = D(p^2+1)
    = delta_- (delta_+ a)^2,
  X_E-D
    = D(p^2-1)
    = delta_+ (delta_- b)^2,
and
  X_E/D=p^2 is a K-square.                        (229L.4)


Therefore the image lies in a distinguished 2-descent class of E_D, and the individual rational squareclasses delta_+,delta_- are recoverable from [X_E-D] and [X_E+D] in K^*/K^{*2}, up to the harmless rational sign kernel.


There is also no hidden moving multiplicity from physical endpoints to p. For a Q-rational base point q=conj(p), while p=uv and q=v/u, hence v^2=pq=N(p). On the positive physical chamber v=(R+e)/z>0, so v is the unique positive rational square root of N(p), then u=p/v. The inverse formulas of 229J recover X/Z,Y/Z,S/Z,T/Z uniquely from u,v, and the primitive positive representative is unique. Thus a physical endpoint determines p injectively.


For fixed canonical twist class d, X_E/d=p^2 determines p up to sign from the elliptic point; the physical first-quadrant condition selects the physical sign. Hence the endpoint-to-E_d router has O(1) (indeed physical-sign-one) multiplicity.


Finally, the apparent excluded values are physically impossible. If p^2=1 then q=p, so u^2=p/q=1 and Y/Z=(u-u^(-1))/(2i)=0, hence y=0. If p^2=-1 then q=-p, so u^2=p/q=-1 and X/Z=(u+u^(-1))/2=0, hence x=0. Both contradict positive physical edges. Therefore no endpoint boundary term remains.

6. Dujella 2025 gives a uniform fixed-twist fiber bound


Marta Dujella, “Uniform bounds for the number of rational points of bounded height on certain elliptic curves,” Acta Arithmetica 217 (2025), 309–332, DOI 10.4064/aa231221-9-10, proves the following theorem species:


For an elliptic curve E over a fixed number field k having a k-rational point of exact prime order ell, the number of E(k) points of exponential height at most H is at most
  exp(C(k) log H / log log H),
with effective constant depending only on k.


Apply this with
  k=Q(i), ell=2,
to every E_d in (229L.3). The curves E_d all have rational 2-torsion, and the constant is uniform in the twist d.


Together with (229L.7), for every canonical twist class d,
  # {Stage37 Gaussian norm-triple points with R<=B and twist d}
      <= B^{o(1)},                                (229L.8)
uniformly in d.


This counts all K-rational points on E_d, so the distinguished 2-descent restriction can only reduce the count. The O(1) router multiplicity has no effect.
