3. Explicit base Hilbert squareclass delta_0

  p=((x+i y)(R+e))/z^2,
  v=(R+e)/z,
  N(p)=v^2.                                      (229AD.6)


Because x,y>0, p is non-real, so the exact Hilbert-class lemma229M.1 applies to zeta=p:
if N(p)=v^2 then the rational squareclass of p is represented by
  2v+Tr(p).


Now
  Tr(p)=2x(R+e)/z^2,
hence
  2v+Tr(p)
    =2(R+e)/z + 2x(R+e)/z^2
    =2(R+e)(z+x)/z^2.                            (229AD.7)


The denominator z^2 is a rational square, so
  [delta_0]=[2(R+e)(z+x)].                       (229AD.8)


Use
  (R+e)(R-e)=z^2,
  (z+x)(z-x)=y^2.
Therefore
  [R+e]=[R-e],
  [z+x]=[z-x]
in Q^*/Q^{*2}, and
  [delta_0]=[2(R-e)(z-x)].                       (229AD.9)

7. Three of the six buckets are split-prime only

Since h|q,
  p|h => p=1 mod4 for every odd p.                (229AD.21)


Likewise n1,n2 are hypotenuses of the primitive face triples in (229AD.4). Therefore every odd prime divisor of n1 or n2 is 1 mod4.
Since
  c12|n1,
  c21|n2,
we have
  p|c12 c21 => p=1 mod4 for every odd p.         (229AD.22)


Thus the six-bucket packet has an alternating structure:
