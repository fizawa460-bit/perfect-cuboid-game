2. Split-prime residue-field lemma


Let ell be an odd rational prime with
  ell == 1 mod 4.
Choose one Gaussian prime pi above ell. Then
  O_K/pi = F_ell.


Let chi_ell be the quadratic character on F_ell^*.


A root i_ell of -1 in F_ell satisfies
  chi_ell(i_ell)
   = i_ell^((ell-1)/2)
   = (-1)^((ell-1)/4)
   = (2/ell),                                    (229AE.3)
where (2/ell) is the ordinary Legendre symbol. Since -1 is itself a square for ell==1 mod4, the two roots +/-i_ell have the same character.

3. Local rule at a split prime dividing Delta+


Assume
  ell | Delta+
with ell==1 mod4.


Because Delta+ is squarefree at ell, v_pi(p^2+1) is odd.


If v_pi(p)>0 then p^2+1 is a unit, contradiction.
If v_pi(p)<0 then
  v_pi(p^2+1)=2v_pi(p)
is even, contradiction.
Therefore
  v_pi(p)=0,
  p^2 == -1 mod pi.                              (229AE.4)


From p=Delta0 c^2 and ell not dividing Delta0 by229AD support disjointness,
  (Delta0/ell)=chi_ell(p)=(2/ell).               (229AE.5)


Also
  p^2-1 == -2 mod pi.
Since p^2-1=Delta- b^2 and ell does not divide Delta-,
  (Delta-/ell)=(-2/ell)=(2/ell).                 (229AE.6)


Thus every odd split prime ell in Delta+ satisfies


  (Delta0/ell) = (Delta-/ell) = (2/ell).         (229AE.7)


RESULT_229AE_SPLIT_PRIME_IN_DELTA_PLUS_RULE=PROVED_EXACT.

  ell | Delta0
with ell==1 mod4.

  (Delta+/ell)=(Delta-/ell)=1.                  (229AE.12)

8. Affine split-prime cut matrix


Write the odd part of D as
  D_odd = product_{j=1}^r ell_j
with distinct odd primes.


Let
  x_j in F_2
encode which packet contains ell_j:
  x_j=1 for Delta+,
  x_j=0 for Delta-.


Let
  e_+,e_- in F_2
be the 2-adic exponents of the chosen squarefree representatives Delta+,Delta-.


For each split row ell_i==1 mod4 define
  lambda_ij in F_2 by
    (-1)^lambda_ij = (ell_j/ell_i),  j!=i,
and
  theta_i in F_2 by
    (-1)^theta_i = (2/ell_i).                    (229AE.18)


The opposite packet at ell_i consists exactly of:
- odd primes ell_j with x_j+x_i=1;
- the factor2 with exponent e_- if x_i=1 and exponent e_+ if x_i=0.


Therefore (229AE.16)-(229AE.17) are equivalent to the affine linear equation


  sum_{j!=i} lambda_ij (x_i+x_j)
   + theta_i [ e_- x_i + e_+ (1-x_i) ]
   = theta_i                                  mod2. (229AE.19)


This equation is imposed for every split prime row ell_i|D.
No row is imposed here for ell_i==3 mod4.


For fixed D and fixed 2-adic packet bits, the physically admissible Delta+/Delta- partitions therefore lie in an affine F_2 solution space of an explicit split-row residue matrix.

9. Why inert rational primes supply no analogous row


Let ell==3 mod4. Then ell is inert in Q(i), and the residue field is
  F_{ell^2}.


Every rational unit in F_ell^* becomes a square in F_{ell^2}^* because
  (ell^2-1)/2
is divisible by ell-1.


Also i is a square in F_{ell^2}: ell^2==1 mod8, so an element of order4 lies in the square subgroup.


Hence the local reductions used in Sections3-5 impose no nontrivial rational Legendre-symbol condition at inert ell.
