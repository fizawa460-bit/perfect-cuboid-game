INDEPENDENT RE-IMPLEMENTATION:
Using
F_{a,b}(p,q) = (b^2 p^2 - a^2 q^2)(b^2 q^2 - a^2 p^2)
and
H_{a,b}(p,q) = (a^4+6a^2b^2+b^4)(p^4+q^4) - 2(a^2-b^2)^2 p^2 q^2,
with (a,b) coprime and (p,q) coprime, three parameter-range interpretations were tried:


1. (a,b) coprime, no parity constraint; p>q>=1, gcd(p,q)=1:
   N=40 -> (first_cover=538, universal_diag=0, offdiag=538, offdiag_second_square=489)
   N=55 -> (1013, 0, 1013, 939)


2. (a,b) coprime, opposite parity (primitive-Euclid style); p>q>=1, gcd(p,q)=1:
   N=40 -> (365, 0, 365, 331)
   N=55 -> (673, 0, 673, 622)


3. (a,b) coprime, opposite parity; all p,q>=1 coprime including p<=q, with p=q classified as "universal":
   N=40 -> (1061, 331, 730, 662)
   N=55 -> (1968, 622, 1346, 1244)


None of these three interpretations match the documented (587,489,98,0) or (1087,939,148,0).
