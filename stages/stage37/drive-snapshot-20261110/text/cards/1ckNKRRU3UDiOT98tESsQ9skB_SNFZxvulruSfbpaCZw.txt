1. Scope


229AJ packetized only the odd inert p≡3 mod4 part of the edge contents a,b. 229AK then refined those inert packets by face/space signs, while 229AO extracted deterministic gap-size information without paying the eight-packet threshold loss.


The same sign geometry is in fact exact for every odd prime dividing a or b, not only inert primes. The missing input is the exact pairwise-content structure from 229D.


This card proves an all-odd gap-gcd square matrix for the edge contents a=gcd(e,y) and b=gcd(e,x). Its p≡3 mod4 restriction is exactly the retained 229AK packetization. Aggregating by the space-gap sign gives a four-packet receiver for rad_odd(ab), including split primes carried by a,b, and extends the 229AO cubic short-gap carrier from a_(3),b_(3) to the complete odd parts a_odd,b_odd.


This is a structural extension only. It does not count the packets and does not control the separate split-only contents c12,c21.

3. Exact full-odd a-side gap-gcd square matrix


For positive integers A,B write gcd_odd(A,B) for the largest odd divisor of gcd(A,B).


For sigma,tau in {-,+}, define alpha_{sigma,tau} by
  alpha_{sigma,tau}^2
    := gcd_odd(U+sigma x, R+tau x).              (229AP.3)


We prove that the right side is always a perfect square and that the four alpha packets partition a_odd exactly.


First let an odd prime p divide both U+sigma x and R+tau x. Then
  p | U^2-x^2 = e^2,
  p | R^2-x^2 = V^2.
Hence p|e and p|V, so by (229AP.2),
  p | gcd(e,V)=a.                                (229AP.4)


Conversely fix p^k || a, p odd. Since p|e and gcd(e,x)=b with gcd(a,b)=1,
  p does not divide x.
Then U^2=e^2+x^2 and R^2=V^2+x^2 show p does not divide U or R.


Thus exactly one of U-x,U+x is divisible by p, and exactly one of R-x,R+x is divisible by p. Let their signs be sigma(p),tau(p). Since the opposite factors are p-units,
  v_p(U+sigma(p)x)=v_p(e^2)=2v_p(e),
  v_p(R+tau(p)x)=v_p(V^2)=2v_p(V).


Using gcd(e,V)=a,
  min(v_p(e),v_p(V))=v_p(a)=k.
Therefore
  v_p(gcd_odd(U+sigma(p)x,R+tau(p)x))=2k.         (229AP.5)


For the other three sign pairs the p-adic valuation is zero.


Consequently every gcd in (229AP.3) is a square and
  a_odd = product_{sigma,tau} alpha_{sigma,tau}, (229AP.6)
with the four alpha_{sigma,tau} pairwise coprime.


RESULT_229AP_A_FULL_ODD_GAP_GCD_SQUARE_MATRIX=PROVED_EXACT.


4. Exact full-odd b-side gap-gcd square matrix


Define beta_{sigma,tau} by
  beta_{sigma,tau}^2
    := gcd_odd(V+sigma y, R+tau y).               (229AP.7)


The same argument uses
  V^2-y^2=e^2,
  R^2-y^2=U^2,
  gcd(e,U)=b,
  gcd(e,y)=a,
  gcd(a,b)=1.


For every odd p^k||b there is a unique sign pair (sigma(p),tau(p)) and
  v_p(gcd_odd(V+sigma(p)y,R+tau(p)y))=2k.
Hence
  b_odd = product_{sigma,tau} beta_{sigma,tau},  (229AP.8)
and the four beta packets are pairwise coprime.


Since gcd(a,b)=1, all eight alpha/beta packets are pairwise coprime.

6. Four aggregated full-odd edge packets


Aggregate by the space-gap sign:
  alpha_tau = alpha_{-,tau} alpha_{+,tau},
  beta_tau  = beta_{-,tau} beta_{+,tau}.          (229AP.9)


Then
  a_odd = alpha_- alpha_+,
  b_odd = beta_- beta_+,                          (229AP.10)
and alpha_-,alpha_+,beta_-,beta_+ are pairwise coprime.


There are also exact aggregated gcd identities:
  alpha_tau^2 = gcd_odd(e^2,R+tau x),             (229AP.11)
  beta_tau^2  = gcd_odd(e^2,R+tau y).             (229AP.12)


Proof of (229AP.11): any odd common prime of e and R+tau x also divides
  R^2-x^2=V^2,
so it lies in gcd(e,V)=a. The p-adic valuation is
  2 min(v_p(e),v_p(V)) = 2v_p(a),
and the unique tau sign partitions the odd primes of a. The b-side is identical using gcd(e,U)=b.


Set
  Abar_tau=rad(alpha_tau),
  Bbar_tau=rad(beta_tau).
Then
  rad_odd(ab)
    = Abar_- Abar_+ Bbar_- Bbar_+.                (229AP.13)


Hence for every T>=1,
  rad_odd(ab)>T
    =>
  max(Abar_-,Abar_+,Bbar_-,Bbar_+)>T^(1/4).       (229AP.14)

7. Full-odd extension of the 229AO short-gap carrier


From the definitions,
  alpha_-^2 | R-x,
  beta_-^2  | R-y.                                (229AP.15)


For the plus aggregates, alpha_{-,+}^2 divides U-x and alpha_{+,+}^2 divides both U+x and R+x. Their difference gives
  alpha_{+,+}^2 | R-U.
Since alpha_{-,+} and alpha_{+,+} are coprime,
  alpha_+^2 | (U-x)(R-U).                         (229AP.16)


Similarly
  beta_+^2 | (V-y)(R-V).                          (229AP.17)


Using a_odd=alpha_-alpha_+ and b_odd=beta_-beta_+,
  a_odd^2 | (R-x)(U-x)(R-U),                     (229AP.18)
  b_odd^2 | (R-y)(V-y)(R-V).                     (229AP.19)


Retain positivity x<U<R and y<V<R from 229AO. Since
  (U-x)+(R-U)=R-x,
  (V-y)+(R-V)=R-y,
AM-GM yields
  4 a_odd^2 <= (R-x)^3,                           (229AP.20)
  4 b_odd^2 <= (R-y)^3.                           (229AP.21)


In particular, for every M>0,
  Abar_->M => R-x>M^2,
  Abar_+>M => R-x>2M,
  Bbar_->M => R-y>M^2,
  Bbar_+>M => R-y>2M.                             (229AP.22)
