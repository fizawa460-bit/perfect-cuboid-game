From 229M's gap lemma,
  [Delta+] = [2^{g_+} b c12],
  g_+ = 1_{ga odd} + 1_{b odd} mod 2,

3. Corrected physical parity router


Write k=v2(the even member of {a,b}); whenever such an even member occurs, 229E gives k>=2 but does not fix k mod2.


G-EVEN:
  d0,c,f are odd; a,b have opposite parity.
If b is odd and a is even with v2(a)=k,
  tau_0=0, (tau_+,tau_-)=(1,k mod2).
If a is odd and b is even with v2(b)=k,
  tau_0=1, (tau_+,tau_-)=(k mod2,1).
Hence the canonical twist bit
  tau_D=tau_++tau_- = 1+k mod2.
So in G-EVEN the canonical twist is even iff k is even.


D-EVEN:
  a,b are odd, d0 is even.
Then
  (tau_+,tau_-)=(0,0),
  tau_D=0,
while
  tau_0 = 1_{c odd}+v2(d0) mod2.
Thus the canonical moving twist is odd in D-EVEN.


BOTH-ODD:
  d0 is odd; a,b have opposite parity.
If b is odd and a is even with v2(a)=k,
  tau_0=0, (tau_+,tau_-)=(1,1+k mod2).
If a is odd and b is even with v2(b)=k,
  tau_0=1, (tau_+,tau_-)=(1+k mod2,1).
Hence
  tau_D=k mod2.
So in BOTH-ODD the canonical twist is even iff k is odd.

  Delta+ = 2^{tau_+} S+ I+,
