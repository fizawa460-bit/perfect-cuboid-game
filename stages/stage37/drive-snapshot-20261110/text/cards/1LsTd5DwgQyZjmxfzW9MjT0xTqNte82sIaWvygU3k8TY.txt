2. Auxiliary thresholds are independent of the primary gate threshold


The previous cards proved uniform low-value bounds for every 1<=Z<=B:
  #{k13<=Z} << Z B^o(1)                         by 229BY,
  #{K_cross<=Z} << Z B^o(1)                     by 229CC,
  #{N_an<=Z} << (1+Z) B^o(1)                    by 229CW/229CX,
and the union of the three 229BL small-pair-product sectors at cutoff Z is
  << Z^(1+o(1)).


These are auxiliary fixed-fiber/scoped bounds. Their cutoff does not have to equal T0.


Hence for every fixed 0<epsilon<1/3, putting
  Z_epsilon:=B^(1/3-epsilon),
the union of all four low-auxiliary events above is
  O(B^(1/3-epsilon+o(1)))=o(B^(1/3)).


In particular, once the primary gate supplies a fixed delta>0, one may canonically choose
  epsilon=delta/2,
  Z:=B^(1/3-delta/2),
which is strictly larger than T0 for B>1.

This is a genuine source hardening and theorem-interface improvement, not a global count.

Likewise K_cross>Z is a strong one-scalar joint-support condition with B^o(1) fixed-k fibers, but fixed fibers plus a lower radical bound do not imply an occupied-tail estimate.

- treat K_cross as independent of its six factors;
- change the primary G0 threshold from B^(1/3-delta);
