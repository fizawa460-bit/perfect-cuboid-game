2. Fixed outer pair product has B^o physical fiber


Define the three labelled pair products
  Q_e := A Bv = (R-U)(R-V),
  Q_y := C Bv = (R-z)(R-V),
  Q_x := C A  = (R-z)(R-U).                       (229BV.3)


Each Q_j is a positive integer <B^2.


Fix n>=1 and first consider Q_e=n. The ordered positive factorization A Bv=n has at most tau(n) choices.


For each such factorization, (229BV.2) gives
  lambda_e | 2n.
Hence lambda_e has at most tau(2n) possible positive values.


For each triple (A,Bv,lambda_e), the retained 229BL reconstruction is injective:
  e=(2ABv/lambda_e-lambda_e)/2,
  U=e+Bv+lambda_e,
  V=e+A+lambda_e,
  R=e+A+Bv+lambda_e,
and then x,y,z are uniquely forced if integral and physical.
All selector, parity, primitivity and canonical-orientation conditions only remove candidates.


Therefore
  #{P:R<=B,Q_e(P)=n}
    <= tau(n) tau(2n)
    = B^o(1),                                      (229BV.4)
uniformly for n<=B^2.


The identical y- and x-chart arguments give
  #{P:R<=B,Q_y(P)=n} <= B^o(1),
  #{P:R<=B,Q_x(P)=n} <= B^o(1).                    (229BV.5)
