1. Exact PAIR target carried forward


PAIR-PREFLIGHT-001 proved that, on the exact occupied physical pair image,
    W_B = {(X,D): some exact M3 object at R<=B maps to (X,D)},
the fixed-pair multiplicity is uniformly B^o(1).


Writing
    w=D/X
and
    n_B(w)=#{(X,D) in W_B : D/X=w},
the endpoint pair count is exactly
    sum_{w in T_Pyth} n_B(w),
where
    T_Pyth={2 tau/(1-tau^2): tau in Q}
is the degree-two Pythagorean thin image.


The required PAIR closure theorem is therefore a source-weighted thin-image estimate:
    sum_{w in T_Pyth} n_B(w)
      <= B^(-delta+o(1)) sum_w n_B(w)             (229G.1)
for some fixed delta>0.


This is not an ambient rational-point count on P^1.
It is a statement about the pushforward of the exact M3 physical counting measure.


2. New theorem input: Corvaja–Zannier value sets


New current input not previously registered in Stage37:
P. Corvaja, U. Zannier, with appendix by D. Masser,
“Rational and integral values of rational functions at rational points”,
arXiv:2608.28255.


The paper studies value sets
    f(X(k)) subset P^1(k)
for rational maps from algebraic varieties, including height questions and common value sets.


Its strongest unconditional general value-set result highlighted in the introduction applies to semi-abelian varieties X_i and finitely generated subgroups Gamma_i subset X_i(k): the complement of a finite union of value sets f_i(Gamma_i) contains infinitely many rational integers. A quantitative height corollary counts many points in that ambient complement.


This theorem species is conceptually much closer to PAIR than ordinary ambient thin-set counting, because PAIR is itself a value-set/pushforward problem.


However it does not satisfy the Stage37 contract.


2.1 Source mismatch
The exact M3 source/pushforward producing n_B(w) has not been identified with a finitely generated subgroup of a semi-abelian variety, nor with a finite union of such sources.
Therefore the unconditional semi-abelian theorem does not apply to the realized M3 support.


2.2 Measure mismatch
Even if one proves that the complement
    P^1(Q) \ T_Pyth
contains many rational points of bounded ordinary height, that gives no bound on
    sum_{w in T_Pyth} n_B(w).


The Stage37 denominator is
    sum_w n_B(w),
not the number of ambient rational w of height <=T.


A weight n_B can, in principle, concentrate almost entirely on a proper or even very thin subset of ambient P^1(Q). Thus an ambient lower bound on the number of omitted values does not imply any fixed-power deficit in the pushed-forward M3 mass.


RESULT_229G_AMBIENT_VALUESET_COMPLEMENT_NOT_ENOUGH=PROVED_AS_MEASURE_FIREWALL.


2.3 Height mismatch
Corvaja–Zannier explicitly note that lower bounds relating h(f(x)) to h(x) become subtle for maps that are not finite degree.
PAIR requires precisely a quantitative relationship between physical source height R and the realized target distribution in w, with multiplicities n_B(w).


No such exact M3 height-pushforward theorem is supplied.


Therefore:
    CZ_VALUESET_REOPEN=false.


3. New theorem input: Kadets–Neftin Hilbert irreducibility for algebraic points


Second new current input:
B. Kadets, D. Neftin,
“Hilbert irreducibility for algebraic points”,
arXiv:2606.10584.


This work proves strong finiteness/irreducibility results for fibers of coverings of curves above algebraic points of bounded degree, under branch/monodromy hypotheses.


It does not match PAIR for three independent reasons:


(a) It is a theorem for finite covers of curves and low-degree algebraic points.
PAIR is a weighted image theorem for the exact M3 pushforward from a higher-dimensional arithmetic source.


(b) Its exceptional set analysis is about reducible fibers outside or around ordinary value sets.
PAIR asks for the total exact M3 weight landing inside one fixed degree-two value set T_Pyth.


(c) It is not a bounded-height fixed-power estimate for the arithmetic weights n_B(w).


Thus:
    KADETS_NEFTIN_PAIR_REOPEN=false.


The result is useful as theorem-species context only: strong qualitative/finiteness Hilbert irreducibility can coexist with no quantitative control of a source-induced height weight.

They prove rationality of universal torsors over smooth quartic del Pezzo surfaces when the torsor has a rational point.


This does not reopen 229F:

- the required theorem is a quantitative count for the simultaneous double-cover locus on X0 under exact height R, not rationality of a torsor over a smooth quartic del Pezzo surface.

5. Conditional higher-dimensional Hilbert irreducibility is also insufficient as stated


Bresciani’s higher-dimensional Hilbert irreducibility theorem, assuming weak Bombieri–Lang, concerns families with geometrically mordellic fibers.


This is structurally relevant to the old fixed-w genus-5 picture, but even granting the conjectural hypotheses it is not the Stage37 receiver:
PAIR needs a quantitative power deficit for the exact R<=B pushforward weight n_B(w), not merely qualitative non-surjectivity or generic irreducibility of fibers.


No conditional counting credit is assigned.


6. Exact logical firewall: qualitative thinness versus weighted thinness


The PAIR issue can now be stated abstractly.


Let nu_B be the finite measure on P^1(Q)
    nu_B({w})=n_B(w).


Then PAIR closure requires
    nu_B(T_Pyth) <= B^(-delta+o(1)) nu_B(P^1(Q)). (229G.2)


Statements of the form
    T_Pyth is thin,
or
    P^1(Q)\T_Pyth is infinite/Zariski dense/large in ordinary height,
do not imply (229G.2) without a theorem relating nu_B to ambient height/counting measure.


Indeed the support of nu_B is itself an arithmetic image and may be concentrated on T_Pyth.


RESULT_229G_QUALITATIVE_HILBERT_PROPERTY_INSUFFICIENT_FOR_PAIR=PROVED.


This is the precise reason why the newest value-set/Hilbert results do not close Stage37 despite being thematically close.


7. Sharpened theorem species: M3 weighted Hilbert property


PAIR-PREFLIGHT-001 asked for a weighted occupied-image theorem.
229G sharpens this into a canonical statement.


Define the exact-M3 weighted Hilbert property for the map
    w=D/X
to mean:


For every fixed degree>=2 rational cover
    phi:C -> P^1
in the specified admissible class, there exists delta(phi)>0 such that
    sum_{w in phi(C(Q))} n_B(w)
      <= B^(-delta(phi)+o(1)) sum_w n_B(w).       (229G.3)


For Stage37, only the single Pythagorean cover is needed:
    phi(tau)=2tau/(1-tau^2).


Thus a sufficient direct theorem is the one-cover specialization of (229G.3).


Name this missing theorem species:
    M3_WEIGHTED_HILBERT_PROPERTY.


This is strictly more tailored than generic ambient thin-set counting and strictly different from M3-LOCAL-GATE-001.
LOCAL remains one possible mechanism to prove it via residue contraction; a direct height/value-set theorem could prove it without LOCAL.


RESULT_229G_PAIR_REOPEN_CONTRACT=M3_WEIGHTED_HILBERT_PROPERTY.


8. Relation to 229F geometry


229F gives a different but compatible fixed-variety formulation:
the physical selector base is the fixed geometric-4A1 surface
    X0: x^2+y^2=z^2,
        z^2+e^2=R^2
with exact projective height R,
and perfect endpoints impose
    U^2=e^2+x^2,
    V^2=e^2+y^2.


The same conceptual firewall applies:
a qualitative Hilbert property for the finite cover of X0, or a proof that its image is thin, does not by itself give an absolute exponent below B^(1/3) or a relative o(M3) theorem.


A usable 229F theorem must be quantitative with the exact R-height or directly relative to the realized M3 measure.
