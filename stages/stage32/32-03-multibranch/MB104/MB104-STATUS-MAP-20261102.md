## 0. Setting and notation

MB104's task is a population-wide finite degree/intersection window (MISSION.json). The population (MB101) is:

- integral, non-exceptional curves C̄ on the box variety B;
- whose normalization Ē has genus g ≤ 1;
- with at least one node carrying two or more normalization branches.

Notation (same in E02–E04 and Drive F70–F82):

- S = the resolution, C̃ = the strict transform;
- d = H·C̃, the degree;
- M = Σ_p C̃·E_p, the total exceptional contact (written μ in E03);
- r = the number of branch points of the Beauville double cover over Ē (odd contacts);
- ε := M − d − 4(1 − g) ≥ 0 (F71 (4.1); the same inequality as E03's r ≥ d − 4g + 4, which is the r-form);
- k = |Stab_G(Z)|, where Z is the component of the preimage in C8×C8 and G ≅ (Z/2)³ is FSM's group.

The F71 identity is ε = (A₁ + A₂)/k + (M − B_o), where A_i are the ramification degrees of the two legs Z → C8 and B_o is the number of odd contacts. In particular, **ε = 0 ⇔ both legs étale and every contact equals 1** (F72 Lemma 3.1).

## 3. Where the remaining difficulty sits

- **The open core** is ε > 0 carriers whose co-image Y_s has genus ≥ 2 for every single involution s ∈ S, and which carry marked multi-branch excess (B_s > 0), outside the layers where F76–F80 give windows with explicit constants.
- **E01's method does not transfer directly.** The congruence argument needs both legs étale, because then K = Γ ∩ a⁻¹Γa. With a ramified leg, the uniformizing group of Z is not a subgroup of Γ, and one étale leg alone does not give a congruence subgroup.
- **Possible directions** (none started):
  1. F74's window is now closed by F83. What remains is F75 with B_s = 0 (d ≤ 92): F83 §5 reduces it to finitely many Hermitian numerical classes (for example 62,223 for h4, g1, r1, k8). Closing it needs geometric existence tests per class, or a structural argument.
  2. Make the F76/F77 constants numerical.
  3. Find a uniform bound on B_s (or on μ − d) for the open core.
