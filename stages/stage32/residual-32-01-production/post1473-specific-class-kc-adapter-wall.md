## 2026 Testa-Stoll source lock

Primary journal item:

- Michael Stoll and Damiano Testa, **Curves on the surface of cuboids**, *Mathematics of Computation*.
- DOI: `10.1090/mcom/4238`.
- Published electronically: `2026-08-10`.
- Accepted manuscript / author PDF used for theorem locators: `https://www.mathe2.uni-bayreuth.de/stoll/papers/Cuboidi.pdf` (the PDF itself retains the earlier internal title **The surface parametrizing cuboids**).
- Existing repository source lock: `stages/stage29/29-02a/source-lock.md`.

Relevant exact locators in the 2026 text:

1. **Section 6, K3 quotients.** The quotient obtained by changing the sign of the long diagonal coordinate `c` is denoted `K_c`; the induced map is `pi:S->K_c` and the involution is `sigma_c`. The map factors through the double quotient and then contracts a specified set `E_pi` of exceptional curves.
2. **Lemma 11.** For every `C in Pic(S)`,

   `pi^* pi_* C = C + sigma_c(C) + sum_{E in E_pi} (C.E) E`.

   Therefore a Testa-Stoll-style `K_c` computation for an arbitrary retained Picard class requires the exact `sigma_c` action and the exact contracted exceptional set in the same Picard marking; a generic group-average quotient is not a substitute.
3. **Lemma 12.** `Pic(K_c)` is generated by the images of the known curves used there; in particular the paper has an explicit `Pic(K_c)` lattice description. To use it on the Stage32 class, its pullback/image must be represented in the retained Stage32 `Pic(S)` basis.
4. **Theorem 17 / Corollary 18.** The paper classifies integral curves only through canonical degree `6`. The present class has `K.C=186`, so that classification does not directly exclude or construct it.
5. **Question 19.** The paper explicitly asks whether all curves of geometric genus at most `1` on `S` are contained in the known collection `G`. Thus the general low-genus classification remains open; there is no source-supported promotion from V8 effectivity to an integral irreducible normalization-genus-one carrier.
