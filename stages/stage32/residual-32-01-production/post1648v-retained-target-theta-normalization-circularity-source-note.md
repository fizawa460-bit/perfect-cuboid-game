## KRR Section 4

KRR fix the Abel–Jacobi embedding `alpha: theta -> J(theta)=A` by sending the point at infinity of `y^2=x^5-x` to the origin. They also describe the torsion points on this embedded theta curve: 16 points of order 6, five nonzero points of order 2, and the origin; the six 2-torsion points are the Weierstrass points.

However, the group preserving this embedded curve is denoted `H48`, whereas the retained Deraux target group is `G48`. Corollary 6 only gives an automorphism `g` of `A` such that

`H48 = g G48 g^-1`.

KRR then explicitly say that one may change the embedding `theta -> A` by composing it with this `g` and *then* identify `H48` with `G48`.

Therefore the theta divisor is not already expressed in the retained `G48` coordinates independently of `g`. Using the KRR theta embedding to select the retained W-line requires transporting it through exactly the automorphism whose marked action Stage32 is missing. This is circular as an absolute-marking selector unless an explicit matrix/action of `g` (or equivalent target characteristic) is supplied.
