## External source

Enric Florit and Benjamin Smith, *An atlas of the Richelot isogeny graph*, arXiv:2101.00917v2.

Exact locators used:

- §2.1: a `(2,2)`-isogeny from a genus-2 Jacobian has kernel a maximal 2-Weil-isotropic subgroup `K subset J(C)[2]`; the dual satisfies `phi^dagger phi=[2]`.
- §4.14, Type-VI vertex: the Type-VI curve is isomorphic to Bolza's normal form `y^2=x(x^4+1)`; its reduced automorphism group is generated by `sigma,rho,omega` and is isomorphic to `S4`.
- §4.14/Table 12/Figure 16: the Type-Sigma neighbour is `[E^2]`, where `E` has `j=8000`; the corresponding kernel orbit is `{K1,K4,K8,K9,K12,K13}` and has weight/orbit size 6.

The displayed kernel-index actions in §4.14 are

- `sigma=(8 9)(10 11)(12 13)(14 15)`,
- `rho=(1 9 8)(2 15 14)(4 12 13)(6 10 11)`,
- `omega=(1 4)(2 14 7 15)(3 10 6 11)(8 9 13 12)`.

The orbit of `K1` under these generators is exactly `{K1,K4,K8,K9,K12,K13}`.

## Why this does not materialize the missing g

The current Stage32 target needs an actual marked automorphism/isomorphism `g` on the retained Bolza torus/lattice, or at least an invertible action on `A[2]`, sufficient to transport the source-bound `delta_0inf` into one absolute retained W-line.

The Florit–Smith bridge is a different object:

1. it is a degree-4 `(2,2)` Richelot isogeny `J(Bolza) -> E^2`, not an automorphism/isomorphism of the same abelian surface;
2. its kernel is a nonzero subgroup `K subset J(Bolza)[2]` of order 4, so its restriction on 2-torsion is not injective and therefore cannot be inverted to recover a marked `J[2]` identification;
3. the relevant `E^2` edge is not singled out by one canonical kernel: six kernels form one reduced-automorphism orbit;
4. the retained Stage32 principal polarization is explicitly non-product, whereas the Type-Sigma vertex is the elliptic-square/product-PPAS vertex in the Richelot graph. Equality of the underlying elliptic square species does not supply the missing retained ppav marking.

Thus the Florit–Smith atlas is useful evidence for the rational/isogeny-level representation of Bolza automorphisms on an elliptic square, but it is not the source-bound integral marked ppav conjugator `g` required by Stage32.

This is only a bounded closure of the specific `Florit–Smith Richelot E^2 representation -> missing marked g` route. It does not claim that no other external source contains an actual integral marked ppav isomorphism/conjugating matrix, and it does not close the separate common branch-labelled geometric-anchor route.
