### Coordinate sign quotients

Projecting away from one of the seven box coordinates is the quotient by the involution changing the sign of that coordinate.  The resulting minimal desingularization is a K3 surface (up to the isomorphisms among the seven displayed quotient models).

For the quotient associated to a coordinate sign involution `sigma`, write

```
pi:S -> K.
```

The paper records, for `C in Pic(S)`,

```
pi^* pi_* C
 = C + sigma(C)
   + sum_(E in E_pi) (C.E) E,                   (K3-PUSH)
```

where `E_pi` is the set of exceptional curves contracted in passing to the K3 resolution.

### K3 Picard and degree parity

For the displayed quotient `K_c`, the Picard group has rank 20 and is generated by the images of the known curves.  Every class has even intersection with the hyperplane class.  In particular there are no odd-degree curves on the singular K3 model.

The same section records 15 elliptic fibrations and an explicit low-degree curve classification.

## W4 scope

The wide scan uses only:

1. the push-pull identity (K3-PUSH);
2. that the quotient is generically degree two;
3. pullback of the K3 hyperplane is the box hyperplane class;
4. the even-degree parity test.

The low-degree classification is **not** extrapolated to the active degree `112l`.
