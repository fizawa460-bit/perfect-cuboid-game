```

deg L=H.C=112l,
dim V=7.
```

```

```

seven-dimensional subsystem, not an l-growing contradiction.

No source located in the shallow syzygy scan converts this fixed quadratic packet into an upper
bound on l.

  controlled orbifold local corrections.

Thus the theorem species is excellent but the exact non-lc adapter is missing.
