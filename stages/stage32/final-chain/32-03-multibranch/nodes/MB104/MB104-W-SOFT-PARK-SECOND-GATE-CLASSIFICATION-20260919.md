# MB104 W-soft second-gate classification — 2026-09-19

Status: **PARALLEL PORTFOLIO RECLASSIFICATION / NO CREDIT**

## Purpose

The earlier W1--W31 audit correctly separated tested-architecture HARD from broader-direction
SOFT, but the 26 SOFT directions are not equally useful. This checkpoint asks one additional
question for every SOFT direction:

> Is there a concrete, source-complete **next lemma** which would materially change the MB104
> frontier without rebuilding the failed architecture?

Classification:

- **HOT** — one specific next lemma/test exists and can plausibly change closure pressure.
- **WARM** — still meaningful, but requires one nontrivial adapter/theorem before a useful exact test.
- **COLD** — the broader words remain mathematically open, but every currently identifiable next
  implementation either reproduces the failed architecture or requires a theorem so strong that it
  should be treated as a new route rather than a continuation.

This is research routing only.

## HOT

### W4 — K3 full lattice / nef-effective cone

Second gate:
compute the exact pushdown ray against a source-complete basis of the rank-20 K3 Neron--Severi
lattice and determine whether it lies outside the effective cone / inside a chamber with a forced
(-2)-component.

Why still live:
the previous test used parity, Hodge, and sampled fibrations only. Those do not decide the full
wall-and-chamber structure.

Promotion condition:
an exact negative pairing with a proved effective (-2)-class, or a chamber theorem forcing a fixed
component uniformly in l.

Classification: **HOT**.

### W5 — adaptive higher symmetric differentials

Second gate:
use the published BTVA graded symmetric-differential machinery to determine whether order m may
grow with l and whether the extension-condition deficit has a positive primitive cubic Hilbert
coefficient after support-hyperplane-generated forms are removed.

Why still live:
fixed order and fixed jet depth are hard-dead, but the obstruction target itself is asymptotic in m.

Promotion condition:
a source-locked general-m module or Hilbert-series certificate with positive primitive coefficient.

Classification: **HOT**.

### W6 — coupled two-factor modular relation

Second gate:
seek an identity/divisor theorem depending simultaneously on both factor maps rather than the
already-saturated one-factor valence counts. The exact input must be the common-cover / common-line
data, not independent scalar modular forms.

Why still live:
factorwise valence is saturated, but P6H-type two-factor coupling is genuinely stronger data.

Promotion condition:
one coupled divisor/square-class relation whose degree or zero divisor cannot scale with l.

Classification: **HOT**.

### W13 — complete effective cone / stable base locus

Second gate:
upgrade the finite known-curve test to a source-complete effective/nef-cone statement for the
hostile ray. The existing 92-curve library and known zero quartics are insufficient but already
provide the finite seed.

Promotion condition:
prove every effective member of lD has a fixed component, or place D outside the movable cone.

Classification: **HOT**.

### W16 — nontrivial canonical Cayley--Bacharach subcluster

Second gate:
construct, from the conductor/adjoint scheme rather than an arbitrary subset, a canonical proper
CB subscheme Z' with c2 small enough that the Serre bundle has positive Bogomolov discriminant and
a source-complete reverse adapter.

Why still live:
the full 112l cluster gives a tautological split bundle; the numerical window for a substantially
smaller canonical cluster remains strong.

Promotion condition:
an intrinsic Z' whose length grows slowly enough and whose CB property is theorem-backed.

Classification: **HOT**.

### W18 — cuboid-specific secant defect

Second gate:
replace generic secant dimension by the actual seven-dimensional canonical coordinate subspace and
test whether the four quadrics force the image of the elliptic normalization into a special
secant-defective configuration.

Promotion condition:
a source-complete rank/catalecticant condition with deficit growing with l.

Classification: **HOT**.

### W26 — cuboid-specific equisingular regularity

Second gate:
derive the zero-dimensional singularity scheme actually imposed by the balanced packet and test a
high-rank-Picard Bogomolov/vanishing criterion directly, rather than importing rank-one NS theorems.

Why still live:
this is the route most directly aimed at the exact 112l superabundance wall.

Promotion condition:
a theorem/derivation controlling H1(I_Z(D_l)) uniformly with coefficient strictly below the retained
112l superabundance.

Classification: **HOT**.

## WARM

### W1 — smaller Reider/Bogomolov package

Next lemma:
identify a canonical smaller CB cluster. Without that, W1 collapses into W16.

Reason for WARM rather than HOT:
its only non-tautological continuation is essentially the W16 construction problem.

### W3 — invariant-ring obstruction

Next lemma:
compute the relevant Aut(S)-isotypic piece of the section ring along lD and show the representation
required by a carrier is absent.

Reason:
plausible finite-group algebra, but no exact carrier-to-required-isotypic-component adapter is
currently retained.

### W7 — singular abelian-cover BMY

Next lemma:
source-lock local cover correction terms from the actual singularity packet.

Reason:
without exact analytic singularity types it repeats P6M/H8; with them it could alter the quadratic
coefficient.

### W11 — special seven-section relation on E

Next lemma:
derive a cuboid-specific algebraic relation among the seven canonical sections on the elliptic
normalization stronger than elliptic RR/Abel counting.

Reason:
the bare linear series is roomy; only the four-quadric equations can make this nontrivial.

### W17 — support-stabilizer dichotomy

Next lemma:
in the invariant case obtain a strict quotient obstruction; in the non-invariant case obtain a
uniform translate-intersection lower bound.

Reason:
the existing numerical dichotomy is compatible. A useful second gate must strengthen one branch,
not split it again.

### W19 — global high-contact forcing

Next lemma:
prove the packet forces high-order ramification/contact in at least one global differential/map.

Reason:
fixed-jet and naive Wronskian charging are dead; a truly global forcing theorem could revive it.

### W20 — cuboid-specific residue/moment identity

Next lemma:
derive an exact global linear relation among principal parts at distinct box nodes from the cuboid
equations or modular structure.

Reason:
ordinary Abel/residue identities are tautological, but a nonstandard global moment relation would
be new information.

### W21 — lower-rank polar degeneracy

Next lemma:
construct a rank-two (or determinantal) polar object whose top degeneracy class counts the relevant
singularities and has coefficient below the packet defect.

Reason:
the rank-three J1 bundle architecture is dead, but a lower-rank cuboid-specific map is concrete
enough to test if found.

### W22 — reduced/cosection stable-map obstruction

Next lemma:
find a canonical cosection/reduced obstruction theory whose vanishing locus excludes the special
elliptic maps.

Reason:
negative expected dimension alone is useless, but a geometric cosection would be decisive.

### W23 — special bidegree-image singularity theorem

Next lemma:
use the fixed branch/grid structure of the image in P1xP1, not Castelnuovo--Severi genus alone, to
force more singularity than a bidegree (n,n) curve can support.

Reason:
the generic inequality is wrong-direction; only packet-specific image geometry remains.

### W24 — distributional conductor identity

Next lemma:
refine total conductor degree into typed/local contributions fixed by the cuboid packet.

Reason:
total degree is exactly adjunction; only a nontrivial distribution theorem can add content.

### W25 — monodromy/slope refinement of pencil

Next lemma:
show any pencil containing such a carrier contributes an anomalously large local signature/slope
term exceeding the global pencil budget.

Reason:
total critical-count capacity is huge, but refined slope contributions may be sharper.

### W27 — reduced semiregularity/cosection

Next lemma:
replace the impossible injective H1(N)->H2(O) map by a packet-adapted reduced obstruction map.

Reason:
classical semiregularity is dimensionally dead; a reduced theory is possible but not retained.

### W28 — multiplier-ideal interpolation

Next lemma:
use multiplier ideals for vanishing/interpolation rather than non-klt connectedness.

Reason:
the sign needed for connectedness is fundamentally wrong; only a different multiplier-ideal
architecture survives.

### W29 — stability forbidding the degree-zero quotient

Next lemma:
prove a stability statement specifically for f^*Omega_S along these special elliptic maps strong
enough to rule out the quotient Omega_E of degree zero.

Reason:
generic nefness is compatible; strict stability would be new and nontrivial.

### W30 — special Gaussian relation

Next lemma:
derive a four-quadric-specific Gaussian/Wahl identity, not a dimension estimate.

Reason:
dimension counts are hopeless, but an exact identity could constrain the seven canonical sections.

### W31 — non-tautological coherent-system / LM construction

Next lemma:
choose a linear subsystem/divisor pair for which the obvious O(D-H) destabilizing subbundle is
absent and recompute discriminant/stability.

Reason:
the first LM bundle was tautologically unstable; a genuinely different coherent system could still
carry geometry.

## COLD

### W9 — finite-characteristic specialization

Why cold:
to become reversible it needs a compactification preserving integrality, normalization genus and the
full packet. Constructing that is essentially a new foundational route, not one more step of W9.

### W12 — receiver-preserving degeneration

Why cold:
same structural issue as W9. Any valid next step is the missing specialization theorem itself;
without it, special-fibre computations have no reverse adapter.

### W32? none

No new W label is introduced. Variants stay attached to their parent route.
