## W5 — adaptive higher symmetric differentials

Previous hard failure:
fixed order and every fixed finite jet depth saturate.  Order two gives a rank-three A1
principal-part image and cannot count `8l` repeated minimal branches.

New input:

```text
V_m(T)=ker(W_m -> direct_sum_(s in T) Q_(s,m))
```

and asks for a primitive Hilbert coefficient after quotienting the support-hyperplane-generated
submodule.  Z32S additionally source-locks the published BTVA perfect-cuboid ancillary
`perfectcuboid_script.m` and the graded symmetric-differential construction.

This is not another vague "higher differential" idea: the missing object is explicit.

Third gate:
recover the ancillary script and determine whether symmetric order is a genuine input to the
module construction.  If yes, extract dimensions/ranks at several m only to identify the eventual
Hilbert/quasi-Hilbert polynomial; fixed-m positivity is not closure evidence.

Stop:
if the script is hard-coded to one/two orders or if local-extension matrices cannot be generated
uniformly without rebuilding the BTVA machinery.

Disposition: **DEEP-CANDIDATE #1**.

Reason:
it directly matches a pre-existing Z32R reopen trigger and has a concrete executable source.

## W26 — cuboid-specific equisingular regularity

Previous appeal:
a high-rank-Picard T-smoothness/vanishing theorem might control the `112l` superabundance.

New current result:
P6F invokes Dedieu--Sernesi and proves, for any integral genus-one carrier with `K.C=112l>0`,

```text
H0(C, A tensor O_C(C)) = 0,
```

hence the reduced equigeneric tangent cone is zero and the equisingular tangent space is zero.

This argument depends on genus one and positive canonical degree, not on the P6 full-deck packet.
It therefore changes the interpretation of W26 for the Z balanced genus-one ray as well:
a hypothetical carrier is already locally isolated.

A continuation based merely on proving "expected regularity" or a positive-dimensional Severi
family is the wrong target.

The only useful W26 continuation would be substantially stronger:

```text
prove a vanishing/T-smoothness theorem whose expected dimension is negative,
so existence itself contradicts regularity.
```

That requires controlling the exact zero-dimensional singularity scheme, not only the tangent
dimension already computed.

Disposition: **CONDITIONAL-HIGH**, not immediate deep work.

Revisit trigger:
a source-complete high-rank-Picard vanishing theorem applicable to the exact packet scheme.

## W6 — coupled two-factor modular relation

P6H has now executed the strongest obvious two-factor character construction:
both factor maps lie in the same line bundle and their three typewise ramification divisors agree
in Pic(E).

P6K then tests the natural basis-character Kummer square classes and proves that

```text
(q_j o psi_1)/(q_j o psi_2) is a square
```

adds no invariant; it is exactly the function-field formulation of the same common-G-cover data.

Thus the most natural "coupled modular relation" has already been absorbed and is tautological.

A viable W6 continuation needs information not contained in the common labelled G-cover itself:
for example an explicit algebraic relation among the **two pencils** inside the common line bundle,
or a modular correspondence cutting a proper subvariety of the pencil-pair space.

Disposition: **DEMOTE to CONDITIONAL**.

Do not deepen by multiplying Kummer equations, recomputing character lines, or one-factor valence.
