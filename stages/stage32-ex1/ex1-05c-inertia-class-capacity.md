## Source-side ordering and the three inertia classes

Stoll–Testa verification code `Cuboids/cuboids.magma` constructs the known curves as `Cs := C1s cat C2s cat C3s`, then appends the 48 singular points `pts` as the exceptional-divisor part of the pairing matrix. Hence the last 48 entries of the retained Stage32 `all140_pairings` use exactly the source-side `pts[1]..pts[48]` exceptional sequence.

The same source lists the 12 `C2` genus-one curves in three consecutive four-curve groups:

- indices 33..36: `b1=0`;
- indices 37..40: `b2=0`;
- indices 41..44: `b3=0`.

In the Stoll–Testa product model the diagonal `G0=(Z/2)^3` invariants satisfy

`U=u1*u2=2b1`, `V=v1*v2=2b2`, `W=w1*w2=2b3`.

Thus the three 16-node single-sign inertia classes are exactly

- u-sign: `b1=0`;
- v-sign: `b2=0`;
- w-sign: `b3=0`.

## Class-mass adapter without guessing the 48-point order

On the minimal resolution, the pullback of a canonical hyperplane `bj=0` is the sum of the four strict-transform `C2` components in that group and the 16 exceptional curves over the corresponding node class. Pairing this divisor identity with V6 gives

`sum_{nodes bj=0} V6.E = K.V6 - sum_{four C2 components} V6.C2`.

The retained V6 all140 pairings give

- `b1=0`: C2 pairings `[11,26,31,22]`, sum 90, hence inertia mass `186-90=96`;
- `b2=0`: `[16,26,25,11]`, sum 78, hence inertia mass `108`;
- `b3=0`: `[28,40,34,22]`, sum 124, hence inertia mass `62`.

Replay: `96+108+62=266`, the exact total exceptional contact mass.

This aggregate computation does not require any guess about the internal ordering of `pts[1]..pts[48]`.

## h=2 capacity consequence

EX1-05B showed that in the `h=2` component-stabilizer case the full stabilizer contains exactly two of the three single-sign inertia types. The omitted type cannot ramify. Also `Q=deg Ram(D/N)>=188`.

Therefore the two allowed inertia classes must carry total V6 contact mass at least 188. The three possibilities are:

- omit u: allowed mass `108+62=170`, impossible;
- omit v: allowed mass `96+62=158`, impossible;
- omit w: allowed mass `96+108=204`, not excluded by mass alone.

So two of the three `h=2` subgroup choices are excluded. If `h=2` survives at all, its full stabilizer is uniquely the subgroup generated by the u-sign and v-sign involutions; equivalently the w-sign / `b3=0` 16-node class is the forbidden inertia class.

## Reduced parity gate

Because ramification is forbidden on the w-sign class, EX1-05B parity gives `q_i=0` on each of its 16 nodes. Since `q_i congruent m_i (mod 2)`, **all sixteen V6 exceptional pairings on `b3=0` must be even**.

The full 48-label inertia table is therefore not needed for the next decision. It suffices to identify the 16 source-side singular points lying on `b3=0`, or equivalently to certify the parity of V6 against those 16 exceptional curves.

If any one of those 16 pairings is odd, `h=2` is excluded and `h=4` is forced at the component-stabilizer layer.

If all are even, `h=2` remains only capacity-compatible, not proved realizable. Then

`188 <= Q <= 204`, `Q=188+2t`, `0<=t<=8`.

The forbidden w-class mass 62 contributes exactly 31 units to the half-contact-excess. On the allowed u/v classes,

`(204-Q)/2 = 8-t`.

Hence at most `8-t` ramified allowed branches can have contact order at least 3, and at least `180+3t` ramified allowed branches are simple transverse contacts of order 1.
