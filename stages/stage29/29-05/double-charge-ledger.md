## C. Dependent but genuinely distinct arithmetic attacks

- **Campedelli:** quotient of F7, retained because Q-point pushforward to a Q-defined quotient can obstruct endpoint Q-points. Dependence on F7 does not make the arithmetic obstruction identical.
- **Beauville:** retained because the audited map direction is `X_cub -> S_endpoint`; it is a cover, not an F7 quotient. Albanese/twist descent is distinct arithmetic.
- **Modular:** retained because the generic birational forgetful quotient and conjugate-self level structure produce a separate descent problem. Ordinary unrestricted 8-congruence is already RED; the twisted route is not.
- **Brauer:** retained as obstruction technology. A Brauer class is not automatically implied by any quotient map and vice versa.

Route ownership is execution bookkeeping, not a theorem of independence or equivalence. Assigning an amber receiver to an umbrella route does not prove that the receiver is redundant, non-independent, or already solved.
