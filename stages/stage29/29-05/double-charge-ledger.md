## B. Same object / named adapter, not a second route

- F7 seven-line sign/Kummer cover and the standard non-Fano/Hirzebruch `N=2` cover are the same geometric cover over `Q(i)` after the audited projective transformation and scalar twist data. The latter imports a classical theorem ecosystem; it does not double the number of endpoint foundations.
- Horie-Yamauchi's `3*h16+h32+3*h8` decomposition and the seven K3 sign quotients are compatible cohomological/geometric descriptions. A new arithmetic theorem on a piece may be credited, but merely recovering the same decomposition twice is not two attacks.

## C. Dependent but genuinely distinct arithmetic attacks

- **Campedelli:** quotient of F7, retained because Q-point pushforward to a Q-defined quotient can obstruct endpoint Q-points. Dependence on F7 does not make the arithmetic obstruction identical.
- **Beauville:** retained because the audited map direction is `X_cub -> S_endpoint`; it is a cover, not an F7 quotient. Albanese/twist descent is distinct arithmetic.
- **Modular:** retained because the generic birational forgetful quotient and conjugate-self level structure produce a separate descent problem. Ordinary unrestricted 8-congruence is already RED; the twisted route is not.
- **Brauer:** retained as obstruction technology. A Brauer class is not automatically implied by any quotient map and vice versa.

## D. Joint V4 dependency

`X_face`, `X_sp`, and `X_cross` are the three quadratic characters of one V4 cover. Their point counts satisfy the audited exact V4 identity. This decomposition is structural; it does not certify three independent rarity probabilities.

The cross non-Tate decomposition and L-function pieces may guide an attack, but `R29-L2-ALG` and `R29-L2-BAD` have a single primary owner `J12-JOINT-V4`.

## E. Population ratios

Literal subset ratios may be used as conditional survival quantities only for the exact host/predicate pair proved in 29-04. Disjoint exact-stratum ratios such as `M2/M1`, `M3/M2`, and `M3/N2` remain population-size comparisons, not probabilities to multiply.

## F. Parametrized families

A parametrized family can provide:

- a construction lower bound;
- a closed curve/slice;
- a fibration chart;
- a local test case.

It cannot provide global endpoint coverage without a proved dominance/coverage adapter. A2 and Saunderson closures therefore cannot be stacked as global obstructions. `R29-PESCH1` remains unclassified as adapter versus independent foundation until 29-08 proves or fails the exact crosswalk.
