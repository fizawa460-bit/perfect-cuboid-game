## Arrangement side

For a projective line arrangement `A` with `n` lines, the projective complement has `H1 ~= Z^(n-1)`. The level-`N` congruence cover is induced by reduction

```text
H1 -> H1/NH1 ~= (Z/N)^(n-1).
```

Its normal compactification over `P2` is the branched Kummer/congruence cover `Xbar_N(A)`; its minimal desingularization is Hirzebruch's `M_N(A)`.

For the non-Fano arrangement `NF`, `n=7`; at `N=2` the deck group is `(Z/2)^6`.

## Equality of the function fields

Let the seven non-Fano branch forms be `L_1,...,L_7`. The projective relation among their square-root sign characters removes the simultaneous global sign, so the Kummer extension is generated by six independent ratios, e.g.

```text
Q(P2)(sqrt(L1/L7),...,sqrt(L6/L7)).
```

After the explicit `PGL3(Q)` base change, this is exactly the Stage29-02ha endpoint function field

```text
Q(P2)(sqrt(x/(x+y+z)),
       sqrt(y/(x+y+z)),
       sqrt(z/(x+y+z)),
       sqrt((x+y)/(x+y+z)),
       sqrt((x+z)/(x+y+z)),
       sqrt((y+z)/(x+y+z))).
```

Therefore the two normal covers are the same normalization of `P2` in the same Galois extension.

## Singularities and resolution

For a triple point (`r=3`) in a seven-line `N=2` cover, the general congruence-cover fiber size is

```text
2^(7-1-3)=8.
```

The local cuboid model audited in 29-02ha is `A1`. The six triple points therefore yield all 48 `A1` points. Ordinary double intersections introduce ramification crossings but no additional normal-surface singularities requiring the cuboid 48-node resolution.

Consequently the unique minimal resolutions agree.
