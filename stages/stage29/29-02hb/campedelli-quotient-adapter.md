# Audited exact Campedelli quotient adapter

## 1. Canonical model, not the resolution, is the finite sign cover

Keep the two surfaces separate:

```text
Sbar = singular canonical cuboid surface
S    = minimal resolution of Sbar
```

The finite degree-64 sign/Kummer morphism is

```text
pi:Sbar -> P2.
```

The composite `S -> Sbar -> P2` is only generically finite because the 48 exceptional curves are contracted. No argument below silently replaces one map by the other.

Write the seven branch inertia generators as `e_A1,...,e_C`. The projective common sign is trivial on `Sbar`, so

```text
Gamma = F2^7 / <1^7>, dim Gamma = 6.
```

The seven inertia classes have the unique relation

```text
e_A1+e_A2+e_A3+e_B3+e_B2+e_B1+e_C = 0.
```

## 2. The Campedelli cover is a quotient of the same global map

Choose a surjection

```text
q:Gamma -> G=(Z/2)^3
```

such that the seven inertia images

```text
g_L=q(e_L)
```

are exactly the seven nonzero elements of `G`, and put `H=ker(q)`.

Define the quotient canonical model literally by

```text
Cbar_H := Sbar/H.
```

Because `Sbar` is normal and `H` is finite, `Cbar_H` is normal. The original finite map factors globally as

```text
Sbar --/H--> Cbar_H --beta_H--> P2,
```

with `deg(beta_H)=8` and deck group `Gamma/H=G`. Its branch inertia along the **same seven lines** is exactly `q(e_L)=g_L`.

Thus the Campedelli branch cover is not a second cover inferred from a similar function field: it is the normal quotient of the audited endpoint cover and the factor `beta_H` of the same morphism `pi`. Over `Qbar` this is exactly the `(Z/2)^3` seven-line Galois cover appearing in the Campedelli literature.

## 3. Local stabilizers and freeness on the canonical model

The seven-line arrangement has six triple points and three remaining double points. At a branch stratum supported on lines `L_1,...,L_r`, the stabilizer in `Gamma` is generated by their inertia classes.

For an admissible labeling:

- on one line, `g_L != 0`;
- at a double point, the two distinct nonzero labels are independent over `F2`;
- at a triple point, the three distinct labels are independent exactly because their sum is nonzero.

Hence

```text
H ∩ I_x = 0
```

for every stabilizer `I_x` occurring anywhere on `Sbar`. Away from the branch divisor the full sign action is already free. Therefore the `H`-action on the entire singular canonical model `Sbar` is free.

Since `H` is a constant finite etale group in characteristic zero,

```text
Sbar -> Cbar_H
```

is a finite etale `H`-torsor, even at the rational-double-point locus.

At each base triple point `Cbar_H` has one `A1` singularity: the local independent three-root model is

```text
u^2=r,
v^2=s,
w^2=r+s,
```

hence `w^2=u^2+v^2`. There are exactly six such `A1` points. Double intersections give smooth local covers.

This agrees directly with the Campedelli source calculation for triple branch points.

## 4. Resolution does not break the etale quotient

Let

```text
rho:S -> Sbar
rho_H:C_H -> Cbar_H
```

be the minimal resolutions. Rational double points have unique minimal resolutions, and minimal resolution commutes with etale base change here: pulling `rho_H` back along the finite etale map `Sbar -> Cbar_H` gives the minimal resolution of the disjoint etale-local copies of the `A1` singularities of `Sbar`.

Consequently the resolved diagram is cartesian and the top horizontal map is genuinely finite etale:

```text
S  --etale degree 8-->  C_H
|                       |
v                       v
Sbar ----etale------->  Cbar_H.
```

Equivalently, the eight `A1` points over each base triple point form a free transitive `H`-orbit; after resolution their eight exceptional curves are permuted freely and descend to the single exceptional `(-2)` curve of `C_H`.

## 5. Campedelli invariants: independent global cross-check

The quotient branch data satisfy the Campedelli arrangement condition: seven distinct lines, no four concurrent, and no dependent triple. Khar'lamov--Kulikov therefore identify `Cbar_H,Qbar` as the canonical model of a Campedelli surface.

The invariants can also be checked directly. For every nontrivial character of `G`, exactly four of the seven nonzero inertia labels pair nontrivially with it, so the abelian-cover building line bundle is `O_P2(2)`. Hence

```text
(beta_H)_* O_Cbar = O_P2 + O_P2(-2)^7.
```

This gives

```text
q(C_H)=0,
pg(C_H)=0,
chi(O_C_H)=1.
```

The finite-cover canonical formula gives

```text
K_Cbar = beta_H^*(K_P2 + D/2),
D ~ 7H,
K_C_H^2 = 8*(1/2)^2 = 2,
```

and the six `A1` resolutions are crepant.

Mendes Lopes--Pardini--Reid then apply to the degree-8 etale cover `S -> C_H`: it is the universal cover after base change to `C`, and

```text
pi1(C_H(C)) ~= H ~= (Z/2)^3.
```

This is a geometric/topological statement; it is not an arithmetic descent statement over `Q`.

## 6. Q-form scope

Every coordinate-sign automorphism and every enumerated subgroup `H` is defined over `Q`, so both `Cbar_H=Sbar/H` and the resolved quotient `C_H=S/H` are Q-defined.

What the literature identifies directly is the geometric Campedelli class after algebraic closure. The particular Q-form inherited from the cuboid quotient may be a nontrivial arithmetic form/twist of another displayed Campedelli model. No arithmetic theorem about a chosen standard Q-model may be transferred without an explicit Q-form adapter.

On the physical endpoint open, all seven branch forms are nonzero, so the quotient map is etale there. A rational perfect-cuboid point therefore gives a rational point of the smooth quotient:

```text
U_endpoint(Q) -> C_H(Q).
```

Thus

```text
C_H(Q)=empty  =>  U_endpoint(Q)=empty
```

for any one audited kernel. No converse is asserted.
