Stage29 is not a new population stage. It first asks whether Stage16–28 missed any materially different foundation or coordinate system that could move the project closer to the perfect-cuboid endpoint. Only after that screening does it synthesize the existing population/interaction results and select the best direct endpoint attack.

The stage uses ordinary incremental numbering. If a genuinely new branch appears, use suffixes such as `29-02a`, `29-07b`; do not force the work into the old 10/20/30/40/50/60/70 template.

## Core policy

Stage29 must distinguish two very different situations:

1. **old frozen gap** — a receiver already explored and closed because the required theorem/weapon was missing;
2. **new foundation** — a genuinely different global model, coordinate system, joint object, coverage viewpoint, or local-global invariant that was not the main object of the earlier stages.

Old frozen gaps are not automatically reopened. A backflow to Stage16–28 is justified only when Stage29 finds a genuinely new lens that changes the receiver, supplies a new adapter, or creates a materially new route.

```text
REPLAY_OLD_FROZEN_GATE_WITHOUT_NEW_INPUT=false
NEW_LENS_CAN_TRIGGER_BACKFLOW=true
BACKFLOW_ONLY_TO_RELEVANT_STAGE=true
SEQUENTIAL_STAGE16_TO_STAGE28_RERUN=false
```

Repository-wide Arsenal / StructureRadar / prior-stage reuse remains a standing preflight throughout Stage29, not a one-time late step.

## New-foundation lenses to test early

Stage29 must explicitly test at least the following cross-stage lenses before committing to the endpoint route.

### F1 — global perfect-cuboid geometry

Study the simultaneous perfect-cuboid equations as one global algebraic object rather than only as successive population restrictions. Record the relation between that global object and the Stage18/19/20 toric/K3 models, its quotients, fibrations, low-genus subvarieties, singularities, and usable height maps.

### F2 — joint completion cover / fiber product

Place the Stage19 space-completion cover and Stage20 third-face-completion cover over their common base and study the simultaneous joint cover/fiber product representing both completion predicates at once. Track involutions, quotient surfaces, branch interaction, singularities, canonical class, and rational/elliptic fibrations.

### F3 — parametrization coverage atlas

Treat the major parametrizations and construction families as maps into the common physical or endpoint geometry. For each, record image dimension, generic degree/fiber, height distortion, coverage status, exceptional locus, and whether the family is dominant, divisor-level, curve-level, or merely isolated/thin.

### F4 — joint local arithmetic

Study the two completion predicates simultaneously at finite primes instead of only through separate marginal sieves. Measure exact local intersection densities/correlation, identify systematic positive or negative interaction, and determine whether the joint local system suggests a new global character/monodromy/local-global receiver.

Additional materially different lenses discovered from Arsenal, StructureRadar, prior stages, or exact computation may be added. Cosmetic reformulations of frozen gaps do not count.

## Incremental roadmap

### 29-01 — GLOBAL_CERTIFIED_MAP_LOCK

Freeze the final certified Stage16–28 population/transition map under common physical conventions. Separate proved facts, open theorem intervals, and deferred endpoint claims. Import the closed/audited/merged Stage28 checkpoint70 surface and materialize Stage29 operating rules.

### 29-02 — NEW_FOUNDATION_SCREENING

Run F1–F4 and any comparably distinct repo-native lenses across the Stage16–28 result surface. The purpose is not to solve each old open gate; it is to detect whether a previously underused global object, coordinate system, coverage map, or joint invariant creates a materially new route.

For each lens record:

```text
NEW_FOUNDATION_FOUND=true/false
AFFECTED_STAGES=...
OLD_GATE_REPLAY=false/true_with_new_input
BACKFLOW_RECOMMENDED=true/false
WHY_IT_COULD_CHANGE_ENDPOINT_ROUTING=...
```

### 29-03 — FOUNDATION_BACKFLOW_DECISION

Decide which, if any, earlier stages need targeted addendum/reentry work. Do **not** create `29-16`, `29-17`, ... merely to mirror the old stages. If a real foundation is missing, return only to the relevant original stage, for example:

```text
Stage18-addendum / Stage18-reentry
Stage20-addendum / Stage20-reentry
Stage28-addendum / Stage28-reentry
```

The backflow task must have a new exact receiver created by 29-02. If no such receiver exists, record `NO_FOUNDATION_BACKFLOW_REQUIRED` and continue.

### GAP_SCAN_A — after 29-03

Check whether any important new foundation was missed before synthesis. `NONE_FOUND` is fully acceptable. This scan must not reopen already frozen theorem gates merely because they remain unsolved.

### 29-04 — CONDITION_COST_AND_MECHANISM_MATRIX

Put one-face, second-face, third-face, and space-diagonal effects on a common ledger. Keep exact asymptotics, power corridors, log effects, zero-density statements, local sieve dimensions, geometry, and construction efficiencies separate.

### 29-05 — DEPENDENCY_AND_DOUBLE_CHARGE_LEDGER

Classify squareclass, Gaussian norm, common-core/divisor, local parity, thin-cover, branch-profile, and construction information as genuinely new, correlated, reused, or different descriptions of the same predicate. Forbid artificial multiplication of proof mechanisms.

### 29-06 — GLOBAL_ENDPOINT_GEOMETRY

Develop the F1 global perfect-cuboid model far enough to compare it with the Stage19/20 approaches. Identify useful quotients, fibrations, low-genus slices, symmetry groups, and exact maps back to physical cuboids. No existence/nonexistence conclusion is assumed.

### 29-07 — JOINT_COVER_ENDPOINT_MODEL

Develop the F2 simultaneous completion cover/fiber product as a direct endpoint model. Compare it with the two marginal K3 covers and isolate any new invariant or quotient not visible when the completion predicates are studied separately.

### 29-08 — PARAMETRIZATION_COVERAGE_ATLAS

Build the F3 atlas for the major Stage14–28/A2/Saunderson/Meskhishvili/Peschmann-style parametrizations and families that are actually available in the repository. Record exact coverage status and prevent family-specific closures from being promoted to global endpoint claims.

### 29-09 — JOINT_LOCAL_ARITHMETIC

Execute F4 on the exact common host/joint endpoint receiver. Begin with exact finite-field/local-density structure where useful, then determine whether a new global sieve, character, Frobenius/monodromy, or local-global receiver is justified.

### GAP_SCAN_B — after 29-09

Ask whether the four new-foundation lenses revealed a missing Stage16–28 input that now has a genuinely new receiver. If yes, targeted backflow is allowed here. If not, freeze the foundation layer and move to endpoint routing.

### 29-10 — ENDPOINT_ENTRANCE_A

Analyze the direct route `Stage19 = two faces + space` plus the third face. Record exact equations, parameter freedom, height, multiplicity, covers/fibrations, local obstructions, and natural low-dimensional slices.

### 29-11 — ENDPOINT_ENTRANCE_B

Analyze the direct route `Stage20 = three faces` plus the space diagonal with the same discipline.

### 29-12 — ENDPOINT_ENTRANCE_C_AND_ROUTING

Treat the global/joint model from 29-06/07 as a third direct entrance rather than forcing the endpoint through Stage19 or Stage20 first. Compare A/B/C by equation complexity, geometry, dimension/genus/fibration type, parameter freedom, height distortion, physical reconstruction multiplicity, known constructions, and available theorem species. Select a primary and reserve route only when justified.

### GAP_SCAN_C — after 29-12

Check whether entrance selection omitted a necessary symmetry quotient, model, height adapter, or naturally large family. Open an interstitial analysis only when the omission is material.

### 29-13 — A2_METHOD_TRANSFER

Use StageA2 only as a method source: parameter reduction, quotient/cover decomposition, low-genus models, Jacobian rank/torsion/descent, and rational-point closure. Test these methods on natural endpoint slices or quotients produced by A/B/C. Preserve the firewall `A2 family-specific exclusion != arbitrary perfect-cuboid exclusion`.

### 29-14 — NATURAL_SLICE_AND_COVERAGE_TEST

Attempt exact descent on one or more natural high-relevance slices while tracking representativeness, height, multiplicity, and coverage. If a slice is too special, state that explicitly rather than generating arbitrary new families.

### 29-15 — ENDPOINT_ARSENAL_REMATCH

Perform a concentrated rematch of StructureRadar, Arsenal, Stage14–28 weapons, A2 methods, geometry, sieve, determinant/incidence, character sums, and rational-point technology against the now-exact endpoint receiver(s). This is in addition to the standing reuse preflight used throughout the stage.

### 29-16 — RESIDUAL_RECEIVER_COMPRESSION

Compress unresolved endpoint work to a small number of precise receivers. Each receiver must state exact population/variety, measure/height, quantifiers, multiplicity, required theorem strength, near-misses already ruled out, and what perfect-cuboid consequence would follow.

### GAP_SCAN_FINAL — before handoff

Perform one final search for an unused repo-native weapon, missing symmetry/height adapter, overlooked endpoint model, or unjustified coverage assumption. `NONE_FOUND` is acceptable.

### 29-17 — PERFECT_CUBOID_HANDOFF

Freeze the primary endpoint entrance, best global/joint model, best analytic/geometric/arithmetic route, StageA2-transfer verdict, residual receivers, and the recommended contract for a later direct perfect-cuboid attack stage.

### 29-close

Close Stage29 after fresh audit. Stage29 succeeds if it either finds a genuinely new foundation and integrates it, or certifies that the existing foundations are sufficient for a sharply routed endpoint attack. It need not solve the perfect-cuboid problem.

## Backflow and anti-loop rule

Stage29 may return to an earlier stage, but only when the new-foundation screening produces a materially new receiver. The earlier stage should then receive a narrow addendum/reentry package; after audit, the result is imported back into Stage29 once.

```text
BROAD_SCREEN_IN_STAGE29=true
TARGETED_BACKFLOW_IF_NEW_FOUNDATION=true
BACKFLOW_TO_EVERY_STAGE=false
OLD_GATE_REPLAY_WITHOUT_NEW_THEOREM_OR_NEW_MODEL=false
BACKFLOW_RESULT_REIMPORTED_ONCE=true
```

## Gap-scan rule

Gap scans are mandatory at the named synthesis points but are not forced to create work. Each scan records one of:

```text
GAP_SCAN=NONE_FOUND
GAP_SCAN=FOUND_NEW_FOUNDATION_INTERNAL_ANALYSIS_REQUIRED
GAP_SCAN=FOUND_TARGETED_BACKFLOW_REQUIRED
GAP_SCAN=FOUND_EXTERNAL_INPUT_REQUIRED
```

A gap may create work only when it is mathematically distinct and necessary for the next decision. Cosmetic subdivisions and renamed theorem gates are forbidden.
