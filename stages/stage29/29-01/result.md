All populations are primitive/canonical physical cuboids under the common Euclidean cutoff `R<=B` unless stated otherwise.

`U(B) ~ [pi/(36 zeta(3))] B^3`.

### One face, no space: M1

`M1(B) ~ [3/(4 pi^2)] B^2 log B`.

### One face + space: N1

`N1(B) ~ [kappa/(24 pi)] B(log B)^3`, with positive leading constant, hence

`N1/M1 ~ (kappa*pi/18)(log B)^2/B`.

`M2/M1 ~ (4 pi^2 C_M2/3)(log B)^4/B`.

`B^(1/4) << N2(B) <<_epsilon B^(1/2+epsilon)`.

`B^(-3/4)(log B)^(-5) << N2/M2 <<_epsilon B^(-1/2+epsilon)(log B)^(-5)`.

The true `N2` exponent is not known. Stage27 also certifies positive-divergent enhancement of two-face-conditioned space survival relative to the ambient and one-face-conditioned space rates. That interaction information cannot be charged again as an independent saving.

The true `M3` exponent and an asymptotic remain unknown.

`P(B)=# {primitive canonical cuboids under R<=B with all three face diagonals integral and R integral}`.
