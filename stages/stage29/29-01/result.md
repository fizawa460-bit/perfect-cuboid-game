## 2. Strongest certified population surface

All populations are primitive/canonical physical cuboids under the common Euclidean cutoff `R<=B` unless stated otherwise.

### Ambient

`U(B) ~ [pi/(36 zeta(3))] B^3`.

### One face, no space: M1

`M1(B) ~ [3/(4 pi^2)] B^2 log B`.

### One face + space: N1

`N1(B) ~ [kappa/(24 pi)] B(log B)^3`, with positive leading constant, hence

`N1/M1 ~ (kappa*pi/18)(log B)^2/B`.

### Exactly two faces, no space: M2

`M2(B) ~ C_M2 B(log B)^5`, `C_M2>0`, hence

`M2/M1 ~ (4 pi^2 C_M2/3)(log B)^4/B`.

This is an adjacent-stratum population-size ratio, not an objectwise survival probability.

### Exactly two faces + space: N2

The certified Stage27 corridor is

`B^(1/4) << N2(B) <<_epsilon B^(1/2+epsilon)`.

Therefore

`B^(-3/4)(log B)^(-5) << N2/M2 <<_epsilon B^(-1/2+epsilon)(log B)^(-5)`.

The true `N2` exponent is not known. Stage27 also certifies positive-divergent enhancement of two-face-conditioned space survival relative to the ambient and one-face-conditioned space rates. That interaction information cannot be charged again as an independent saving.

### Exactly three faces, no space: M3

The strongest certified lower is

`M3(B) >> B^(1/3)`

with the audited explicit Saunderson-cone liminf coefficient, while

`M3(B) <<_eta B(log B)^(5-eta)` for fixed `0<eta<1/46`.

The true `M3` exponent and an asymptotic remain unknown.

### Perfect-cuboid endpoint: P

Define symbolically

`P(B)=# {primitive canonical cuboids under R<=B with all three face diagonals integral and R integral}`.

No Stage16–29 theorem currently gives an asymptotic, positive lower bound, zero count, or nonexistence theorem for `P(B)`. Stage29-01 makes no endpoint count claim.

## 6. Backflow policy

Stage29 first screens broadly. If a new lens reveals that an earlier stage genuinely lacks a load-bearing foundation, work returns only to that relevant stage as a narrow addendum/reentry. It does not create `29-16`, `29-17`, ... to replay the old sequence, and it does not rerun a frozen receiver merely because it remains unsolved.

A backflow is legal only when the Stage29 screening creates at least one of:

- a new exact equation/model;
- a changed receiver;
- a new zero-loss adapter;
- a new invariant or quotient;
- a new theorem species that was unavailable at freeze time.

After audit, the backflow result is imported into Stage29 once.
