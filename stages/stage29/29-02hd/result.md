## 1. Question

After the genuinely new F7 sign/Kummer foundation (`29-02ha`), the Campedelli quotient layer (`29-02hb`), and the non-Fano/Hirzebruch named recognition adapter (`29-02hc`), is there another materially independent endpoint foundation that should earn `29-02hd` before Stage29 moves to the backflow decision?

This is not an exhaustiveness theorem. The criterion is practical Stage29 routing: a candidate earns a new independent foundation only if it provides a genuinely different full-coverage endpoint model, invariant, obstruction framework, or theorem adapter that is not already naturally housed in F1–F8.

### 2.1 Peschmann 2026

The new Euclid-pair/quartic/genus-3 route is materially useful research input. Its condition pattern is, however,

```text
two Pythagorean faces built in
+ third-face square
+ space-diagonal square.
```

This is the same residual two-square architecture already carried by the Stage28/29 common two-face host and joint V4 completion. It is therefore routed as a likely **explicit chart/fibration adapter for F2**, pending an exact rational crosswalk, not as an independent foundation.


### 2.2 Dual web of the four endpoint quadrics

The dual `P3` web is exact and unusually simple:

```text
t1*t2*t3*t4
*(t1+t3+t4)
*(t1+t2+t4)
*(t2+t3+t4)=0.
```

A dependency-free exact checker gives

```text
rank<=4 locus: 17 projective points, no positive-dimensional component
rank<=3 locus: exactly 6 points, all rank 3.
```

This rules out the specific Adler–van Moerbeke rank-4-curve trigger for an Abelian-surface route. General Clifford technology remains available, but the sharp rank-4 quadric-surface section/Brauer criterion is not transferable by analogy to this generic rank-7 web.

The dual web is therefore an exact F1/F7 adapter, not a new foundation.

### 2.3 Terasoma four-quadric/K3 theory

Terasoma supplies a general complete-intersection-of-quadrics/K3 theorem ecosystem. For the cuboid specialization its most natural role is to repackage or strengthen provenance for the already-audited seven K3/cohomological pieces in F6/02e. The 48-node specialization still requires an exact adapter.

```text
TERASOMA=HIGH_VALUE_THEOREM_ADAPTER_CANDIDATE
TERASOMA_INDEPENDENT_FOUNDATION=false
```

### 2.4 Fundamental-group / Chabauty–Kim ecosystem

Jarossay–Saettone–Svoray study the cuboid surfaces directly and provide a new theorem ecosystem for fundamental groups. The projective endpoint/minimal resolution is simply connected, while selected open face-cuboid loci have nontrivial fundamental groups. The paper motivates future higher-dimensional Chabauty–Kim use, but the required effective surface theory is not currently an endpoint obstruction.

This is routed to F1/F2/F7 as future open-surface arithmetic infrastructure.

### 2.5 Super-4 and other necessary projections

A perfect cuboid projects to the classical four-squares-with-all-square-differences configuration, but that target is itself an unresolved general-type rational-point problem. The projection does not currently strengthen the endpoint attack.
