## Certified source-level content to be audited

The cited work studies the cuboid and face-cuboid surfaces directly. In particular it proves simple-connectedness results for the projective cuboid surface/minimal resolution and computes nontrivial fundamental groups for selected open face-cuboid loci. It explicitly discusses the possible future arithmetic relevance of unipotent fundamental groups / Chabauty–Kim philosophy, while also noting that the required higher-dimensional theory is not presently available in the form needed to solve the cuboid endpoint.

## Routing
