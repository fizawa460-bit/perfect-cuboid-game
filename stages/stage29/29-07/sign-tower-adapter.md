## 1. Coordinates and the six projective Kummer classes

Write the three physical edges as `e,x,y` and the face/space diagonals as

```text
p^2=e^2+x^2,
q^2=e^2+y^2,
z^2=x^2+y^2,
d^2=e^2+x^2+y^2.
```

The audited F7 base is

```text
P2_F7 : [E:X:Y]=[e^2:x^2:y^2].
```

On `E!=0`, the six independent projective Kummer classes are

```text

Y/E,
(E+X)/E,
(E+Y)/E,
(X+Y)/E,
(E+X+Y)/E.
```

The first two recover the rational edge ratios `x/e,y/e`; the last four are exactly the three face predicates plus the space predicate on the physical square-base locus.

## 2. Edge-square floor



```text

  e^2+x^2=p^2,
```

```text

[e:x:y:p:q] -> [e^2:x^2:y^2]
```


