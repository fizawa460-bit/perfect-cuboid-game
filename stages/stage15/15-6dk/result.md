# Stage15-6dk — graph collision test and delta/sigma ledger

Base: Stage15-6dj. This substage tests whether the exact dispersion inequality already yields a fixed exponent from existing internal inputs.

## 1. Safe unconditional collision bound

For one off-diagonal pair `(x,y)`, every positive same-orientation collision modulus satisfies
\[
q=de\mid J(x,y)
\]
with
\[
J(x,y)=\gcd(|m_xn_y-m_yn_x|,|r_xs_y-r_ys_x|).
\]
All coordinates are polynomially bounded in `B`, so `J(x,y)` is polynomially bounded. For `de<=Q0`,
\[
\varphi(d)\varphi(e)\le de\le Q_0,
\]
and the number of divisor decompositions/orientations is `B^{o(1)}`. Hence pairwise
\[
\sum_{\substack{de\le Q_0\\de\mid J(x,y)}}
\varphi(d)\varphi(e)\,B^{o(1)}
\ll Q_0B^{o(1)}.
\]
Summing trivially gives only
\[
\boxed{
\mathcal C_P(Q_0)
\ll |G(P)|^2Q_0B^{o(1)}.
}
\]
Inserted into 6dj, this yields no fixed `B`-saving over the graph mass. Therefore the exact collision lock alone does **not** certify `delta>0`.

## 2. Existing internal pair-energy search

Stage15-6ah proves a large-common-support energy bound for a different pair parameter inside a fixed low-core Gaussian-transfer fiber. Its support `J_6ah` is the good squarefree common support of two `N(w)` values and is then converted to parallel/orthogonal lines in the Gaussian square parameter.

The present dispersion collision modulus is instead generated by equality of the two toric root ratios and is controlled by
\[
J_{disp}(x,y)=\gcd(m_xn_y-m_yn_x,r_xs_y-r_ys_x)
\]
on the reconstructed three-variable/Pell graph.

There is no proved identity or monotone divisibility
\[
J_{disp}\mid J_{6ah},\qquad J_{6ah}\mid J_{disp}
\]
that preserves the current graph measure. Therefore the quantitative 6ah energy exponent cannot be cross-promoted. The same firewall applies to Stage14 fixed-packet Type-II, spectral large-sieve and projective-spacing estimates.

This is an exact adapter search result, not an assertion that such an adapter is impossible.

## 3. The new internal theorem species

The small-side dispersion route is now reduced to one explicit statement:

> **Reconstructed graph pair-collision energy gate.** Prove, uniformly over legal physical packets,
> \[
> \mathcal C_P(Q_0)
> \ll |G(P)|Q_0B^{o(1)}
> \]
> or any quantitatively comparable bound with a genuine power saving over the trivial `|G(P)|^2Q0` scale, while retaining `R<=B`, the 6da completion graph, `(q,H)=1`, `kg^2|Delta`, exact local orientations and all masks.

If the square-root-scale form holds and packets sum without polynomial loss, 6dj gives for `Q0=B^theta`
\[
E(Q_0)\ll B^{1/2+3\theta/2+o(1)},
\]
which would certify every
\[
\boxed{0<\delta<\frac12-\frac\theta2}
\]
when `theta<1`.

At present this is conditional; no positive `delta` is certified.

## 4. Large side: why dispersion alone does not yet give sigma

The large switched receiver has positive exact weights `phi(d)phi(e)` for `de>D0`. Even perfect control of the **centered** discrepancy does not by itself make the positive local main density
\[
\frac{|G(P)|}{(de)^2}
\]
acquire an inverse power of `D0`. After multiplying by the exact divisor weight, the generic main-density scale is only `1/(de)` up to divisor-like orientation factors. Summing a divisor-many collection above `D0` is not automatically `D0^{-sigma}`.

Therefore
\[
\boxed{\sigma>0\text{ is not certified by reconstructed-graph dispersion alone}.}
\]
A future large-side gain must additionally exploit sparsity of which large switched moduli actually occur on graph nodes, or a graph-support theorem tying large `de` to a rare determinant/core event. The existing `kg^2|Delta` lock is retained but does not identify `de` with `kg`.
