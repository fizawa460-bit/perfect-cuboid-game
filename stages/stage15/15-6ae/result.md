## 2. Minimal real form of the anti-linear transfer

Write

\[
K_\alpha=A+iB,\qquad z=a+ib,
\]

and define

\[
X=A(a^2-b^2)-2Bab,
\qquad
Y=B(a^2-b^2)+2Aab.
\]

Thus `K_alpha z^2=X+iY`. Stage15-6ad reconstruction gives

\[
r=\frac{h_\alpha X}{m},\qquad
s=\frac{h_\alpha Y}{n}.
\]

Hence

\[
\beta_0
=\frac{ms}{h_\beta}+i\frac{nr}{h_\beta}
=\frac{h_\alpha}{h_\beta}
\left(\frac{m}{n}Y+i\frac{n}{m}X\right).
\]

Equivalently, after clearing the fixed denominator,

\[
\boxed{
mn h_\beta\,\beta_0
=h_\alpha\left(m^2Y+i n^2X\right).
}
\]

Since `beta0=K_beta w^2`, the entire low-core square test is exactly

\[
\boxed{
mn h_\beta K_\beta w^2
=h_\alpha\left(m^2Y+i n^2X\right),
}
\]

where `(X,Y)` are the two fixed binary quadratic forms above in the **single** primitive pair `(a,b)`.

This is the minimal real receiver: a diagonal anisotropic rescaling of the real and imaginary coordinates of one fixed-core Gaussian square, followed by the test that the result lies in another fixed-core Gaussian-square class.

## 3. Norm quartic is necessary but not sufficient

Taking norms gives

\[
(mn h_\beta)^2 k |w|^4
=h_\alpha^2\left(m^4Y^2+n^4X^2\right),
\]

because `N(K_beta)=k`. Therefore every survivor satisfies

\[
\boxed{
m^4Y(a,b)^2+n^4X(a,b)^2
=k\left(\frac{mn h_\beta}{h_\alpha}\right)^2 |w|^4.
}
\]

After the already-fixed divisibility data are imposed, this is a binary quartic squareclass condition in the primitive pair `(a,b)`.

But the norm equation alone forgets the Gaussian orientation/square condition. It is only a necessary projection of the exact receiver. Replacing the Gaussian-square target by the quartic norm-square condition would enlarge the population and is forbidden as an equality step.

## 6. Counting boundary

For fixed charged outer data, Stage15-6ae has reduced the low-core problem to counting primitive `(a,b)` of polynomial size satisfying:

1. `K_alpha(a+ib)^2=X+iY`;
2. `m | h_alpha X`, `n | h_alpha Y`;
3. reconstructed `r,s` lie in the original low-core physical dyadic fiber;
4. the anisotropically rescaled Gaussian integer
   \[
   \frac{h_\alpha}{mn h_\beta}(m^2Y+i n^2X)
   \]
   lies in `K_beta*Z[i]^2`;
5. all original primitivity/canonical/exactly-two masks survive as filters.

This is a legitimate one-pair counting receiver. What is **not** yet proved is a uniform bound better than its primitive host size.

The next useful operation must therefore obtain new information from this one-pair receiver itself. Legal possibilities include an exact factorization/conic decomposition if one exists, or a pair-energy/cross-resultant formulation that introduces information shared by two distinct `z` points. A private modulus manufactured from one `z` is not legal.

No genus-one theorem is triggered merely by the quartic projection; first one must prove a uniform model and show that its coefficient/height measure matches the physical fiber.
