## 1. AR-025 — valuation-reduced character recombination

**Verdict: `PARTIAL_EXACT_ADAPTER / EXPONENT_NOT_TRANSFERRED`.**

AR-025 takes unit/nonunit character strata, records a valuation pattern, descends to a reduced modulus and recombines the receiver into principal plus nonprincipal character mass. Stage15-6dp has an exact analogue:
\[
t=(u,v,q),\qquad Q=q/t,
\]
with primitive reduced frequency `(u_1,v_1,Q)=1` and inherited decorated channel pair `(d_Q,e_Q)`.

The adapter is legal because:

- it is a reindexing of the already-centered nonzero Fourier expansion;
- `G(P)`, `R<=B`, survivor masks and `kg^2|Delta` are unchanged;
- valuation/orientation lift multiplicity is only `B^o(1)`;
- distinct outer `(d,e)` slots retain their original exact weights.

But AR-025 explicitly warns that the reduced character family can still have polynomial size. Its Stage14 theorem species also lived in valuation-averaged scalar or `(E,m)` measures, not this reconstructed physical graph. Therefore AR-025 supplies the **normal form only**, not a same-measure large-sieve exponent.

```text

```

## 2. AR-026 — target-class mass countermodel

**Verdict: `DIRECT_NEGATIVE_FIREWALL`.**

The current occupancy residual is precisely a distinguished target-class discrepancy. AR-026 therefore applies logically: total mass, nonnegativity, Parseval, or an undirected `L^1/L^2` character bound does not by itself control the mass in the required moving target.

In Stage15 language, the identity

This rejects any attempt to count the character family and infer cancellation from family size alone.

## 3. AR-027 — averaged-versus-every-cell firewall

**Verdict: `DIRECT_FIREWALL_WITH_NATIVE_MODULUS_AVERAGE_CLARIFIED`.**

The desired quantity already averages over decorated switched moduli `(d,e)` with weight
\[
\lambda(d,e)/\Omega(d,e).
\]
Therefore an average-over-`(d,e)` theorem is **not** an illegal exceptional-set promotion if it is stated in exactly this measure. Likewise, a theorem directly on the disjoint union of physical graph packets is legal if the packet partition, weights and masks are exactly the Stage15 population and no exceptional packet is silently discarded.

What remains forbidden is:

- an almost-all-moduli theorem used at one frozen modulus without paying the exceptions;
- an average over Stage14 fixed-U/fixed-tag packets promoted to Stage15 graph packets;
- an average over a larger algebraic superset whose exceptional portion is not bounded in the physical charged measure;
- a theorem with undecorated `q` that merges distinct `(d,e)` channel assignments and changes their weights.

No available Arsenal theorem supplies the required same-measure exceptional-set bridge plus `kappa<1`.

```text
AR-027=DIRECTLY_ACTIVE
NATIVE_DECORATED_MODULUS_AVERAGE=LEGAL
DIFFERENT_PACKET_AVERAGE_CROSS_PROMOTION=false
```

## 4. AR-033 — weighted coprime rectangle convolution

**Verdict: `NO_CURRENT_ADAPTER`.**

AR-033 requires a two-variable scalar host
\[
\sum_{r,s}\beta(r)\beta(s)1_{(r,s)=1}
\]
whose Dirichlet series splits into marginal factors times an absolutely summable cross correction with a certified weighted coefficient norm. It then gives the genuine `3/4+epsilon` rectangle tails.

The present nonzero-frequency receiver is instead a reconstructed three-residual/Pell graph with a `B^o(1)` fourth completion and frequency-dependent phases. No factorization
\[
\text{graph coefficient}=\beta(r)\beta(s)\times\text{summable cross correction}
\]
has been proved, and no required half-plane coefficient norm exists for the graph exponential sums.

Slicing one residual variable does not create the adapter: the Pell completion phase leaves coefficients depending on the frequency/modulus, so the scalar rectangle coefficient measure is not recovered.

Therefore the `3/4+epsilon` tail is **not** imported.

```text
AR-033_ADAPTER=false
AR-033_THREE_QUARTER_EXPONENT_TRANSFER=false
```

## 5. AR-035 — fixed-prime overlap sieve with ordered limits

**Verdict: `LIVE_QUALITATIVE_ROUTE / NO_KAPPA_GAIN`.**

AR-035 is genuinely relevant to the broad Stage15 square-survivor problem: if Stage15 proves a fixed-modulus congruence-refined asymptotic in the correct ambient measure, finitely many independent local rejection primes can force an additional square-condition overlap to be `o` of the ambient main scale.

However its certified quantifier order is
\[
\text{fix finitely many primes}
\to B\to\infty
\to\text{then increase the number of primes}.
\]
Without an effective theorem uniform in the number/product of those primes, the output is qualitative `o(1)` only. It does not provide a fixed polynomial `B^{-eta}` rate and hence does not prove a fixed `kappa<1` for
\[
\mathfrak B_P(Q_0).
\]
Moreover no fixed-modulus refined asymptotic has yet been proved for the **reconstructed survivor graph packet** used in the present second moment.

AR-035 is therefore preserved as a useful separate qualitative thinning route, but is rejected as the quantitative character-large-sieve adapter requested in Stage15-6.

```text
AR-035=LIVE_QUALITATIVE_ONLY
AR-035_FIXED_KAPPA_LT_1=false
AR-035_LIMIT_ORDER_PRESERVED=true
```

## 6. AR-037 — finite-order Selberg--Delange contract

**Verdict: `NO_CURRENT_EULER_FACTORIZATION_ADAPTER`.**

AR-037 applies after an exact one-variable factorization
\[
F(s)=\zeta(s)^zH(s)
\]
with analytic region, vertical growth, coefficient majorant and conductor-uniformity all proved. The current primitive reduced-frequency sums have moving modulus/conductor and no one-variable Euler product for their graph coefficients has been derived.

Even if such a factorization were later produced, the certified output of AR-037 is an arbitrarily strong **fixed logarithmic** remainder after fixing the expansion depth. By itself that does not imply a fixed power `B^{-eta}` and therefore is not a substitute for `kappa<1` in the current polynomial overlap problem.

```text
AR-037_ADAPTER=false
AR-037_LOG_SAVING_IS_FIXED_POWER=false
```
