The Stage20 and Stage27 completion problems share the same physical two-face anticanonical host and both define degree-two covers with branch class `-2K_Y`. This supports reuse of proof architecture only.

A legal Stage27 quantitative transfer requires a theorem proved for the actual space-diagonal cover `Z_sp` with all of the following checked on the same physical population:

1. the exact primitive/canonical Stage27 N2 host measure and Euclidean cutoff;
2. the actual branch divisor of `1+t1^2+t2^2` rather than the Stage20 third-face branch;
3. any geometric hypotheses used by the thin-cover theorem on `Z_sp` itself;
4. uniformity strong enough to produce a fixed-power saving in the Stage27 horizontal/support problem, not merely a logarithmic saving on the Stage20 host;
5. no multiplication of independent-looking local factors unless a joint theorem on the same measure is proved.

The Stage20 explicit upper `M3(B)<<B(log B)^(5-eta)` does not imply a Stage27 bound. Likewise, K3 type alone does not imply a fixed-power saving.
