The exact receiver is therefore:

1. a Stage18-parametric family contained in the primitive canonical exactly-two population;
2. `sf(A)=sf(D)` identically (or on a moving subfamily of source-count exponent `rho`);
3. physical height exponent `h` under `R<=B`;

5. resulting lower exponent `rho/h` strictly exceeding the currently certified Stage19 lower exponent `1/4`.

Importing those unresolved gates here would not constitute new Stage27-18 progress.

Thus Stage27-18 does not independently improve the lower exponent at this point.  Its contribution is to show that any useful lower transfer must come from a high-mass Stage18 source family whose survival is structural, not from the global `B(log B)^5` source count alone.
