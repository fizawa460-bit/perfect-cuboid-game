## 1. Frozen Stage27 question

Stage27 is the strict reattack of the literal subset transition

\[
M_2(B)\longrightarrow N_2(B),
\]

where `M2` counts primitive canonical exactly-two-face cuboids under `R<=B`, and
`N2` counts the same physical objects with the additional integral-space-diagonal
condition.

The purpose of the stage was to shrink the inherited exponent corridor or identify
the true growth scale if possible. The strict reattack did not improve either
numerical endpoint.

The strongest certified whole-family surface at closeout remains

\[
\boxed{B^{1/4}\ll N_2(B)\ll_\varepsilon B^{1/2+\varepsilon}},
\]

with all three directional lower bounds

\[
N_{2,j}(B)\gg_j B^{1/4},\qquad j=a,b,c,
\]

and Stage18 gives

\[
M_2(B)\sim C_{M_2}B(\log B)^5,\qquad C_{M_2}>0.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
B^{-3/4}(\log B)^{-5}
\ll \frac{N_2(B)}{M_2(B)}
\ll_\varepsilon B^{-1/2+\varepsilon}(\log B)^{-5}
}
\]

and in particular

\[
N_2(B)\to\infty,
\qquad
N_2(B)=o(M_2(B)).
\]

No point exponent, asymptotic formula, matching lower/upper pair, strict
sub-square-root upper, or lower exponent above `1/4` is proved.
