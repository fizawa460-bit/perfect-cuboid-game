# Stage32EX3 — O210 modular cover / monodromy closure roadmap

Status: **ACTIVE GOAL-DIRECTED MAINLINE**. Stage32EX3 is operationally independent from Stage32 MAIN authority until an explicit hostile-audited promotion adapter is accepted.

## Fixed target and scope

The Stage32EX3 target is the exact fixed recovered V6 branch at

- Picard class `V6` / row `g1-d186`;
- hypothetical integral irreducible genus-1 carrier normalization `N`;
- modular factor degrees from `N -> X(4)` equal to `(105,81)`;
- product-cover branch `q'=4`;
- extremal overlap/contact parameter `O=210`;
- first product-cover projection degree `105` and ramification remainder `0`;
- second product-cover projection degree `81` and ramification remainder `192` before the retained V4 étale descent, equivalently descended ramification total `48` where that exact adapter applies;
- retained O210 contact histogram `210 x m1 + 28 x m2` as an exact necessary extremal profile;
- retained correspondence/Rosati boundary `Q(T)=602` only through exact source-locked adapters.

EX3 studies whether the entire cover/branch-cycle/monodromy package required by an actual O210 carrier can exist. It does not assume that a numerical cover ledger is realized by an actual V6 member.

## Terminal decision

`FULL_TARGET_CLOSURE` for Stage32EX3 is a decision state with exactly one of the following terminal outcomes.

### `O210_COVER_GEOMETRY_EXCLUDED`

Prove that **no hypothetical integral irreducible V6 genus-1 carrier in the fixed target can realize the exact O210/q'=4 modular-cover package**. The proof must be population-preserving from the V6/O210 carrier hypothesis to every cover/monodromy obstruction used. A contradiction for one guessed branch-cycle type, one monodromy subgroup, one marked gauge, or one local contact pattern is not enough.

### `GENUINE_O210_COVER_CONFIGURATION_ESTABLISHED`

Construct or otherwise establish an exact cover configuration satisfying the complete fixed target **and** attach it to the same actual V6 carrier object through a verified carrier-to-cover adapter. At minimum this requires the relevant normalization, both modular maps, degrees, ramification/branch data, connectedness/transitivity conditions, V4/common-cover compatibility, and every claimed marked or correspondence interface. An abstract Hurwitz/Nielsen class or standalone cover with no V6-carrier adapter is not terminal success.

The intended direction is exclusion. A genuine positive configuration is nevertheless a valid terminal decision for EX3 because it proves that the cover/monodromy layer itself is not the obstruction.

A necessary condition, finite branch-cycle ledger, finite monodromy candidate set, source-gap diagnosis, abstract realizable Nielsen class, or residue pruning without O210 closure is intermediate only.

## Dependency roadmap

### EX3-00 — source lock and typed cover tower

Freeze the exact objects and maps before any monodromy calculation. Distinguish at least:

- the hypothetical carrier and its normalization `N`;
- the Beauville/common-cover pullback normalization `Y` when used;
- any further connected product-cover component `D` used in the q'=4 argument;
- the factor curves `X(4)`, `X(8)`, and the genus-2 quotient `C0` when used;
- the maps whose degrees are `105` and `81`;
- which ramification number belongs to which map and on which curve;
- the V4 deck group `H`, the order-8 group `G`, and quotient maps only where exact source locks identify them;
- the correspondence `Gamma` and Jacobian operator `T` only through a typed map from the same carrier/cover object.

Source-lock the fixed numerical data rather than copying them from prose. In particular, verify the exact provenance of `(105,81)`, `q'=4`, `O=210`, first-projection étaleness, second-projection ramification `192`, the `210 x m1 + 28 x m2` histogram, and the common-cover identity.

Exit: a replayable exact target contract in which every degree, ramification total, group action, and model transition has a named source and object type. No O210 exclusion or realization credit yet.

### EX3-01 — extremal first projection: degree-105 étale cover

Analyze the first projection as an actual connected finite cover under the hypothetical-carrier assumption. Use Riemann--Hurwitz only on the exact source curve and target curve fixed in EX3-00.

Tasks:

1. verify the genus forced by degree `105` and zero ramification;
2. determine the exact connectedness/transitivity consequence available from the source construction;
3. identify the allowed monodromy representation of the étale cover as a finite-index subgroup / transitive permutation representation of the appropriate fundamental or orbifold group;
4. record all deck, imprimitivity, factorization, or parity constraints that are actually forced;
5. distinguish an étale cover of the smooth modular curve from statements about normalization bijectivity, the ambient surface, or the second projection.

Exit: either this first-projection cover is impossible under the exact target or an exact family/finite description of admissible first-projection monodromy remains.

### EX3-02 — degree-81 projection and branch-cycle ledger

Analyze the second projection independently, with total ramification exactly as typed in EX3-00.

Build a complete branch-cycle ledger containing, where justified:

- branch points and whether they are cuspidal, interior, or unresolved;
- inertia cycle partitions of `81`;
- local ramification indices and contribution `e_p-1`;
- the product-one relation;
- transitivity/connectedness;
- parity/sign restrictions;
- Riemann--Hurwitz total `192` before descent and the exact descended total only where the V4 adapter applies;
- possible imprimitive factorizations or intermediate covers.

A coarse partition of `192` is not enough. Every retained branch-cycle species must state its realizability obligations and its relation to the same carrier.

Exit: a complete parameterized or finite residual ledger for the second projection, or an exact contradiction.

### EX3-03 — O210 contact-to-ramification adapter

This is a critical semantic gate. Determine exactly what the O210 exceptional-contact histogram says about ramification of the two modular maps.

Do not assume that every exceptional contact contributes one ramification point. For each local contact species, derive from the source-locked local model whether it forces:

- ramification or no ramification;
- a specific inertia index;
- a marked branch value;
- multiplicity in a cusp fiber;
- no direct branch-cycle information.

Separate the `210` odd/unit contacts and `28` multiplicity-two contacts. Prove any aggregation formula before identifying contact mass with ramification mass.

Exit: an exact local-to-global adapter from the O210 contact ledger to branch-cycle data, or a typed statement of which contacts remain invisible to monodromy. A missing adapter is a blocker, not terminal success.

### EX3-04 — common V4 cover and simultaneous two-projection compatibility

Use the retained common-double-cover/cartesian identity only at its exact scope. Analyze the fact that the two projections arise from the same carrier and same common V4 torsor rather than as unrelated covers.

Tasks may include:

- character-plane compatibility;
- simultaneous lifting to the same connected V4 cover;
- induced `Aut(H)` action and normalizer constraints;
- compatibility of branch cycles under the two factor maps;
- fiber-product connectedness;
- common intermediate quotient restrictions;
- constraints from the `G/H` degree-two layer where source-locked.

Do not re-promote a compatibility identity already automatic for a genuine carrier into a new obstruction unless an additional nonautomatic condition is proved.

Exit: a simultaneous-cover contract that is strictly stronger than separate Riemann--Hurwitz ledgers, or a certified nonpruning result with a clear reopen condition.

### EX3-05 — monodromy group / Nielsen-class exhaustion

Convert the exact data from EX3-01..04 into a finite or finitely parameterized monodromy problem.

Depending on what is source-locked, enumerate or classify:

- admissible transitive monodromy groups for degree `105` and degree `81`;
- compatible inertia conjugacy classes;
- product-one generating tuples;
- imprimitivity systems/intermediate covers;
- simultaneous V4-normalizer constraints;
- Nielsen classes up to simultaneous conjugacy and permitted Hurwitz moves.

A computer enumeration is admissible only after the finite input/output contract is explicit. A zero hit can support exclusion only if the enumeration is proved exhaustive for the exact population.

Exit: either no admissible simultaneous monodromy object exists, or a residual set of exact Nielsen/monodromy classes remains.

### EX3-06 — geometric realizability of residual monodromy data

Group-theoretic tuples are not automatically algebraic covers with the required modular/carrier semantics. For each residual monodromy class, decide geometric realizability through an appropriate exact theorem or explicit construction.

Check at least:

- the correct base curve/orbifold rather than an abstract punctured sphere if they differ;
- required marked cusps and local coordinates;
- connectedness and normalization;
- field/complex realization assumptions;
- compatibility with the common-cover tower;
- whether the two covers coexist on the same source curve `D`/`Y`/`N` as required.

Exit: dispose every abstract-only tuple that cannot realize the exact geometry, or retain an exact realizable cover candidate with all still-missing carrier adapters listed.

### EX3-07 — correspondence/Rosati/Q602 coupling

Only after the cover geometry is typed, ask whether it induces a new predicate on the retained correspondence `Gamma` or operator `T`.

Potential outputs include exact formulas for:

- diagonal intersection or self-intersection of `Gamma`;

The adapter must identify the same operator/basis/object used by the retained Q602 computation. Abstract monodromy conjugacy, an unmarked representation, or a branch-cycle parity does not by itself choose a retained W-line or Q602 residue.

Exit: either obtain a new exact O210 contradiction, a genuine residue contraction with stated scope, or an exact proof that the current monodromy data do not yet couple to Q602.

### EX3-08 — residual configuration disposal

Treat every surviving cover/monodromy configuration as a work queue. For each residual case, obtain one of:

- an exact Riemann--Hurwitz/branch-cycle contradiction;
- an exact common-cover incompatibility;

Do not repeatedly rerun the same finite group search under different names. Record route identity and reopen conditions in `MAIN-STATE.json`.

Exit: every residual configuration is disposed for the exclusion outcome, or one genuine carrier-attached O210 cover configuration is fully established.
