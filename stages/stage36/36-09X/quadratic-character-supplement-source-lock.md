# Stage36 36-09X source lock: supplementary quadratic-character laws

Accessed: 2026-09-06

Source:
- David A. Vogan Jr., *Continued fraction expansion / quadratic reciprocity cheat sheet*, MIT Mathematics: https://math.mit.edu/~dav/cheatSheetCORR2.pdf

Exact facts used:

For an odd positive denominator Q (and hence for an odd prime q), the Jacobi/Legendre base formulas are

`(-1/Q)=(-1)^((Q-1)/2)`

and

`(2/Q)=(-1)^((Q^2-1)/8)`.

Therefore for an odd prime q,

- `(2/q)=+1` iff `q = 1 or 7 (mod 8)`,
- `(-1/q)=+1` iff `q = 1 or 5 (mod 8)`,
- simultaneously `(2/q)=(-2/q)=+1` iff `q=1 (mod 8)`.

No higher reciprocity law or density statement is used.
