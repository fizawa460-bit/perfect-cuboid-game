# Stage36 36-09DL full rational-component factorwise-square adelic recipe

The fixed curve is

`C3_2: z^2=f0(t)f1(t)f2(t)f3(t)`

with

- `f0=t^2+4`,
- `f1=t^2+1/4`,
- `f2=t^2+9`,
- `f3=t^2+1/9`.

36-09DK source-binds the full rational-component Brauer image as generated by `(d,fj(t))_2` for arbitrary rational squareclass `d` and `j=0,1,2,3`.

## Local recipe

Choose the following retained-open parameters:

- over `R`, take `t=2`;
- over `Q_2`, take `t=8`;
- over `Q_3`, take `t=9`;
- over every `Q_l` with prime `l>3`, take `t=l`.

At the real place all four factor values are positive and hence squares.

At `Q_2` the values are

`68`, `257/4`, `73`, `577/9`.

Each has even 2-adic valuation and odd unit congruent to `1 mod 8`, hence each is a `Q_2` square.

At `Q_3` the values are

`85`, `325/4`, `90`, `730/9`.

Each has even 3-adic valuation and unit residue `1 mod 3`, hence each is a `Q_3` square.

For every prime `l>3`, writing each constant as `a_j^2` with `a_j in {2,1/2,3,1/3}` gives

`fj(l)=a_j^2*(1+(l/a_j)^2)`.

The second factor is congruent to `1 mod l`, so it is a `Q_l` square by Hensel's lemma. Thus every `fj(l)` is a local square.

Choose local square roots `s_{j,v}^2=fj(t_v)` and set `z_v=product_j s_{j,v}`. This gives a retained-open local receiver point at every place and hence an adelic point.

At this adelic point every generator `(d,fj(t_v))_2` is pointwise trivial at every place because its second entry is a square. Therefore the adelic point is orthogonal to the entire rational-component Brauer image, without invoking a finite-generator truncation.

## Firewalls

This construction controls only the full rational-component image. It does not show orthogonality to `F5` or to all non-rational `Q(i)`-component classes, does not compute the full Creutz--Viray image/full Brauer group, and grants no fixed-parameter, receiver, or endpoint credit.
