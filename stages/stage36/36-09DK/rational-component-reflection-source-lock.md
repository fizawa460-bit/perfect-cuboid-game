# Stage36 36-09DK rational-component reflection source lock

## Bound construction

The 36-09CW Creutz--Viray specialization identifies the branch algebra for `C3_2` as

`L = Q(i)^4`

with factors

`f0=t^2+4`, `f1=t^2+1/4`, `f2=t^2+9`, `f3=t^2+1/9`.

A rational-component input is a tuple `(d0,d1,d2,d3)` with every `dj in Q^*`. Each coordinate has norm `dj^2`, so every such tuple lies in `L_1`. By additivity of the symbol/corestriction construction and the projection formula already frozen in 36-09CW, its Brauer image is the sum

`sum_j (dj,fj(t))_2`.

Thus the full rational-component image is generated by the one-coordinate classes `(d,fj(t))_2`, with arbitrary rational squareclass `d` and factor index `j`.

## Reflection annihilator

Every `fj` is an even function of `t`. Hence for any local field and any two receiver points with parameters `t=u` and `t=-u`, provided the displayed representatives are defined,

`fj(u)=fj(-u)`

literally. Therefore every class in the full rational-component image has identical local evaluation at the two reflected points. The difference-of-evaluations functional

`ev_u - ev_-u`

annihilates the entire rational-component image modulo constants.

Consequently, if any Creutz--Viray class has different local invariants at two reflected receiver points, that class cannot lie in the full rational-component image modulo `Br(Q)`.

## Stage36 specialization

36-09DJ gives an explicit Rosset--Tate/Creutz--Viray corestriction evaluator for `delta=F5+R5`, while `R5=(5,f0(t))_2` itself is rational-component and even in `t`. Therefore a nonzero reflected evaluation difference for `delta` is also a nonzero reflected evaluation difference for `F5`.

## Firewalls

This test separates `F5` from the full rational-component image only. It does not compute `Pic^0(C3_2)/2`, the full `L_1` quotient, the full Creutz--Viray explicit image, or the full Brauer group, and it grants no Brauer--Manin, fixed-parameter, receiver, or endpoint credit.
