## External structural source

E. Kani and M. Rosen, *Idempotent relations and factors of Jacobians*, Mathematische Annalen 284 (1989), 307--328.

For a Klein four group `G={1,tau,sigma,rho}` acting on a smooth projective curve, with the three order-two subgroups generated by `tau`, `sigma`, and `rho`, the Kani--Rosen idempotent relation gives

`Jac(C) x Jac(C/G)^2 ~ Jac(C/<tau>) x Jac(C/<sigma>) x Jac(C/<rho>)`.

The 36-09DR verifier independently checks that the three named involutions preserve the fixed equation, that the three quotient equations have genus one with rational points, and that `C/G` has genus zero. The same Q-isogeny is also independently certified by the three pullback differential directions spanning `H^0(C,Omega^1)` with determinant 2.

## 2-primary firewall
