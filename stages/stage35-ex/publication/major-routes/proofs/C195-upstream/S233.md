1. Original unchanged physical packet and hypotheses
Assume exactly Section232's retained positive rational r,s,p,q and primitive A²+B²=C² (A,B,C positive, C odd, pairwise coprime), E: Y²=X(X−A²)(X+C²), P=(AC,ABC), T0=(0,0), D_n=T0+2nP=(-r²,-rpq), signed |n|>=3. Retain the original same Q=(rs+pq,(r+s)(rs+pq)) and Qsharp=(-(rs+pq),(s-r)(rs+pq)), Q-Qsharp=D_n, and the infinite-order class [Q] in E(Q)/(E(Q)[2]+ZP) for active Route3. No odd finite scalar relation dQ=mP is assumed.
Write r=R/b,s=S/b,p=U/b,q=V/b, with positive integers R,S,U,V,b, gcd(R,b)=1, and
R²+S²=B²b²; U²−R²=A²b²; V²+R²=C²b². (233.1)
Section232 provides b>1, gcd(b,2ABC)=1, each ell|b has ell=1 mod4 and ord(Pbar)=4h, h||n|, |n|/h odd. These are retained necessary conditions; their upstream audit credit is not upgraded here.
2. NEW exact factorization without rank assumptions
Let J=UV+RS and K=UV−RS. Expanding (233.1) and using C²=A²+B² gives
(UV)²−(RS)² = A²C²b⁴, hence JK=(AC b²)². (233.2)
All four numerators are positive, so UV>RS and J,K are strictly positive integers.
For any prime ell|b, k=v_ell(b)>=1, (233.1) gives ell∤R,S,U,V. As ell is odd, J+K=2UV and J−K=2RS are ell-units; therefore J,K cannot both be ell-divisible. Since v_ell(JK)=4k, exactly one of the two has valuation 4k and the other valuation 0:
{v_ell(J),v_ell(K)}={0,4k}. (233.3)
This removes the unresolved 'higher N valuation' subcase of Section232: v_ell(J) is EXACTLY 0 or 4k, not an arbitrary positive integer.
3. NEW global squareclass and denominator partition
Because J,K>0 and JK is an integer square, there exist a uniquely determined positive squarefree integer d and positive integers U1,V1 with
J=d U1²; K=d V1²; d U1 V1=AC b². (233.4)
At primes outside ACb, both J and K are units. At ell|b, (233.3) gives even valuation 0 or 4k. Consequently d is supported only on primes dividing AC, i.e. d|rad(AC) (and in particular d|AC).
Partition the exact prime powers of b by whether ell|J or ell|K:
b_J=product ell^v_ell(b) over ell|b with ell|J;
b_K=product ell^v_ell(b) over ell|b with ell|K.
Then b=b_J b_K, gcd(b_J,b_K)=1; both factors have all primes =1 mod4 and are coprime to 2ABC. Equation (233.3) and (233.4) yield U1=u b_J², V1=v b_K² for positive integers u,v coprime to b. Therefore
d u v=AC,
J=d u² b_J⁴, K=d v² b_K⁴, (233.5)
where d is squarefree dividing AC, u,v positive divisors subject to d u v=AC.
These identities are necessary for every original physical Route3 packet, without assuming Q has finite order modulo K.
4. Exact local reductions and torsion translate
Since x(Q)=J/b² and y(Q)=(R+S)J/b³, (233.5) gives
x(Q)=d u² (b_J/b_K)²,
x(Q+T0)=−A²C²/x(Q)=−K/b²=−d v² (b_K/b_J)². (233.6)
The elliptic identity x(W+T0)=−A²C²/x(W) applies for W with x(W)≠0, and Q has positive x. For each ell|b, ell∤R+S: otherwise S≡−R contradicts R²+S²≡0 with ell odd and R a unit. Thus:
ell|b_J: v_ell(x(Q))=+2k and v_ell(y(Q))=+k, so Q reduces to the nonsingular T0;
ell|b_K: v_ell(x(Q))=−2k and v_ell(y(Q))=−3k, so Q reduces to O.
Both cases hold in the nonsingular reduction group at good primes and at Section232's H=A²+C² nodal primes. The original Qsharp has x=−J/b² and y=(S−R)J/b³ with ell∤S−R by the same unit argument; consequently Qsharp has the SAME O/T0 reduction as Q. This is compatible with reduction of D_n=Q−Qsharp to O.
Section232's 2n Pbar=T0 and order condition remain simultaneous obligations. The local O/T0 dichotomy alone does not enforce a global relation dQ=mP.
5. Additional exact duplication receiver (not rank elimination)
For E: y²=x³+B²x²−A²C²x, the chord/tangent duplication formula is
x(2Q)=(x(Q)²+A²C²)²/(4y(Q)²).
