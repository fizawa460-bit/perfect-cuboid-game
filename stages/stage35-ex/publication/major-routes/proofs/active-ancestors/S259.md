3. NEW ROLEWISE PROPER-ENTRY THEOREM
Claim. Suppose a complete H-only packet has at least one role (J or K) with NO active critical prime whose first entry h_ell equals m=|n|. That role may contain arbitrarily deep critical primes with proper entries h<m. Then
(52/49)*lambda*m² <= log m + (1/4)log(C³*T_e^4/A) +(2g_e+1)c_E/12.      (259.1)
Consequently with Z_e=ceil(C³*T_e^4/A), and
K_equal=bit_length(Z_e)/4 +(2g_e+1)c_E/12,
N_equal=max({N integer >=0:(52/49)*lambda*N² -N/2 -K_equal<=0}),
one has m<=N_equal. N_equal exists and is finite because lambda>0.
Proof. For each active critical first entry h<m, the quotient q=m/h is a proper positive odd integer q>=3. Distinct h give distinct q; hence
S_proper=sum_distinct_proper_active_h (h/m)² <=sum_(odd q>=3)1/q²

For the chosen role containing NO h=m critical prime, collect all noncritical initial powers in T_e, all additional index powers in m, and all critical proper first-entry products in distinct b_h. Then
log b_role <=log(T_e*m) +sum_(proper critical h)log b_h
