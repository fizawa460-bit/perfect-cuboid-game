## 0. Scope and notation firewall
Retain the ORIGINAL finite-odd physical Branch-B packet:
A^2+B^2=C^2 positive primitive;
E: Y^2=X(X-A^2)(X+C^2);
P=(AC,ABC), R=2P, T0=(0,0);
D_n=T0+nR=(u,v), |n|>=3;
d>=3 odd, m odd, gcd(d,m)=1, dQ=mP;
Q-Q#=D_n, X(Q#)=-X(Q);
all original strict-real, chord, subgroup, same-packet and sign masks.
Retain Section227's strict physical chord gate. Write the reduced D_n x-coordinate as
u=a/b^2, gcd(a,b)=1, b>0.
This section's b is the ROOT DENOMINATOR OF X(D_n). It is NOT the odd quotient denominator d and not the normalized conic coordinate B/C.
Section227 gives
a=-R0^2,
P0^2=A^2 b^2-a,
Q0^2=C^2 b^2+a,
S0^2=B^2 b^2+a,
with R0,P0,Q0,S0 positive integers in the strict chamber, and
R0^2+S0^2=B^2 b^2.                                  (228.1)
It also gives the unique physical chord point
Q=(Z/b^2,(R0+S0)Z/b^3),
Z=P0Q0+R0S0,
and the root denominator nu of X(Q) satisfies nu|b.
The goal here is to combine this integral gate with the SAME odd scalar relation dQ=mP. No independent-density or finite-zero inference is used.
## 1. Any prime of b puts D_n in the formal identity subgroup
Let ell be an odd prime with ell|b. Since u=a/b^2 is reduced,
v_ell(u)<0.
On every minimal odd-prime model considered below this means
D_n in E_1(Q_ell), hence D_n reduces to the identity O in the nonsingular reduction group and has trivial component class.                     (228.2)
The discriminant support is
Delta=16 A^4 C^4 (A^2+C^2)^2.
Thus an odd ell outside AC is either good, or H-type with ell|A^2+C^2.
## 2. Good primes outside AC cannot divide b
Assume ell∤AC(A^2+C^2). Reduction is good. From D_n=Q-Q# and (228.2),
bar Q=bar Q#.
If this common reduction is O, reduce dQ=mP:
m bar P=O
because d is odd. Also
O=bar D_n=T0+2n bar P,
so 2n bar P=T0.
Therefore the cyclic group generated by bar P contains the nonzero order-two point T0, so ord(bar P) is even; but ord(bar P)|m with m odd, contradiction.
If the common reduction is finite, exact opposite X gives
X(bar Q)=X(bar Q#)=-X(bar Q).
Since ell is odd, X(bar Q)=0. On the good reduced cubic the only finite point with X=0 is T0. Thus bar Q=bar Q#=T0. Reducing dQ=mP and using d odd gives
m bar P=T0.
Together with 2n bar P=T0 this gives
(m-2n) bar P=O.
The coefficient m-2n is odd, while <bar P> contains T0 and therefore has even order. Contradiction.
Hence no odd good-reduction prime outside AC can divide b. In particular odd primes of B are excluded from b because primitive Pythagorean support makes them good here.
## 3. H primes outside AC cannot divide b either
Assume ell|A^2+C^2 and ell∤AC. Retained S200/S202 gives split multiplicative reduction of type I_(2delta), delta=v_ell(A^2+C^2), with unique node X=A^2=-C^2. P and T0 are nonsingular.
By (228.2), D_n is in E_1, so Q and Q# have the same component. If that common component were nonzero, both endpoints would specialize to the node and therefore
X(bar Q)=X(bar Q#)=A^2.
But X(Q#)=-X(Q), so 2A^2=0 mod ell, impossible because ell is odd and A is a unit. Therefore Q,Q# lie in E_0.
Now D_n in E_1 makes their E_0/E_1 reductions equal. The same two cases as in Section2 remain:
common reduction O, or finite with opposite X and hence X=0=T0.
The identical odd-order parity contradiction excludes both.
Therefore
ell|b and ell odd  =>  ell|AC.                       (228.3)
This statement is about the coordinate denominator b. It does NOT exclude H primes from d. Sections219/221/223 remain live for H primes occurring in the odd quotient denominator d.
## 4. A/C bad-prime components force n odd
Let ell be an odd prime dividing A or C, and e=v_ell(A) or v_ell(C). The retained primitive-model local calculation gives split multiplicative type I_(4e). The current R=2P lies on the unique nonzero order-two component 2e mod 4e.
For ell|C this is exactly the component computation retained in S202. For ell|A the reduced node is again X=0; T0 specializes to the node and is a rational nonzero 2-torsion point, so its component is also the unique order-two class 2e. Hence in either A or C placement
component(D_n)
=component(T0+nR)
=2e(1+n) mod 4e.                                   (228.4)
If ell|b, (228.2) says D_n is in E_1, so its component is zero. Equation (228.4) then gives
2e(1+n)=0 mod4e
iff n is odd.
Thus
b>1  =>  n is odd.                                  (228.5)
Equivalently, when n is even, no odd prime can divide b: good and H primes are excluded by Sections2-3, while A/C primes are excluded by the component parity.
## 5. The Section227 square gate eliminates 2 and forces p=1 mod4 support
Because a=-R0^2 and gcd(a,b)=1,
gcd(R0,b)=1.                                         (228.6)
If 2|b, then R0 is odd. But (228.1) has right side divisible by4, whereas R0^2+S0^2 is 1 or2 mod4. Contradiction. Hence
b is odd.                                             (228.7)
Now let p be any odd prime dividing b. Reduce (228.1) mod p. By (228.6), R0 is a p-unit, and
S0^2=-R0^2 mod p.
Therefore -1 is a quadratic residue mod p and
p=1 mod4.                                             (228.8)
Combining (228.3), (228.7), (228.8):
b is odd;
rad(b) | rad(AC);
every p|b satisfies p=1 mod4;
and b>1 is possible only for odd n.                   (228.9)
Since Section227 proves nu|b for the root denominator nu of X(Q), the same prime-support restriction applies to nu.
## 6. Exact even-index collapse
If n is even, (228.5) and (228.7) give the exact conclusion
b=1.                                                  (228.10)
Therefore D_n has integral X coordinate u=-R0^2, and Section227's reconstructed Q and Q# are integral on the displayed model:
Q=(Z,(R0+S0)Z),
Q#=(-Z,(S0-R0)Z),
with Z=P0Q0+R0S0 in Z.
Moreover the square identities become
R0^2+S0^2=B^2,
A^2+R0^2=P0^2,
A^2+S0^2=Q0^2,
A^2+R0^2+S0^2=C^2.                                  (228.11)
Thus any surviving EVEN-n packet already carries an integral perfect-cuboid-shaped six-square packet with edges (A,R0,S0), face diagonals (P0,Q0,B), and space diagonal C.

NEW exact scoped results:
1. every odd prime of the D_n coordinate denominator b lies in AC;
