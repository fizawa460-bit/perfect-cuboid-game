## 1. New exact common depth for Q, Q#, and Ptau
Put
Aq=[M]Q,  Bq=[M]Q#,  Tq=[M]Ptau,  Pm=[M]P,  Rm=[M]R.
All five relevant multiples lie in E_1(Q_q). Since M is even, [M]T0=O.


From dQ=mP and q not dividing m,
d*Aq=m*Pm.
The S200 formal logarithm is valuation-preserving on E_1, hence
e+v_q(z(Aq))=L,
so
v_q(z(Aq))=r.


Also
Aq-Bq=[M](Q-Q#)=[M](T0+nR)=n*Rm.
Because q is odd, doubling preserves formal depth, so
v_q(z(Rm))=v_q(z(Pm))=L.
Therefore
v_q(z(Aq-Bq))=L+v_q(n)=r+e+v_q(n).


This is strictly deeper than Aq because e>=1. The strict isometry of the formal logarithm on differences gives
v_q(z(Aq)-z(Bq))=r+e+v_q(n).
Consequently Bq has the same depth:
v_q(z(Bq))=r.


Finally Aq+Bq=[M](Q+Q#)=[M]Ptau=Tq, and q is odd, so the leading terms add without cancellation because Aq and Bq agree to strictly higher depth. Thus
v_q(z(Tq))=r,
agreeing with the independent S200 reciprocal-depth equality.


Hence the SAME physical packet satisfies the exact three-way depth identity
v_q(z([M]Q))
 =v_q(z([M]Q#))
 =v_q(z([M]Ptau))
 =L-e >=1,
together with the exact separation
v_q(z([M]Q)-z([M]Q#))

Equivalently,
v_q( z([M]Q)/z([M]Q#) - 1 )
 = e+v_q(n).
This is valid in the split high-valuation residual cases q|v_q(C) or q|v_q(H); no coprime-M hypothesis is used.


## 2. Reciprocal-coordinate form
For a formal point on E_int put w=1/X. The exact curve equation and z=-X/Y give
z^2 = w/(1+B^2*w-A^2*C^2*w^2),
equivalently
w=z^2(1+B^2*w-A^2*C^2*w^2).


Thus w=f(z^2) for a unique integral power series
f(T)=T+T^2*g(T),  g(T) in Z_q[[T]].
For Aq and Bq, both z-values have valuation r and their difference has valuation r+s with
s=e+v_q(n)>0.
Because q is odd, z(Aq)+z(Bq) has valuation r, so
v_q(z(Aq)^2-z(Bq)^2)=2r+s.
Since f(T)-f(T')=(T-T') times a q-adic unit for T,T' in q^2 Z_q,
v_q(w(Aq)-w(Bq))=2r+s.
As v_q(w(Aq))=v_q(w(Bq))=2r, this yields the exact normalized congruence
v_q( w([M]Q)/w([M]Q#) - 1 )
 = e+v_q(n).


Because w=1/X, equivalently
v_q( X([M]Q)/X([M]Q#) - 1 )
 = e+v_q(n).


This converts the formal-depth condition into a symmetric x-coordinate condition on the ORIGINAL two chord endpoints.

## 4. Universal even-multiplication collision factor
Since M is even, write M=2N. On
Y^2=X^3+B^2 X^2-A^2 C^2 X,
the duplication map has
X_2(X)=(X^2+A^2 C^2)^2 /
       (4X(X^2+B^2 X-A^2 C^2)).


A direct subtraction gives
X_2(X)-X_2(-X)
=(X^2+A^2 C^2)^2 (X^2-A^2 C^2)
 /(2X((X^2-A^2 C^2)^2-B^4 X^2)).


Hence the canonical unreduced cross-difference for any even multiplication M=2N inherits the collision factor
X^2-A^2 C^2
through the divided difference of the N-multiplication x-map.


At the physical chord input X0=C^2*rho,
X0^2-A^2 C^2
 = C^4(rho^2-a^2).
Using rho=pq+rs and (pq+rs)(pq-rs)=a^2,
rho^2-a^2=2*rho*r*s.
Therefore
X0^2-A^2 C^2=2 C^4 rho r s,
with r^2=-u_n and s^2=beta+u_n from the exact S197 lift.


This identifies a universal physical boundary-collision factor inside the symmetric multiplication receiver. It does NOT prove that this factor alone carries the required q-adic valuation: the remaining divided-difference factor may also contribute, especially in the q-primary component residual.
