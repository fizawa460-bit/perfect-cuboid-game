## 1. Exact sextic-to-quartic factorization
Put L0=A²C²,K0=L0−2B²u−2u²,
H(X)=X³−uX²+[2u(B²+u)−L0]X−L0*u.
The compact chord is H(X)+2vY=0. On the curve, eliminate Y:
H(X)²−4v²f(X)=(X−u)²*[X⁴−2K0 X²+L0²].
This identity follows by coefficient expansion using v²=f(u); verify.py implements independent polynomial multiplication and exact curve group calculations.
In the physical chamber X>AC>0 and u<0, X=u is impossible and v≠0. Thus the quartic is exactly equivalent to existence of a rational curve point on the chord, with Y=−H(X)/(2v), subject to the original Y/sign/chamber checks. Squaring alone is not permitted to forget these checks.

## 2. Uniform integral denominator containment

Z⁴−2KZ²+L²=0.

Consequently the denominator of X divides b². Write X=xi/nu² in lowest terms on this integral monic Weierstrass model; then nu|b. Proof of square denominator: at any negative valuation of X, X³ is the unique lowest-valuation term in f(X), so 2v(Y)=3v(X); v(X) is even.
