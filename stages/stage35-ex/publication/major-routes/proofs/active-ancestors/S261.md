## Step 1: Exact Top Entry Gives Order `4m`

At exact first entry `h=m`, `D_m=T0+2mP` reduces to `O`. Therefore

`2m Pbar = T0`

and `Pbar` has exact order `4m`. This is the top-entry strengthening needed by Section260; it is not implied merely by the Section254 seed divisibility packet.

## Step 2: The Section243 Torsor Halves Role Elements

Section243 gives `Q-P in 2E(Q)`. Reducing this relation at a GOOD prime and using the role reductions:

- If `ell | b_K`, then `Qbar=O`, so `-Pbar in 2E(F_ell)`.
- If `ell | b_J`, then `Qbar=T0`, so `T0-Pbar in 2E(F_ell)`.

The element `-Pbar` has order `4m`. Also `T0-Pbar = (2m-1)Pbar`, since `T0=2mPbar`, and `gcd(2m-1,4m)=1`; hence it also has order `4m`.

If an element of order `4m` is twice another finite-field point, the half has order `8m`. Thus every top-entry GOOD role witness satisfies

`8m | #E(F_ell)`.

## Step 3: Full 2-Torsion Kummer Criterion

Over `F_ell`, with `ell` GOOD and outside the excluded support, the curve has full 2-torsion. For a non-2-torsion point `W`, the standard Kummer criterion is:

`W in 2E(F_ell)` iff `x(W)`, `x(W)-A^2`, and `x(W)+C^2` are all quadratic residues modulo `ell`.

| role | halved element `W` | Kummer coordinates |

## Step 4: Parity Collapse

Denominator primes in the GOOD branch have `ell == 1 mod 4`, so `-1` is a square.

If `A` is even, then in a primitive parametrization `A=2uv` and

`C-A=(u-v)^2`, `C+A=(u+v)^2`.

`(A/ell)=(C/ell)=+1`.

`C-A=2v^2`, `C+A=2u^2`.

`(2A/ell)=(2C/ell)=+1`.
