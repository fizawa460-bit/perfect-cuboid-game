## 3. Two local centers and why a universal local cap cannot be assumed

If w=2sigma and c is a square, define the actual analytic function

  Hloc(eta)=I B u_M(tstar+ell^sigma eta)/ell^(2sigma)
           =-L_M/ell^(2sigma)+eta²G(ell^sigma eta).

Modulo ell this is -c+D0 eta². It has precisely two simple unit roots eta0=±2t0 sqrt(c), with unit derivatives 2D0 eta0. Hensel lifting produces exactly two local centers eta_center. In either residue disk, the analytic divided difference from its center is a unit, giving

  beta=2sigma+v_ell(eta−eta_center).                (258.5)

Outside both disks beta=2sigma. These are zeros/centers of the actual [ell−1]P in the moving conic, not full physical or rational witnesses. This is the S221 point-only center theorem transferred by (258.3).

Conditional barrier: IF such a balanced-square prime and a branch having t_ell divisible by4 exist, then for every prescribed k>=1 one can choose positive integer t>1 with eta at exact ell-adic distance k from one center. Choose t modulo ell^(sigma+k+1) to realize the distance, then arbitrarily large positive integer lifts. The conic (t²−1,2t,t²+1), divided by its gcd1 or2, is a positive primitive integer Pythagorean base. The gcd is an ell-unit, sigma and the reduced nodal order/entry remain fixed, and (258.3)/(258.5) give beta=2sigma+k. Thus such a branch has no local beta upper bound depending only on ell and sigma.

This is a conditional constructive auxiliary statement. ## 5. New known-side bound: critical primes must occur on BOTH roles

Suppose an H-only full packet has NO active critical prime on at least one of b_J,b_K. That side uses only noncritical primes, so its initial exponent product divides T_e; its additional exponent product divides m.   b=b_J b_K<sqrt(C/A)(T_e m)².                    (258.11)

  4lambda m² <= 2log m + (1/2)log(C³T_e^4/A)+c_E/6. (258.12)

  4lambda N²−N−K_role<=0.                         (258.13)

  H-only full packet with m>N_role
