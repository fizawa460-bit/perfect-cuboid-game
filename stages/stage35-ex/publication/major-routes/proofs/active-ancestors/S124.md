124.1 Purpose and correction of the current effectivity gate


Section123 proved that GENERIC_IMAGE_MULTIPLE forces a Zsigmondy-defective denominator index


  M=max(|n|,|k|)+1>=5,


and used a specialization-height existence argument to obtain a uniform but nonnumerical Zsigmondy bound.


This section replaces the nonexplicit family-height existence step by a direct explicit inequality on the retained primitive-Pythagorean models.


The new exact provisional family statement is


  boxed:
  hat h(R) >= (1/400) log |Delta_min(E)|


for every rational t>1 specialization, using the canonical-height normalization


  hat h(P)=lim h(x(mP))/m^2


used by Everest-McLaren-Ward.


Thus the family-specific Lang constant is explicit. The remaining lack of a numerical Z_B is no longer a specialization-height problem; it is the explicit expansion of constants suppressed as O(log Delta) in the published proof of Everest-McLaren-Ward Theorem 2.4.


124.2 Retained primitive Pythagorean models


Let


  A^2+B^2=C^2,
  gcd(A,B)=gcd(B,C)=gcd(C,A)=1,


with C>=5 and exactly one of A,B even. Put


  D=A^2+C^2,
  E4=B^4+3A^2 C^2.


Retain


  R=2P3,


with displayed-model x-coordinate


  x(R)=A^2 C^2/B^2=(AC/B)^2.


Put


  N=A^4+A^2B^2+B^4.


The exact duplication formula gives:


B even:
  x(2R)=N^2/(4A^2B^2C^2).


B odd, on the retained 2-minimal model:
  x(2R)=N^2/(16A^2B^2C^2).


The retained coprimality calculation says N is odd and coprime to ABC, so these fractions are reduced at the root-denominator level.


The two global minimal discriminants are:


B even:
  Delta_even=16 A^4 C^4 D^2.


B odd:
  Delta_odd=A^4 C^4 D^2/256.


The j-invariant in both models is


  j=256 E4^3/(A^4 C^4 D^2).


124.3 Uniform elementary coefficient bounds


Let


  L=log C.


Since A^2+B^2=C^2,


  D<=2C^2.


Write x=A^2/C^2 in [0,1]. Then


  E4/C^4
  =(1-x)^2+3x
  =1+x+x^2
  <=3.


Hence


  E4<=3C^4.


Therefore the rational height of j satisfies


  h(j)<=12L+log(6912),


because


  256 E4^3<=256*27*C^12=6912 C^12


and


  A^4 C^4 D^2<=4C^12.


For the discriminants:


B even:
  log|Delta_even|<=12L+6log2.


B odd:
  log|Delta_odd|<=12L-6log2.


Now


  N/C^4
  =1-x+x^2
  >=3/4,


so


  N>=(3/4)C^4.


Also


  AB<=(A^2+B^2)/2=C^2/2.


Hence


B even:
  2ABC<=C^3<N


for C>=5;


B odd:
  4ABC<=2C^3<N


for C>=5.


Thus in both minimal models the numerator controls the naive x-height of 2R:


  h(2R)=2log N
        >=8L+2log(3/4).


124.4 Explicit Silverman lower bound


Use Silverman's explicit height-difference theorem in the same full x-height normalization used by Everest-McLaren-Ward:


  hat h(Q)-h(Q)
  >= -h(j)/4 - log|Delta_min|/6 - 1.946.


Apply this to Q=2R and use


  hat h(2R)=4 hat h(R).


B even gives


  hat h(R)
  >= (3/4)L + c_even,


where


  c_even
  =( (5/4)log3 - 7log2 - 1.946 )/4
  > -1.357.


B odd gives


  hat h(R)
  >= (3/4)L + c_odd,


where


  c_odd
  =( (5/4)log3 - 5log2 - 1.946 )/4
  > -1.010.


These constants come only from the exact formulas above and Silverman's explicit theorem.


124.5 Uniform explicit Lang constant c=1/400


B odd.


For every primitive triple C>=5. Comparing the previous lower bound with


  (1/400)(12L-6log2),


the difference is increasing in L and already positive at C=5. Hence


  hat h(R) >= (1/400)log|Delta_odd|.


B even, C>=7.


Comparing


  (3/4)L+c_even


with


  (1/400)(12L+6log2),


the difference is increasing in L and positive at C=7. Hence the desired inequality holds for every B-even primitive triple with C>=7.


The only remaining primitive-hypotenuse case below 7 is C=5, necessarily


  (A,B,C)=(3,4,5)


in the B-even orientation.

  D=34,

  h(2R)=2log481.

  hat h(R)>=0.1989...


while


  (1/400)log(936360000)<0.052.


Thus the same inequality holds.


Conclusion:


  boxed:
  hat h_t(R_t) >= (1/400) log|Delta_min(E_t)|


for every rational t>1 primitive specialization.

124.6 Compatibility with Everest-McLaren-Ward


Everest-McLaren-Ward, "Primitive divisors of elliptic divisibility sequences", Journal of Number Theory 118 (2006), Theorem 2.4, assumes a family-uniform inequality


  hat h(P)>=c log|Delta(E)|


on global minimal models.


The exact source statement also requires rational 2-torsion Q and, for the odd Zsigmondy bound, either a real-component condition or


  x(P)-x(Q) is a rational square.


Our family uses


  P=R,
  Q=(0,0),


and


  x(R)-x(Q)
  =(AC/B)^2,


so the odd-index condition is exact.


Section124.5 supplies the theorem with the explicit uniform value


  c=1/400.


Therefore the Section123 conclusion remains valid, now without a nonexplicit family-height input:


there is a finite Z_B independent of t such that every denominator term above Z_B has a primitive divisor, hence every GENERIC_IMAGE_MULTIPLE physical reciprocal pair satisfies


  max(|n|,|k|)<=Z_B-1.


124.7 What is and is not effective after this section


The published proof of Everest-McLaren-Ward Theorem 2.4, Section 5, explicitly states that the needed height comparison has the form


  |hat h(P)-h(P)|<=c log Delta,

  O(log Delta)


terms and says the proof proceeds as before.


Therefore Theorem 2.4 as printed proves that the uniform Zsigmondy bound depends only on c, but does not print a numerical function f(c).

124.8 Verzobio 2023 effectivity audit


Matteo Verzobio, "Some effectivity results for primitive divisors of elliptic divisibility sequences", Pacific Journal of Mathematics 325 (2023), gives an explicit pointwise primitive-divisor bound C(E/K,M), Equation (13).


This is a genuine effective theorem for a fixed curve/model.


However its displayed constant depends on the model through quantities including


  h=max{1,h(1:g2:g3),h(j(E))}


and contains terms such as


  exp(e h),


as well as further curve-specific discriminant/conductor data.


For the moving E3 specialization family these model heights grow with t. The theorem therefore does not by itself give a t-independent Z_B.


Thus:


  VERZOBIO_2023_POINTWISE_EFFECTIVE=true
  VERZOBIO_2023_DIRECT_FAMILY_UNIFORM_ZB_ADAPTER=false.


It remains a possible source for explicit local ingredients, but it does not supersede the EMW family-uniform route.
