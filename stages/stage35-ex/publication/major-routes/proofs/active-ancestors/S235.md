Fix positive primitive A,B,C with A²+B²=C², C odd, and positive rational original edges r,s and diagonals p,q satisfying r²+s²=B², p²=A²+r², q²=A²+s²=C²−r². No finite scalar relation is introduced.
Set X=rs+pq=x(Q)>0. Section233: X=d t², with d>0 squarefree, d|rad(AC), t=u*b_J/b_K>0 rational, and positive u,v integers with d*u*v=AC and b=b_J*b_K, gcd(b_J,b_K)=1. 2. NEW exact two simultaneous rational square identities
Let L=rq+sp and M=rp−sq. Direct expansion, using p²−r²=q²−s²=A², gives
X²−L²=(p²−r²)(q²−s²)=A⁴. (235.1)
Direct expansion, using C²=A²+r²+s², gives
X²+M²=(A²+r²+s²)²=C⁴. (235.2)
Therefore L²=X²−A⁴ and M²=C⁴−X². In particular physical rational points lie in the STRICT interval A²<X<C²; X>A² because L=rq+sp>0, and X<C² because M≠0. To see M≠0, rp=sq would yield r²(A²+r²)=s²(A²+s²), hence (r²−s²)(A²+r²+s²)=0. This forces r=s, but 2r²=B² has no positive rational r when B is a nonzero integer; contradiction.
These are global exact identities, independent of the orbit n and rank, not numerical evidence.
3. NEW universal genus-one intermediate curve independent of d
Define the smooth projective normalization G_(A,C) of
L²=X²−A⁴; M²=C⁴−X². (235.3)
Over Qbar, the two independent squareclasses have distinct simple branch sets X=±A² and X=±C². Hence G→P¹_X is a connected degree-4 (Z/2)^2 cover with 4 geometric simple branch values (each contributing two ramification points). Riemann–Hurwitz gives 2g−2=4*(-2)+4*2=0, so g(G)=1. It may have no rational point a priori, so call it a genus-one curve/torsor, not an elliptic curve with a chosen rational origin.
Every original physical packet maps explicitly to (X,L,M)∈G(Q). This is a NECESSARY intermediate receiver, not a converse characterization.
4. NEW fixed-d genus-five intermediate curve and branching
For d squarefree |rad(AC), let S_d be the smooth projective normalization of
X=d t², L²=X²−A⁴, M²=C⁴−X². (235.4)
The map S_d→G has generic degree2, obtained by adjoining sqrt(X/d). The function X on G has four simple geometric zeros at X=0 and four simple geometric poles over X=∞ (both are unramified in G→P¹_X); the quadratic extension is branched at exactly those 8 points. Riemann–Hurwitz gives 2g(S_d)−2=2*(2*1−2)+8=8, so g(S_d)=5. Equivalently S_d→P¹_t has degree4 and eight simple geometric branch values at d t²=±A²,±C², giving g=5 directly.
For fixed base, there are finitely many such d. Thus a first-stage rational-point search may be performed on GENUS-FIVE S_d rather than requiring the full genus-nine curve immediately. This still does not compute S_d(Q) or prove physical emptiness.
5. NEW geometric factorization: Section234 genus-nine cover is an étale double lift
Normalize Section234 t to the same t=u*(b_J/b_K), and normalize its three original quartic-root variables by multiplying them by u. Then the Section234 genus-nine curve C_d has three radicals
Z1²=(X−A²)(X+C²)/d,
Z2²=(C²−X)(X+A²)/d,
Z3²=(X+A²)(X+C²)/d, with X=d t². (235.5)
The products Z1*Z3 and Z2*Z3 recover the genus-five radicals:
L=d*Z1*Z3/(X+C²);
M=d*Z2*Z3/(X+A²). (235.6)
Conversely, over S_d, adjoining
Z3²=(X+A²)(X+C²)/d (235.7)
recovers C_d generically, with Z1 and Z2 determined rationally from (235.6). The extension is connected of degree2 because Section234 established three independent radicals over Qbar(t).
It is everywhere UNRAMIFIED over Qbar: (X+A²)(X+C²) has even valuation at every geometric place of S_d. At X=−A² and X=−C², S_d→P¹_X is already ramified of order2, so each relevant zero has even order; at X=∞, X has pole order2 on S_d, so the product has pole order4; elsewhere valuations vanish. Therefore C_d→S_d is a connected degree-2 ÉTALE cover. By Riemann–Hurwitz, 2*9−2=2*(2*5−2), consistent with the earlier genus-nine calculation.
Thus the exact tower is
C_d (geometric genus9) —étale degree2→ S_d (genus5) —degree2 branched at eight points→ G_(A,C) (genus1) —degree4→ P¹_X. (235.8)
The extra Z3-square condition is a genuine 2-covering lift / Jacobian-2-torsion obstruction to test on S_d(Q); a rational S_d-point need not lift to a rational C_d-point. Conversely, the original physical packet supplies all of L,M,t,Z1,Z2,Z3 and hence lifts, subject also to original positivity/integrality/orbit/Kummer masks. This is a rigorous change of strategy, NOT closure.
6. NEW uniform 2-adic restriction when B is even
In the B-even case of a primitive Pythagorean triple, A,C are odd and v2(B)>=2. Write original r=R/b,s=S/b,p=U/b,q=V/b with the Section232 common reduced denominator b odd. From R²+S²=B²b² and v2(B)>=2 it follows 4|R and 4|S (otherwise reducing by the minimum power of 2 makes the sum of two integer squares have 2-adic valuation 0 or1, incompatible with 16|B²b²). From U²−R²=A²b² and V²+R²=C²b², U,V are odd. Thus J=UV+RS is odd and X=J/b² is a 2-adic unit.
Meanwhile L=rq+sp=(RV+SU)/b² lies in 4 Z_2, so (235.1) gives
X²≡A⁴≡1 (mod16); hence X≡+1 or −1 (mod8). (235.9)
Section233 gives X=d*(u*b_J/b_K)², where d,u,b_J,b_K are ALL odd here (AC odd and b odd); any odd 2-adic unit square ≡1 mod8. Consequently
d≡+1 or −1 (mod8) whenever B is even. (235.10)
Therefore in this case squarefree divisors d|rad(AC) with d≡3 or5 mod8 are UNIFORMLY IMPOSSIBLE for original physical packets. This is a genuine arithmetic elimination of squareclass subroutes, not all d and not rank-growth itself.
Example filtering only (NOT a physical solution): (A,B,C)=(3,4,5), AC=15, squarefree d∈{1,3,5,15}, so only d=1 or15 survive the universal mod8 gate. (A,B,C)=(5,12,13), AC=65, squarefree d∈{1,5,13,65}, so only d=1 or65 survive. No claim that either survivor has any physical point.
