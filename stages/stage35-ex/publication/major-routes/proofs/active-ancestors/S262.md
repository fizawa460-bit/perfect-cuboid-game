    ell == 1 mod 4, chi_ell(cA)=chi_ell(cC)=+1.       (262.1)

## 3. Both integral boundary charts

On role J put alpha=z, with z in ell Z_ell. The four unit radicands are

    (-A+Cz^2, A+Cz^2, C-Az^2, C+Az^2)/c.

On role K put alpha=1/z and v_i=z u_i. The radicands for the v_i are

    (C-Az^2, C+Az^2, -A+Cz^2, A+Cz^2)/c.

All four constants have square roots modulo ell exactly when (262.1)
holds: A/c and C/c have the same squareclasses as cA and cC, and the
simultaneous A/c,-A/c roots also force -1 to be a square. At either
boundary the four roots are nonzero, so each has two simple choices.
Hensel lifting gives 16 analytic branches over Z_ell on each disk.
Every coefficient is integral because 2 and the root constants are units.

For J write T=c u1u4, W=-c u2u3. For K use the scaled products
T=c v1v4, W=-c v2v3. In both charts (262.5) becomes the SAME integral formula

    f(z)=t(Phi(z))=z(W(z)-T(z))/(D(1+z^4)).          (262.6)

Their constant squares and derivatives are

| Role | T(0)^2 | W(0)^2 | f'(0) |
|---|---|---|---|
| J | -D | D | (W(0)-T(0))/D = 2/(T(0)+W(0)) |
| K | D | -D | (W(0)-T(0))/D = -2/(T(0)+W(0)) |

Neither T(0)-W(0) nor T(0)+W(0) can vanish modulo ell: their equality
would imply D=-D, impossible since ell is odd and ell does not divide D.
Hence f'(0) is a unit for EVERY branch. Also f(z)=z times a unit,
so valuations are preserved exactly.

## 4. Full disk bijection, not merely a first-order heuristic

On each chosen branch f is an integral convergent analytic function,
f(0)=0 and f'(z)=f'(0) modulo ell throughout ell Z_ell. Given any
tau in ell Z_ell, start with z=0 modulo ell. Suppose z solves
f(z)=tau modulo ell^k. The next digit z+d ell^k is uniquely determined by

    d f'(0) = (tau-f(z))/ell^k mod ell.

The unit derivative gives one digit at every step. The compatible sequence
converges to a unique z in ell Z_ell with f(z)=tau. The formal parameter
t identifies the disk at O with ell Z_ell, including on the nonsingular
identity neighborhood of the nodal H fiber. Therefore equality of these
formal parameters is equality of the POINTS Phi(z)=R, not merely equality
of x squareclasses. This proves the stated unbounded-depth theorem.

For R!=O, z!=0, so alpha and all original formulas are defined. All four
root numerators in (262.3), in their appropriate scaled chart, are units;
they have the same denominator depth v_ell(t(R)). At J, Q and Qsharp reduce
to T0; at K they reduce to O. The local role and depth masks are preserved.
