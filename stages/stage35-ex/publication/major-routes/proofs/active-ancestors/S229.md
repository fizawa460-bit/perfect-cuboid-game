## 1. Exact local A/C formulas with no assumption q|d
Let q be ANY odd prime dividing A or C, put e=v_q(AC)>0. The primitive conic gives q∤B and q divides exactly one of A,C. Set D=AC, S=D_1=T0+R=(-B^2,-BD), and W=2R=4P.

v_q(z(W))=e,  z=-x/y.                              (229.2)
This uses only the displayed minimal odd-prime model and the concrete W, NOT q|d. The same proof applies at A and C. The preceding S203/S205 calculations were originally scoped to q|d on the C-side, but their local identities contain no use of q|d in the portions reused here.
The point S has integral q-unit x=-B^2, reduces to a smooth nonidentity point (the reduced singularity at q|A/C is x=0). Since W is formal, any [j]W is formal and adding it to S preserves its smooth finite q-unit reduction.
For odd n=2j+1, the exact group identity is
