## 2. Retained packet and notation

## 3. GOOD first-entry adapter on the original torsion coset

Let ell be an odd prime outside 2ABCH. The displayed model has good reduction at ell, P and T0 reduce nonsingularly, and denominator divisibility for D_n is equivalent to D_n reducing to O.

If ell|b_n, then

T0+2nP = O mod ell,
so 2nP=T0 mod ell and 4nP=O mod ell. The cyclic subgroup generated by P has a unique element of order 2. Therefore ord(P mod ell)=4h and n/h is an odd integer. Conversely, if ord(P mod ell)=4h and n/h is odd, then 2nP=T0 and D_n reduces to O, so ell|b_n.

Set q=n/h, q odd, and Q_h=D_h=T0+2hP. Since 2hP=Q_h+T0 and q is odd,

D_n=T0+2qhP=T0+q(Q_h+T0)=qQ_h.                        (260.4)

The integral formal group at O gives [q](z)=qz+z^2 Z_ell[[z]]. Thus if ell has first entry h,

v_ell(b_n)=v_ell(b_h)+v_ell(q).                       (260.5)

Consequently all extra powers ell^{v_ell(q)} over any set of active GOOD primes divide m. This is the GOOD analogue of the retained H-entry extra-power law, proved directly on the same D_n=T0+2nP orbit. It does not assert that a GOOD prime is physical by itself; it is only a denominator support adapter.

## 4. Full-packet role theorem

Claim. Suppose a complete full packet has a denominator role, J or K, containing no active exact top-entry h=m prime of either of the following types:

- GOOD prime ell outside 2ABCH;
- ACTUAL critical H prime ell in X_e.

The role may still contain noncritical H primes with h=m, critical H primes with proper h<m, and GOOD primes with proper h<m. Then

(52/49)*lambda*m^2
 <= log(m)+(1/4)log(C^3*T_e^4/A)+c_E/12+(m*c_E)/12.   (260.6)

Proof. Noncritical H initial powers on the chosen role are bounded by T_e, including any noncritical H top entries. By Section3 and the retained H laws, all extra index powers from active GOOD/H primes on the role together divide m.

Every unbounded initial factor not absorbed by T_e has a proper first entry h<m. Such h are of the form h=m/q with q a proper odd divisor q>=3. Distinct h give distinct q, hence

S_proper=sum (h/m)^2 <= sum_{odd q>=3} q^{-2}
=pi^2/8-1 < 23/98,                                  (260.7)

using pi<22/7 for the final strict rational bound. Equal-entry primes are charged once by b_h.

The number of possible proper odd quotients is at most floor(m/2), so the accumulated c_E/6 terms are at most m*c_E/12. Thus for the chosen role,

log b_role
 <= log(T_e*m)+4*S_proper*m^2*hhat(P)+(m*c_E)/12.     (260.8)

The full role chamber (260.1) gives both sides

log b_role > (1/2)log b -(1/4)log(C/A)
 >= 2m^2*hhat(P)-(1/2)log C-c_E/12-(1/4)log(C/A).    (260.9)

Combining (260.8) and (260.9),

(2-4*S_proper)m^2*hhat(P)
 < log(m)+(1/4)log(C^3*T_e^4/A)+c_E/12+(m*c_E)/12.

Since S_proper<23/98 and hhat(P)>=lambda, the safe weak inequality (260.6) follows. QED.

## 5. Computable top-entry threshold and receiver

Define

Z_top=ceil(C^3*T_e^4/A),
K_top=bit_length(Z_top)/4+c_E/12,

and let N_top be the largest integer N>=0 satisfying

(52/49)*lambda*N^2 - (1/2+c_E/12)*N - K_top <= 0.     (260.10)

This threshold is finite because lambda>0. It follows from (260.6), log(m)<=m/2 and log(Z_top)<=bit_length(Z_top).

Therefore every complete full packet satisfies

m<=N_top
OR an exact top-entry GOOD denominator prime h=m occurs
OR two distinct exact top-entry ACTUAL critical H primes h=m occur on opposite roles.   (260.11)

The more precise role statement is: for m>N_top, each denominator role contains at least one exact top-entry prime of type GOOD or ACTUAL critical H. Since gcd(b_J,b_K)=1, if no GOOD top-entry prime occurs, the two role witnesses are distinct critical H primes with the same first entry h=m. This recovers the Section259 exception type in the H-only case.

Important limits:

- N_top need not numerically improve Section259's N_equal or S258's N_total; it has a new linear c_E*m term.
- Section259 remains the sharper H-only critical theorem.
- A GOOD top-entry prime is only a necessary support witness, not a full-packet point and not emptiness.
- All receivers must still be intersected with S259/S258/S257/S256/S253/S254/S255, compulsory H poison, S237 allocation and S243 full lift/infinite-Q masks.
