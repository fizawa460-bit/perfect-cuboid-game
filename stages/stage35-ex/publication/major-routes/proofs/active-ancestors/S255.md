## 3. Parity-sensitive exact strong gcd law

For nonzero signed d,e, let g=gcd(|d|,|e|). Then

  gcd(b_d,b_e)=b_g if v2(|d|)=v2(|e|);
  gcd(b_d,b_e)=1 otherwise.                                  (255.2)

For equal valuations, d/g and e/g are odd. S254 odd propagation gives b_g|b_d,b_e. Choose Bezout integers a,c with ad+ce=g. Reducing after division by g modulo2 gives a+c odd, so

  aD_d+cD_e=(a+c)T0+2gP=D_g.

At a common denominator prime ell, the points D_d,D_e lie in the integral formal filtration of depths v_ell(b_d),v_ell(b_e). Addition and integer multiplication keep the minimum depth. Therefore v_ell(b_g)>=min(v_ell(b_d),v_ell(b_e)); the opposite inequality follows from divisibility. This proves the exact equality, including prime powers.

For unequal valuations, assume a common prime ell. By (255.1), ell is odd and outside2ABC. If ell∤H use the good reduction group; if ell|H, use the smooth split-nodal group.
