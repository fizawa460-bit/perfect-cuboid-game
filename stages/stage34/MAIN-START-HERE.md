## Stage34 proof firewall

A primitive divisor existence theorem alone is insufficient. Closure requires:

1. the exact EXT-C recurrence/divisor object and admissible index set;
2. primitive-divisor existence beyond an explicit threshold;
3. a proof that a required primitive divisor occurs with **odd multiplicity**,
   or an equivalent parity obstruction;
4. certified finite discharge below the threshold and of all exceptional cases;
5. an exact adapter from that divisor/parity fact back to the
   `R29-EXT-CHANG-C` parent-edge/gcd/representability obstruction;
6. an all-multiples conclusion for the same audited moving/thin family.

Do not promote closure of this family to Master-Hit coverage or perfect-cuboid
nonexistence. Stage29 explicitly records that this kernel is narrow-specific and
not endpoint-decisive alone.
