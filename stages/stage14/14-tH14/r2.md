## 4. Exact one-state squareclass compression after the legal local stage

Let `z` denote the retained exact physical/Gaussian label after the preceding roadworks.  It still remembers `R`, `U`, `V`, branch/orientation/reconstruction data and the hyperbola provenance.  Let its coefficient be `A_z`.

\[

\tag{H14R2.6}

Now form a **single-state** coefficient, with the signed common-refinement sum kept inside the coefficient,

\[
\boxed{

\tag{H14R2.7}

\[
\boxed{

\tag{H14R2.8}

Define the weighted physical squareclass-fiber energy

\boxed{

\tag{H14R2.9}
\]

The crucial point is that tH5 controls `E_A`, not `E_sq(A)`.

## 5. New proved adapter: dual quadratic large sieve on the product rows

Let

\[
p,q\in[L,2L],
\qquad p\ne q,
\qquad p\equiv q\equiv1\pmod4,
\]

and let `P=#mathcal P`.  Every unordered pair gives one odd squarefree integer

\[

\]

Each unordered product occurs exactly twice in the ordered `(p,q)` sum.  Therefore

\[
\mathcal M(A)
\le
2\sum_{\substack{n\le4L^2\\n\ {\rm odd\ squarefree}}}
\left|\sum_D C_D\chi_D(n)\right|^2.
\tag{H14R2.10}

\tag{H14R2.11}

where

\[
K=\max_{C_D\ne0}|D|.

This is a genuine theorem/adapter.  It uses no ordered state-pair coefficient and no fourth energy.

## 7. Why this is the minimal obstruction: an alias-free coherent-fiber countermodel

The old mod-13 subset countermodel showed that complete cancellation does not imply subset cancellation.  R2 records a stronger countermodel that survives t51's no-alias diagonal.

Take `r` distinct exact Gaussian labels

\[

\]

which are pairwise distinct modulo every active product `pq`, but suppose

\[
[\widetilde F(z_1)]
=\cdots=
[\widetilde F(z_r)]
=\kappa_0

with the common squareclass nonzero at all active primes.  Take unit weights on those labels.

Then the exact-pair coefficient energy is only

\[
E_A=r,
\]

and there is no residue alias at all.  Nevertheless every trace column is identical:

\[
\Phi_{p,q}(z_j)=\chi_{\kappa_0}(pq).

Hence for every ordered distinct prime pair

\[

and

\[
\boxed{

\tag{H14R2.19}

The desired RHS is only `P^2 r B^o(1)`.

Therefore:


unless the actual physical geometry rules out or cancels large same-squareclass fibers.

This countermodel also shows that any universal weighted theorem of the form (H14R2.1) necessarily contains a squareclass-fiber anti-coherence statement equivalent in strength to (H14R2.17) on one-class-supported coefficients.
