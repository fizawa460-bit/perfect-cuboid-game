# Stage14-toolbox-au — adapter certificates and promotion checklist

Stage14-toolbox-au consumes merged toolbox-at, 14-4cf and t57. It does not prove either live analytic estimate; it makes every later promotion auditable and rejects partial certificates.

## Current receivers

The s-side refinement is now `BalancedFourHostGaussianSquareDivisorIncidence`. A valid certificate must retain the same physical pair, all eight cells, the four hosts `Z_beta,Z_gamma,Z_S,Z_T`, the four oriented Gaussian square divisors and residual norms. Fixed-host divisor multiplicity is not a moving-family saving.

The fixed-U invisible receiver is now `SharedUPhysicalToroidalMellinCorrelation`. t57 has already proved the rank-one Kummer certificate, all-order Mellin packet, bounded spectral L2 energy and zero-loss kernel-side two-prime tensorization. These are imported prerequisites, not the missing estimate. Promotion requires correlation of the actual centered physical selector with the toroidal modes, uniformly with divisor fan, moving pi,V,delta and all masks retained.

The invisible/visible mixed packet remains a separate certificate.

## Promotion rule

A receiver may be marked proved only when one certificate contains: exact source stages; identical physical coefficient space and centering; uniform parameter range; exceptional/bad-prime treatment; quantitative target with no fixed-power recombination loss; and regression evidence. Every required Boolean must be true. Structural identities cannot fill analytic-estimate fields.

## Decisions

The s and fixed-U lines remain disjoint and parallel-safe. Neither waits for toolbox-H. No additional toolbox-H line is needed: 14-4cf identifies a receiver, while t57 resolves its new algebraic ambiguity internally. Reconsider H only if 4cg or t58 invokes a genuinely external theorem with unverified hypotheses.
