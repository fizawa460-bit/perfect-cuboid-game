## 2. X36 abstract principle — threshold localization before arithmetic comparison

Let one already-charged branch require physical support or mass exponent at least `mu`. Suppose a legal ambient family has an exact scale parameter `lambda` and deterministic capacity exponent `c(lambda)`.

If

```text
c(lambda) <= mu-eta
```

for some fixed `eta>0`, then that scale is closed before any correlation or distribution theorem is charged.

If the branch survives, one may restrict to the threshold region

```text

```

A theorem that covers only a subrange of this threshold region may discharge that subrange, but the uncovered threshold region remains a principal-scale receiver.
