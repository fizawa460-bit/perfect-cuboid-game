# Stage14-4bc notation lock

In `14-4bc/result.md`, the symbol

```text
e = rad_odd(E)
```

is used in the **squareclass-kernel** sense:

```text
e = product of odd primes p with v_p(E) odd,
```

so that `e` is squarefree and

```text
E = e*t^2
```

for an integer `t` after removing the 2-adic squareclass separately.

It is **not** the ordinary radical `product_{p|E} p` when an odd prime occurs to even valuation. All whole-E Jacobi identities and the split `e=e_23*e_0` transfer use this squareclass-kernel convention.
