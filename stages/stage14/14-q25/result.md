## Unit stratum

The exact unit-stratum reduction is a Dirichlet-character expansion modulo `Q=2UV`, but the weight is not an ordinary unconstrained divisor sum: it retains the filtered-tau3 witness, the moving common-core average, the two coprime side hosts, and all frozen q17 kernel predicates.

Nearby primary-source architectures include character-twisted divisor sums over binary forms (Frei--Sofos, arXiv:1609.04002) and distribution of divisor-type functions in arithmetic progressions. They do not directly prove that the total nonprincipal contribution is uniformly smaller than the principal mass on every Stage14 principal cell.
