# Literature radar — 14-t

Current frontier: Stage14-t is waiting for concrete `M`-degree-4 bisections from `14-4`. Generic restriction of the third-square double cover to such a rational bisection has branch degree 8 and genus 3; only special branch contact can lower this to genus 0/1.

## DIRECT / NEAR

### Katz--Rabinoff--Zureick-Brown — Uniform bounds for rational points on curves of small Mordell--Weil rank
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.00694
- Theorem: explicit uniform bounds for `#X(F)` when the Jacobian Mordell--Weil rank satisfies `r <= g-3`
- Gives: unconditional uniform rational-point bounds for genus `g>=2` curves over fixed-degree number fields in the small-rank range.
- Stage14 hook: a generic restricted triple lift over an `M`-degree-4 bisection has genus `3`; if its Jacobian rank can be forced to `0`, this theorem gives a genuine uniform fixed-curve point bound independent of the individual genus-3 curve.
- Fit: NEAR
- Missing: Stage14 currently has no rank-zero theorem for the genus-3 lift Jacobians across the relevant bisections, and a per-curve bound must still be summed over the bisection family under physical height.
- Checked: 2026-08-09

### Ezome--Miayoka Moussolo--Freguin Babindamana — Genus 3 curves from the rank-0 quotient strategy
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.03986
- Theorem: algorithmic rational-point determination for genus-3 curves admitting a degree-2 map to a genus-1 curve with rank-zero Jacobian
- Gives: a concrete rank-zero elliptic-quotient strategy for determining all rational points on certain genus-3 curves.
- Stage14 hook: if a restricted triple lift inherits a usable elliptic quotient after a special branch-contact pattern, this is a practical template for eliminating or exactly computing its rational lifts.
- Fit: NEAR
- Missing: the generic Stage14 genus-3 restriction is only known as a double cover of a rational bisection; an elliptic quotient and rank-zero property are not automatic and must be constructed case by case.
- Checked: 2026-08-09

### Dimitrov--Gao--Habegger — Uniform bound for rational points on a pencil of curves
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.07268
- Theorem: family-level rational-point bound depending on the Mordell--Weil rank of the fiber Jacobian
- Gives: for a one-parameter family of smooth projective genus `>=2` curves over a number field, the number of rational points on a fiber is bounded in terms of the family and the Jacobian rank.
- Stage14 hook: remains the cleanest general interface for converting control of the moving genus-5/genus-3 Jacobian ranks into a global per-fiber point bound.
- Fit: NEAR
- Missing: no uniform rank control is proved for the Stage14 moving Jacobians; the physical-height sum over bases/bisections remains separate.
- Checked: 2026-08-09

## BLOCKED

### Gillibert--Hallouin--Levin — Counting rational points over function fields
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.13292
- Theorem: bounded-height point counts for elliptic and hyperelliptic curves over function fields via descent and Riemann--Roch
- Gives: recent quantitative point-counting technology for curves over function fields.
- Stage14 hook: conceptually close to treating the moving triple family before specialization.
- Fit: BLOCKED
- Missing: Stage14 ultimately counts `Q`-rational points on number-field fibers under the physical cuboid height; the function-field theorem does not directly supply the specialized arithmetic bound needed here.
- Checked: 2026-08-09
