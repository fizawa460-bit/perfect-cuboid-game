### Shimada — The elliptic modular surface of level 4 and its reduction modulo 3
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.05787
- Theorem: Theorem 1.1 gives the six `I_4` fibers and `(Z/4Z)^2` torsion; Theorem 1.4 records the Keum--Kondo automorphism generators; Section 2 explicitly constructs the Neron--Severi embedding; Remark 1.3 gives an algorithmic test for degree-2 polarization vectors and contracted `(-2)` classes.
- Gives: the exact elliptic modular surface already identified with Stage14, with Picard number 20 and explicit Neron--Severi/automorphism machinery. Crucially, the paper states that fixed lattice bases, vectors, and matrices used in the calculations are supplied as separate computational data on Shimada's author site because they are too large for the article.
- Stage14 hook: this is the same level-4 modular K3 used in `14-4ag`. The `14-4ai` conditions `C^2=-2`, fiber degree `F.C=2`, and physical degree `M.C=4` should therefore be translated directly into Shimada's fixed lattice coordinates. The external computational-data package is now the highest-priority artifact to acquire; it may remove the need to reconstruct the NS Gram matrix manually.
- Fit: NEAR (highest priority; one artifact away from potentially DIRECT computational use)
- Missing: the Stage14 classes `F` and `M` still have to be identified in Shimada's basis, and the author-site computational data timed out in the current web fetch. Once obtained, test whether the three numerical conditions reduce to finitely many automorphism/chamber orbits and then audit fields of definition/physicality.
