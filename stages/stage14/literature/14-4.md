# Literature radar — 14-4

Current frontier: classify `Q`-rational physical `M`-degree-4 bisections on the Stage14 Kummer/K3 surface and determine whether they account for the observed `sqrt(B)` first-hit signal.

## DIRECT / NEAR

### Clingher--Malmendier — Neron-Severi lattices of Jacobian elliptic K3 surfaces
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.01929
- Theorem: classification results for Jacobian elliptic fibrations in specified Neron-Severi lattice classes; exact theorem/table entry to verify before import
- Gives: lattice-theoretic classification and multiplicities of Jacobian elliptic fibrations for several K3 classes with finite Mordell--Weil behavior.
- Stage14 hook: `14-4ai` is naturally a lattice enumeration problem for classes with `C^2=-2`, fiber degree `2`, and physical degree `M.C=4`; this paper is a direct methodological route for translating elliptic-fibration questions into Neron-Severi constraints.
- Fit: NEAR
- Missing: the Stage14 level-4 singular K3 must be matched to one of the paper's classified lattice classes, and the paper does not directly enumerate the specific `M.C=4` rational bisections over `Q`.
- Checked: 2026-08-09

### Greer--Helfer--Sheridan — Severi curves of rational elliptic surfaces
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.03200
- Theorem: main results on parameter spaces of rational bisections of elliptic fibrations on rational elliptic surfaces; exact numbering to verify before import
- Gives: structural results for Severi curves parametrizing rational bisections, including connectedness/reducedness and genus bounds in the rational-elliptic-surface setting.
- Stage14 hook: confirms that rational bisections admit a natural Severi/moduli treatment and may suggest how to organize the `14-4ai` bisection classification rather than solving ad hoc equations.
- Fit: NEAR
- Missing: their surface is a rational elliptic surface from a general cubic pencil, whereas Stage14 is a special elliptic K3; no direct theorem transfers without adapting the surface and degree data.
- Checked: 2026-08-09

### Tayou — Rational curves on elliptic K3 surfaces
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.07975
- Theorem: main rational-curve existence theorem; exact numbering to verify before import
- Gives: non-isotrivial elliptic K3 surfaces contain infinitely many rational curves (with characteristic-zero existence covered by earlier work as noted there).
- Stage14 hook: confirms that rational curves on the Stage14 elliptic K3 are structurally natural and should not be treated as a finite exceptional phenomenon.
- Fit: NEAR
- Missing: existence is far weaker than the needed classification of `Q`-rational physical curves with the minimal physical degree `M.C=4`; it gives neither field of definition nor `M`-degree nor first-hit counting.
- Checked: 2026-08-09

### Chen — Rational Curves on K3 Surfaces
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9804075
- Theorem: existence/nodality results for rational curves in linear systems on general K3 surfaces; exact theorem number to verify before import
- Gives: rational-curve existence in prescribed linear systems for general K3 surfaces.
- Stage14 hook: potentially useful methodological background for interpreting low-degree linear systems on a K3.
- Fit: BACKGROUND
- Missing: Stage14 surface is a very special Kummer/elliptic K3 over `Q`, not a general polarized K3, and the problem is arithmetic classification of rational bisections rather than geometric existence over an algebraic closure.
- Checked: 2026-08-09
