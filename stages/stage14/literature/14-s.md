# Literature radar — 14-s

Current frontier: explain the first-small-point gate after Stage14-s3: physical hit `d<=B` forces a non-torsion elliptic point in a logarithmic canonical-height window, but no uniform least-generator/small-point distribution theorem has been imported.

## DIRECT / NEAR

### Le Boudec — Height of rational points on congruent number elliptic curves
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.07136
- Theorem: Theorem 1
- Gives: a positive proportion of squarefree congruent-number twists have a strong lower bound on the canonical height of the lowest non-torsion rational point; the proof explicitly uses complete 2-descent and a large-prime-factor sieve.
- Stage14 hook: this is the closest structural analogue found so far to the actual `14-s3` missing object: not rank, not total point multiplicity, but the *lowest non-torsion rational point* across a moving family with 2-descent structure.
- Fit: NEAR (high priority method transfer)
- Missing: Le Boudec works in a fixed quadratic-twist family (`j` constant), whereas Stage14 has nonconstant `j` and a Pythagorean-base family. The large-prime-factor/2-descent counting argument would have to be rebuilt for the Stage14 split Kummer equations; the theorem itself cannot be imported directly.
- Checked: 2026-08-09

### Petsche — Small rational points on elliptic curves over number fields
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0508160
- Theorem: quantitative Hindry--Silverman refinements (main theorems; exact numbering to extract before import)
- Gives: explicit polynomial-dependence lower bounds for the canonical height of non-torsion rational points in terms of field degree and the Szpiro ratio, plus torsion bounds.
- Stage14 hook: could convert arithmetic invariants of the integral family `W^2=Z(Z-S^2)(Z+X^2)` into a uniform *lower* canonical-height barrier, complementary to `14-s3`'s upper window for a physical hit.
- Fit: NEAR
- Missing: the resulting lower bound must be computed along the Stage14 Pythagorean family and shown strong enough to thin the base count. A generic lower bound by itself may be far below the logarithmic physical window and hence give no asymptotic saving.
- Checked: 2026-08-09

### Naccarato — Counting rational points on elliptic curves with rational 2-torsion
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.04032
- Theorem: main bounded-height point-counting theorem
- Gives: bounded-height rational-point bounds for elliptic curves over `Q` with rational 2-torsion, adjacent to the Stage14 full-2-torsion family.
- Stage14 hook: alternate uniform per-fiber multiplicity control.
- Fit: NEAR
- Missing: the bottleneck is least physical point over moving bases, not total multiplicity.
- Checked: 2026-08-09

### Loughran--Salgado — Rank jumps on elliptic surfaces and the Hilbert property
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.01987
- Theorem: rank-jump non-thinness results under geometric hypotheses
- Gives: qualitative abundance of rank-jump fibers on suitable elliptic surfaces.
- Stage14 hook: separates rank-jump frequency from the stronger first-small-point condition.
- Fit: NEAR
- Missing: no physical-height count or least-generator control.
- Checked: 2026-08-09

### Wong — Heights and the Specialization Map for Families of Elliptic Curves over P^n
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.3255
- Theorem: main specialization-height theorem
- Gives: canonical-height control for specialization of fixed generic sections.
- Stage14 hook: quantitative family/base-height bridge.
- Fit: NEAR
- Missing: rank-jump points in Stage14 do not arise from a fixed generic non-torsion section.
- Checked: 2026-08-09

## BLOCKED

### Melistas — Low rank specializations of elliptic surfaces
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.02419
- Theorem: upper bounds on specialization rank for infinitely many parameters under a 2-torsion/discriminant hypothesis
- Gives: infinitely many low-rank specializations in certain non-isotrivial elliptic families with rational 2-torsion.
- Stage14 hook: superficially close to the Stage14 rank-jump family.
- Fit: BLOCKED for current goal
- Missing: controlling infinitely many low-rank fibers does not count the high-rank/positive-rank fibers with a *small first point* under physical height, so it does not close `V(B)`.
- Checked: 2026-08-09

### Silverman — Lehmer-type lower bound over function fields
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14771
- Theorem: main Lehmer-type lower bound
- Gives: explicit positive lower bounds over function fields.
- Stage14 hook: resembles a generator-height barrier.
- Fit: BLOCKED
- Missing: Stage14 counts specialized `Q`-fibers, not function-field points.
- Checked: 2026-08-09
