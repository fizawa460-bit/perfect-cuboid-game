# Literature radar — 14-s

Current frontier: explain the first-small-point gate after Stage14-s3: physical hit `d<=B` forces a non-torsion elliptic point in a logarithmic canonical-height window, but no uniform least-generator/small-point distribution theorem has been imported.

## DIRECT / NEAR

### Naccarato — Counting rational points on elliptic curves with rational 2-torsion
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.04032
- Theorem: main bounded-height point-counting theorem; exact numbering to verify before import
- Gives: for elliptic curves over `Q` with a rational 2-torsion point, bounded-height rational points satisfy a subpolynomial exponential upper bound; the paper also records the stronger full-2-torsion Bombieri--Zannier setting and a Petsche-based refinement.
- Stage14 hook: every Stage14-s fiber has full rational 2-torsion, so this is directly adjacent to the existing uniform per-fiber multiplicity input and may provide a second route to show that multiplicity cannot create a positive power of `B`.
- Fit: NEAR
- Missing: the present bottleneck is not total point multiplicity on a fixed fiber but the distribution of the *least physical non-torsion point* over moving Pythagorean bases; coefficient/discriminant dependence must also be made uniform in the Stage14 family.
- Checked: 2026-08-09

### Loughran--Salgado — Rank jumps on elliptic surfaces and the Hilbert property
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.01987
- Theorem: rank-jump non-thinness results under explicit geometric hypotheses; exact theorem number to verify before import
- Gives: for suitable elliptic surfaces over number fields, sets of fibers with Mordell--Weil rank above generic rank can be non-thin.
- Stage14 hook: Stage14-s counts positive-rank specializations of a generic-rank-zero elliptic K3 and must distinguish rank-jump frequency from the stronger first-small-point condition.
- Fit: NEAR
- Missing: non-thinness is qualitative and does not give the physical-height counting law or first-generator height; applicability of the paper's geometric hypotheses to the level-4 Stage14 K3 must be checked.
- Checked: 2026-08-09

### Wong — Heights and the Specialization Map for Families of Elliptic Curves over P^n
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.3255
- Theorem: main specialization-height theorem; exact numbering to verify from full text before import
- Gives: for a section of an elliptic family over projective space, specialized canonical height is controlled relative to the degree of the specializing hypersurface for almost all hypersurfaces; also yields infinitely many injective specializations.
- Stage14 hook: `14-s3` needs a quantitative bridge between family/base height and canonical height on specialized fibers before any first-small-point distribution argument can be made uniform.
- Fit: NEAR
- Missing: Stage14 needs the least non-torsion point on fibers not arising from a fixed generic section. Wong controls specialization of fixed generic points/sections, so it does not by itself count rank-jump fibers or bound their first generator.
- Checked: 2026-08-09

## BLOCKED

### Silverman — Lehmer-type lower bound over function fields
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14771
- Theorem: main Lehmer-type lower bound
- Gives: an explicit positive lower bound for canonical height of non-torsion points over finite extensions of a one-variable function field in terms of `h(j)` and extension degree.
- Stage14 hook: superficially resembles the missing lower bound for first generators.
- Fit: BLOCKED
- Missing: Stage14-s3 needs rational points on specialized number-field fibers `E_t/Q`, whereas this theorem is over function fields; it does not give the needed distribution of least specialized rational points.
- Checked: 2026-08-09

### Quantitative equidistribution of small points for canonical heights (Yap, 2024)
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21679
- Theorem: quantitative small-point equidistribution; exact theorem number to verify before import
- Gives: quantitative distribution results for small canonical-height algebraic points in polarized dynamics/abelian settings.
- Stage14 hook: superficially resembles the `14-s3` small-point gate.
- Fit: BLOCKED
- Missing: Stage14 needs rational points on a moving elliptic family and the least non-torsion rational point per fiber, not equidistribution of a generic sequence of algebraic small points on a fixed polarized system.
- Checked: 2026-08-09
