## Global main/s consolidation

Merged 4di and s7-50 independently remove every fixed-power conductor-loss
stratum.  Possible square-root saturation is confined to

```text
q=C_* B^o(1),
gcd(h_0,q)=1,
rho^2=-1 (mod q).
```

The 4diH and sH50 audits certify no positive delta.  Their useful common
output is that a purely oscillatory Kloosterman estimate cannot settle the
whole family: the positive principal density and all X15 pairwise/triple
covariance terms remain.

Merged s7-51 then removes every conditioning cell with a fixed-power marginal
deficit.  Hence a saturating global packet must lie in a dense cell with

```text
mu_+ mu_- mu_k = B^(-o(1)).
```

The current global receiver is therefore

```text
FullConductorDensePrimitiveQuarterPythagoreanThreeProjectionJointPrincipalDensityOrMainTermScaleSignedCovariance.
```

This is a strict receiver contraction, not a strict exponent improvement.

## X16 common interface

There is one exact cross-route interface.  Both surviving problems admit a
decomposition of the form

```text
nonnegative principal density + centered signed orientation/covariance part.
```

The X15 Pythagorean three-projection coordinates are retained on the global
side, and the t91 split-prime Boolean cube is retained on the fixed-U side.
Thus future adapters may share centering and coefficient-complexity language.

This common interface is not a charged-once bridge.  The global family sums
over quarter pairs, full conductors and three physical weights; the fixed-U
family first fixes `(U,epsilon,k,h,kappa,beta)` and then sums a canonical-LPF
cofactor orientation cube.  No merged result identifies their coefficient
spaces, their principal measures, or their quantifier order.  Consequently:


finite-fiber equivalent and nonmultiplicable, and the Pythagorean cone cannot
be charged again as a determinant-method saving.
