# Theorem boundary

What is proved here is restricted to an odd bad prime that is actually present in the squarefree support of the full-2-descent class.  Such a prime has a forced factor-to-label routing and two explicit quadratic-character conditions.

This stage does **not** claim a complete Selmer local matrix.  Odd bad primes omitted from the class and the full Q2 lookup are still open, and no family large-sieve or sqrt(B) theorem follows yet.
