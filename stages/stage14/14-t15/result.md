# Stage14-t15 — simultaneous two-conic plus x-square fiber product

## Purpose

Stage14-t14 reduces the compatible shared-`q` triple gate to

\[
U^2=A_t(x):=(x-1)^2-4t^2x,
\]

\[
V^2=B_t(x):=x^2+(4t^4-2)x+1,
\]

with the additional lift condition

\[
x=r^2.
\]

This stage classifies the resulting fixed-`t` curve and checks whether the reduction creates a physical low-genus component.

No estimate `T(B)=o(sqrt(B))` is asserted here.

## 1. The x-level two-conic fiber product is genus one

Let

\[
C_{0,t}:\quad U^2=A_t(x),\qquad V^2=B_t(x).
\]

The two quadratic branch divisors are generically disjoint. Their discriminants and resultant are

\[
\operatorname{disc}_x(A_t)=16t^2(t^2+1),
\]

\[
\operatorname{disc}_x(B_t)=16t^4(t-1)(t+1)(t^2+1),
\]

and

\[
\operatorname{Res}_x(A_t,B_t)=16t^4(t^2+1)^2.
\]

Hence for every genuine physical rational Pythagorean parameter (`t>0`, `t!=1`, `1+t^2` a rational square), the four branch points are distinct and the biquadratic cover

\[
C_{0,t}\to \mathbf P^1_x
\]

has degree `4` and four inertia-2 branch points. Riemann--Hurwitz gives

\[
2g(C_{0,t})-2=4(-2)+4\cdot2=0,
\]

so

\[
\boxed{g(C_{0,t})=1.}
\]

Thus the simultaneous two-conic condition before imposing `x=r^2` is an elliptic curve (after choosing a rational base point where available), not a rational curve.

## 2. Reimposing x=r^2 restores genus five

Define the full compatible curve

\[
C_t:\quad U^2=A_t(r^2),\qquad V^2=B_t(r^2).
\]

Equivalently, `C_t -> C_{0,t}` is the double cover obtained by adjoining `r` with `r^2=x`.

On `C_{0,t}`, `x=0` has four geometric points

```text
(x,U,V)=(0, ±1, ±1),
```

and there are four geometric points above `x=infinity`, distinguished by the independent leading signs `U/x=±1`, `V/x=±1`. The function `x` has odd valuation at each of these eight points. Therefore the square-root cover `r^2=x` is branched at exactly eight points.

Riemann--Hurwitz for the degree-two map `C_t -> C_{0,t}` gives

\[
2g(C_t)-2=2(2\cdot1-2)+8=8,
\]

hence

\[
\boxed{g(C_t)=5.}
\]

This recovers the original Stage14-t genus-five triple fiber from the t12--t14 lower-dimensional reduction.

## 3. V4 quotient structure

Over the `r`-line the full function field is

\[
\mathbf Q(t,r)(\sqrt{A_t(r^2)},\sqrt{B_t(r^2)}),
\]

with sign group `(Z/2)^2` generated by `U -> -U` and `V -> -V`.

The three nontrivial quotient curves are:

1. `U^2=A_t(r^2)`, a genus-one quartic;
2. `V^2=B_t(r^2)`, a genus-one quartic;
3. `(UV)^2=A_t(r^2)B_t(r^2)`, the genus-three discriminant cover from Stage14-t13.

Consequently the standard biquadratic/Kani--Rosen decomposition gives, up to isogeny over `Q(t)`,

\[
\boxed{J(C_t)\sim E_{A,t}\times E_{B,t}\times J(G_t),}
\]

where `g(E_A)=g(E_B)=1` and `g(G)=3`. The dimensions add to `5`.

This is the same low-degree structure already visible in the Humbert--Edge description, now derived directly from the conditional square-root gates.

## 4. No physical degeneration or component splitting

The only parameters at which the x-level branch data can collide or become singular lie in

```text
t = 0, ±1, ±i.
```

None is a genuine physical rational Pythagorean base. Moreover `A_t(0)=B_t(0)=1`, so the extra `x=r^2` branch points never collide with the quadratic branch divisors on a physical fiber.

Therefore every genuine physical rational base has:

```text
x-level two-conic fiber product genus       1
full x-square lift genus                    5
full curve connected / nonsplit             true
physical low-genus degeneration             none
```

The universal elliptic quotients remain real arithmetic structure, but a rational point on either elliptic quotient, or even on the x-level elliptic fiber product `C_0`, is not sufficient: it must lift through the eight-branch square cover `r^2=x` and satisfy the physical height condition.

## 5. What remains

The geometric simplification has now reached a stable endpoint: the full compatible curve is genuinely genus five on every physical fiber, while its quotient skeleton consists of an elliptic `x`-level fiber product and the two elliptic plus genus-three V4 quotients.

The next useful arithmetic target is therefore not another generic-genus calculation. It is the **square-lift problem on the moving elliptic curve `C_{0,t}`**:

\[
(x,U,V)\in C_{0,t}(\mathbf Q),\qquad x\in(\mathbf Q^\times)^2,
\]

with the inherited physical small-height window. Equivalently one must count rational points on the moving elliptic family whose `x`-coordinate lies in the square image.

This is a thin-lift/2-cover condition on an elliptic family and is the natural Stage14-t16 interface.
