# Stage14-tH11 — second-cycle stress gate and PARK decision

## Purpose

Stage14-tH7 parked the first independent roadworks cycle `tH1--tH6` after it had standardized Gaussian normalization, hyperbola decomposition, conductor bookkeeping, weighted large-sieve transfer, coefficient collision energy, and exponent ledgers.

The second cycle was opened because merged live Stage14-t39 exposed a genuinely new need: an external-auxiliary Gaussian Dirichlet-symbol / Type-I/II dispersion interface.  The second cycle then produced:

- `tH8`: exact external-auxiliary trilinear packet and the two one-Cauchy dispersion routes;
- `tH9`: squareclass-difference / cross-ratio atlas and autocorrelation formulation;
- `tH10`: principal/fourth-energy receivers, including generic/exceptional and heavy/light interfaces.

Meanwhile merged live `t40` proved that the Route-A one-Cauchy cross-kernel is a genuine quadratic Dirichlet character in the auxiliary norm and a norm-induced quadratic Hecke character over `Q(i)`.  Merged live `t41` then identified the true remaining obstruction: global squareclass collision/fourth energy, with off-fiber principal collisions lying on a Kummer-type surface.

Stage14-tH11 asks only whether the **roadworks** are still missing an independently buildable adapter.  It does not invent a t42 theorem before a stable t42 interface exists.

The conclusion is:

> the second tH cycle is complete and reusable; no currently merged live-t result creates a new independent infrastructure requirement.  The support route should therefore PARK until a new stable merged t result exposes a new adapter-level need.

No analytic power saving is claimed here.

## 1. Frozen second-cycle interfaces

### tH8 — dispersion adapter

For the external-auxiliary packet

\[
\mathcal T=\sum_{\varpi}\sum_x a_\varpi d_x\,\chi_{N\varpi}(P_x),
\]

tH8 proved the two exact one-Cauchy expansions

\[
K_{\mathcal L}(x,y)=\sum_\varpi \chi_{N\varpi}(P_xP_y)
\]

and

\[
H_{\mathcal X}(\varpi,\omega)
=\sum_x\chi_{N\varpi}(P_x)\chi_{N\omega}(P_x).
\]

It also froze strict FI / quadratic-Hecke readiness certificates, preventing a merely suggestive symbol resemblance from being promoted into a theorem application.

### t40 upgrade of Route A

Merged t40 proved that after one Cauchy step,

\[
\kappa_{ij}=\operatorname{sqf}(F_iF_j)
\]

and, for a good auxiliary rational prime `lambda`,

\[
\chi_\lambda(F_iF_j)=\chi_{D(\kappa_{ij})}(\lambda).
\]

Pulling back along Gaussian norm gives

\[
\eta_D(z)=\chi_D(Nz),
\]

a genuine norm-induced quadratic Hecke character over `Q(i)`.

Thus the Route-A multiplicativity obstruction that motivated tH8 is **closed** at the adapter level.

### tH9 — squareclass group atlas

Writing the state squareclass as `sigma_i`,

\[
\kappa_{ij}=\sigma_i\oplus\sigma_j,
\]

and, after squareclass compression,

\[
A(\kappa)=\sum_s W(s)\overline{W(s\oplus\kappa)}.
\]

Hence

\[
A_1=A(1),\qquad E_4=\sum_\kappa |A(\kappa)|^2.
\]

The four-state cross-ratio certificate

\[
\rho(i,j;k,l)=\sigma_i\oplus\sigma_j\oplus\sigma_k\oplus\sigma_l
\]

satisfies

\[
\rho=1\iff \kappa_{ij}=\kappa_{kl}.
\]

### tH10 — incidence/energy receiver

For any partition `P`, tH10 proved the exact principal identity

\[
\boxed{A_1=L_P+I_P^{\rm off}}.
\]

After a generic/exceptional split,

\[
\boxed{A_1=L_P+I_{\rm gen}+I_{\rm exc}}.
\]

For positive counting coefficients it also proved

\[
\boxed{E_4\le A_1^2+R_{\rm non}(H^2-A_1)}
\]

and the heavy/light refinement

\[
\boxed{
E_4\le A_1^2+T(H^2-A_1)+(R_{\rm non}-T)M_T.
}
\]

Finally,

\[
R_{\rm non}\le M^2D_{\rm non}
\]

separates squareclass-fiber control from support-difference expansion.

## 2. What merged t41 says is still missing

Merged t41 establishes three decisive facts.

1. Row and reverse-column local energies can both be near-linear without implying global near-linear `A1`.
2. The off-fiber same-squareclass locus for a fixed packet pair is Kummer-type, birational to a quotient of a product of two elliptic curves.
3. Near-linear `A1` alone does not imply near-quadratic `E4`; nonprincipal squareclass convolution needs independent control.

These are **new mathematical inputs to be proved by live t**, not missing adapters.

The tH10 receiver already accepts precisely the plausible outputs of a future live stage:

- generic transverse incidence bound;
- exceptional/isogenous incidence bound;
- uniform nonprincipal kernel bound;
- or a heavy/light theorem controlling only the total mass of large kernels.

Therefore there is no reason to manufacture a tH11 geometry theorem duplicating live t.

## 4. Reopen contract

The second tH cycle should reopen only if a **stable merged** live-t result produces at least one of the following concrete needs:

```text
REOPEN_1  prime-power or materially different conductor geometry
REOPEN_2  signed/complex energy where positivity receivers are insufficient
REOPEN_3  a higher-than-fourth moment that needs a new correlation algebra
REOPEN_4  a genuinely multi-modulus (>2) post-dispersion packet
REOPEN_5  a non-Gaussian number field / leaving Q(i)
REOPEN_6  a proved physical-route multiplicative parameterization needing FI/Hecke packaging
REOPEN_7  a new live theorem whose fixed-power bookkeeping cannot be expressed by tH6/tH10 ledgers
```

A future stage should **not** reopen tH merely because `A1`, `E4`, or the Kummer incidence is difficult.  Difficulty of the mathematical theorem is not itself an infrastructure defect.
