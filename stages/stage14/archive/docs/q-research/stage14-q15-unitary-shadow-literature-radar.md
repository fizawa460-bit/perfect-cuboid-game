## 3. Ford ordinary divisor-in-an-interval theory — still NEAR, not DIRECT

```text

```

across the full ordinary-divisor parameter range.

This remains the closest established architecture for a bare localized-divisor support count. However q15's witness is not an arbitrary divisor: it must satisfy

```text
gcd(u,m/u)=1.
```

```text

#{m : exists ordinary d in I(m)}
```

on the Stage14 moving exponent cells.

## 6. New 2026 result: Drappeau–Mounier localized-divisor density

Sary Drappeau and Adrien Mounier, *Computing sieve integrals using LattE, and the density of integers with a localized divisor* (arXiv:2606.30428, 2026), study the natural density

```text
h(alpha,beta)
 = density{n : exists d|n, n^alpha <= d <= n^beta}
```

and give a refined formula enabling rigorous numerical evaluation, with the announced numerical region including `beta-alpha >= 0.02`.

This is a genuinely new post-q14 literature item and is highly relevant as an **ordinary-divisor shadow benchmark**. It is not a direct Stage14 theorem for three separate reasons:

1. the divisor is unrestricted, while Stage14 requires a unitary divisor;
2. the advertised numerical regime has a fixed exponent-width floor, while Stage14 short windows may shrink with the charged packet and can be `B^o(1)`-multiplicatively short;
3. it does not include the canonical/reverse completion predicate, which belongs to mechanism C rather than mechanism U.

Thus it can benchmark the size of an ordinary localized-divisor shadow after an adapter is proved, but cannot itself close the heavy branch.

## 9. Squarefree bridge is algebraically exact but not yet a charged adapter

If an outer integer `m` is squarefree, every divisor of `m` is unitary. Hence on the squarefree subfamily the unitary/ordinary distinction disappears exactly.

Recent work on squarefree numbers in short intervals, e.g. Mayank Pandey (arXiv:2401.13981; explicit version arXiv:2608.06682), proves strong existence/density information for squarefree integers in short additive intervals. It does **not** show that the Stage14 accepted/bare outer mass is carried by squarefree `m`, nor does it preserve the moving divisor-window witness.

Therefore squarefree-number results do not currently supply the missing Ford adapter.
