# Theorem boundary

The reverse-count relation is conditional on the standard fixed-curve height exponent for an M-degree `d` rational stratum and on the target `V(B)` scale one wishes to explain. It is a necessary exponent budget only.

No claim is made that the observed finite `sqrt(B)` signal is asymptotic, that higher-degree strata exist in sufficient abundance, that different strata are disjoint, or that finite arithmetic signatures are algebraic families.
