## 3. The two arithmetic masks cannot be double-charged

Balanced allocation chooses admissible divisor/prime splits inside the disjoint packets. Reciprocal completion tests whether the resulting split reconstructs the required complementary physical square data. They are sequential filters on the same allocation state, not independent probabilistic events.

Therefore a bound of the schematic form

```text
saving_bal * saving_recip
```

is illegal unless an independent tensorization or conditional-decoupling theorem is proved. No such theorem is merged.

The legal receiver is the signed incremental response obtained by adjoining the pair of filters in one charged-once filtration. Equivalently, any square-root-saturating sequence must carry exponent-zero response in at least one of these two increments, but the two responses may not be multiplied.
