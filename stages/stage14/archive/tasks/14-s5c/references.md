# Stage14-s5c reference boundary

The s5c odd supported-prime rows are derived directly from the repository's s1 split 2-covering equations and elementary p-adic valuation parity / Hensel lifting; no external average-Selmer theorem is imported.

For general explicit 2-covering local minimization and p-adic handling, see:

- T. A. Fisher, *Minimal Models for 2-coverings of Elliptic Curves*, LMS Journal of Computation and Mathematics 5 (2002), 220–243.

This reference is background for the local 2-covering framework only.  The Stage14-specific support routing and character rows are the explicit calculation recorded in this stage.
