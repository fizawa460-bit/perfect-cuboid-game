## 1. Nonnegative E-scale decomposition

Merged 4fm partitions the heavy weighted-unitary incidence into the disjoint exponent alternatives

```text
(A) E=B^o(1),
(B) E=B^(epsilon+o(1)), epsilon>0,
```

after the usual `B^o(1)` exponent localization. The total heavy incidence is a nonnegative sum of these cells. Hence a surviving exponent-`mu` heavy packet forces one localized cell with exponent `mu-o(1)`.

On branch (A), freeze exact `E=E0`. Stage14-4fn proves exponent equivalence with

```text
S_fix(E0)
 := #{m : A_E0(m)=1}.
```

On branch (B), Stage14-4fo proves exponent equivalence with

```text
S_poly
 := #{(E,m) : A_poly(E,m)=1}.
```

Therefore every heavy survivor obeys at least one of

```text
S_fix(E0) >= B^(mu-o(1))                           (1)
```

for one exact admissible `E0=B^o(1)`, or

```text
S_poly >= B^(mu-o(1)).                             (2)
```

No inner unitary-divisor multiplicity can supply the missing polynomial mass.
