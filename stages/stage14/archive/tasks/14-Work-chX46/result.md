## 1. Pushforward lower-coverage lemma

Let `pi: Lambda -> Theta` be the deterministic pushforward from retained conditioned s witnesses to q17 reciprocal-CRT packets. Let

```text
a_s(theta) := #{lambda in Lambda : pi(lambda)=theta}
```

on a frozen principal cell. Merged s7-140 proves only

```text
a_s(theta) <= B^o(1)
```

pointwise. This upper multiplicity bound alone gives no lower bound for the mass of `pi(Lambda)` on any q17-good subset `G subset Theta`.

In particular there is no implication

```text
a_s(theta) <= B^o(1) for all theta
=>
sum_{theta in G} a_s(theta) >= B^(-o(1)) * #G.
```

The right side requires an independent lower-coverage / lower-domination statement.
