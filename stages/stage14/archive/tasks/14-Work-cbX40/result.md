## 2. X40 lemma — localized external gates do not propagate across unmatched charged alternatives

Let a charged family be decomposed into labeled alternative realizations

```text
F = union_j F_j,
```

with every count charged once. Suppose an unresolved external theorem gate `G_A` has been proved equivalent to the required support statement only on one realization `F_A`. If no measure-, baseline-, witness-, and quantifier-preserving adapter from another realization `F_B` to `F_A` is proved, then:

1. `G_A` may block further claims on `F_A`;
2. `G_A` gives no saving on `F_B`;
3. `G_A` does not justify stopping internal reductions on `F_B`;
4. failure to resolve `G_A` cannot be promoted to a whole-family obstruction theorem.

This is a dependency/quantifier statement, not an arithmetic saving.
