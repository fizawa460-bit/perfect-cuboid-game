## 1. Entering receiver

Merged `4do` reduces the zero-mode square-root obstruction to positive conditional uplift generated by disjoint prime allocations in

```text
plus packet : (S,T),
minus packet: (u_*,R,J),
```

under the exact complementary-square reconstruction. Fixed-power common-prime and common-gcd explanations are already removed.

Merged `s7-58` and `t98` make part of the physical selector explicit: orientation admits a `B^o(1)` Hecke/Walsh expansion, finite gcd predicates admit Mobius expansion, endpoint projective tests have subpolynomial conductor, and a one-bit orientation change is localized to explicit XOR boundary types.

Merged `Work-bgX19` identifies the remaining quantifier gap: the global conditional response toggles an entire admissibility condition, whereas `t98` controls only one generic orientation bit in a fixed packet.

## 2. Charged-once telescoping is algebraically available

Fix an ordering of the disjoint allocation coordinates and write the physical plus-side admissibility indicator as `F(omega)`. For two allocation states `omega,omega'`, the exact identity

```text
F(omega)-F(omega')
 = sum_j [F(omega^(j))-F(omega^(j-1))]
```

holds along any coordinate-by-coordinate interpolation path.

Thus the global conditional response can formally be decomposed into single-coordinate boundary increments. This is a bookkeeping identity only. It does not provide a useful bound unless the weighted total variation of those increments is controlled uniformly after conditioning on the complementary-square reconstruction.

In particular, the number of active prime coordinates can be `B^o(1)` without each increment having a fixed-power deficit. Summing `B^o(1)` exponent-zero increments preserves an exponent-zero obstruction.

## 4. Exact remaining obstruction

After peeling all currently controlled decorations, a square-root-saturating sequence can survive only if there is an exponent-zero accumulated boundary response of the form

```text
Sum_{p in active disjoint allocation primes}
    E[ Delta_p F * W_- | frozen physical core ]
 = B^(-o(1)),
```

where `Delta_p F` is charged once along an interpolation of plus-side allocation states and `W_-` is evaluated through the exact complementary-square reconstruction.

The dangerous case is not one large common-prime effect; that was removed in `4do`. It is coherent accumulation of many individually explicit or partially explicit disjoint-prime boundary increments.

A fixed-power saving would follow from any one of the following stronger statements, none presently merged:

```text
(A) total weighted boundary variation <= B^(-delta+o(1));
(B) square-sum/orthogonality giving B^(-delta+o(1)) after aggregation;
(C) multiplicative/Hecke factorization of the full physical mask with a nonprincipal phase;
(D) reduction of all exponent-zero accumulation to one residual full-conductor covariance family already controlled elsewhere.
```

Accordingly the minimal unresolved object is now the **charged-once disjoint-prime physical-boundary accumulation norm**.
