# StageA1 — Anchored Hilbert Cube reconnaissance

StageA1 is an experimental side line, intentionally separate from the Stage27/StructureRadar proof line.

## Question

Treat the perfect-cuboid problem as the anchored dimension-3 Hilbert-cube problem in the squares:

`H(0; A^2, B^2, C^2) subset {n^2}`.

The aim is not to import Stage27 conclusions. The aim is to test whether the 2026 Hilbert-cube parametrizations expose a new algebraic obstruction at the anchor boundary `a0=0`.

## Source seed

Andrew Bremner, Christian Elsholtz, Maciej Ulas, *There are infinitely many Hilbert cubes of dimension 3 in the set of squares*, arXiv:2604.05459 (2026).

The paper explicitly asks whether a dimension-3 Hilbert cube with `a0=0` exists and identifies this with the perfect cuboid/Euler brick problem. It also gives explicit infinite parametric families of dimension-3 Hilbert cubes in the squares.

## Research firewall

- StageA1 is independent of Stage27 and does not weaken any Stage27 receiver.
- Excluding a particular Hilbert-cube parametrized family at `a0=0` is not a perfect-cuboid nonexistence theorem.
- A numerical search is diagnostic unless converted to a proved algebraic/local statement.
- No family is assumed universal without a proved dominance/coverage theorem.
- Results from earlier stages may be reused only after an exact equation/measure/parameter adapter is written.

## Operating rule

Run a short reconnaissance first. If A1-1 through A1-3 produce no new necessary condition on an arbitrary anchored cube and only exclude special published families, stop StageA1 rather than creating an endless refinement chain.
