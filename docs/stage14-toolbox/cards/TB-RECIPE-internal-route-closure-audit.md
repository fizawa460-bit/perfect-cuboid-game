## INPUT

- A frozen physical target, population, cutoff, charged measure, and success criterion.
- A candidate ledger containing every materially distinct internal receiver, including consumed routes, current-input negative certificates, live routes, dominated routes, and external theorem species.
- Exact current equations from which candidate mechanisms can be rediscovered without relying on route names.
- Completed dependency, measure, quantifier, reconstruction-multiplicity, and no-double-charge audits.

## OUTPUT

Run the following closure sequence:

1. Freeze the newest positive theorem and its exact limitations.
2. Perform an exhaustive-view audit of all named materially distinct internal routes.
3. Perform blind rediscovery from the exact equations and physical measure.
4. Search the reusable arsenal and exact-reconstruction directions once more.
5. Classify each remaining item as internal live route, consumed/negative-certified route, or external/different-theorem species.
6. Permit closure only when a fresh audit returns no internal route and records the known limitations and external future gates.

A valid closure may retain a stronger unproved objective. Closure means that the declared investigation and its internal route ledger are complete; it does not mean that every conceivable stronger theorem is proved.

## VARIABLE DICTIONARY

- Internal route = a materially distinct mechanism executable from the current project inputs and physical contract.
- Current-input negative certificate = proof that a route cannot meet the declared target from the presently certified inputs.
- External gate = a theorem, uniformity statement, literature input, or changed mathematical species not currently available internally.
- Blind rediscovery = reconstruction of candidate mechanisms from the exact equations without consulting the existing route labels.

## USED BY

- Stage or checkpoint closure candidates after many routes have accumulated.
- Separation of legitimate parking from premature “no ideas remain” declarations.
- Prevention of reopening the same consumed route under new notation.

## DO NOT USE FOR

- Do not treat a long candidate list as proof of completeness.
- Do not classify a difficult internal lemma as external merely to permit closure.
- Do not demand proof of every stronger future theorem before closing a bounded investigation.
- Do not let controller PASS substitute for fresh mathematical audit.
- Do not reopen a negative-certified route without a genuinely new theorem, adapter, data source, or population change.
