# Stage14-q7 — Negative-results archive / anti-regression ledger

## Purpose

Stage14-q7 freezes the durable negative knowledge accumulated by the literature-radar route and the Stage14 proof tracks through merged q6.

The purpose is not to celebrate failed approaches. It is to prevent future workers from repeatedly spending time on a tempting theorem, heuristic, or geometric mechanism whose mismatch is already understood.

Every entry below has five fields:

- **STATUS** — `BLOCKED`, `CONSUMED`, `SUPERSEDED`, `DEFERRED`, or `DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY`;
- **FALSE INFERENCE PREVENTED** — the specific claim that must not be made;
- **WHY** — the mathematical incompatibility or later theorem that invalidates the shortcut;
- **REOPEN TRIGGER** — the exact new information required before revisiting the route;
- **OWNER / TARGET** — the active track to which the decision matters.

A route marked `BLOCKED` is not claimed impossible forever. It is blocked under the current formulation and may be reopened only by its stated trigger.

```text
STAGE14_Q7=COMPLETE_NEGATIVE_RESULTS_ARCHIVE

NEGATIVE_KNOWLEDGE_IS_PROOF_INFRASTRUCTURE=true
BROAD_UNTRIGGERED_LITERATURE_SEARCH_ALLOWED=false
NEXT_Q_STAGE=TRIGGER_DRIVEN_Q8_PLUS_ONLY
```

## A. Analytic / local-character anti-regression entries

### Q7-A01 — arbitrary-matrix quadratic large sieve

**STATUS:** `BLOCKED`

**FALSE INFERENCE PREVENTED:**

> A quadratic large-sieve theorem for separated coefficients can be applied directly to the original correlated Euclid incidence matrix `W(u,v)`.

**WHY:** Heath-Brown/Liu and Wilson control sums with genuinely separated coefficient sequences. q2 found no theorem allowing an arbitrary two-variable coefficient matrix. Later s5i--s5k went further and showed that the right move is not to search for such a theorem: the pure Euclid bulk separates after state/modulus freezing, while the discrepancy is handled by explicit projective/determinant incidence.

**REOPEN TRIGGER:** only if a future stage produces a new two-variable weight not reducible to the present state-frozen incidence and proves that it satisfies the precise hypotheses of a more general bilinear theorem.

**OWNER / TARGET:** `14-s`, `14-4`.

### Q7-A02 — Wilson / hyperbolic Jacobi theorem before separation

**STATUS:** `BLOCKED`

**FALSE INFERENCE PREVENTED:**

> Hyperbolic or lopsided Jacobi bilinear estimates automatically handle Stage14 endpoint correlation.

**WHY:** Wilson's theorem is directly useful only after the Stage14 coefficient dependence is separated. It can sum a legitimate separated block over a hyperbolic/product region; it does not manufacture separation or prove the Euclid discrepancy estimate.

**REOPEN TRIGGER:** none needed for separated blocks; for an unresolved correlated block, first prove separation or a valid reduction to Wilson's coefficient class.

**OWNER / TARGET:** `14-s`, `14-4`.

## H. q-route operating rules after q7

q7 closes the fixed scheduled literature-radar sequence. There is deliberately no automatic `q8` topic.

A new q-stage may be opened only when one of the active lanes reports a stable named obstruction. The new stage header must contain:

```text
TRIGGER_STAGE=
EXACT_OBSTRUCTION=
CURRENT_BEST_BOUND=
WHY_EXISTING_Q_LEDGER_DOES_NOT_ALREADY_ANSWER_IT=
SEARCH_FAMILIES=
PROMOTION_STANDARD=
```

### Approved reopen triggers visible at the q7 baseline

The following are legitimate examples, but they are not automatic tasks:

1. **Norm medium signed-root obstruction:** after s5m+ states the exact surviving `m^2+n^2` medium-range sum, reopen DKSZ/DFI/Ngo/root-energy literature narrowly.
2. **Linear boundary switch obstruction:** if complementary-divisor rewriting inside the full character polynomial fails for a named algebraic reason, reopen divisor-switching literature narrowly.
3. **Height transfer obstruction:** after a genuine Le-Boudec-style large-prime experiment identifies the exact failing large-prime or covering-variable count, search that specific arithmetic-statistics problem.
4. **Triple active-direction obstruction:** after t22+ freezes the active generalized-Pell/elliptic-direction second moment, search literature matching that exact family and height.
5. **New geometry:** only if a new fibration/divisor class/orbit problem appears that is not the consumed fixed-M4 mechanism.
6. **Numerical contradiction:** if a larger exact observatory detects a reproducible structural pattern contradicting a proof model, formulate the mathematical contradiction first, then search it.

Broad periodic browsing with no named obstruction is not a q-stage.
