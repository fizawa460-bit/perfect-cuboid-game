# Stage14-q6 — Cross-track weapon routing

## Purpose

Stage14-q6 converts the focused literature passes q2--q5 into an explicit routing matrix for the active Stage14 proof and numerical tracks. The goal is to prevent two common failures:

1. rediscovering or reproving a subproblem that another Stage14 lane has already reduced or closed;
2. importing a human theorem outside the exact hypotheses under which q2--q5 classified it as usable.

This stage does not claim a new asymptotic theorem. It is a proof-traffic-control result.

## Snapshot used for routing

At the q6 snapshot:

- `Stage14-4ax` is merged. Its remaining local frontier is medium six-linear determinant dispersion, microscopic small-side modes, and the state-split norm-column mixed-sign `D*S` kernel.
- `Stage14-s5k` is an open draft. It proves an elementary six-linear medium dispersion theorem and leaves dyadic integration plus the norm-column mixed-sign `D*S` kernel.
- `Stage14-t20` is merged. The triple correction is reduced to partition-resolved missing-face squareclass collision energy `Q_split(B)`, where any fixed power saving suffices for the t-track target.
- `Stage14-num5` is merged. The finite census through `B=100m` remains diagnostic only and shows no stable square-root scaling law.
- q2, q3, q4, and q5 are merged literature-radar results.

The open `s5k` result is treated as a pending dependency, not as a merged theorem.

## 1. Weapon inventory

### W2-A — separated quadratic Jacobi large sieve

Source class: Heath-Brown / Liu quadratic large sieve.

Status: `DIRECT` only after the actual coefficient block is genuinely separated into one-variable coefficient sequences.

Valid role:

```text
sum_{u,v} alpha_u beta_v (u/v)
```

on rectangular dyadic blocks, with the q2 hypotheses checked.

Invalid role:

- arbitrary correlated two-variable coefficient matrices;
- hiding an unresolved Euclid-incidence weight inside `alpha` or `beta`;
- treating state-split norm-root correlation as already separated.

### W2-B — hyperbolic/lopsided Jacobi bilinear forms

Source class: Cameron Wilson.

Status: `DIRECT` after separation.

Use when the dyadic region is naturally hyperbolic/product-constrained or strongly lopsided and the coefficient dependence is already legitimate.

Do not use to bypass the Stage14 incidence/discrepancy reduction.

### W2-C — elementary covariance / determinant dispersion

Source class: q2 architecture plus the internal s5i--s5k reductions.

Status: `PRIMARY_INTERNAL_ROUTE`.

The active linear-four problem is no longer an arbitrary-matrix problem. It has been reduced to projective residue collisions and determinant divisibility. This route should be exhausted before spectral escalation.

### W2-D — divisor switching / complementary divisor

Source class: Hooley/Fiorilli-style architecture.

Status: `NEAR`.

Use only for genuinely sparse large-modulus endpoint ranges that remain after the direct projective/determinant analysis.

### W2-E — modular-root additive energy / spectral quadratic-root methods

Source class: Dunn--Kerr--Shparlinski--Zaharescu; DFI/Ngo-style quadratic-root technology.

Status: `RESERVE_NEAR`.

Trigger only if the state-split `m^2+n^2` norm column remains after elementary same-sign/opposite-sign `D/S` analysis. Do not escalate the six linear edges to spectral machinery once the elementary determinant method closes them.

### W3-A — Petsche canonical-height lower bound

Status: `DIRECT_PER_FIBER`, not a family-saving theorem.

Useful for certifying lower bounds on a fixed Stage14 fiber after minimal-discriminant/conductor data are known.

Not sufficient by itself for a positive power saving in the number of fibers satisfying the physical logarithmic height window.

### W3-B — Naccarato bounded-point counting

Status: `DIRECT_PER_FIBER`.

Useful for bounding the number of bounded-height points on a fixed elliptic curve. It does not control how often the moving Stage14 family has an unusually small first non-torsion point.

### W3-C — Le Boudec large-prime-factor + complete-2-descent architecture

Status: `PRIMARY_NEAR_HEIGHT_ROUTE`.

This is the preferred family-level transfer test for the Stage14 first-small-point gate. The Stage14 version must be rebuilt on

```text
m, n, m-n, m+n, m^2+n^2.
```

The transfer is not considered proved until one can isolate a useful large prime, force it through the exact Stage14 split-descent equations, impose the physical height inequalities on the covering variables, and count the surviving configurations with a fixed power saving.

### W3-D — ambient density-one Lang results

Status: `BLOCKED_DIRECT_TRANSFER`.

The Stage14 Pythagorean family is thin and has structurally fourth-powerful displayed discriminant. Do not infer a density-one Stage14 height statement from an ambient all-elliptic-curves theorem.

### W4-A — square sieve / polynomial sieve for collision values

Source class: Heath-Brown/Browning polynomial-square sieve; Bonolis--Pierce-type polynomial sieve beyond separation of variables.

Status: `PRIMARY_NEAR_T_ROUTE`.

The t20 collision identity makes this the first external architecture to test once t21 writes an explicit pair collision cover

```text
Y^2 = K(theta) K(theta').
```

Before applying any sieve, remove diagonal/square components and verify nonsquareness/irreducibility, parameter dimension, singular locus, and the physical-height conversion.

### W4-B — Peschmann perfect-cuboid square-value / 2-descent methods

Status: `NEAR_FIBERWISE`.

These are close structural precedents for reducing perfect-cuboid conditions to square-value and descent problems. They are not currently a moving-family collision-energy theorem.

### W4-C — generic Selmer-distribution machinery

Status: `DEFERRED`.

Reopen only if the collision parameterization produces a genuine fixed elliptic-twist or otherwise literature-compatible family. Do not prefer generic Selmer language over the more explicit square-value problem currently available.

### W5-A — Shimada level-4 modular K3 computation package

Status: `CONSUMED_FOR_FIXED_M4_ROUTE`.

The fixed `M`-degree-four rational-bisection mechanism has already used the exact NS lattice, automorphism/fibration/torsion data, the physical `M` vector, and the deck anti-invariant lattice. The required norm-16 parity coset is empty.

Do not reopen broad K3/root enumeration for the present analytic frontier.

### W5-B — later Shimada MW/automorphism algorithms

Status: `RESERVE_ONLY`.

Reopen only if a future stage produces a new explicit fibration, a finite orbit problem, or a new divisor-class classification whose remaining obstruction is genuinely lattice/Mordell--Weil/automorphism-theoretic.

## 2. Routing by active track

### 2.1 Stage14-s route

Current pending result: `s5k`.

If `s5k` merges, the six linear reciprocal edges should be regarded as having the medium determinant-discrepancy theorem available. Then `s5l` should:

1. combine the s5h rank-one separated Jacobi bulk with the s5k discrepancy estimate across all dyadic blocks;
2. use W2-A/W2-B only on the already-separated bulk pieces;
3. handle microscopic side-one/lower-dimensional modes explicitly rather than pretending they have a two-variable Jacobi saving;
4. isolate the state-split `E=m^2+n^2` mixed-sign kernel as the first remaining genuinely different analytic object;
5. attack that kernel elementarily through its same-sign determinant / opposite-sign anti-determinant structure before invoking W2-E.

Reopen q-literature on the s-route only if the norm mixed-sign kernel survives this explicit reduction as a stable named estimate.

### 2.2 Stage14-4 main route

Current merged stage: `4ax`; canonical next stage: `4ay`.

Critical anti-duplication rule:

> `4ay` must not independently re-prove the six-linear medium determinant theorem if `s5k` has merged. Import it as a theorem-level dependency and spend the main-track effort on the pieces not closed there.

After the s5k dependency is available, the intended 4ay focus becomes:

1. transfer the six-linear medium estimate into the full local-indicator / `rho_loc,E_loc` accounting;
2. close or inductively organize microscopic small-side modes;
3. isolate the norm-column mixed-sign `D*S` contribution;
4. determine whether the assembled local retainer has an actual positive fixed-power saving or only diagonal-scale control.

Use W2-A/W2-B on separated bulk, W2-D only on surviving sparse endpoints, and W2-E only on a persistent norm-root problem.

Do not reopen W5-A K3 geometry merely because the analytic local retainer remains difficult.

### 2.3 Stage14-t route

Current merged stage: `t20`; canonical next stage: `t21`.

The t-route should not return to fixed-prime local squareclass thinning: t18 already found the selected local image full.

`t21` should first write explicit raw-edge parameter charts for the partition-resolved collision condition and form the pair square-value cover. Then apply the q4 decision tree:

1. identify and remove diagonal/square components;
2. prove the relevant polynomial is non-square / geometrically nontrivial on each surviving chart;
3. compare exact dimensions and heights with W4-A;
4. attempt square/polynomial sieve before generic Selmer machinery;
5. use W4-B Peschmann as a fiberwise structural comparison, not as a family collision theorem.

If the cover degenerates into a fixed elliptic twist family, then and only then reopen W4-C.

### 2.4 Stage14 height / small-point gate

This gate is logically distinct from the local-character cancellation problem.

Do not treat successful q2/s5 local averaging as automatically solving the q3 first-small-point retainer.

Once a receiving stage is ready to attack height frequency, use this order:

1. W3-C Le Boudec transfer test on the five Euclid factors;
2. W3-A Petsche to control individual fibers and identify the Szpiro/radical regimes where the physical height window is impossible or restricted;
3. W3-B Naccarato for per-fiber bounded-point multiplicity;
4. never use W3-D ambient density-one Lang results as a Stage14 family theorem without a new restriction argument.

The most useful numerical companion is to measure the radical defect and largest-prime-factor statistics of the five Euclid factors on active/inactive matched fibers.

### 2.5 Stage14-num route

Current merged stage: `num5`; next: `num6`.

The numerical track remains diagnostic, but q6 assigns three high-value measurements that directly support proof-route falsification:

1. **q2/norm diagnostic:** distribution of same-sign `D` versus opposite-sign `S` norm-root collision multiplicities on increasing exact cutoffs;
2. **q3/height diagnostic:** largest prime factor and radical defect of `m,n,m-n,m+n,m^2+n^2`, stratified by active first-hit / inactive controls where available;
3. **q4/collision diagnostic:** multiplicity spectrum of missing-face squareclasses and partition-resolved `(alpha,beta)` collision classes, not just total `Q`.

All remain `FINITE_DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY`. They can choose which theorem transfer to test first but cannot promote a finite trend to an asymptotic law.

## 4. Stop rules

The following moves are explicitly disallowed unless a later stage produces a new hypothesis check.

1. **No arbitrary-matrix quadratic large sieve.** Separation must be proved first.
2. **No duplicate six-linear determinant proof** in main after s5k merges; import it.
3. **No fixed-prime local thinning on the selected t squareclass.** The local image is full.
4. **No ambient density-one Lang theorem as a Stage14 thin-family theorem.**
5. **No reopening fixed `M`-degree-four K3 root searches.** The required lattice parity coset is empty.
6. **No finite `T=0`, injective squareclass census, or effective exponent promoted to nonexistence/asymptotic proof.**
7. **No square/polynomial sieve invocation before the collision polynomial's square components and singular/diagonal loci are audited.**
8. **No spectral escalation before the elementary `D/S` incidence structure is exhausted.**

## 5. Reopen triggers for future q8+ passes

After q7, a new literature radar pass is justified only by a concrete trigger such as:

- the norm mixed-sign `D*S` kernel survives s5l/4ay as a stable quantitative estimate with explicit modulus/box ranges;
- t21 produces a specific collision polynomial that fails the current square/polynomial-sieve hypotheses for one named reason;
- the Le Boudec transfer test reaches a specific largest-prime-factor or covering-variable counting obstruction;
- a new geometric low-degree class/fibration appears that is not covered by the consumed Shimada fixed-M4 analysis;
- num6 detects a reproducible structural anomaly that directly contradicts one of the proof-route heuristic models and can be expressed as a theorem-level question.

Broad searches without one of these triggers are postponed.
