# StageX-70 synthesis policy

This file is a normative addendum to `docs/stage16-28-population-roadmap.md`.

StageX-70 should perform a final bounded synthesis of the certified results from StageX-10 through StageX-60. It should derive additional consequences that follow from existing inputs, compare causal explanations, record useful lower-stage reinterpretations, identify evidence-backed refinement candidates, and separate proved, computed, heuristic, and open claims.

The synthesis must stop when further work would require a substantially new theorem, a new large computation, a new literature program, or a branch outside the current stage question. Those items should be recorded as future questions or open gates instead of expanding StageX-70 indefinitely.

A finer classification discovered in an upper stage does not by itself reopen the lower stage. Reopen and recompute lower stages only when a foundational population definition, cutoff convention, canonicalization rule, theorem, or baseline computation is invalidated.

Required fields:

```text
KNOWN_RESULTS=
ADDITIONAL_DEDUCTIONS=
CAUSAL_SYNTHESIS=
LOWER_STAGE_REINTERPRETATIONS=
REFINEMENT_CANDIDATES=
NEW_HEURISTICS=
OPEN_GATES=
NEXT_STAGE_QUESTIONS=
SYNTHESIS_STOP_REASON=
SYNTHESIS_STOP_RULE_SATISFIED=YES|NO
```
