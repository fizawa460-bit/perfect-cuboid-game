## Product-square chain decomposition

The Stage35 chain is **not** one giant reusable weapon.

1. Primitive normalization and the particular two-Pythagorean branch extraction remain source-dependent.
2. The reusable core inside `A01` is the elementary coprime product-rectangle contract: for `RS=UV` with `gcd(R,S)=gcd(U,V)=1`, prime-power allocation gives pairwise-coprime cells `alpha,beta,gamma,delta` with `R=alpha beta`, `S=gamma delta`, `U=alpha gamma`, `V=beta delta`.
3. `B01` is a separate transformation: an exact square ratio factoring into four terms gives only a **product-square** receiver.
4. `A02` and `A03` are downstream support/compatibility methods. They are not consequences of product-square alone.

Firewall: `product-square != each factor square`.

## Squareclass abstraction

`A02` survives as a method turning exact pairwise gcd identities into a bounded **dynamic reservoir incidence table** with separate odd-prime and 2-adic bookkeeping. `A03` survives as a method turning complete pair-product squareclass relations into an edge-labelled compatibility graph and solving the vertex squareclasses in terms of independent reservoirs.

Neither method proves that the live reservoirs are supported on a fixed finite set of primes. Therefore neither alone reaches the finite exhaustive branch-family output required by `S34-W01`.

## Gaussian abstraction

The three concrete face factorizations `D03` are Stage35-specific payload; the underlying use of `Z[i]` is classical. The surviving abstraction is only `A04`: when two **genuinely distinct, source-locked orientations** factor the same norm datum, normalize the quotient after controlling unit and conjugation ambiguity and extract the joint quadratic-field squareclass condition. A mirrored orientation related by a source symmetry is not an independent receiver.

Firewall: `Gaussian factorization != new obstruction` and `necessary norm/squareclass condition != global contradiction`.

## Elliptic / Kummer abstraction

The Kummer sequence remains decomposed:

- `A05`: exact involution quotient + fixed/boundary-locus accounting + converse reconstruction;
- `A06`: restore the rational-source lift predicate before choosing a Kummer normal form;
- `A07`: internalize remaining source squares and complete the finite Kummer receiver;
- `A08`: compress a completed reciprocal receiver by exact common-factor identities and inverse scaling maps.

These are not merged: quotient, source-faithful lift, completion, and compression have different input/output contracts. `B07` remains only partially abstractable: diagonal high-genus families with exact sign involutions can yield elliptic quotient inventories, but quotient structure does not control specialization-new points. Exact quartic-to-elliptic birational certification remains the separate `S31-W01` responsibility.

Firewalls: `finite quotient != rational point classification`, `genus-one/elliptic model != MW closure`, `generic function-field information != all-specialization theorem`.

## Picard / Brauer abstraction

`B03` and `B04` are only partially abstractable because their validity depends on an exact source-marked full Picard G-module. Their reusable portions are respectively: exact integral marked-G-module identification, and group cohomology of that **already certified** proper Picard module. These strongly overlap `S33-PW04` / `S33-PW07` and are not final-new-weapon decisions in Harvest 2.

The proper result cannot be relabelled as the open result. `F03` similarly requires one exact global unramified class with source-bound local evaluations before any Brauer-Manin credit.
